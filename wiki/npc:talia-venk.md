**Name.** Talia Venk

**Lineage.** Splicer

**Age.** 29

**Height and Weight.** 5'9", 153 lbs

**Pronouns.** they/them, it/its

**Affiliation.** V.V. Incorporated

**Features.** Cute tail, sensitive nose, has a collar

**Status.** Alive

Talia Venk is always at [Miranda Vastholm](/npc:miranda-vastholm)'s beck and call. They're bonded in a way most couldn't even fathom—they operate for Miranda and themselves only. Besotted is not merely a word for them. Though they're often lightly clothed, reports of them being seen in gleaming armor and fighting in the underground arena exist. If they're not with Miranda directly, you can bet they have eyes or constant contact with her regardless. They are incredibly smart, organized and ferociously loyal to those they love and care for, though they rarely speak to anyone other than Miranda herself.
