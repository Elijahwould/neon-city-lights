##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Summon Fiend)

*6th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 90 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (some idiot's social media password and a RUBY microprojector worth $$60,000 credits)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You manifest a virus in a hardlight profile. It appears in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. This hardlight form uses the Virus.MNFST stat block. When you cast the tech, choose Payload, Encryption, or Clone. The creature resembles a virus of the chosen type, which determines which varietal enhances its statblock. The creature disappears when it drops to 0 hit points or when the tech ends.

The creature is an ally to you and your companions. In combat, the creature shares your initiative count, but it takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don’t issue any, it takes the Dodge action and uses its move to avoid danger.
