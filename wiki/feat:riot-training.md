##### Source: [Feats for the Fearsome](https://www.patreon.com/posts/feats-for-16156471) (Veteran Hoplite)

Through rigorous training, you have become exceptionally skilled when wielding a baton or spear in one hand and a shield in the other. While you are wielding both a shield and a baton or spear you gain the following benefits:

- Your weapon's damage die increases to 1d8, if it was lower.
- When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can use your bonus action to make one additional attack with a baton or spear you are holding.
- While you are adjacent to one or more friendly creatures who are also holding shields, and are not restrained or unconscious, you can use your action to form a shield wall. Doing so grants you and any of those creatures 3/4 cover. This cover remains as long as two or more shielded creatures in the shield wall remain adjacent to one another.
