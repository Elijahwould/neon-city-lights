**Corporation Name.** Orgasmic Artifice

**CEO.** Anna Ziegley

**Years in business.** ~20

**Industry.** Intimacy aides and other explicit products

**Motto.** *"Reaching new heights of joy with your fan favorite toys."*

**Impact.** Subsidiary ([Ignorance](/corp:ignorance))

A corporation originally founded by a former Spicy Athlete, Orgasmic Artifice was bought out by Bliss Co. five years before the fall of the tower. In the aftermath, Kelly Climax, who still holds a legacy managerial role, passed ownership of the company to former PACT correspondent Anna Ziegley, as the company was re-subsidized by the forming [Ignorance](/corp:ignorance). OA specialized in high end adult intimacy aides and other explicit products with a near-monopoly on toys based on other Spicy Athletes.

Their testing programs were expansive, with warehouses full of testers subjected to long-term tactile feedback experiments, chemically maintained and fed through drips. The testers were paid well, although with the extensive nature of the former programs, it was rare for them to last a week, and unheard of for them to last past two months with sound minds. Under their new parent company, OA operates much the same, with a greater focus on testers longevity and wellbeing, though these efforts have bred rumors of addictive additives in the drip feeds… Luckily, Miss Ziegley's experience and still active role in the now Ignorance-aligned Partnered Autonomous Commercial Title of HentaiHub has made addressing these concerns a piece of well-tested and user-accommodating cake.
