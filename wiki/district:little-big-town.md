**District.** Little Big Town

**Population.** ?

**Type.** Residential

A small district in the southeast of Neon City, where people of much larger size live. This is the home of Kaiju Alley, where a large concentration of Kaijuborn live. It is also home to many New Humans, Remakes, Elfs, and Orccs.

This specific area possesses almost everything the downtown residential holds, only in plus size to support those on a scale larger than most. Notable places of interest consist of a bar similar to Poncy's, well-known for their smallest serving cup being a whopping 32 ounces. (Which, when you think about it, isn't all that big to a Kaiju, for example.)

Multiple clothing retail stores are present, each with attire supporting those up to thirty feet tall, the signs proudly state!

This is where [Maeve](/npc:maeve) currently resides, thanks to Azeria, Katze, and Tobi Greene's efforts of not only providing a safe home, but a supply of income to afford her cost of living.
