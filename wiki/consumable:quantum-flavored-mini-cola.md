*Consumable (soda), uncommon*

Often adding onto the default red and white packaging, or simply tinting the white color, Quantum Cola's™ assorted Flavored Mini-Colas come in a variety of flavors to modify that iconic, dark, sugary fizz, and one drinkable, if disappointing, reduced size. Market trends suggest city-favorite flavors to be Nano-Cherry and Limebot Lick, but you might also find Crystal Clear, Giga-Ginger, Radical Razzberry, or more!

Mini-Cola bubbles with a faint pastel of varied colors when agitated, but the carbonation fades too quickly to properly chug, and the can is too small to sip over time. You have to have another!

You regain 2d8+2 hit points, cease to be poisoned, and are cured of any disease when you drink this Mini-Cola. Mini-Colas can be bought in four-packs for $$44,000, but such packs are considered a Quantum™ classics item and cannot be sold or refunded.
