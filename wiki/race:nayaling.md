## "Well, there's like a coinflip's chance that calling them horny is *actually* offensive."

##### Source: Player's Handbook, [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/the-nayaling-race), Neon City NightsThanks to reny#8234 for the design and lore overhaul!

Similar to Gobbos, Nayalings are a last-ditch experiment by Old World scientists to improve the human species for the rapidly changing climate, but unlike Gobbos, Nayalings were bred to out-live and conquer the rest of humanity under the extreme weather conditions that were rocking the world in the ages after the Great Collapse. Not only were Nayalings developed from beastial DNA splices, they were the first attempt of aggressive nanotechnical alteration.

Their prehensile tails allow for excellent balance and the ability to grip to sturdy objects during tropical storms, while their hooves grant them mountain goat-like traversal through difficult terrain. Their horns cut an imposing image which their strength can readily back up. The extreme bioalteration and high sync rate of their nanites grants them technical gifts and further durability in their intended environments.

While the Old World nation that created Nayalings is long forgotten, and their attempt to conquer humanity since ended, rumors still proliferate around them regarding their alien appearance and as such Nayalings are still treated with suspicion and wariness. A wariness that has no doubt only redoubled since the return of the Thulians to common perceptions.

### Roleplaying a Nayaling

When roleplaying a Nayaling, keep in mind that they are often treated with suspicion, largely due to silly and misplaced rumors. Still, lifelong sideways glances and whispers have had a profound effect on Nayalings. Some try their hardest to blend in with the crowd, wearing earthy colors, living quiet, unassuming lives or taking jobs that rely on stealth, darkness and subterfuge, while others take every effort to stand out with loud, extroverted personalities, bright colors and skimpy clothes, relishing in the attention. Do you prefer to keep yourself on the down low? If your personality is loud, is it to the contrary of rumors, or living right up to them? Or do you possibly ride a very thin and dangerous line of every possible expectation?

### Nayaling Naming Conventions

Nayalings name their children after sensations, either audio-visual, olfactory or tactile, often incorporating the color of their skin, which ranges from deep reds and blues to pastel pinks, purples, and cyans. Nayalings don’t have typical family names, rather using two or three words as their full names, shortening it to the first word. The bizarre naming convention often leaves them with nicknames given by colleagues and adopted by themselves.

Some examples are Velvet Smooth, Crush Atwixt Digits, Amethyst Grasp, Ruby Stained Pitch, Lavender Quivering Lips, Tyrian Stalwart Stance, Whiff Of Perfume, Teal On Sand, Swindle Rouge, etc.

# Nayaling Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1, or increase three different scores by 1.
- **Age.** Nayalings reach maturity at 18. Nayalings tend to live as long as New Humans, with a few exceptions.
- **Size.** Your size is Medium. Nayalings are between 5 and 7 feet tall.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Geohazardous Bioengine.** You were designed with overcoming the threats of a specific environment in mind. You know and can cast the *[energy converter](/tech:energy-converter)* tech with this trait, except that when cast in this way, the tech triggers off of and can only target a singular damage type as decided by your caprinae, even if the tech could not normally target those damage types. You can cast the tech with this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest. You can also cast this tech, without the singular damage type restriction, using tech slots you have of the appropriate level.
- **Huge Horns.** Your horns are natural weapons, which you can use to make unarmed strikes. If you hit with them, you deal piercing damage equal to 1d6 + your Strength modifier, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike. You also have access to the following two traits:
  - **Headbutt.** Immediately after you hit a creature with a melee attack as a part of the Attack action on your turn, you can use a bonus action to attempt to shove that target with your horns. The target must be within 5 feet of you and no more than one size larger than you. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw against a DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier, you push it up to 10 feet away from you.
  - **Bullrush.** Immediately after you use the Dash action on your turn and move at least 20 feet, you can make one melee attack with your horns as a bonus action. If you hit with them, you deal your horns' damage and can use your Headbutt against the target as a free action instead of a bonus action.
- **Prehensile Tail.** Nayaling tails are effectively a third arm, though not quite as capable as your actual hands. Your tail cannot be used to wield weapons or shields, activate technical items, provide material components, use techcasting foci, or initiate a grapple. In addition, you do not gain benefits from technical items held in these appendages. The tail can be used like a hand in every other capacity and you can interact with a second object or feature of the environment for free during your turn.
- **Sure Footed.** You have advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws made against effects that would knock you prone.
- **Extremophile.** As a Nayaling, you were designed to thrive and conquer in the harshest of environments. You automatically succeed on any save prompted by an extreme climate as described in chapter 5 of the Dungeon Master's Guide.

## Nayaling Caprinae

### Carpathian

Probably the most common caprinae of Nayalings, the Carpathians live up to a few tropes that they themselves inadvertently set. They tend to be softer, pastel colors with a lighter tone, their horns curl simply up and back, their assets equally well defined, and they fit the image of a loud Nayaling relishing in the attention their presence provides.

- **Snug.** You know the *[hovering helping hand](/tech:hovering-helping-hand)* trick. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the *[flame vent](/tech:flame-vent)* tech with this trait. When you reach 5th level, you can cast the *[hardlight burning blade](/tech:hardlight-burning-blade)* tech with this trait. Casting these techs with this trait doesn't require material components. Once you cast either of these techs with this trait, you can’t cast that tech with it again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast these techs using tech slots you have of the appropriate level. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your techcasting ability for these techs when you cast them with this trait (choose when you select this race).
- **Pyrohazardous Fuel.** The variation of the *[energy converter](/tech:energy-converter)* tech cast from your geohazardous bioengine trait triggers off of and targets fire damage.

### Pyrenean

A fair counter to the Carpathians, Pyrenean Nayalings tend to fill the countering social niche of an individual most interested in downplaying their presences. They tend to be of darker and duller hues, their horns are much more vertical with a right-angled bend back, their figure tends to favor either the top or the bottom, and with their technical gifts, there seems to be far less of them than there might actually be.

- **Sneaky.** You know the *[hardlight chicanery](/tech:hardlight-chicanery)* trick. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the *[palette swap](/tech:palette-swap)* tech with this trait. Starting at 5th level, you can cast the *[physical cloaking](/tech:physical-cloaking)* tech once with this trait. Casting these techs with this trait doesn’t require material components. Once you cast either of these techs with this trait, you can’t cast that tech with it again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast these techs using tech slots you have of the appropriate level. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your techcasting ability for these techs when you cast them with this trait (choose when you select this lineage).
- **Necrohazardous Fuel.** The variation of the *[energy converter](/tech:energy-converter)* tech cast from your geohazardous bioengine trait triggers off of and targets entropic damage.

### Kamorian

Nayalings of the Kamorian caprinae catch attention not for their being Nayalings, but for their sheer beauty, being the most stand-out of all Nayalings despite not being as loud as Carpathians. Their body's color tends to be faintly patterned in a phenomena similar to vitiligo, their horns have a gentle corkscrew shape, and they tend to be large in every meaning of the word.

- **Gorgeous.** You know the *[compliment fishing](/tech:compliment-fishing)* trick. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the *[meet cute](/tech:meet-cute)* tech with this trait. Starting at 5th level, you can cast the *[subliminal message](/tech:subliminal-message)* tech with this trait. Casting these techs with this trait doesn’t require material components. Once you cast either of these techs with this trait, you can’t cast that tech with it again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast these techs using tech slots you have of the appropriate level. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your techcasting ability for these techs when you cast them with this trait (choose when you select this lineage).
- **Neurohazardous Fuel.** The variation of the *[energy converter](/tech:energy-converter)* tech cast from your geohazardous bioengine trait triggers off of and targets software damage.

### Maduraian

Almost rivaling the size of the Kamorian Nayalings with their musculature alone, Maduraian Nayalings tend more towards anger or irritation than demure beauty. Their hues tend to be simple but sometimes clash as they develop secondary full-body coloration, their horns sweep out to either side and forward, and their features tend to be bolstered by the strength of their frames.

- **Threatening.** You know the *[spooky bullshit](/tech:spooky-bullshit)* trick. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the *[exothermal strike](/tech:exothermal-strike)* tech with this trait. Starting at 5th level, you can cast the *[neon strike](/tech:neon-strike)* tech with this trait. Casting these techs with this trait doesn’t require material components. Once you cast either of these techs with this trait, you can’t cast that tech with it again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast these techs using tech slots you have of the appropriate level. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your techcasting ability for these techs when you cast them with this trait (choose when you select this lineage).
- **Photohazardous Fuel.** The variation of the *[energy converter](/tech:energy-converter)* tech cast from your geohazardous bioengine trait triggers off of and targets neon damage.

### Himalayan

The Himalayan Nayalings are set apart by an odd mutation of their hair follicles, as they tend to grow patches of fur and neck fluff far more often than they develop odd pigmentations. Their bodily colors tend to deviate the least from human skin tones, their horns curl over themselves, and they tend to either be tall and lithe, or short and stacked, with laidback or docile personalities to match.

- **Relaxed.** You know the *[hydroscript](/tech:hydroscript)* trick. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the *[carapace of kelvin](/tech:carapace-of-kelvin)* tech with this trait. Starting at 5th level, you can cast the *[nightfall](/tech:nightfall)* tech with this trait. Casting these techs with this trait doesn't require material components.
