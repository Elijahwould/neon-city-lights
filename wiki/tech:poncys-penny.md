##### Source: Acquisitions Inc. (Jim's Glowing Coin)

*2nd-level conception*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** S, M (a coin, $$200, which is consumed as tax for using the tech)

**Duration:** 1 minute

When you cast the tech, you hurl the coin that is the tech's material component to any spot within range. The coin lights up as if under the effect of a *[light transfer](/techs:light-transfer)* tech. Each creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet of the coin must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be distracted for the duration. While distracted, a creature has disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and initiative rolls.
