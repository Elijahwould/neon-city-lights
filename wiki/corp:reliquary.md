**Corporation Name.** Chasse Artifact Acquisition and Archival Co. (alias The Reliquary)

**CEO.** *Unknown*

**Years in business.** ~220+

**Industry.** Acquisition, recovery, conservation and control of old-world artifacts, technology and knowledge

**Motto.** *"We are watching."*

**Impact.** Major

A company founded long time ago, primarily concerned with the acquisition and archival of the relics discovered during the mid-13th century. They are mostly a shadowy organization, not particularly interested in hogging the public light, instead preferring to deal mostly with contractors and runners to maximize deniability to the public. They are one of few corporations to do no advertising, as they are not interested in the clamor that comes with being a large public entity—that's not how they make their money. While they maintain incredibly detailed records, very little about them is known outside of their own corporate offices.

They are, in part, responsible for the original methods of collection and cataloguing of old-world technology that helped to further prevent the world's rapid spiral into what was becoming possibly world war. Since their founding, access to cultural relics and technology has come at a steadier pace, their introductions allowing for great advances in the state of the world—but always in a controlled, deliberate fashion.

They seem to have a headquarters based in the Neon City, in the vicinity of what used to be the Osaka HQ. Their primary area of operations seems to be a smattering of compounds out in the desert wastelands, as well as several compounds along the bays to the north, as well as one major installation out to the western sea. They spend much of their time currently recovering old world artifacts that were sunken millennia ago but have not made any announcement as to what exactly they've found.

Their long-standing CEO, [Maximillian Tark](/npc:max-raynor), held the position since their founding over 220 years ago, but was recently replaced by their prior CFO, [Matías Rojas](/npc:matias-rojas). Strangely enough, he seems to have fallen ill in recent times, and the current CEO seems to be a stand-in, with rumors being that Mr. Tark is no longer interested in the position.
