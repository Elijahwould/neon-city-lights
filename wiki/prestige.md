# Prestige Levels and Ranks

After undergoing the rigorous processes necessary to relearn the basics and alter your course fundamentally as a character, your character advancement is handled a little differently. When you undergo Prestige, you permanently make one class a **Prestige Class**. A Prestige Class is a permanent secondary class whose class features you inherit from levels 1 through 10.

You obtain your first rank in your Prestige class immediately upon hitting Scrub and inherit the features for its level.

You must be a certain rank before you can advance your Prestige Class, as shown in the Prestige Leveling Table. Your Prestige Class is taken from any base class, and also inherits the features of any subclass you acquire through it, but **you cannot choose a Prestige Class from a class you have already taken**. You may only advance in your Prestige Class when you spend MP on it.

You gain the following from your Prestige Class:

- Techs Known
- Armor, Weapon and Tools proficiencies
- One of its skills
