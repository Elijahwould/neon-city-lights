##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Armor of Invulnerability)

*Armor ([polysteel](/armor:polysteel)), legendary (requires installation)*

**Specs:** 6 lb., $$6,000,000

You have resistance to nontechnical damage while you wear this armor. Additionally, you can use an action to make yourself immune to nontechnical damage for 10 minute or until you are no longer wearing the armor. Once this special action is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
