**Corporation Name.** Ishtar Reserve

**CEO.** Dexter Nukléor-Acirad

**Years in Business.** ~90+

**Industry.** Covert security services, military-grade cybernetics and biological augmentations

**Motto.** "*Protecting your interests. Ishtar.*"

**Impact.** Minor

A secretive faction within the hidden backrooms of the corporate world, it is only recently that the Reserve has gone public with some of their wares. Standing at the helm of cutting-edge bionics is the supposed founder of Ishtar, Mr. Acirad, and if they are to be believed, Ishtar has been producing the latest in tailored cybernetic upgrades and services for a premium clientele for well close to a century.

The Ishtar Reserve does not currently own public stores or facilities. Instead, Ishtar prefers to lease their product lines to other contracted sellers—save for people with heavier corporate affiliations, who may find themselves able to purchase a direct "tasting", installation and/or delivery of their wares. With the recent upheaval of corporate hierarchies after the fall of [Bliss Co.](/corp:bliss-co), the interest in Ishtar's covert security services became all the more lucrative to protect their own interests, which the Reserve happily provides.

In later days, almost in tandem with the public revelation of their presence, more and more edgerunners have begun to pour into the dimly lit fixer bars of Neon City claiming to have once worked for the prestigious corp. When those stories have rung true—most often by battle-chassis remakes—they speak of off-grid boot camps held in hidden digital realms, Undercity and topside labs alike, with friends made in the training grounds turned into metal-hearted killers.

Supporting the creation of consciousness chips is another well-known endeavor of Ishtar, openly stating their desire to let people have a chance at life eternal. In turn, it has been revealed that the Reserve has long been behind the widespread origin of several models of remakes, only recently going forward with their in-house designs as official products available to their usual customers.

Even before going public, budding competition with [Armata](/corp:armata) and [Axigon Arms](/corp:axigon-arms) has now grown into obvious marketing schemes at the corporate floors. Keen tech eyes have also noticed Ishtar's products secretly interferes with the functions of their oppositions' merchandise, making it necessary to either enter into Reserve exclusive contracts or jailbreak their wares like most runners and outlaws, the latter not being all too popular in elite circles where you change implants like you change clothes.
