**Location Name.** Neon City Police Department

**Owner/Leader.** Commissioner Terrence Masters

**Location Type.** Government

**Years in existence.** ~300

**Type.** Law Enforcement

**Area.** [Neon City Downtown](/district:downtown)

**Impact.** Major

The beatcops. The pigs. The bitches in blue. A variety of names but mean all the same, the NCPD's organization is a far cry from what one might consider a police force to be. To nobody's surprise, the corruption within the ranks of the NCPD is far too obvious. From the 'mandatory donation stops' to enhanced frisk procedures, the NCPD act with little regard for the concerns of the people. While on paper they are a paramilitary force independent of other brands and corporate ties, they receive very generous donations from corporate entities, which in turn determines the sectors they patrol most heavily from day to day.

The public servants they fashion themselves after do not employ a standard uniform that is one size fits all. Indeed, a variety of the force is oversexed and well endowed, often using it to their advantage with either those desperate to avoid a ticket, those trying to avoid the mandatory donation that is often financial in structure, or simply walking up to the horniest crowd and having their pick. And in Neon City, that's just about everyone.

While a lot of the donation money is spent lining pockets, much of it is also spent funding the lavish lifestyles and future war culture that the NCPD finds themselves worshipping the most. This is padded with the various 'gifts' received from major corporate entities. This includes sports vehicles from [Kazanari Automotive](/corp:kazanari-automotive) that are street illegal for civilian use, and [Armata Solutions](/corp:armata) armaments that the force is often decked out in. And they regularly seek opportunities to use these, even when the justification is minimal at best.

Those that find themselves on the wrong side of the law are often not taken to a traditional jail cell but hauled away and thrown into an Undercity penitentiary. While leaving the Undercity is just an elevator ride away, those that do have a limited view of the penitentiary, giving them access to work the server farms, factories, and product testing. There is a limited understanding of the word 'penitentiary', but there is a mandatory work program that allows one to walk a couple months out. At that point, they may choose to return to life or volunteer to stay behind for guaranteed work that some might even envy.

After all, those products don't test themselves.
