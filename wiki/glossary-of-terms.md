This page has been constructed for the purposes of easing confusion surrounding the glossary of updated terms that Neon City Nights uses to flavor the setting as a post-apocalyptic cyberpunk dystopic utopia. As the world in this setting is highly advanced to the degree that technology is virtually indistinguishable from what we today would consider magic, it leads to a lot of shifting of language to adapt a system based around myth and fantasy into one revolving around more techno-fantasy—as such, we wanted to provide a handy-dandy guide!
