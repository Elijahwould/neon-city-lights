A rechargeable portable electric lamp. It casts bright light in a 30-foot radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet. It lasts 6 hours on a full charge and takes about 10 minutes to fully recharge if plugged into a power outlet, or about an hour if left in direct sunlight.
