## "Listen to the radio, don't you remember? We built this city."

The City Synergy is primarily concerned with the citizenry, commerce, traffic and even architecture of Neon City. To an Ordinator of the City, the center of modern life is a sense and spirit of community. A more freelance approach to this may consider the gravest enemies of the city to be those who seek to harm the common weal of its citizens—whoever that might be. A more corporate or rebellious approach might see the opposing side as the primary source of turmoil for the city, instead.

##### Source: Unearthed Arcana: Modern 5E (City)

### Bonus Trick

When you choose this synergy at 1st level, you gain the *[on/off](/tech:on-off)* trick in addition to your chosen tricks.

### Bonus Proficiencies

Also starting at 1st level, you gain proficiency with civilian and military ranged weapons, and proficiency with vehicles (land).

### Heart of the City

From 1st level, you are able to tap into the spirit of community found in the city. While you are within any city, you can gain advantage on a single Charisma (Deception, Intimidation or Persuasion) check, and are considered proficient in the appropriate skill. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1). You regain any expended uses when you finish a long rest.

### A.I. Assistance: Whispers in the City

Starting at 2nd level, you can ask your A.I. for assistance to reach out to the city's remaining fragments of memory for aid. As an action, you can reach out to the city, and any city utility within 30 feet of you either works perfectly or shuts down entirely for 1 minute (your choice).

Additionally, each hostile creature within 30 feet of you must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is knocked prone or restrained (your choice) by hazards, such as entangling wires, high pressure water erupting for hydrants, pavement collapsing into unseet potholes, and so on. A restrained creature can escape by making a successful Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check against your tech save DC.

This effect is entirely local and affects only utilities within 30 feet of you. Determination of what utilities are available within range and how the physical effects of those utilities manifest are left to the fixer.

### Block Watch

Starting at 6th level, your awareness while in the city extends preternaturally. While in an urban environment, you are considered proficient in the Insight and Perception skills, and you add double your proficiency bonus to Wisdom (Insight) and Wisdom (Perception) checks, instead of your normal proficiency bonus.

### Power of the City

At 8th level, you gain the ability to infuse your weapon strikes with energy drawn from the very essence of the city's thoughts. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can cause the attack to deal an extra 1d8 software damage to the target. When you reach 14th level, the extra software damage increases to 2d8.

### Express Transit

At 17th level, you can use mass transit route to transport instantaneously to other points in the city. Starting from a bus stop, train station, subway stop or other suitable mass transit site within the city, you can teleport to any other similar transit stop within the city, as if you had cast a *[transmit](/tech:transmit)* tech whose destination is a permanent *[transmission beacon](/tech:transmission-beacon)* you know. Once you use this feature, you must finish a short or long rest before using it again.
