##### Source: Player's Handbook (Contact Other Plane)

*5th-level observation (script)*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V

**Duration:** 1 minute

You mentally contact a powerful AI, virus, or hacked over the NET. Contacting this inhuman intelligence can strain or even break your mind. When you cast this tech , make a DC 15 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, you take 6d6 software damage and are insane until you finish a long rest. While insane, you can’t take actions, can’t understand what other creatures say, can’t read, and speak only in gibberish. A *[primary system flush](/tech:primary-system-flush)* tech cast on you ends this effect.

On a successful save, you can ask the entity up to five questions. You must ask your questions before the tech ends. The fixer answers each question with one word, such as "yes," "no," "maybe," "never," "irrelevant," or "unclear" (if the entity doesn’t know the answer to the question). If a one-word answer would be misleading, the fixer might instead offer a short phrase as an answer.
