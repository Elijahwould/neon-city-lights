**Organization Name.** Lex Talionis

**Leader.** Carmilla Viszla

**Organization Type.** Criminal

**Years in existence.** <1

**Goals.** Ensure the safety of those without living in [the Undercity](/undercity).

**Creed.** *"An eye for an eye, a life for a life."*

**Impact.** Minor

Founded when [the Arcanum](/loc:arcanum) changed hands, the Lex was founded with the idea that what Lucienne did there would not happen again. As such, they behave similar to a neighborhood watch on steroids—if someone targets lower class individuals in their territory, they will target that individual as reprisal.

Currently its member count is low but growing.
