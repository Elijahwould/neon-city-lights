##### Source: Player's Handbook (Armor of Agathys)

*1st-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S, M (a cup of water)

**Duration:** 1 hour

A protective force surrounds you, manifesting as a spectral frost that covers you and your gear. You gain 5 temporary hit points for the duration. If a creature hits you with a melee attack while you have these hit points, the creature takes 5 cold damage.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 2nd level or higher, both the temporary hit points and the cold damage increase by 5 for each slot.
