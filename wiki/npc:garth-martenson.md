**Name.** Garth Martenson

**Lineage.** Human

**Age.** 50

**Height and Weight.** 6'2", 210 lbs.

**Pronouns.** he/him

**Affiliation.** [N.C.A.P.S.](/org:ncaps)

**Features.** Tall, shaggy, wears a trench coat over matte-black armor, aviator shades

**Status.** Alive

Garth is what you’d imagine what the cross between a surfer and a metal head might look like in a cyberpunk future. Featuring shaggy brown hair, a permanent nine o’clock shadow over a tight, square jaw and wearing a pair of aviator shades, a large black trench coat sits over murdered out, matte-black polysteel armor. The frame of this large, burly man is pretty impressive, and yet despite the gruff exterior, the big guy exudes a bit of charm.

Clearly seeming more interested in talking shop than having to really put the hurt on anyone, that does not mean he’s incapable. On his back is a wicked-looking polearm that looks old world, even for the old world. He carries what looks to be a sledgehammer on a toolbelt affixed at his waist, along with many other small odds and ends.

Garth is well-acquainted with [Rucker](/npc:rucker-beauregard), the man he usually sends people on missions to go haggle with out in the desert wastelands out in the east side of town.

Garth's background is not well-known, but there are rumors that he was once a corporate guard for Quantum Cola. He does not appreciate it when people ask about that information, and actively rebukes runners who prod into his past.
