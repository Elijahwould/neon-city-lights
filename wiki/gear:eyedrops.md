As an action, you may apply eyedrops to your eyes. For the next 10 minutes after application, you do not suffer disadvantage on sight-based skill checks due to exhaustion (such as Wisdom (Perception) to spot something with your eyes, or an Intelligence check made with hacking tools to notice a change in some written code). A typical bottle of eyedrops has enough liquid for ten uses.
