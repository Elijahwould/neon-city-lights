## "Have you seen my hardlight projector anywhere? …It's called Rudolph now and wants to play digital rugby? I see."

##### Source: [Unearthed Arcana](https://dnd.wizards.com/unearthed-arcana/travelers-multiverse), Neon City NightsAll of @reny, @pikavoca, @phase_beam, @aluereon and @celicaxs worked on these; special thanks to Pika for taking care of a lot of the work on Matter Core!

This lineage is currently in playtesting. This splits the Hardlight Core lineage into a Hardlight side and SLUDGE side, with the inclusion of a Matter version (based on Surges) coming as well.

This is currently otherwise roughly just like the existing Hardlight Core but split into sublineages!

### Roleplaying a Core

Do you have fragments of recollection of your previous duties, before you became what you are now, in this projector? Were you originally part of a hive AI that split off, now consumed by a curiosity for the environments and endeavors around you? Or did those who have passed by you enlighten you to becoming something more, yourself? You understand the city is unkind, and it isn’t often you will find comfort.

With the ability to morph into a replica of any medium or small creature, a Core may keep close track of every species it has ever encountered. It may find that one day, it needs—or wants—to become one.

### Core Naming Conventions

After taking a more physical form, a Core may find themselves attracted to the numerous sounds that reference someone's name, choosing to take on the name of something or someone they've come to learn.

Some examples include Jo Gurngam, Emmy Array, Saturn Ringer, Skies Above, Diamies Rojes, Murky Emerald, Cocobutter, Duul Kovak, GilGilGil, McManus, Adem Davis, Aatzer Pitche, Freddy Lubarg, etc.

# Core Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1, or increase three different scores by 1.
- **Age.** Cores do not age in any traditional sense, but they tend to burn out their projectors every ten years, so some count their age by the decade on replacements.
- **Size.** You are Small or Medium, selected when you choose this lineage.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Core Resilience.** You’re not organic, and don’t suffer from the issues most humanoids do.
  - You have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned and you have resistance to poison damage.
  - You are immune to disease.
  - You don’t need to eat, drink or breathe, though can choose to.
  - You can choose to carry a spare battery with you to negate the need to spend an amount of hours inactive, as determined by your stereopticon, to gain the benefits of a long rest. You can only make use of a spare battery once a long rest, and a negated inactivity period does not restore it.
- **Core Stabilizer.** The stereoptical projection-core that functions as your heart, brain, and namesake is outfitted with various magnetic photorelays keyed to humanoid form. You appear as you wish, even totally organic, though perhaps of obvious core nature, but must have two arms, two legs, a central torso, and a head. You can spend one minute integrating a weapon or piece of armor directly into your core, projecting the equipment like a sense tank does with a digital avatar or creating tethered recreations within your body. Equipment worn or wielded in this way cannot be removed against your will and can be uninstalled from your core as an action.
  - This trait is necessary for the Photorealism, Display Redefinition, Hololight Variance, Radiant, Amorphous Cling, Amorphous Body, Shifting Nature, Emergency Shift and Vivacious Redefinition traits, which do not function without it.
- **Photorealism.** As an action, you can redefine the visage maintained by your core, changing your appearance and voice. You determine the specifics of the changes, including your coloration, hair length, and sex. You can also adjust your height and weight, so much so that you can change between the Medium and Small size categories. You can make yourself appear as a member of another lineage, though none of your game statistics change and you are limited to the humanoid shape described in your core stabilizer.

### Hardlight

Projections that are bound only by their own imagination, the Hardlight Core has no specific limitation to what single entity it can morph into, capable of transforming itself entirely with every recalibration. A Hardlight Core often changes shape often, sometimes doing so to imitate the last interesting thing it found—for instance, observing a fascinating species from afar—to taking a more pragmatic approach to its form selection, such as discovering a location restricted to only Elfs will require it take an Elf shape to proceed without suspicion.

Unknown circumstances led to these simple projectors becoming sentient Hardlight Cores at will, with little to no resistance from the megacity as a whole. After all, there were always stranger things happening. The most accepted theory as to why Hardlight Cores came to be is that an errant, underdeveloped or fragmented piece of AI winds up finding its way into a hardlight projector, and with the limitations and perspective that comes with its inhabited shell, it developed its own method of navigating its new environment.

- **Robits and Bytes.** For the purposes of observation techs and creature type detection abilities, you read as machine in addition to humanoid.
- **Low Power Mode.** You can finish a long rest in 4 hours if you spend those hours in an inactive, motionless state in an area with sunlight or UV light, during which you retain consciousness.
- **Display Redefinition.** When you change your visage with photorealism, you can obscure the fact that you are a hardlight core and can even duplicate the appearance of others with no obvious tells. You cannot duplicate the appearance of a creature you've never seen, and when duplicating an appearance, a creature can see through it with an active insight check. The DC for this check is 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier.
- **Hololight Variance.** As a bonus action, you can disable the electromagnetic field that stabilizes your body into tangible photomass, or reenable said field. While the field is disabled, you are more hologram than hardlight, and gain the following traits.
  - You can move through a space as narrow as five inches wide without squeezing.
  - You have advantage on ability checks to avoid or escape a grapple or restraint.
  - Your walking speed increases by 10 feet and you gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed.
  - You drop any weapons or armor that are not integrated into your stabilizer and cannot use or benefit from any weapons or armor or cast any techs with material or somatic components.
- **Radiant.** As a bonus action, you can adjust your brightness setting and radiate bright light for 30 feet and dim light for an additional 30 feet. You continue to radiate light until you take a bonus action to lower your brightness setting.

### SLUDGE

- **Biochemical Caution.** For the purposes of observation techs and creature type detection abilities, you read as hazard in addition to humanoid.
  - Additionally, you have the ability to communicate in a limited manner with hazards. They can understand the meaning of your words, though you have no special ability to understand them in return.
- **Amorphous Cling.** You can move through a space as narrow as five inches wide without squeezing as long as you are carrying and wearing nothing that isn't integrated into your stabilizer. You can drop and doff every non-stabilizer linked item you are carrying and wearing as part of moving through a space that would normally require you to squeeze. You also have advantage on ability checks to initiate, avoid, or escape a grapple.
- **Vivacious Redefinition.** When you change your visage with photorealism, you are not bound by the body shape outlined in core stabilizer and you can redefine your visage without using an action as long as you are not incapacitated, but you are always visibly and obviously of SLUDGE nature.
- **Pseudopod.** As a bonus action, you can extrude or retract a pseudopod. A pseudopod can carry no more than 10 pounds and cannot activate or gain the held benefits of technical items. A pseudopod has 10 feet reach, allowing you to make object interactions and attacks at that range. While you have a pseudopod extruded, your unarmed attacks deal acid damage and when you take damage, you can retract your pseudopod as a reaction to reduce the damage you took by 2d4. You can reduce damage with this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all uses on a long rest.
- **Enveloping Hunger.** SLUDGE is designed to feed, but you are hungry. Once per turn, when you would make a grapple check against a creature, you can instead attempt to engulf the target, causing them to make their choice of a Strength of Dexterity saving throw. A creature can choose to fail this save. The DC for this saving throw is 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier, and any numerical bonus to your unarmed attacks applies as a bonus to this DC. On a failed save, the target is grappled and while grappled in this way it is restrained and takes 1d6 acid damage at the beginning of each of its turns. If the target dies while engulfed, you can expend 1 hit die, rolling it, and regaining hit points equal to the roll plus your constitution modifier.
  - When you initiate a grapple in this way and at the start of each of your turns, you can choose to prevent the target from suffering the penalties of the grappled and restrained conditions and grant it 1d4 temporary hit points instead of dealing damage at the start of the target's turn.

### Matter

- **Human Element.** For the purposes of observation techs and creature type detection abilities, you read as surge in addition to humanoid.
  - Additionally, you have the ability to communicate in a limited manner with surges. They can understand the meaning of your words, though you have no special ability to understand them in return.
- **Amorphous Body.** You can move through a space as narrow as five inches wide without squeezing as long as you are carrying and wearing nothing that isn't integrated into your stabilizer. You can drop and doff every non-stabilizer linked item you are carrying and wearing as part of moving through a space that would normally require you to squeeze.
- **Shifting Nature.** Choose one of the following options when you finish a long rest. This becomes your phase of matter until the next time you take a long rest, where you will be able to choose again.
  - ***Liquid.*** You gain resistance to either acid or software damage. While in this form, you may meld with any liquid surfaces you are nearby as a bonus action, granting you a swimming speed equal to your walking speed. If you already have a swimming speed from another source, instead gain 10ft of additional swimming speed. By melding in this way, you are unable to be knocked prone as a result of slipping and falling. As an action, you may fully meld into a surface, spreading your senses out across the surface, granting you advantage on any checks made to hide within it.
  - ***Gaseous.*** You gain resistance to neon damage. While in this form, you have a flying speed equal to half your movement speed, and you become immune to falling damage. You do not need to breathe whilst in this form, but still need to intake air to fulfill a verbal component for a spell. You have advantage on checks and saves that involve resisting forced movement. Additionally, you may now slip through a space as narrow as an inch wide.
  - ***Solid.*** You gain resistance to either cold or thunder damage. While in this form, you are unbreakable. Your feet meld with the ground, and grant you tremorsense for up to 15 feet around yourself, which you may expand by sacrificing half of your movement to meld further with the ground, granting you sixty feet of tremorsense instead. While melded like this, you cannot be knocked prone by any means.
  - ***Plasmic.*** You gain resistance to either fire or lightning damage. While in this form, you constantly radiate fifteen feet of bright light, thirty feet of dim light, and anyone who stands within your circle of bright light is immune to the effects of exhaustion via cold. As a bonus action, you can light a flammable item ablaze simply by touching it. Your unarmed strikes deal the same type of damage that you selected when you took this form, and their damage is increased to a d4. Additionally, whenever you grapple a creature, at the start of your turn, if the creature is still grappled, it takes 1d4 + your proficiency bonus of your chosen damage type.
  - ***Phasic.*** You gain resistance to kinetic damage. As a bonus action, you can meld with a piece of technology, granting you +1d4 to all ability checks made to use this technology. You and any creatures you allow may use your body as a tech-casting focus. As a bonus action, you may dematerialize yourself and rematerialize in a location of your choosing within 30ft. When you do so, you may also touch one willing creature within 5 feet of you and transport them with you. You may do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, divided by two, (minimum of 1), and regain all uses on a long rest.
- **Emergency Shift.** As a bonus action, you can forcefully change your state of matter. Change the nature of your Shifting Nature to a different one. Starting at 5th level, you may also take the potential energy created by this sudden shift and expel it from yourself rather than safely dissipating it, dealing damage to yourself and everything around you. When you do, you and each creature within 5ft of you take 1d10 elemental damage determined by your current damage resistance. This damage increases to 2d10 at Level 9, 3d10 at Level 13, and 4d10 at Level 17. This damage dealt to you cannot be reduced in any way. You must finish a long rest before you use this trait again.
