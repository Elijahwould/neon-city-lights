##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Metamagic Adept)

*Prerequisite: Techcasting or Vibe Tech feature*

You've learned how to exert your will on your techs to alter how they function:

- You learn two Metatech options of your choice from the Source Coder class. You can use only one Metatech option on a tech when you cast it, unless the option says otherwise. Whenever you reach a level that grants the Ability Score Improvement feature, you can replace one of these Metatech options with another one from the Source Coder class.
- You gain 2 executable points to spend on Metatech (these points are added to any executable points you have from another source but can be used only on Metatech). You regain all spent executable points when you finish a long rest.
