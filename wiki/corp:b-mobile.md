**Corporation Name.** B-Mobile

**CEO.** Faebella Wendrasastrish

**Years in business.** Under a year

**Industry.** Telecommunications

**Motto.** "*We're the fastest, so you can B-Mobile.*"

**Impact.** Minor

The downfall of Bliss Co. created a new wave of ISPs and telecom providers, and B-Mobile was one of the few that survived beyond their start-up phase, managing to avoid being absorbed by a bigger provider.

B-Mobile is known for their fast and affordable internet and mobile services, though they have been criticized repeatedly for their use of quite lewd ads and suggestive slogans to market their products, with people claiming they do not reflect the company's actual capabilities.

Despite these criticisms, B-Mobile's subscriber base continues to grow. Their continued success endures by appealing to a younger, tech-savvy audience (primarily to people in corporate college), who praise their services as being ideal for gaming and streaming.

Much more recently, they have begun sponsoring multiple influencers and streamers in a bid to grow further and be a serious competitor against [NordCorp](/corp:nordcorp).
