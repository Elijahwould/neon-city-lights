## "We don't need no education…"

Public education is readily available in Neon City—and strictly controlled. People who are not yet of age are often shipped off to boarding schools outside of the city, sponsored by different Neon City organizations and corporations. Students are generally taught history, mathematics, natural and practical sciences, with broad curriculums available to pursue for arts and other trades. Many corporation-sponsored schools tend to be stricter in their curriculums and focus more on things that paint them in a positive light, while those who are run by not-for-profit or archaeological organizations tend to have more liberal curriculums who go to great lengths to be as free with information as they can possibly be.

Subjects from the old world are usually unavailable, given the relative lack of access to reliable sources for some things—and alarming trepidation at the introduction of others. The old world war itself is only covered to some extent, to explain people's ability to somehow survive the Fall, and to explain concepts such as the Bezosphere (a colony of rich folk built as a ring satellite around the planet that has since fallen into disrepair, with the vast majority of it having already fallen and burnt up upon re-entry into the planet), to the advances in genetic technology which introduced Nayalings, Thulians, Jerboans, Gobbos and other races long ago in the past.

As much of the old world is only known through surviving Media, many schools rarely offer a history course—though it is becoming more common for students to acquire said knowledge through content downloaded through many free online history courses, made readily available by the [N.C.A.P.S.](/org:ncaps)

*No player character is expected, for any reason, to be actively involved with or participating in any of these aspects of the game. This bit of lore exists primarily to help isolate the debauchery of Neon City from minors, and to help give some characters the ability to fill out their backstory, if necessary.*
