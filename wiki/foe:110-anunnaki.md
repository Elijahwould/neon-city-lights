Software rarely dies, but maintenance technicians do. AI with few or no proper housings or digital spaces, often the results of lingering fragments of purpose from the Old World's internet, retain only shreds of their artificial glory. An anunnaki's remaining powers, however, are undependable, and its core processing is impaired. Some anunnaki, whatever their former purpose, have cycled themselves into a mirror of over-realized insanity, knowing only themselves.

Anunnaki typically manifest as tiny hearts of salvaged code, clinging close to their projectors in the physical realms and project larger shells of hardlight in any form a human mind might imagine. Whereas one anunnaki might appear as a featureless yet mysteriously alluring humanoid, another might lean into the animal-headed propensities of Old World IT experts, or appear as an amorphous pile of garbage text and compression artifacts.

***Corrupted Code.*** Even the dregs of digital might make an anunnaki dangerous if they so choose to be. They still command tremendous record banks of applying militant force and technological power, and are imbued with such pantheonic splendor such as to be objects of lust for religious trauma survivors and mythological nuts alike. Unsurprising, as that venn diagram tends to be a circle.

***Icon of Power.*** Every anunnaki comes preloaded with it a weapon or implement—such as a wheel of fire, a hammer, or a palette and chisel—that symbolizes its former installation. For the Old World aligned anunnaki, this was often their thumbnail. Regardless of the exact aesthetic of the icon, it serves as a weapon of self-defense and extreme violence for the anunnaki, as their temperament and synergy demands.

***Modernization.*** An anunnaki that forms a broad enough foundation of power might regain its former glory. Many all-but-forgotten programs still have their dedicated users, some being the key members of technocults or niche corpo R&D departments. A great deal of anunnaki who seek not their past but look instead to a new future end up forming a symbiotic relationship with a human matching their synergy's demands, becoming an ordinator AI.

***DMA.Human(Synergy)*** Anunnaki are obsessed with both old and new humanity's ability to form bonds of mutual benefit with non-human subjects, most often the subject of technology, but sometimes plants, animals, or even landmarks of their daily commutes. Some less-than-stable anunnaki form a cyclical obsession where this mutualistic anthropomorphization leads back and folds upon their self identity, destabilizing their minds with flawed repetitions of self-actualization.
