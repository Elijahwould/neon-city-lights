##### Source: Player's Handbook (Magic Circle)

*3rd-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute

**Range:** 10 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (powdered silver and iron worth at least $$10,000, which the tech consumes)

**Duration:** 1 hour

You create a 10-foot-radius, 20-foot-tall cylinder of technical energy centered on a point on the ground that you can see within range. Glowing lines of code appear wherever the cylinder intersects with the floor or other surface.

Choose one or more of the following types of creatures: AI, surges, spam, viruses, or hacked. The circle affects a creature of the chosen type in the following ways:

- The creature can’t willingly enter the cylinder by nontechnical means. If the creature tries to use teleportation or internet travel to do so, it must first succeed on a Charisma saving throw.

- The creature has disadvantage on attack rolls against targets within the cylinder.

- Targets within the cylinder can’t be charmed, frightened, or possessed by the creature.

When you cast this tech, you can elect to cause its tech to operate in the reverse direction, preventing a creature of the specified type from leaving the cylinder and protecting targets outside it.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 4th level or higher, the duration increases by 1 hour for each slot level above 3rd.
