##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Trident of Fish Command)

*Weapon ([spear](/weapon:spear)), uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** 3 lbs, $$100,000

This spear is a technical weapon. It has 3 charges. While you carry it, you can use an action and expend 1 charge to cast [ACCESS_ROOT(Critter)](/tech:access-root-critter) (save DC 15) from it on a critter that has an innate swimming speed. The spear regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.
