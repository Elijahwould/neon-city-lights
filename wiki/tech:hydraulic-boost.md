##### Source: Player's Handbook (Jump)

*1st-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a grasshopper’s hind leg)

**Duration:** 1 minute

You touch a creature. The creature's jump distance is tripled until the Tech ends.
