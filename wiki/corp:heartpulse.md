**Corporation Name.** Heartpulse Industries, Inc.

**CEO.** Deshawn Rubin-Sharpe (AKA Mr. Sharp, Mr. Glass)

**Years in business.** ~30+

**Industry.** Creation, distribution and preservation of old and new world music

**Motto.** *"Always one beat ahead."*

**Impact.** Major

Originally nicknamed by many hackers and common citizens as "Heartless Studios", the old Heartpulse Studios centered on the creation, distribution and preservation of music both old world and new. They have exclusive radio stations for casual listeners and their most… devout supporters, and their live shows were once-in-a-lifetime opportunities to get close to their favorite stars. Running heavily on a prosperity gospel form for their customers to feed into, many sacrifice their life savings—and quite often, their own mental sanity—just to get their hands on this music.

After acquiring Vox Syndicate, they have absorbed their designer clothing and lifestyle brands into their own, making them a true media conglomerate. CEO Rubin-Sharpe introduced The Dollhouse shortly thereafter, a streaming variety channel for in-house content creators, purportedly to allow those creators interactions with their fans. Sales have never been higher…
