**Location Type.** Business

**Area.** [Neon City Downtown Residential District](/district:downtown-residential)

**Impact.** Major

![azulia.bartender.png](https://neoncitylights.wdfiles.com/local--files/loc:poncys-bar/azulia.bartender.png)

Poncy's is a middle-end bar that managed to survive corporate onslaughts thanks to its owner's vast connections among the corporate elite. The bar was originally a refuge for people who were wanting to avoid the public eye and pick up a job or two—its policy towards runners has always been friendly, and an ample backroom area exists for fixers and aspiring runners to use, keeping the front of the establishment mostly open to all other visitors who wish to just enjoy food and drink.

Originally a red-hazed bar with old-world "oriental" stylings, after what seemed like some kind of senseless attack from a terrorist organization named [The Black Hawks](/org:black-hawks), much of the place was rebuilt, and expanded upon, trading in their slightly insensitive design leanings for a sleeker look, emphasizing comfort and ample space.

There is a second floor where managers and Poncy himself runs business operations, while the ample underground area has rooms for rent, as well as a laundry room. Runners often use cellar rooms as temporary digs between jobs.
