**Organization Name.** The Cult of Thorn

**Leader.** [The Speaker](/npc:the-speaker)

**Organization Type.** Technocult

**Years in existence.** <1

**Goals.** Create equality and independence for the [Undercity](/undercity) by near any means possible.

**Creed.** "*Enough thorns can kill a giant.*"

**Impact.** Minor

The Cult of Thorn is a relatively new organization that has arisen in the dregs of the Undercity with the stated goal of freeing the Undercity from the grasp of the corporations. Many who join chip themselves with a large quantity of cyberware to embody the appearance of the apparent leader, known only as The Speaker. This has resulted in what amounts to a growing, ready-made army of cybernetic citizen soldiers holding the desire to burn the corps to the ground.
