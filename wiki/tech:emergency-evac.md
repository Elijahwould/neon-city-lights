##### Source: Player's Handbook (Expeditious Retreat)

*1st-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

This tech allows you to move at an incredible pace. When you cast this tech, you can take the Dash action as a bonus action on each of your turns until the tech ends.
