## "You're talking about him as if he were a human being. That part of him died years ago."

Crazed killers are not honorable or chivalric. They are violent butchers who revel in hunting down and murdering their targets. Unknowable, unseen and unstoppable, they are some of the most terrifying foes imaginable.

##### Source: [Coco's Homebrew (Crazed Killer)](https://cocoshomebrew.tumblr.com/post/697737087956516864/happy-halloween-heres-the-link-to-my-patreon-if) ([Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cocoshomebrew/posts))

### Dreadful Presence

Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, as a bonus action on your turn, you can activate your Dreadful Presence for 1 minute. You can end your Dreadful Presence early using another bonus action, and it also ends early if you fall unconscious. You determine how this presence visually manifests by rolling on the following table or deciding for yourself.

*Dreadful Presence Manifestation*

| d6 | Appearance |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | You don a creepy mask which hides your face. |
| 2 | Your body shrinks and morphs, taking on the appearance of a child or a doll. |
| 3 | You become horribly scarred or deformed. |
| 4 | Your face becomes stuck in an eerie smile, and your eyes get wide and unblinking. |
| 5 | You become monstrous, taking on a terrifying inhuman form. |
| 6 | All color drains from your clothes and body, replaced with shades of black and white. |

While your Dreadful Presence is active, whenever a hostile creature starts its turn within 30 feet of you, it must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier) or become frightened of you for 1 minute. This condition ends early if the creature ends its turn out of line of sight or more than 60 feet away from you, or if your Dreadful Presence ends. A frightened creature can also repeat this save at the end of each of its turns, ending this effect on a success. Once a creature succeeds on its saving throw against this feature, it is immune to your Dreadful Presence until the next time you activate it. You can activate your Dreadful Presence twice, and you can't activate it again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Killer Skills

At 3rd level, to represent your murderous methods, you have a number of killer dice equal to your proficiency bonus, which are d6s. You regain all of your expended killer dice when you finish a short or long rest. Your killer dice improve as you gain fighter levels, becoming d8s at 10th level and d10s at 18th level.

You can spend your killer dice in the following ways:

- **Killer's Stalk.** When you make a Stealth (Dexterity) check or a Survival (Wisdom) check, you can expend one killer die and add it to the ability check.
- **Killing Blow.** When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can expend one killer die and add it to the attack's damage roll.
- **Killer's Glare.** When a creature makes a saving throw against your Dreadful Presence, you can use your reaction to expend one killer die and subtract it from its saving throw.

### Soulless Efficiency

At 7th level, you have become eerily detached from emotion. While your Dreadful Presence is active, you are immune to being charmed or frightened. If you are being affected by one of these conditions when you activate your Dreadful Presence, the effect is suspended for the duration.

Additionally, your Dreadful Presence now ignores immunity to the frightened condition, however creatures with this immunity have advantage on their saving throws against it.

### Inescapable Hunter

At 10th level, while a creature is frightened by your Dreadful Presence, for the next hour, you always know its location so long as it is on the same plane as you and is not invisible, and nonmagical difficult terrain costs you no extra movement while you are moving directly towards it.

### Ceaseless Torment

At 15th level, when a creature succeeds on its save against your Dreadful Presence, it only remains immune to that feature until you hit it with a weapon attack. Additionally, when you roll initiative and have no killer dice remaining, you regain 1 killer die.

### Jump-Scaring Stalker

At 18th level, you are able to seemingly move with the shadows themselves and appear as if from nowhere to scare the life out your quarry. While your Dreadful Presence is active, you can use a bonus action to become invisible until the start of your next turn or until you take an action. While you are invisible, your walking speed increases by 10 feet. If you end your invisibility by taking the Attack action and hit a creature with an attack as part of that action, the creature has disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes against your Dreadful Presence.
