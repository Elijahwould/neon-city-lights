##### Source: Player's Handbook (Nondetection)

*3rd-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a pair of sunglasses worth $$2,500 placed upon the target, which the tech consumes)

**Duration:** 8 hours

For the duration, you hide a target that you touch from observation tech. The target can be a willing creature or a place or an object no larger than 10 feet in any dimension. The target can’t be targeted by any observation tech or perceived through technical sensors.
