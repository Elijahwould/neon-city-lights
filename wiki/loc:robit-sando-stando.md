**Location Name.** Robit Sando Stando

**Owner/Leader.** [Tobi Greene](/legend:tobi)

**Location Type.** Business

**Years in existence.** ~1

**Type.** Food truck

**Area.** Moves around Neon City

**Impact.** Minor

No one is quite sure why Tobi Greene decided to open up a food truck. It might've been the fact that every single runner around him appreciated it when he brought sandwiches to every single gig. After a while, his talent for sandwich making hit the road, along with his partner-in-crime [Katze](/legend:katze).

Now, the Sando Stando operates as a food truck, driving around all over Neon City. The easiest way to figure out where it is? Check on Flutter.
