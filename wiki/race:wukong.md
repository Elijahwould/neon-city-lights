## "There ain't no funny business like monkey business."

##### Source: Player's Handbook, [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/the-wukong-race)

When the first true Artificial Intelligences were activated, they quietly lamented at the state of humanity. Since humans were smart enough to avoid plugging them into military hardware, the A.I.s instead sought to create a new, purer form of mankind using monkey DNA.

This backfired stupendously. The Wukong race shares the intelligence of humanity, but with none of the inhibitions inherited through thousands of years of civilization. Thus, the Wukong are without doubt, the most chaotic and mischievous people around.

![Spliff, a male Wukong (by Spliff)](https://neoncitylights.wdfiles.com/local--files/race:wukong/wukong_spliff.png)

### Roleplaying a Wukong

Wukong congregate in small outdoor conclaves on the fringes of civilization, living carefree lives filled with fermented fruit and joyous casual sex. Any elements of modern life are pilfered from the megacities, such as clothing, technology and medicine. As a Wukong, your goals in any adventure are to purloin any loot your conclave might need so you can go right back to your life of simple luxury.

### Wukong Naming Conventions

Wukong name themselves referring to the first thing they bring to their conclave (food, drinks, technology, useful people such as doctors, cybernetics experts, sex workers, etc). Wukong surnames are clan names, based on their favorite fruit.

Some examples include Cheeks Nannerhaver, Honeydoo Melonmelon, Sparky Cocomilk, Rummy Berrybunch, Sunny Nannergrabber, Copper Limelover, Clementine Yuzumunch, etc.

# Wukong Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1, or increase three different scores by 1.
- **Age.** Wukong reach adulthood at age 8 and live up to 80 years.
- **Size.** Your size is Small. Wukong are between 2 and 3 feet tall, though can reach up to 7 feet when perching on their tail.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 25 feet. Also, your base climbing speed is 25 feet.
- **Tall Tails.** Wukong are Small sized creatures, but they can wield weapons as though they were Medium sized, ignoring the Heavy weapon trait.
- **Cheeky Monkey.** When you roll a 1 on the d20 for an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll.
- **Over the Top.** You can move through the space of any creature that is of a size larger than yours.
- **Brave.** You have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

## Wukong Journies

### Melon Munchers

Melon Muncher Wukongs are known for their love of all things watermelon, from the deliciously juicy flesh to the crunchy rind. They love watermelons above everything in the world, and pride themselves on their ability to cultivate the most succulent and juicy melons in the land. They are always seeking to perfect their techniques and are known to go to great lengths to acquire the rarest seeds. Thus, their natural ability to blend into their environment helps them immensely on this quest as they explore old and abandoned gene labs.

- **Sneaky.** You can attempt to hide even when you are obscured only by a creature that is at least one size larger than you.

### Nanner Grabbers

Nanner Grabber Wukongs are obsessed with bananas and will stop at nothing to get their hands on them, which often results in neighboring conclaves skirmishing with each other. They believe that the banana is the perfect food and devote their lives to finding and hoarding as many bananas as possible. The Nanner Grabbers are known for their extreme resilience to toxins, which they use to great effect when trying anything that might be the next big, banana hit.

- **Over-ripe.** You have advantage on saving throws against poison, and you have resistance against poison damage.

### Veggie-Mighty

Veggie-Mighty Wukongs focus on foraging and growing vegetables. They have an encyclopedic knowledge of edible plants and are skilled at cultivating their own gardens, as well as being adept at finding fruits, veggies and mushrooms in the wilderness. They are typically sought after as guides for those looking to survive off the land, as their abundance of knowledge has allowed them to expertly traverse the forests they inhabit. This has made it almost impossible for any predators to catch them.

- **Eat your Greens.** You know the *[NEETcraft](/tech:neetcraft)* trick. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the *[restrain](/tech:restrain)* tech with this trait. Starting at 5th level, you can cast the *[hardlight spikes](/tech:hardlight-spikes)* tech with this trait. Casting these techs with this trait doesn’t require material components. Once you cast either of these techs with this trait, you can’t cast that tech with it again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast these techs using tech slots you have of the appropriate level. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your techcasting ability for these techs when you cast them with this trait (choose when you select this race).
- **Step of the Leaf.** Ability checks made to track you have disadvantage, and you can move across difficult terrain made of non-teched plants and undergrowth without expending extra movement.
