##### Source: Player's Handbook (Acid Splash)

*Manifestation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You hurl a small packet filled with caustic goo. Choose one or two creatures you can see within range. If you choose two, they must be within 5 feet of each other. A target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 acid damage.

This tech's damage increases by 1d6 when you reach 5th level (2d6), 11th level (3d6), and 17th level (4d6).
