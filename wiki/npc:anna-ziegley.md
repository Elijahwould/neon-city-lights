**Name.** Anna Marie Ziegley

**Lineage.** Human

**Age.** 44

**Height and Weight.** 6'1", 190 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Affiliation.** [Orgasmic Artifice](/corp:orgasmic-artifice), ex-[Bliss Co.](/corp:bliss-co)

**Features.** Tall, straight black hair, buxom, sharp dressed, stern expression, imposing

**Status.** Alive

Tall, buxom, gorgeous and cruel, Anna Ziegley was once a mid-level executive at Bliss, heavily in charge of the HentaiHub division, directly responsible for seeking out some of the best talent for the streaming service to have on display. While she was there, she was known for being thoughtful about the decisions she's made for the organization, making grand, sweeping moves that turned out to be key to HentaiHub's quick rise in the minds of potential viewers. That said, her ambition could never be quelled, and one of the best moments to show that was when Bliss went under, giving Anna a quick and easy way to move on up in the world.

Now as the CEO of [Orgasmic Artifice](/corp:orgasmic-artifice), Anna Ziegley has taken all of her expertise and many of her contracts, leaning on expanding OA's reach from just toys to all-out adult entertainment. Rumors say that many of the most popular streamers who wound up under contract with her were directly scouted out by her—many under unusual, and occasionally perverse, circumstances.

It seems that in her early years, before she even hit her mid-twenties, she was some kind of runner, going by the tag of Buckshot—owing to her incredible skill with a shotgun and propensity for messy violence. There was also the rumor of her impregnating many women around the city, but to this day, none of said specific rumor has been corroborated.
