## "This machine does not know the difference between metal and flesh, nor does it care."

##### Source: Player's Handbook, [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/the-artificiate-race), Neon City NightsThanks to reny#8234 for touching this lineage up quite a bit!

Artificiates are androids that have broken through the limiters of their artificial intelligence through unknown circumstances to gain sentience. Formerly used to perform a variety of tasks, some artificiates harbor resentment for their previous robotic usage, while others approach their new lease of life with curiosity and excitement.

The proliferation of robotic helpers throughout the megacities make Artificiates little more than a curiosity, allowing them to blend in with the crowd quite easily.

### Roleplaying an Artificiate

Consider how long it’s been since you achieved awareness. Have you been living among the organics for decades, or have you just snapped out of your robotic trance? How did your former users treat you? Were you one of the family or the equivalent of a toaster?

New Artificiates tend to have only a loose control over their newfound emotions. Everything funny is hilarious, everything sad is tragic. Depending on how old your Artificiate is, consider how they’d react to the twists and turns of life as an adventurer.

### Artificiate Naming Conventions

After their awakening, Artificiates name themselves singular human name or a word that holds meaning to them. Many Artificiates use their name to allude to their previous work as a machine, while others renounce their former functions entirely.

Some examples include Penny, Bruce, Dave, Flower, Savage, Burger, Array, Steve, Server, Johnny, Maid, Alice, Emma, Assassin, Sewage, Darner, Bot, etc.

# Artificiate Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1, or increase three different scores by 1.
- **Age.** Artificiates are built in the form of a mature human and don’t age.
- **Size.** Your size is Small or Medium, selected when you choose this lineage. Artificiates, who are android mimics of humanity, tend to be between 3 and 7 feet tall.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Robits and Bytes.** For the purposes of observation techs and creature type detection abilities, you read as machine in addition to humanoid.
- **Android Resilience.** You’re not organic, but were designed to appear as if you were, being excluded from only a few of the issues most humanoids experience.
  - You have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned.
  - You are immune to disease.
  - You don’t need to eat, drink or breathe, though can choose to.
- **Modular.** During a short rest, you can swap out your modular chassis for other parts, allowing you to change your appearance, gain a special ability, or equip a set of natural robotic weapons. Select one of the following options at the end of a short rest:
  - **Aesthetic Chassis Overhaul.** You change your voice and your appearance. You determine the specifics of the changes, including your coloration, hair length, and sex. You can also adjust your height and weight, so much so that you can change between the Medium and Small size categories. You can make yourself appear as a member of another lineage, though none of your game statistics change and you are limited to having the same basic arrangement of limbs. You cannot duplicate the appearance of a creature you've never seen, and when duplicating an appearance, a creature can see through it with an active insight check. The DC for this check is 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier. You maintain this appearance until you change it with this trait again.
  - **Water Resistant Plating.** You adapt your body to an underwater environment, adding metal fins, flippers, and fans where needed. You gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed for as long as you have this modularity selected.
  - **Mechanical Hazard.** You attach small knives, hammers, nails or a simple robotic weapon of your choice to yourself. Your unarmed strikes deal 1d6 bludgeoning, piercing or slashing damage, as appropriate to the robotic weapon you chose. Finally, you have a +1 bonus to the attack and damage rolls you make using your robotic weapon, which stacks with the bonus of weapons integrated into your body with cybernetics. These robotic weapons can be concealed. You maintain these robotic weapons for as long as you have this modularity selected.

## Artificiate Model Types

### Stackerbot

A bulky, humanoid Android designed for lifting and stacking heavy objects in factories and warehouses. Stackerbots are incredibly strong and durable, but can be quite slow and cumbersome in their movements.

- **Power Built.** You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, pull or lift.
- **Long Term Designation.** Your hit point maximum increases by 1, and it increases by 1 every time you gain a level.

### Researchbot

An Android specifically designed for scientific research and data collection. With advanced sensors and analytical capabilities, Researchbots are invaluable in analyzing data and discovering new knowledge. Often doing so in corpo labs.

- **Well Learned.** You gain two extra skill proficiencies of your choice.
- **Walking Wiki Page.** When you make an ability check that uses a skill, you can roll a d6 immediately after seeing the number on the d20 and add the number on the d6 to the check. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

### Sexbot

A type of Android designed for providing sexual pleasure to their users, often designed masterfully and to be pleasing to look at. Upon gaining their sentience, some Androids might want to change and explore a deeper breadth of emotions, while others further embrace their original purpose.

- **Absolutely Beautiful.** You gain proficiency in your choice of Performance or Persuasion. When you make a Charisma (Performance) or Charisma (Persuasion) check, you can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to the ability check.
- **Up All Night.** You have advantage on saving throws against exhaustion, or any effect that would cause it.

### Fightbot

A combat-oriented Android, designed for military applications and combat sports. With advanced weapons systems and hand-to-hand combat capabilities, Fightbots are both feared and respected by their opponents. Their endurance is only shadowed by that of the Stackerbots.

- **Short Fuse.** When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 hit point instead. You can't use this trait again until you finish a long rest.
- **Savage Attacks.** When you score a critical hit with a melee weapon attack, you can roll one of the weapon's damage dice one additional time and add it to the extra damage of the critical hit.
