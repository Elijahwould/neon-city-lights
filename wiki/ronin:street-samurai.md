## "A samurai should always be prepared for death—whether his own or someone else’s."

The street samurai is a warrior who draws on an implacable fighting spirit to overcome enemies. A street samurai's resolve is nearly unbreakable, and the enemies in a street samurai’s path have two choices: yield or die fighting. Enemies often flee before a street samurai's intensity, understanding that street samurai approach every battle as if it were their last. They are often referred to on the streets as street sams or sammies and often come from military, paramilitary or corporate security backgrounds—though street gang violence often breeds street samurai of their own.

##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Samurai)

### Bonus Proficiency

When you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: History, Insight, Performance, or Persuasion. Alternatively, you gain proficiency with a vehicle or tool kit of your choice.

### Spirit of the Samurai

Starting at 3rd level, your intensity in battle can shield you and help you strike true. As a bonus action on your turn, you can give yourself advantage on all weapon attack rolls until the end of the current turn. When you do so, you also gain 5 temporary hit points. The number of hit points increases when you reach certain levels in this class, increasing to 10 at 10th level and 15 at 15th level.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus. You regain all expended uses of it when you finish a long rest.

### Intense Self-Control

Starting at 7th level, your discipline and attention to detail allow you to excel in social situations. Whenever you make a Charisma (Persuasion) check, you gain a bonus to the check equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 0).

Your self-control also causes you to gain proficiency in Wisdom saving throws. If you already have this proficiency, you instead gain proficiency in Intelligence or Charisma saving throws (your choice).

### Raging Spirit

Starting at 10th level, when you roll initiative and have no uses of Spirit of the Samurai remaining, you regain one use.

### Rapid Strike

Starting at 15th level, you learn to trade accuracy for swift strikes. If you take the Attack action on your turn and have advantage on an attack roll against one of the targets, you can forego the advantage for that roll to make an additional weapon attack against that target, as part of the same action. You can do so no more than once per turn.

### Last Stand

Starting at 18th level, your spirit can even delay the hand of death itself. If you take damage that reduces you to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction to delay falling unconscious, and you can immediately take an extra turn. While you have 0 hit points during that extra turn, taking damage causes death saving throw failures as normal, and three death saving throw failures can still kill you. When the extra turn ends, you fall unconscious if you still have 0 hit points.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest.
