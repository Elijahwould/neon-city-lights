##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Eldritch Adept)

*Prerequisite: Techcasting or Vibe Tech feature*

Studying Streamer techniques, you’ve learned to mimic their entertaining approach to combat. You learn one Partnership Program option of your choice from the Streamer class. If the Program has a prerequisite, you can choose that Partnership Program only if you’re a Streamer and only if you meet the prerequisite.

Whenever you gain a level, you can replace the Program with another one from the Streamer class.
