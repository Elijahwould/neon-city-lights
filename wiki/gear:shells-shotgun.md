A shotgun shell, shotshell or simply shell is a type of rimmed, cylindrical cartridges used specifically in shotguns, and is typically loaded with numerous small, pellet-like spherical sub-projectiles called buckshot. A shell can sometimes also contain only a single large solid projectile known as a slug, fired usually through a rifled slug barrel. This is a pack of 20 non-technical shells, which can be either slugs or buckshot.
