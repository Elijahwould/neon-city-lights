**Corporation Name.** R.E.D. Genetics

**CEO.** Jeremy Randolph

**Years in business.** ~60+

**Industry.** Genetics, splicing, transhumanism, cloning

**Motto.** "*Within ourselves lies the key to our future.*"

**Impact.** Minor

An upstart corporation founded by Adalberto Rojas that surfaced within the last century, R.E.D. Genetics' primary focus was the direct application of splicing technology that was recovered from old world records to assist in the advancement of modern splicing methods. They have collaborated with many corporations in the past, from Toncheva to Bliss; from the Reliquary to Bright Future.

R.E.D. Genetics are responsible for some of the more interesting breakthroughs in splicing technology of the modern era. They are responsible for the modern [Splicer](/race:splicer), with its dramatic yet safe deviations from the standard genome of Humanity, as well as for a few of the applications of sequencing that have aided humanity in toppling several diseases outright.

Their most recent research seems to have been in cloning, though no one has really seen the fruits of their labors to this day. As a result, stock prices haven't grown quite as much as they historically have. Their current high-end product offerings seem to revolve around the cloning of individual body parts for people in a method that is removed from tech use so as to be made more affordable and readily available to the masses.
