**Corporation Name.** Super Henshin Guild!

**Leader.** Blood Knight Driver "Sanguine"

**Organization Type.** Guild

**Years in existence.** ~100+

**Goals.** Combat, security and performance

**Creed.** *"The Burning Heart never gives into Evil! Henshin!"*

**Impact.** Moderate

The Super Henshin Guild is a contractor guild, well known for primarily three things: anonymous membership outside of the guild, refusal to take anything but security, bounty hunting and acting requests as contracts, and an absolute commitment to staying in character while in their Henshin suits and masks. A member of the guild performing their job tends to attract crowds and attention wherever they go due to their commitment to high production values and explosive encounters—all the while being as boisterous and hammy as possible.

At their core, the Super Henshin Guild is a not-for-profit guild of like-minded individuals who heavily align with Super Sentai media from the old world, heavily involving themselves in the revival of a once-lost art form. While people who are part of the guild do take requests and gigs, they are primarily interested in show fighting, as well as showing off classic tokusatsu culture, inspired by the actions and heroics of the old media they represent.

They do not have any natural means of generating profit—they refuse corporate sponsorship at every turn, and while they are friendly with NCAPS (they do, after all, need to obtain their old world media from somewhere), they would much rather prefer to retain their independence. As a result, the guild collects dues from those members who represent them while out on requests and gigs. Many members moonlight in other professions and perform other jobs while not in sentai or rider form.

### Notable Classes and Subclasses

The guild’s ranks heavily tend to be Sentai Saviors, Techno-Blades, Armorers, Cyber Valkyries and E-Sports Streamers—surprisingly all in equal amounts.

- **Sentai Saviors.** The embodiment of what it means to be a Henshin hero. From saving people to the over-the-top SFX, everything they do is to the point of Quixotic delusion. They are usually the bulk of the command squad, but fit basically any role as jacks of all trades.
- **Techno-Blades.** Almost one-to-one like the Sentai Saviors, although they tend to be somewhat more passive—they often find themselves in the roles of Bodyguards, Enforcers and the like.
- **Armorers.** The geniuses of the bunch, preferring to design and create armors, weapons, suits and more. They are the main creators and makers of guild merchandise, though they are just as ready as any other member of the guild to throw down in combat.
- **Cyber Valkyries.** The faces of the guild. Often found as fixers and in other public-facing positions, they run the daily workings of the guild alongside the guildmaster. They are often in contact with runners and other fixers, negotiating deals, gigs and jobs for members of the guild—if not joining themselves, given their skills as medics and ace blasters.
- **E- Sports Streamers.** The charismatic lancers of the Sentai squads, they are often second-in-command in teams, with their work in the guild closely related to the Sentai Saviors and Cyber Valkyries in internal affairs. They often spend the most time screening gigs and requests to ensure none that make it to the guild proper are undesirable.
- **Bikers.** Stuntmen and women, these riders are the most prone to risk taking and thrill seeking, doing difficult stunts in both filming and fighting. They are responsible for the maintenance and repair of fellow riders' vehicles.
- **Dragon Claw Clan.** A small but popular group of riders, Dragon Claw riders are more extreme in their henshins, inspired by classic old-world media such as *Shin Kamen Rider*. They not only use tech but are also willing to add biomodifications to transform their whole body as a weapon. Their appearances are more visceral and gruesome compared to the other riders.

### Hierarchy

The guild’s hierarchy is separated into four major tiers, which derive their names from the three major eras of Tokusatsu and Super Sentai media.

At the very top stands the ***Guildmaster***. This is a special rank among the members of the Showa rank, leading the guild on its day-to-day jobs. This rank is usually held by the most powerful member of the guild.

The second tier is the ***Showa*** rank. Consisting of the older and more experienced members, they typically act as solo runners. They are often powerful enough to complete a Streetwise gig all on their own and are usually specifically requested by fixers for specific gigs and jobs befitting their rank.

The third tier is the ***Heisei*** rank. They make up the bulk of the guild, being the most involved in gigs and requests and most often seen by the public. Though many members of the Heisei aspire to rank up to the Showa, a good number of them seem to be career Showa lifers—either by choice or happenstance.

The fourth and final tier is the ***Reiwa*** rank, composed primarily of newbies and of those people who may have been reinstated into the guild after potential time off. Newbies in the Reiwa rank typically last six months to the year, during which time they are taught and trained by those in the Heisei rank in preparation for riskier gigs and jobs. Aside from this, they mostly serve to help the Guildmaster in other tasks, such as checking up on the statuses of gigs and jobs to report directly to the Guildmaster.

Returning members who are part of this rank often last three months at most, during which time they are primarily whipped back into shape by members of the Heisei—and sometimes Showa—to once again prepare them for active duty.

### Relationships

**[NCAPS.](/org:ncaps)** NCAPS’ primary goal is the recovery of old-world artifacts, media and other tidbits. As a result, the Guild often interacts with them, looking to obtain more Tokusatsu and Super Sentai media for their own reference. NCAPS is more than happy to hire Guild workers to protect recovery expeditions.

**[The Reliquary.](/corp:reliquary)** One would think that the Guild’s penchant towards their own freedom would put them at odds with the Reliquary. This is not so: the Reliquary also run their own expeditions in the recovery of old-world artifacts and media, but while NCAPS makes the vast majority of what they find freely available to the public domain in the interest of the growth and enrichment of the public, the Reliquary often sequesters technological and military advancements away from Neon City until such a time as they find them ready (and sometimes that time never comes).

That said, there have been many Reliquary expeditions that have turned up plenty of old-world media and artifacts that have been of interest to the Guild, and the Reliquary has historically seen no harm in relinquishing said media to the Guild as payment for their services. This relationship in particular is one major reason why the Guild is not beholden to being fully associated with any corporation or organization: their independence allows them to further their own needs, first and foremost.
