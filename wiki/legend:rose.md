**Name.** Rose "Carmilla" Viszla neé Kell

**Lineage.** Le Fanu Netsucker, ex-Street Elf Netnibbler

**Age.** 31, physically locked at 27

**Height and Weight.** 5'6", 145 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Classes/Subclasses.** Street Fighter (Street Detective) 20

**Features.** Pink hair with a shock of orange at the front, heterochromia, wolf ears, wolf tail, sharp fangs

**Status.** Alive

Three and a half years ago, Rose was your average late twenty-something street kid running minor jobs for scraps. Then, she was "selected" by a member of the Besson "family" to become a thrall. Fortunately for her (and unfortunately for the fresh sucker who tried), it didn't take, and she was able to kill her date-turned-assailant.

The next three years, however, were a red-tinted haze of anger, vengeance, and murder.

This period came to a close thanks to the assistance of a band of runners who slew the final two members of the Besson "family"… though, due to complications, Rose began to turn as a result.

Initially, she fought like hell against it—but after a chance meeting with the progenitor of the Féval strain? She came to accept her changing fate, and even wrangle control of it. Now, she walks the Undercity as a fully fledged Netsucker, progenitor of an entirely new strain. While reluctant to take thralls, she has unwittingly already passed her heated viral touch to others, leading them to walk the same immortal half-life she faces. Nonetheless, she has begun to take control of the role the universe thrust upon her, and now runs [The Arcanum](/loc:arcanum) and the burgeoning organization that has begun to grow around her and her ideals, the [Lex Talionis](/org:lex-talionis).

Alongside this, she has become aware that some old connections she thought long dormant may still weigh heavy on her life. But until they make the first move? She's biding her time, keeping her ear to the ground and working to make the Undercity a safer place. At least, until fate comes calling once more…

Who knows what could happen then…?

Married to [Natasha Viszla](/legend:natasha).

**Player.** JessicaShtebs, Assorted Beanie#0512
