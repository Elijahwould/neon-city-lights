## "Everybody has a plan until they get hit."

##### Source: adapted from [Benjamin Huffman's Pugilist v3](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/184921/The-Pugilist-Class)

### Burning Knuckles

Starting when you choose this discipline at 3rd level, your unarmed strikes score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.

### Turn Around Punch

Also starting at 3rd level, you can slip past the brunt of an attack and launch one of your own in the same fluid motion. When you take damage from a melee weapon attack, you can use your reaction and spend 2 mettle points to reduce the damage by 1d10 + your Strength modifier + your pugilist level.

If you reduce the damage to 0, you can make an unarmed strike or street fighter weapon attack against a creature within range as part of the same reaction.

### Hard Knockdown

Starting at 6th level, when you use your bonus action to use the Basic Combination and hit the same creature with both attacks, you can spend 1 mettle point to make an additional unarmed strike against that creature as part of the same bonus action. If you hit, the creature is knocked prone in addition to taking damage as normal for your unarmed strike.

### Jolt Counter

Starting at 11th level, when you reduce damage from an attack to 0 and successfully hit an enemy creature using your Turn Around Punch feature, you regain 1 expended mettle point.

### Gigaton Uppercut

Starting at 17th level, when you hit with an unarmed strike or street fighter weapon, you can spend 1 or more mettle points to try to knock out the opponent instead of dealing damage. Roll 3d12, plus an additional 2d12 for every mettle point spent after the first, and add your street fighter level to the result. If the total is equal to or greater than the creature’s remaining hit points, it is reduced to 0 hit points.
