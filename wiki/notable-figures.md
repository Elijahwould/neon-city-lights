Sometimes there are those that keep a low profile or prefer to keep to themselves. Some are in everyone's face like a mask. And others are simply the right people caught in the wrong place. Notable figures of Neon City, while not always a runner or former runner, possess a quality about them that make their names more common in everyday conversation. Each of the following may not partake in the overarching story of the city, but they play a role in building the bridge.█
