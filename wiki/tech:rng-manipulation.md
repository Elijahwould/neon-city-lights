##### Source: Player's Handbook (Chromatic Orb)

*1st-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 90 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a diamond worth at least $$5,000 credits)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You hurl a 4-inch-diameter sphere of energy at a creature that you can see within range. You choose acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison, or thunder for the type of orb you create, and then make a ranged tech attack against the target. If the attack hits, the creature takes 3d8 damage of the type you chose.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.
