##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Ring of Air Elemental Command)

*Keyring, legendary (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$3,000,000

This ring is linked to the gaseous state of matter.

While wearing this ring, you have advantage on attack rolls against surges from the linked state of matter, and they have disadvantage on attack rolls against you. In addition, you have access to the following properties.

The ring has 5 charges. It regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. Spells cast from the ring have a save DC of 17.

You can expend a 2 of the ring's charges to cast [ACCESS_ROOT(Target)](/tech:access-root-target) on an gaseous surge. In addition, when you fall, you descend 60 feet per round and take no damage from falling.

If you help slay a gaseous surge while attuned to the ring, you gain access to the following additional properties:

- You have resistance to lightning damage.
- You have a flying speed equal to your walking speed and can hover.
- You can cast the following techs from the ring, expending the necessary number of charges: [Arc Ricochet](/tech:arc-ricochet) (3 charges), [Aeroblast](/tech:aeroblast) (2 charges), or [Aerobarricade](/tech:aerobarricade) (1 charge).
