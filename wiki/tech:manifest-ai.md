##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Summon Celestial)

*5th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 90 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a statuette of a golden reliquary worth at least $$50,000)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You call forth the projection of an AI. It manifests in an angelic form in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. This corporeal form uses the AI Projection stat block. When you cast the tech, choose Avenger or Defender. Your choice determines the creature’s attack in its stat block. The creature disappears when it drops to 0 hit points or when the tech ends.

The creature is an ally to you and your companions. In combat, the creature shares your initiative count, but it takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don’t issue any, it takes the Dodge action and uses its move to avoid danger.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 6th level or higher, use the higher level whenever the tech’s level appears in the stat block.

***Tech Lists.*** [Enforcer](/techs:enforcer), [Ordinator](/techs:ordinator)
