If a partnership program has prerequisites, you must meet them to become part of the program. You can join the program at the same time that you meet its prerequisites. A level prerequisite refers to your level in the streamer class, not your overall character level.

## Addicted to the Knife

**Source: Player's Handbook (Master of Myriad Forms)**

***Prerequisite: 15th level***

You can cast *[temporary surgery](/tech:temporary-surgery)* at will, without expending a tech slot.

## Advanced Weeb

**Source: Player's Handbook (Thirsting Blade)**

***Prerequisite: 5th level, Vibe of the Weeb feature***

You can attack with your vibe weapon twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. This counts as the Extra Attack feature.

## Anomalous Vision

**Source: Player's Handbook (Eldritch Sight)**

You can cast *[anomaly detector](/tech:anomaly-detector)* at will, without expending a tech slot or material components.

## Armor Array

**Source: Unearthed Arcana 62 - Class Feature Variants (Armor Array)**

Prerequisite: Vibe of the Weeb feature

As an action, you can touch a suit of armor that isn’t being worn or carried by anyone and instantly don it, provided you aren’t wearing armor already. You are proficient with this suit of armor until it’s removed.

## Armor Projector

**Source: Player's Handbook (Armor of Shadows)**

You can cast *[hardlight armor](/tech:hardlight-armor)* on yourself at will, without expending a tech slot or material components.

## Auto-Stabilizer

**Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Gift of the Protectors)**

***Prerequisite: 9th level, Vibe of the Tech Guru feature***

With your permission, a creature can use its action to enter its number and biometric data into your cyberdeck. Your ‘deck can contain a number of individuals equal to your proficiency bonus. If you attempt to add a new individual to your deck when it is full, an older entry of your choice is deleted. As an action, as long as you can touch your cyberdeck, you can remove an individual’s information.

When any creature whose information is in your ‘deck is reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, the creature drops to 1 hit point instead. Once this effect is triggered, no creature can benefit from it until you finish a long rest.

## Auto-Translation Overlay

**Source: Player's Handbook (Eyes of the Rune Keeper)**

You can read all writing.

## Basilisk Code

**Source: Player's Handbook (Dreadful Word)**

***Prerequisite: 7th level***

You can cast *[conspiracy theory](/tech:conspiracy-theory)* once using a streamer tech slot. You can't do so again until you finish a long rest.

## Booster Jump

**Source: Player's Handbook (Otherworldly Leap)**

***Prerequisite: 9th level***

You can cast *[hydraulic boost](/tech:hydraulic-boost)* at will, without expending a tech slot.

## Branded Mascot

**Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Investment of the Chain Master)**

***Prerequisite: Vibe of the Avatar feature***

When you cast *[Sim-U-Pet](/tech:sim-u-pet)*, you call up an avatar that utilizes the brand recognition of your sponsor, granting the creature the following benefits:

- The mascot gains either a flying speed or a swimming speed (your choice) of 40 feet.
- As a bonus action, you can command the mascot to take the Attack action.
- The mascot’s weapon attacks are considered tech for the purpose of overcoming immunity and resistance to mundane attacks.
- If the mascot forces a creature to make a saving throw, it uses your tech save DC.
- When the mascot takes damage, you can use your reaction to grant it resistance against that damage.

## Butt Monkey

**Source: Player's Handbook (Thief of Five Fates)**

You can cast *[debuff](/tech:debuff)* once using a streamer tech slot. You can't do so again until you finish a long rest.

## Caffeine Freak

**Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Aspect of the Moon)**

***Prerequisite: Vibe of the Tech Guru feature***

You no longer need to sleep and can’t be forced to sleep by any means. To gain the benefits of a long rest, you can spend all 8 hours doing light activity, such as browsing the internet and keeping watch.

## Cheery Mascot

**Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Gift of the Ever-Living Ones)**

***Prerequisite: Vibe of the Avatar feature***

Whenever you regain hit points while your mascot is within 100 feet of you, treat any dice rolled to determine the hit points you regain as having rolled their maximum value for you.

## Chuuni Strike

**Source: Player's Handbook (Lifedrinker)**

***Prerequisite: 12th level, Vibe of the Weeb feature***

When you hit a creature with your vibe weapon, the creature takes extra entropic damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

## Circle of Jerks

**Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Bond of the Talisman)**

***Prerequisite: 12th level, Vibe of the Squad feature***

While someone else is wearing your amulet, you can use your action to teleport to the unoccupied space closest to them. The wearer of your amulet can do the same thing, using their action to teleport to you. The teleportation can be used a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and all expended uses are restored when you finish a long rest.

## Cloaking Field

**Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Shroud of Shadow)**

***Prerequisite: 15th level***

You can cast *[physical cloaking](/tech:physical-cloaking)* at will, without expending a tech slot.

## Disrupting Mark

**Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Maddening Hex)**

***Prerequisite: 5th level,* [dislike](/tech:dislike) *tech or a streamer feature that curses***

As a bonus action, you inflict a neural disruption on the target marked by your *[dislike](/tech:dislike)* tech or by a streamer feature of yours, such as Sportman’s Mark and Viral Load. When you do so, you deal software damage to the marked target and each creature of your choice within 5 feet of it. The software damage equals your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1 damage). To use this program, you must be able to see the marked target, and it must be within 30 feet of you.

## Electric Slide

**Source: Player's Handbook (Bewitching Whispers)**

***Prerequisite: 7th level***

You can cast *[cha-cha slide](/tech:cha-cha-slide)* once using a streamer tech slot. You can't do so again until you finish a long rest.

## Emergency Stasis Pod

**Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Tomb of Levistus)**

***Prerequisite: 5th level***

As a reaction when you take damage, you can lock yourself in a stasis field, which collapses at the end of your next turn. You gain 10 temporary hit points per streamer level, which take as much of the triggering damage as possible. Immediately after you take the damage, you gain vulnerability to fire damage, your speed is reduced to 0, and you are incapacitated. These effects, including any remaining temporary hit points, all end when the field collapses.

Once you use this program, you can’t use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

## Engram Whisperer

**Source: Player's Handbook (Whispers of the Grave)**

## Freeze Program

**Source: Player's Handbook (Chains of Carceri)**

You can cast *[seize target](/tech:seize-target)* at will—targeting an AI, surge, or virus—without expending a tech slot or material requirements. You must finish a long rest before you can use this invocation on the same creature again.

## Gravity Gun

## HUD Vision

**Source: Carbon Pink (HUD Vision)**

When you look at another person, you can see all publicly available information about them as a handy infographic surrounding them in your heads-up display. This will typically include their name, employer, major media events, news articles linked to them, and similar information at the DM’s discretion. You gain advantage on Culture rolls made to know information about people you meet.

## Improved Vibe Weapon

**Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Improved Pact Weapon)**

***Prerequisite: Vibe of the Weeb feature***

You can use any weapon you summon with your Vibe of the Weeb feature as a tech focus for your streamer techs.

In addition, the weapon counts as a high-end weapon with a +1 bonus to its attack and damage rolls, unless it is a high-end weapon that already has a better bonus to those rolls.

Finally, the weapon you conjure can be a hunting rifle, light pistol, revolver, automatic rifle, combat rifle, heavy pistol, hunting bow, shotgun or submachine gun.

## Inverted Stream

**Source: Player's Handbook (Gaze of Two Minds)**

You can use your action to touch a willing humanoid and perceive through its senses until the end of your next turn. As long as the creature is on the same plane of existence as you, you can use your action on subsequent turns to maintain this connection, extending the duration until the end of your next turn. While perceiving through the other creature's senses, you benefit from any special senses possessed by that creature, and you are blinded and deafened to your own surroundings.

## Kinetic Blow

**Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Eldritch Smite)**

***Prerequisite: 5th level, Vibe of the Weeb feature***

Once per turn when you hit a creature with your vibe weapon, you can expend a streamer tech slot to deal an extra 1d8 kinetic damage to the target, plus another 1d8 per level of the tech slot, and you can knock the target prone if it is Huge or smaller.

## L337 Hacker

## Mascot Vtuber

**Source: Player's Handbook (Voice of the Chain Master)**

***Prerequisite: Vibe of the Avatar feature***

You can communicate mentally with your mascot and perceive through your mascot’s senses. Additionally, while perceiving through your mascot’s senses, you can also speak through your mascot in your own voice, even if your mascot is normally incapable of speech.

## Maybe You’re Born With It…

**Source: Player's Handbook (Beguiling Influence)**

You gain proficiency in the Deception and Persuasion skills.

## Nanite Aura

**Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Cloak of Flies)**

***Prerequisite: 5th level***

As a bonus action, you can surround yourself with a field of buzzing, hungry nanites. The aura extends 5 feet from you in every direction, but not through total cover. It lasts until you’re incapacitated or you dismiss it as a bonus action.

The aura grants you advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks but disadvantage on all other Charisma checks. Any other creature that starts its turn in the aura takes poison damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 0 damage).

Once you use this program, you can’t use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

## Script Kiddie

**Source: Player's Handbook (Book of Ancient Secrets)**

***Prerequisite: Vibe of the Tech Guru feature***

You can now program scripts in your cyberdeck. Choose two 1st-level techs that have the script tag from [any class’s tech list](/techs); these scripts needn’t be from the same tech list. The techs appear in the ‘deck and don’t count against the number of techs you know. With your cyberdeck in hand, you can cast the chosen techs as scripts. You can’t cast the techs except as scripts, unless you’ve learned them by some other means. You can also cast a streamer tech you know as a script if it has the script tag.

On your adventures, you can add other scripts to your cyberdeck. When you find such a tech, you can add it to the book if the tech’s level is equal to or less than half your streamer level (rounded up) and if you can spare the time to transcribe the tech. For each level of the tech, the transcription process takes 2 hours and costs $$5000 for the licenses, peripheral upgrades, and software improvements needed to cast it.

## Untraceable Digits

**Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Far Scribe)**

***Prerequisite: 5th level, Vibe of the Tech Guru feature***

With your permission, a creature can use its action to enter its number and biometric data into your cyberdeck. Your ‘deck can contain a number of individuals equal to your proficiency bonus. If you attempt to add a new individual to your deck when it is full, an older entry of your choice is deleted. As an action, as long as you can touch your cyberdeck, you can remove an individual’s information.

You can cast the *[neurosynaptic text](/tech:neurosynaptic-text)* tech, targeting a creature whose information is in your cyberdeck, without using a tech slot or material requirements. You can either write the message down in your ‘deck or speak it aloud and have the ‘deck transcribe it as speech-to-text. The target hears your message in their mind, and may respond as normal. If they choose to respond, the message appears as text on your cyberdeck’s display. The message deletes itself after 1 minute.

As an action, you can technically erase a name on the page by touching it.

## Water Sports Entertainment

**Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Gift of the Depths)**

***Prerequisite: 5th level***

You can also cast *[oxygen filter](/tech:oxygen-filter)* without expending a tech slot. You regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest.

## X-Ray Vision

**Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Ghostly Gaze)**

***Prerequisite: 7th level***

As an action, you gain the ability to see through solid objects to a range of 30 feet. Within that range, you have nightvision if you don't already have it. This special sight lasts for 1 minute or until your concentration ends (as if you were concentrating on a tech). During that time, you perceive objects as ghostly, transparent images.

Once you use this invocation, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
