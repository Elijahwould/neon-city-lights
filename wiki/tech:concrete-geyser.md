##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything/Elemental Evil Player's Companion (Erupting Earth)

*3rd-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a piece of concrete, a vial of vinegar, and a pouch of baking soda)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a point you can see on the ground within range. A fountain of churned concrete and stone erupts in a 20-foot cube centered on that point. Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 3d12 bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Additionally, the ground in that area becomes difficult terrain until cleared away. Each 5-foot-square portion of the area requires at least 1 minute to clear by hand.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d12 for each slot level above 3rd.
