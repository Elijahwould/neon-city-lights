**Name.** Master Chief Nayomi "Red Sun" Suzuki

**Lineage.** Heavily Augmented Human (potentially part Remake)

**Age.** Unknown

**Height and Weight.** 10'6", 620 lbs

**Pronouns.** he/his

**Affiliation.** [The Jingos](/org:jingos)

**Features.** Possesses a near fully mechanized body with only the upper portion of his head being the last organic part of him; wields a highly modified railgun attached to his right arm

**Status.** Alive

Nayomi is the current leader of the Jingos and a veteran of the corporate wars. It is unknown which side or corporation Nayomi is originally from, nor is it known where he obtained his mechanical body. Nayomi is a cold, calculative leader compared to his constituents that seeks results above all else. While rare, he will occasionally undertake higher value contracts, of which he always does so solo.
