## "Smell like I sound, I'm lost in a crowd, and I'm hungry like the wolf…"

##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/the-splicer-race), Neon City NightsThanks to Celica#1988! Wow, I really hate patting myself on the back!

The proliferation of gene therapy and cosmetic surgery has led to an increase in cheap back-alley alternatives, using animal DNA to replicate the traits and features of biological augments once only available to the wealthy. Splicers are the result of this black-market gene manipulation. Mostly human, but with noticeable animal features dotted around their body, Splicers come in all shapes and sizes, and are made hardier and healthier than their pure human counterparts. But this unregulated genetic altering comes with an ever-present danger, as Splicers that undergo one too many surgeries may end up as the wild and lycanthropic Beastmen.

### Roleplaying a Splicer

You are mostly human, and carry the same motivations as all of humanity; good food, disposable income, and a comfortable home. Your animalistic side might bleed into your decision-making; perhaps wolfen augments keep you eager for the next hunt, while rabbit-like features might make you more cautious and alert to dangers. In any case, your spliced DNA has made you tough and protective of your pack.

### Splicer Naming Conventions

Splicers, being essentially human with extra bits, use human personal and family names, though their nicknames often stem from whatever animal parts they might have attached.

Some examples include Lucero Van Leeuwen, Bunny Koloski, Jack Pederson, Bull Jankowski, Natasha Viszla, Elanie Fernandez, Seamus Ionescu, Wolf Kovacs, etc.

# Splicer Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2. Increase a different one by 2, or increase two different scores by 1.
- **Age.** Splicers reach maturity at 18. Taking from some of nature's hardiest, Splicers tend to live well into their second century.
- **Size.** Your size is Small or Medium, selected when you choose this lineage. Varying wildly in build and height, Splicers can be anywhere from bone-thin and 3 feet tall to thicc bricc and an amazonian 9 foot.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Majorly Spliced.** You can choose one option from the Major Splices list. New Humans cannot select this trait with Natural Biodiversity.
- **Minorly Spliced.** You can choose two options from the Minor Splices list. New Humans who select this trait with Natural Biodiversity gain only one option from the Minor Splices list.

## Splicer Phenotype Generalizations

### Major Splices

- **Aquatic.** You can breathe underwater and gain a swimming speed equal to your base walking speed.
- **Bull-Headed.** Your horns are natural weapons, which you can use to make unarmed strikes. If you hit with them, you deal piercing damage equal to 1d6 + your Strength modifier, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike. You also have access to the following two traits:
  - *Slam.* Immediately after you hit a creature with a melee attack as a part of the Attack action on your turn, you can use a bonus action to attempt to shove that target with your horns. The target must be within 5 feet of you and no more than one size larger than you. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw against a DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier, you push it up to 10 feet away from you.
  - *Goring Charge.* Immediately after you use the Dash action on your turn and move at least 20 feet, you can make one melee attack with your horns as a bonus action. If you hit with them, you deal your horns' damage and can use your Slam against the target as a free action instead of a bonus action.
- **Darkvision.** You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light and in darkness as if it were dim light. You discern colors in that darkness only as shades of gray.
- **Kaiju Scales.** Choose one of the following damage types: fire, cold, lightning, acid. You gain resistance to that damage type.
- **Leporine Instincts.** You add your proficiency bonus to initiative rolls. You also have access to the following trait:
  - *Bunny Hop.* As a bonus action, you can jump a number of feet equal to five times your proficiency bonus, without provoking opportunity attacks. You can use this trait only if your speed is greater than 0. You can use it a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
- **Natural Armor.** When you aren't wearing armor, your AC is 13 + your Dexterity modifier. You can use your natural armor to determine your AC if the armor you wear would leave you with a lower AC. A shield's benefits apply as normal while you use your natural armor.
- **Powerful Build.** You count as one size larger for the purposes of grappling and shoving, as well as when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.
- **Retractable Claws.** Because of your claws, you have a climbing speed of 20 feet. In addition, your claws are natural weapons, which you can use to make unarmed strikes. If you hit with them, you deal slashing damage equal to 1d4 + your Strength or Dexterity modifier, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.
- **Serpentine Body.** Your base form becomes that of a serpent from anywhere between the waist to the end of your hips down, while our upper body remains humanoid in nature, making you akin to the lamia of legend. You can move at your full speed while crawling. You also have access to the following trait:
  - *Constrict.* Your constricting serpentine body is a natural weapon, which you can use to make unarmed attacks. If you successfully make an unarmed attack with your serpentine body, you deal bludgeoning damage equal to 1d6 + your Strength modifier. Once per turn, during your turn, when you successfully make an unarmed attack with your serpentine body, you may make a Grapple check. If you succeed, the creature is [grappled](/con:grappled) and [restrained](/con:restrained), and your walking speed is reduced to 0 until the grapple ends or you willingly end the restrained condition on them. A creature restrained in this way cannot use their hands as a somatic component for spells.
- **Taur Body.** Your base form becomes that of a quadruped animal from the waist down, while your upper body remains humanoid in nature. Your base walking speed is 35 feet. You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push or drag, and to determine who can use you as a mount. In addition, any climb that requires hands and feet is especially difficult for you because of your unusual legs. When you make such a climb, each foot of movement costs you 4 extra feet, instead of the normal 1 extra foot.

### Minor Splices

- **Adorable Tail.** You gain proficiency in the Persuasion skill. After succeeding on a Persuasion check against a single creature, you can use your bonus action to hypnotically sway your tail. Until the start of your next turn, the creature takes a penalty to its passive Perception and Insight equal to half your proficiency bonus.
- **Animal Tongue.** You gain proficiency in the Animal Handling skill. You gain a limited ability to comprehend and communicate with critters. You cannot directly speak with them, but can discern their intentions and communicate basic ideas to them.
- **Bestial Senses.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks related to sound and smell.
- **Bloodhound.** You gain proficiency in the Survival skill. When you deal damage to a creature, you can choose to cut off a piece of their clothes, chassis, skin, or bleed them. When you make a Survival check to track such a creature, you can treat a d20 roll of 4 or lower as a 5.
- **Digitigrade Legs.** Your walking speed is increased by 5 feet.
- **Exotic Colors.** You gain proficiency in the Performance skill. When you would make a Dexterity (Stealth) or Charisma (Deception) check to conceal your presence or motives in plain sight, such as attempting to play a flashy role, you can instead make a Charisma (Performance) check for as long as you maintain the ruse.
- **Expanded Airways.** You can hold your breath for up to 15 minutes at a time.
- **Frog Legs.** Your long jump is up to 25 feet and your high jump is up to 15 feet, with or without a running start.
- **Full Udders.** As a bonus action, you can provide nourishment to sustain two humanoids, granting them temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus. Once you use this splice, you can’t use it again until you finish a short rest.
- **Goat Hooves.** You ignore difficult terrain when making a Dash action.
- **Heightened Senses.** You gain proficiency in the Perception skill. If a creature's check would be contested by your passive Perception, you may instead treat your passive Perception as if it were an active Perception check, meaning that the check does not beat your passive Perception when meeting it.
- **Long Tail.** You gain proficiency in the Acrobatics skill. When you move as a result of an effect that is not your movement, such as the longsword's step in or the shockwave tech, you can use your reaction to move either an additional 5 feet or 5 feet less.
- **Monkey Feet.** You gain a climbing speed equal to your base walking speed.
- **Natural Camouflage.** You gain proficiency in the Stealth skill. On your turn, if your movement would break your Stealth, such as by bringing you into direct line of sight of a creature you had previously hid from, you may move up to half your speed as a reaction, moving back towards the position you started your turn in, maintaining Stealth.
- **Oxen Muscles.** You gain proficiency in the Athletics skill. Pushing or dragging weight in excess of your carrying capacity only applies a 10 foot penalty to your movement speed. This penalty is further reduced to only a 5 foot penalty if you take the Dash action.
- **Pointed Quills.** A creature that grapples you takes 1d8 damage from the quills at the beginning of each of its turns in which it is grappling you. If a creature swallows you, it takes damage from the quills each round at the start of your turn.
- **Sharp Teeth.** Instead of your natural weapons, you can make a bite attack. If you hit with it, you deal piercing damage equal to 1d6 + your Strength modifier, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.
- **Terrifying Visage.** You gain proficiency in the Intimidation skill. If your Strength is higher than your Charisma, you can make Intimidation rolls as Strength (Intimidation) rolls, instead.
- **Tough Hide.** Choose one of the following damage types: bludgeoning, piercing, slashing. You gain resistance to non-technical instances of that damage type.
