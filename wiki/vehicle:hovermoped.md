*Vehicle, civilian*

A hovermoped is basically a single-seated scooter with a wide base that has several hover generators mounted on the back: one on the front that controls the steering, and two on the back aimed downwards at ~30" angles to stabilize, with a small propulsion engine mounted there to give it motion. They are not particularly sturdy but can be quite evasive, reaching decently fast speeds despite being primarily built to be compact.
