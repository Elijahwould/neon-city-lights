Just down the coast a little way from Neon City is a wonderful little town that reeks of cheap rum, sex, sunscreen, and the sea. Having found a slightly less polluted than usual stretch of beach down the coast from NC the corps found a flawless opportunity to establish a vacation town they can peddle to the masses. Gobbo Beach, aptly named for its high resident and out of town gobbo population, is a perpetual spring-break town.

The beach and surrounding hills are lined with mass produced and frequently renovated condos rented out by the week, larger older condos are held by the locals who actually work the bars, grills, and utilities to keep the place running. Gene tailored palm trees are all over the place and the hastily poured concrete sidewalks are all just the right amount of cracked and chipped yet safe to walk on barefoot.

Being at the mouth of the Rum Veins means fresh water and sugarcane… which means rum! Sugarcane farms and distilleries exist in droves upstream to the east of the city itself, producing one of the city's major exports.

The town also has the one, the only, the best… HentaiHub water park!

Given the nature of the town, there is a reason that statistically 10% of all gobbos are probably conceived here. Constant parties, cheap booze, copious amounts of nude sunbathing, and an aura of poor decision making back up these numbers. Business only ever slows down in the winter, and thus renting a condo is at its cheapest for those who want to spend the little time they have off throwing snowballs on the freezing beach. Fishing, jet-skis, bars, grills, rental speed boats, and middle managers trying to show off for their friends always ensures there's something going on.
