**Organization.** Red Light Rabbits (RLR)

**Leader.** Shylyss "The Ara-Ara Arsonist" Arnou

**Organization Type.** Criminal Gang

**Years in Existence.** <1 year

**Goals.** Unilateral control and protection over the [Red Light District](/district:red-light-district)

**Creed.** "*Crowns instead of chains.*"

**Impact.** Minor

Founded recently by [Shylyss Arnou](/legend:shylyss), the Red Light Rabbits are situated in a unique position in the Red Light District: founded far enough away from main corporate entities and attention from police forces, while also just outside the poorer areas where rival gangs and danger runs amok. Despite initial headaches during their founding, their presence is entrenched within the district as an entity that seeks to grow and bring much of the district under its control. Though the RLR is a fusion of a biker gang as well as an Old World Mafia, the code of conduct within is rigid. They largely refrain from engaging in immoral acts thanks to Shylyss' leadership, though that is suspected to be from a stance of fear of reprisal and respect for their rules. For all her bellicose nature, though, she does take exceedingly good care of those who are a part of her gang, treating them like family.

The Red Light Rabbits see all of their namesake district as ripe for the taking, expanding rapidly through absorption. The allure of a home where one can rest their head and be protected by one of the most fearsome criminals in the city is a difficult thing to resist. Those who do not accept assimilation under the Rabbits have their territory besieged by harriers and agents, sent in to sabotage gang supplies, siphon protection funds, and sway public opinion to align with the Rabbits. All-out war is only a last resort to forcibly subjugate the most stubborn of gangs.

The RLR is held to a strict standard and, much like the Old World Mafia, contains its own code of conduct. Offenses or breaks from the code can result in the offender being maimed, shot, branded or even killed for their transgressions at the hands of a superior. For this reason, they are generally respected amongst other gangs within their sphere of influence. Rumors of their deeds include providing protection to individuals in exchange for loyalty (rarely), sparing high-value-targets of rival gangs (sometimes), and capturing heinous criminals to allow them to be punished by those they've wronged (common).
