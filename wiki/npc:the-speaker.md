**Name.** The Speaker (aka The Voice of Thorn)

**Lineage.** Nayaling

**Age.** 30 (assumed)

**Height and Weight.** 5'11", 250 lbs

**Pronouns.** any

**Affiliation.** [The Cult of Thorn](/org:cult-of-thorn)

**Features.** Septuple Ocular Array, [APEX](/corp:apex) Warlock-Class Cyberarms, APEX Cheetah-class Cyberlegs

**Status.** Alive and active.

While not as cybered up as their partner [Lilith](/npc:lilith), The Speaker nonetheless carries a somewhat bewildering amount of chrome in her body. On its face is an hardened Ocular Array set with seven red multi-purpose optics. Their arms are replaced with lithe cyberarms, and his unguligrade legs have been replaced by a pair of plantigrade cyberlegs.

The Speaker knows they live in a world of almost literal demigods. And as a result? She's got zero sense of self preservation. However, this doesn't mean he's constantly ready to die - instead, it means that it is able to truly worm under the skin of others with a level of confidence hitherto unheard of.
