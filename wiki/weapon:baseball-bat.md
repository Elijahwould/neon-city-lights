*1d8 bludgeoning, 4 lb., two-handed*

A baseball bat is a smooth wooden or metal club used in the sport of baseball to hit the ball after it is thrown by the pitcher. By regulation it may be no more than 2.75 inches (7.0 cm) in diameter at the thickest part and no more than 42 inches (1.067 m) in length.

It could be any kind of baseball bat, really: aluminum, wooden, for college use, for softball, for baseball—it doesn't really matter. What matters is that it's a glorified club, and that you can swing it at people's faces.

### Features

- ***Slowing Blow.*** Once per turn per enemy, hitting an opponent reduces their movement speed by 5 feet until the start of your next turn.
- ***Home Run.*** Whenever you roll a 20 on an attack with this weapon, you may knock your target prone.
