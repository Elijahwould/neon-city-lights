*Vehicle, civilian*

A hoverbike is basically a motorcycle-shaped hover vehicle with a wide base that has several hover generators mounted on the back: two in-line at the front to control the steering and manage weight, and two on the back aimed downwards at ~30" angles to stabilize, with a powerful propulsion engine mounted to provide forward motion. They are somewhat sturdy and a bit faster than a hovermoped, owing to the heavier engine.
