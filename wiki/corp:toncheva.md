**Corporation Name.** Toncheva Applied Healthcare Services

**CEO.** Yana Toncheva

**Years in business.** ~57

**Industry.** Healthcare equipment and medicine

**Motto.** *"Serving the people, until the last."*

**Impact.** Major

TonchCorp is known for two major activities: being violently competitive, and being overwhelmingly generous. Much of Neon City's day-to-day health care services are provided by specialists using TonchCorp equipment or prescription medication.

There are few companies that have the clout on the medical market that TonchCorp carries, and those that do either keep to themselves, or actively dissuade TonchCorp from aggression in a bid to acquire market share.

But the mind behind TonchCorp is cold, calculating, and is excellent in reading her foes. She will complete her primary goal, no matter the cost: make health care in Neon City as easy and cheap as possible.
