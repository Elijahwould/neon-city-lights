##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings)

It takes a certain caliber of person to serve the perfect drink, lend the perfect ear and occasionally toss the perfect drunk, and over the years you’ve become a master at all three. But with no prospects beyond the vain hope of inheriting a hole-in-the-wall bar, it’s time to get out there.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Insight, Sleight of Hand
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Microbrewing kit, Microlab
- **Equipment.** Bartenders start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A microbrewing kit
  - A microlab
  - A white shirt
  - A waistcoat
  - A bow tie
  - A pair of black trousers or a black skirt
  - A bartender's apron
  - Three bottles of mango liquor
  - A credchip worth $$1,500

### Feature: What'll You Have?

The ritual of providing alcohol to strangers, and forming an instantaneous bond over it, is ancient and powerful. As an initiate of the ancient order of bartenders, you're familiar with this rite, and its power.

If you provide someone with an alcoholic beverage, whether it's a gift or a purchase, you gain advantage on the next Insight, Persuasion or Deception check you use while interacting with them.
