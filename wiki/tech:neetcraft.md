##### Source: Player's Handbook (Druidcraft)

*Manipulation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Utilizing code fragments and a few drops of custom-made SLUDGE, you create one of the following effects within range:

- You create a tiny, harmless sensory effect that predicts what the weather will be at your location for the next 24 hours. The effect might manifest as a golden orb for clear skies, a cloud for rain, falling snowflakes for snow, and so on. This effect persists for 1 round.
- You instantly enrich and help grow a small organic plant or a digital facsimile of such.
- You create an instantaneous, harmless sensory effect, such as falling CD cases, a puff of ventilation, the sound of a small animal, or the faint odor of burnt carpet. The effect must fit in a 5-foot cube.
- You instantly light or snuff out a light bulb, a flashlight, or a small camp oven.
- You cause a holo-message in Shut-In Slang to appear on a piece of dorky memorabilia, such as an anime model, organic plant or a digital facsimile of such, within 100 miles that you have seen before. Selecting this effect when casting the tech again can erase the message.
