##### Source: Player's Handbook (Friends)

*Conception trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** S, M (a small amount of applied makeup and a selfie taken as the tech is cast)

**Duration:** 1 round

For the duration, you have advantage on all Charisma checks directed at one creature of your choice that isn't hostile toward you. If you roll a 1 on the ability or skill check, the creature realizes that you used tech and social pressure to influence its mood and becomes hostile toward you. A creature prone to violence might attack you. Another creature might seek retribution in other ways (at the DM's discretion), depending on the nature of your interaction with it.
