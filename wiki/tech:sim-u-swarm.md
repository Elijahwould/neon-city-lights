##### Source: Lost Laboratory of Kwalish (Flock of Familiars)

*2nd-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You temporarily summon three SimUPets– autonomous projection of small creatures that take animal forms of your choice. Each SimUPet uses the same rules and options for a familiar conjured by the *[Sim-U-Pet](/tech:sim-u-pet)* tech. All the SimUPets conjured by this tech must be the same type of creature (A.I., Spam, or Virus; your choice). If you already have a SimUPet conjured by the *[Sim-U-Pet](/tech:sim-u-pet)* tech or similar means, then one fewer Sim-U-Pets are conjured by this tech.

Sim-U-Pets summoned by this tech can telepathically communicate with you and share their visual or auditory senses while they are within 1 mile of you.

When you cast a tech with a range of touch, one of the Sim-U-Pets conjured by this tech can deliver the tech, as normal. However, you can cast a touch tech through only one Sim-U-Pet per turn.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 3rd level or higher, you conjure an additional SimUPet for each slot level above 2nd.
