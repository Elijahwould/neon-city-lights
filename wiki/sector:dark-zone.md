**Sector.** Downtown Dark Zone

**Population.** ?

**Type.** Commercial / Residential

Living up to its name by consistently being subjected to blackouts (which is bad enough given the Sun doesn't shine down in the Undercity), the Downtown Dark Zone is where most of the middle to lower class infrastructure of the Undercity is located. Housing for datacenter workers, commercial business employees and the like line the streets along the northern edge nearest to uptown. If you're looking for a quick job and have a friend to keep your back guarded in the likely event of a mugging, this is the perfect place to go. Well, if you can stand the shitty pay, and even shittier benefits.

The southernmost edge is the place to be, however, as it holds [the Arcanum](/loc:arcanum). It is also known to be the stomping grounds for members of the [Cult of Thorn](/org:cult-of-thorn).
