##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Efreeti Chain)

*Armor ([ceramiron](/armor:ceramiron)), legendary (requires installation)*

**Specs:** 6 lbs., $$2,100,000

While wearing this armor, you gain a +3 bonus to AC, and you are immune to fire damage. In addition, you can stand on and walk across molten rock as if it were solid ground.
