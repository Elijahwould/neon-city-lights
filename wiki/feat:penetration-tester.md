##### Source: [Feats for the Fearsome](https://www.patreon.com/posts/feats-for-16156471) (Escape Artist)

You are practiced in picking and bypassing locks and slipping any type of bond and can even do so blindfolded. You gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You have advantage on ability checks you make to slip bonds or escape grapples.
- You can hold your breath for twice as long as normal for your race/ability scores.
- You have advantage on ability checks you make to pick mechanical locks, and you can do so without a locksmith's kit, as long as you have some suitable instrument. Moreover, you can pick locks one-handed and when blinded.
- If it is feasible for an electronic lock to be bypassed through mechanical means (such as shimming, magnet usage, etc.), you are able to identify which ways you can get through the lock, bypassing the use of a hacker's kit, and can do so without one, as long as you have access to suitable instruments and materials.
