People say "sweep the legs" isn't a valid technique, but consider, *think fast chucklenuts!* And now you're on your ass.

- A prone creature’s only movement option is to crawl, every 1 foot of movement costing 1 extra foot, until it stands up.
- A prone creature can stand up by using half of its movement.
- A prone creature makes melee attack rolls with disadvantage.
- An attack roll against a prone creature has advantage if the attacker is within 5 feet of the creature. Otherwise, the attack roll has disadvantage.
