**Organization Name.** Rhyton Brewer's Union

**Leader.** Jarl Erikål

**Organization Type.** Myriad Moonshiners

**Years in existence.** ~60

**Goals.** Financial Aid, Protections, and Distribution of Independently Brewed Alcohols

**Creed.** "*A tankard shared is a greeting unto Valhalla.*"

**Impact.** Minor

Rhyton is the name attributed to both a collective movement of lesser known independent and anonymous liquor and spirit labels, and the decentralized distilleries, owned by the orccish Erikål, that supply and support them. The primary financial backer of the organization named himself its Jarl to hold the group to his high standards, citing the days of his youth; first venturing into Neon City proper as [Quantum Cola](/corp:quantum-cola) was still young, mystified and enamored by the love and effort put into every drink he found.

It is rumored that his backing and supplying of Rhyton's efforts might only be a small portion of his wealth, as it is public knowledge that the Jarl accrued the distilleries and legal protections he extends to his alcoholic allies through many an ancient and preserved land deed.

The truth remains that if you find yourself thirsty for a right, proper drink that hasn't been touched by Quantum or the mass-produced swill of its knock-offs, then you'll be drinking from a glass filled with Rhyton Brewer's Union Approved.
