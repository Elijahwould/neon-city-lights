##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Zephyr Strike)

*1st-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V

**Duration:** 1 minute

You move and strike with tactical military precision. Until the tech ends, your movement doesn’t provoke opportunity attacks.

Once before the tech ends, you can give yourself advantage on one weapon attack roll on your turn. That attack deals an extra 1d8 force damage on a hit. Whether you hit or miss, your walking speed increases by 30 feet until the end of that turn.
