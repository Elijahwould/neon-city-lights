##### Source: Eberron: Rising from the Last War (Ventilating Lungs)

*Gizmo, rare (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., N/A

Outside an *[EMP Field](/tech:emp-field)* or any other effect that suppresses tech, these lungs allow you to breathe normally in any environment (including a vacuum), and you have advantage on saving throws against harmful gases such as those created by a *[riot control agent](/tech:riot-control-agent)* tech, *[aerosol stink spray](/tech:aerosol-stink-spray)* tech, inhaled poisons, and gaseous breath weapons.

As an action, you can use these lungs to exhale a gust of wind, as if you had cast the *[aeroblast](/tech:aeroblast)* (tech save DC 15) with no components. This property of the lungs can't be used again until the next dawn.

If your installation to the lungs ends, your original lungs reappear.
