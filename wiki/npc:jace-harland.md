**Name.** Jace Harland

**Lineage.** Gobbo…?

**Age.** Appears to be the Gobbo-equivalent of late 20s

**Height and Weight.** 4'0", 250lbs

**Pronouns.** she/they

**Affiliation.** Vagrant, Hemlock's place; formerly 47th Infantry, Viking division

**Features.** Standard Gobbo—green skin with amber eyes, sleeves of tattoos that stop at the wrist and curiously enough, a tail that is kept hidden

**Status.** Alive

In previous lives, then-Jason was a grunt through and through, burning through blood and gunpowder (and patience) in conflicts they didn't start but were going to have to finish. For a paycheck. From a government that crashed and burned. When the world finally saw the bright blue flash across the horizon, they took one last body hop to anywhere but here.

Jace ran with gangs to survive and learn the new world and its wiles, hopping from place to place. With the most recent one torn apart by mercs, she now resides with Azerial and company. A group of anachronistically placed individuals trying to survive the same.
