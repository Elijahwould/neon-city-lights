##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Sword of Life Stealing)

*Weapon (any sword or [machete](/weapon:machete)), rare (requires installation)*

**Specs:** variable, $$140,000

When you attack a creature with this technical weapon and roll a 20 on the attack roll, that target takes an extra 10 entropic damage if it isn't a construct or a hacked. You also gain 10 temporary hit points.
