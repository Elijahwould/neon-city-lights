*Vehicle, civilian*

A motorcycle, often called a motorbike, bike, cycle, or (if three-wheeled) trike, is a two- or three-wheeled motor vehicle. Motorcycle design varies greatly to suit a range of different purposes: long-distance travel, commuting, cruising, sport (including racing), and off-road riding, though this particular class of motorcycles primarily focuses on speed and agility.

While not as common among the general populace, they are a favorite among many runners due to their speed and look. The most popular models in circulation among runners as a result are both the KAI MCEX-750 from [Kazanari Automotive](/corp:kazanari-automotive) due to its sleek design and ease of customizability, and the KAI YCX-1050, also from Kazanari, for its sheer focus on power. Still, people who'd like to stand out—or who want to eke out a better bang for their buck—often find themselves favoring different models like the STX-775 for its strange but appealing stylings, or the KAX-950 for its lower price point and still-impressive power.
