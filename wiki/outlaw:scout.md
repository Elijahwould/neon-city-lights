## "Hear that? We’re gonna have company soon."

Out in the wastelands beyond the mega-cities, life is cheap. Mutants and monsters are everywhere, and if the critters don’t get you, the raiders probably will. Food and bullets are worth more than cash, and the tribes will happily kill each other over a crate of nutri-bars. In conditions like this, it pays to be aware of one’s surroundings. Scouts are outlaws who focus their skills on surviving in the wild places beyond corporate control—primarily the wastes, but also the abandoned and overgrown parts of the undercity.

##### Source: Xanathar’s Guide to Everything (Scout)

### Skirmisher

Starting at 3rd level, you are difficult to pin down during a fight. You can move up to half your speed as a reaction when an enemy ends its turn within 5 feet of you. This movement doesn’t provoke opportunity attacks.

### Survivalist

When you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency in the Nature and Survival skills if you don’t already have it, and you gain expertise in those skills as well.

Additionally, you gain proficiency with hunting bows. During a long rest, you can craft or recover 1d6 arrows for a hunting bow as long as you have access to basic materials.

### Superior Mobility

At 9th level, your walking speed increases by 10 feet. If you have a climbing or swimming speed, this increase applies to that speed as well.

### Bushwhacker

Starting at 13th level, you excel at leading ambushes and acting first in a fight.

You have advantage on initiative rolls. In addition, the first creature you hit during the first round of a combat becomes easier for you and others to strike; attack rolls against that target have advantage until the start of your next turn.

### Sudden Strike

Starting at 17th level, you can strike with deadly speed. If you take the Attack action on your turn, you can make one additional attack as a bonus action. This attack can benefit from your Sneak Attack even if you have already used it this turn, but you can’t use your Sneak Attack against the same target more than once in a turn.
