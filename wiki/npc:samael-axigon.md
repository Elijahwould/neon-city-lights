**Name.** Samael Axigon

**Age.** 50 (physically appears to be early 30s)

**Height and Weight.** 6’4”, 180 lbs

**Race.** Human

**Pronouns.** he/him

**Affiliation.** [Axigon Arms Dealing](/corp:axigon-arms)

**Notable Features.** Golden cybernetic arms and gold business attire, stoic personality and charisma

**Status.** Alive

Unlike most executives and corporate leaders in Neon City, Samael was actually born outside the city limits in the deserts. The son of an unknown nomad family, he was always an ingenious tinkerer. Often, he’d build gear from scrap metal and parts or help repair equipment he and his people used. It wasn’t until he stumbled across what looked to be a borg of mysterious make that his life would change forever.

This borg brought with it a strange device. An engrammatic cube stored with a wealth of knowledge and technology unknown to Samael and his people. It, however, was not alone in this venture. Pursued by bandits, they quickly captured Samael and his people, forcing them to work within a once abandoned factory to fabricate and solve the secrets behind the technology left behind by this borg.

Samael took this time to work diligently and seemingly without question. After almost a year of toiling, Samael developed the first prototype in a long-standing line of robotic marvels and weaponry. Marvels that he swiftly turned on his bandit masters to free his people. While his people were thankful for what he had done for them, they were equally fearful of the dangers this technology could do; potentially bringing even more bandits after them.

They were forever thankful. But he couldn’t be allowed to stay. Many of the tribe's younger members wanted to be like him, to emulate and share in his genius. He was their hero. Because of that, he had to leave.

And so, with the engrammatic cube in hand, Samael left for the wall of lights upon the horizon. For Neon City.
