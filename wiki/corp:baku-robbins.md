**Corporation Name.** Baku-Robbins Body, Image, and Cream

**CEO.** "Papa" Juan Valdez

**Years in business.** ~50

**Industry.** Fast Food, body mods, and fertility

**Motto.** "*Thirty-one Flavors of Fun!*"

**Impact.** Minor

Containing elements of an actual old-world remnant corporation, the origin of Baku-Robbins is lost to time, its practices and traditions passed down by rote before they were eventually encoded into the corporation’s three-ring binder. Previously a titan of industry, the corporation decided to reduce its profile and status in order to preserve its corporate integrity.

Baku-Robbins now works with relatively minor therapy, body-sculpt/implant, and food service that has "service contracts" with other corporations and significant depot in Neon City. At their many stores across the city, their employees produce what is known as "icing cream", along with coffee, milk, and donut filling, and finally, ice cream base. All of this is utilized as a reminiscence to their Old World Traditional "31 Flavors".

While customers at Baku-Robbins shops most often receive their order "to-go" in various sizes of cup, the constant stream of (relatively mild) aphrodisiacs and/or hormones produced from their various implants makes it extremely easy to persuade a Baku-Robbins wage worker to allow the customer to consume the requested product directly from the employee-dispenser, often receiving more than they paid for.
