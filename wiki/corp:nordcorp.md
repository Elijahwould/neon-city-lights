**Corporation Name.** Nord Corporate Offices

**CEO.** Dymra'nyx AKA "Big Sister"

**Years in business.** ~160+

**Industry.** Internet Service Providers and Cybersecurity

**Motto.** *"Trust your connection to Nordcorp."*

**Impact.** Major

Starting life in the ancient past as a service provider of secure internet connection, NordCorp eventually gained control of most modern internet providers through either buyouts or hostile takeovers of controlling ownership. They provide internet connections to all, all the while policing the dark web with the best of cybersecurity.

They are currently headed by the reclusive Dymra'nyx, someone who is only seen through her avatar and username "Big Sister".

Since the fall of Bliss Co., Nord's stranglehold on the internet has loosened once again—many rogue ISPs have begun to pop back up around Neon City, much to the chagrin of Nord's board members.
