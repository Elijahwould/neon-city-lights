##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Spell Scroll, 5th Level)

*Burner, rare*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$300,000

A tech burner bears the words of a single tech, contained within as an encoded cipher. If the tech is on your class's tech list, you can read the burner and cast its tech without providing any material components. Otherwise, the burner is unintelligible. Casting the tech by reading the burner requires the burner's normal casting time. Once the tech is cast, the energy stored within the burner is expended, and the burner becomes inert. If the casting is interrupted, the burner is not lost.

If the tech is on your class's tech list but of a higher level than you can normally cast, you must make an ability check using your techcasting ability to determine whether you cast it successfully. The DC is 15. On a failed check, the tech disappears from the burner with no other effect.

Once the tech is cast, the words on the burner fade, and the burner becomes inert.

A tech cast from this burner has a save DC of 17 and an attack bonus of +9.

A *[Techwizard](/class:techwizard)* tech on a tech burner can be copied just as techs in *[cyberdecks](/gear:cyberdeck)* can be copied. When a tech is copied from a tech burner, the copier must succeed on a DC 15 Intelligence (Technology) check. If the check succeeds, the tech is successfully copied. Whether the check succeeds or fails, the burner becomes inert.
