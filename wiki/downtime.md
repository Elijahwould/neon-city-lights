## "What, you thought this was only job? Oh, honey, not even close."

How you gain DT:

- Grant 2 DT for every MP gained in a job.
- Can forego 1 MP to instead gain 2 DT.
- Mercurian grants levels for activity in roleplay and job channels. Each level can be exchanged for 1 DT.

*Downtime Costs*

| Cost | Amt. Buyable | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | ∞ | Work for money (using a skill) |
| 20 | ∞ | Learn an old world language |
| 20 | Proficiency | Gain a vehicle or tool proficiency |
| 30 | 1/2 proficiency | Gain a skill proficiency |
| 40 | Up to 90 base AS | Rank Credits on a Street Legend+ character |
| Variable | ∞ | Crafting an item |
