#### Stokerian Thrall Master

*Shameful Sucker · **Skills** Persuasion +4 · ***Stokerian Weaknesses.*** Stoker thralls' weaknesses are UV light and bare concrete. When the thrall ends its turn in contact with one of its weaknesses (such as being bathed in florescent light or forced onto unworked ground), it takes 10 neon damage and 10 software damage. While in contact with its weakness, it can't use its Regeneration trait. · ***Final Transmission.*** The thrall's mind and lingering digital presence are downloaded and scrubbed three rounds after it dies, or immediately if it was killed by a critical hit. Each creature within 5 feet of the thrall's body must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 14 (3d8) software damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The thrall becomes an invalid target for any form of resurrection until the netsucker it was subservient to allows it or dies. The netsucker the thrall was subservient to becomes technically aware of the events leading up to the thrall's death within 24 hours of the final transmission. · ***Modified Bite.*** When the thrall hits a creature with its bite attack, the target must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or take 1 level of exhaustion if it does not already have any. · Actions · ***Black Claw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage plus 4 (1d6) entropic damage.
