## "I'm a professional; you're an amateur. You can at least tell the difference, right?"

##### Source: adapted from [Benjamin Huffman's Pugilist v3](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/184921/The-Pugilist-Class)

### Grappling

Starting when you choose this discipline at 3rd level, you gain the following additional mettle features.

- **Leg Control.** When a creature attempts to break your grapple and succeeds, you can use your reaction and spend 1 mettle point to force the creature to roll again. The creature must use the second result.
- **Quick Clinch.** When a hostile creature’s movement provokes an opportunity attack from you, you can use your reaction and spend 1 mettle point to attempt to grapple the creature instead of making an opportunity attack.
- **Takedown.** You can use a bonus action and spend 1 mettle point to make a Strength (Athletics) check with a DC equal to the AC of one creature within 5 feet of you. On a success, you grapple the creature and knock it prone.

### Jacked

Also at 3rd level, you gain proficiency in Athletics if you don’t already have it. If you do already have proficiency in Athletics, your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check that uses that proficiency.

### Entangled Wrestler

Starting at 6th level, when you have an enemy creature grappled, you gain half cover against all attacks made against you by a creature you are not grappling. When a weapon attack made by a creature you are not grappling misses you, you can use your reaction and spend 1 mettle point to have that creature make the same attack with a new roll against an enemy creature you are grappling.

### Larger Than Life

Starting at 11th level, you count as being one size larger for the purposes of grappling and shoving. In addition, you can move your full movement speed when you are dragging or carrying a grappled creature your size or smaller.

### Ground and Pound

At 17th level, your mastery of grappling reaches its peak. While you have a creature grappled, you gain advantage on all attacks against it. Additionally, when you make an unarmed strike or streetfighter weapon attack against a creature you have grappled, you score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.
