##### Source: Player's Handbook (Bless)

*1st-level conception*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a sprinkling of divine juice)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You buff up to three creatures of your choice within range. Whenever a target makes an attack roll or a saving throw before the Tech ends, the target can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to the attack roll or saving throw.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this Tech using a Tech slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st.
