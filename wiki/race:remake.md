## "For some people, a backup plan. For others, an ultimate goal. Me? I just think this robit is hot."

##### Source: Player's Handbook, [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/the-remake-race)

Remakes are fully robotic shells housing a digitized human consciousness, either to continue working after the death of their original body, or to enjoy an everlasting retirement. Depending on the package purchased, Remakes can be a vague collection of distant memories encapsulated in a heavy duty high load laborer, a raw personality implanted in a tactile and versatile android, or a one-to-one replication of a customer’s psyche in the hand-crafted mannequin of their choosing.

Having experienced their own death, Remakes often live lives of either chaste philanthropy or wild hedonism, though those in cheaper bodies often return to work to afford a more human-like body.

Remakes have the sensation of touch and the ability to feel emotions, though the sensation is muted on cheaper models.

### Roleplaying a Remake

When roleplaying a Remake, think about how you came to afford the exorbitant price of having your consciousness digitized and implanted into a robot, as well as how your original body died. Were you a high level executive that was killed in a targeted assassination by a rival corporation? Were you a gang leader that amassed a fortune in the smuggling racket, and needed a contingency? Were you just a lucky guy that happened upon a relic of the Old World and sold it to an eccentric collector, using your newfound wealth to fund a robotic retirement?

Think about how your character’s outlook might change after experiencing their own death. Are you exceedingly cautious or filled with reckless abandon? Did you die a wizened old soul, or in the prime of your life? All these questions could affect your decisions in a roleplaying situation.

### Remake Naming Conventions

Remakes, being encoded personality engrams in a robotic host, use the same name as they did when they were alive. People that tend to be able to afford being remade are Elfs, Humans and Nayalings, and so use the naming conventions of their former race.

# Remake Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1, or increase three different scores by 1.
- **Age.** You decide your age by how old you were when you originally died + how long you’ve been a Remake. Some Remakes maintain the distinction (for example, 25+26 years old). The oldest Remakes are 255 years old.
- **Size.** Your size is Medium. Heavy Loader and Nimble 36 pattern stand from just under 5 foot, up to just over 8, and Life-Lite's adhere to their customizations.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Robits and Bytes.** For the purposes of observation techs and creature type detection abilities, you read as a machine in addition to humanoid.
- **Legacy Programming.** If you replace a different lineage with this one, you can keep the following elements of that lineage: any skill proficiencies you gained from it and any climbing, flying, burrowing, or swimming speed you gained from it.
  - If you don't keep any of those elements or you choose this lineage at character creation, you gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.
- **Robotic Resilience.** You’re not organic, and don’t suffer from the issues most humanoids do.
  - You have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned and you have resistance to poison damage.
  - You are immune to disease.
  - You don’t need to eat, drink or breathe, though can choose to if you're of the Life-Lite pattern group or have been properly modified.
  - You can choose to carry a spare battery with you to negate the need to spend 4 hours inactive to gain the benefits of a long rest. You can only make use of a spare battery once a long rest, and a negated inactivity period does not restore it.
- **Low Power Mode.** You can finish a long rest in 4 hours if you spend those hours in an inactive, motionless state in an area with sunlight or UV light, during which you retain consciousness.
- **Polysteel Protection.** Your chassis has built-in defensive features, which determine your armor class without armor and can be modified with armor:
  - You can only use armor with which you have proficiency. To don armor, you must incorporate it into your chassis over the course of 1 hour, during which you remain in contact with the armor. To doff armor, you must spend 1 hour removing it. You can rest when removing or incorporating armor in this way.
  - While you live, your armor cannot be removed from your body against your will.
- **Insurance Policy.** While you live, your engrammatic consciousness chip is locked into your body by regulatory safety designs and can not be targetted or removed beyond special circumstance, such as your chassis being repossessed. If you die, your consciousness chip can be removed from your robotic corpse, or the last space you occupied if you leave no corpse, with an object interaction and a replacement chassis can be supplied for $$10,000.

Additionally, techs with the sole purpose of returning you to life offer the techcaster a refund for half the expenses of any consumed material component.

## Remake Pattern Groups

### Heavy Loader

The Heavy Loader model is designed for manual labor, with a bulky and robust frame capable of lifting heavy loads and enduring harsh conditions. While they're not pretty, they'll get the job done.

- **Maximum Loader Plating.** When not wearing armor, your armor class is 18. You have disadvantage on Stealth checks. Any feature that modifies how you interact with heavy armor modifies your maximum loader plating. If you would integrate armor that supresses disadvantage on Stealth checks, you do not have disadvantage on Stealth checks due to maximum loader plating, but can not gain advantage on Stealth checks by any means.
- **Power Built.** You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, pull or lift.
- **Heavy Hitter.** You have extreme endurance hydraulics built into your arms that you can use to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with them, the strike deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike. The die size for this special unarmed strike increases by 1 for every size larger you are than your opponent, to a maximum of 1d12.
- **Like a Damn Forklift.** When you make a Strength (Athletics) or Constitution check, you can roll a d6 and add the number rolled to the ability check.

### Nimble 36

The Nimble 36 model is designed for versatility and agility, with a sleek and compact design that allows for swift movements and nimble maneuvers. They are more robotic in nature than their Life-Lite counterparts, but with an undeniably graceful aura.

- **Nimble Sleek Multilayers.** When not wearing armor, your armor class is 13 + your Dexterity modifier (maximum of 2). Any feature that modifies how you interact with medium armor modifies your nimble sleek multilayers. If you would integrate armor that would reduce your speed, through either encumbrance weight or unmet Strength requirements, you can suppress the reduction for a number of minutes equal to your proficiency bonus once during a long rest.
- **Optimized Gait.** Your walking speed is increased by 5 feet.
- **Bounding Strider.** Once per round while moving at least 10 feet in one direction, you can make a Long Jump or High jump that does not count towards your movement.
- **Light Footed.** You can move stealthily at your base walking speed or when dashing.

### Life-Lite

The Life-Lite model is designed for the most realistic human-like appearance and behavior, with a highly detailed and customized appearance tailored to the customer's specifications. Often a replica of their former self, with all imperfections removed, they are usually reserved for the wealthiest customers.

- **Lifelike Subdermal Reinforcement.** When not wearing armor, your armor class is 11 + your Dexterity modifier. Any feature that modifies how you interact with light armor modifies your lifelike subdermal reinforcement. When you select this pattern group, select one other lineage you were designed after. Your size is identical to that lineage's size, and you can pass yourself off as a member of that lineage or as an Artificate without a check.
- **Custom Made.** You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice and one tool of your choice.
- **Bonus Feature.** One tool you are proficient with is integrated into your wrist, and you double your proficiency bonus for any ability checks you make with it.
- **Blurring Bleeding Edge.** You gain one Trait of your choice from any Artificiate model type of your choice.
