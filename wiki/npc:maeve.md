**Name.** Maeve

**Lineage.** Giantess

**Age.** Unknown

**Height and Weight.** ~30', 2,500lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Affiliation.** N/A

**Features.** Flowing violet hair, white button top with a green pencil skirt. A past before the transformation.

**Status.** Active

Very little is currently known of Maeve, a 30' tall giantess rescued from an underground storage facility as a result of a job led by a group of shadowrunners. She was primarily tasked with lugging massive boxes beyond a typical human's capability.

Given an apartment by Azeria, and after valiant efforts—notably those of [Tobi](/legend:tobi), [Ruby](/legend:rsp), and [Katze](/legend:katze)—they were able to remove the collar and heal her of every ailment that kept her from operating at full capacity. Now, with that done, further information was learned—primarily of an experimental T-Break in development… a drug that caused her growth, amongst other things.

Now, visited occasionally by Katze and Ruby for various check-ups, they've come to learn she seems to have regained a large majority of her past self, and is more than comfortable with disclosing the possible whereabouts of other, similar creations.

Once a week, she has a movie night. Tobi Greene is invited only if he brings sandwiches. He always brings sandwiches.
