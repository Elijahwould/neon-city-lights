##### Source: Player's Handbook (Aura of Purity)

*4th-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self (30-foot radius)

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Healing nanites emerge from you in an aura with a 30-foot radius. Until the tech ends, the aura moves with you, centered on you. Each non-hostile creature in the aura (including you) can’t become diseased, has resistance to poison damage, and has advantage on saving throws against effects that cause any of the following conditions: blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, and stunned.
