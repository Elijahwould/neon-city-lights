*2d6 slashing, 16 lb., heavy, two-handed*

The Zweihänder is a large two-handed sword primarily in use during the 16th century. Why does this matter? It matters because very few swords are anywhere as close as a buster sword actually is, and there is no real information about what they actually are. Zweihänder swords are significantly thinner than a buster sword, which are actually more like huge slabs of sharpened metal than proper swords.

It is said that buster swords were actually quite impractical in the old world, relegated to the adventures of gloomy, spiky-haired protagonists in video games and anime. In Neon City, people who can swing these weapons with relative ease **do** exist, and these weapons are made to be much better balanced than they used to, making them a lot easier to use—and a lot more popular.

### Features

- ***Cleaving Strike.*** If an attack reduces a target's health to 0, you can deal the excess damage to an additional target within range of your attack.
- ***Powerful.*** A buster sword's damage rolls of 1 are treated instead as rolling a 2.
