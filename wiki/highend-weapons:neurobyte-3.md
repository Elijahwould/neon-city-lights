##### Source: Neon City Nights

*Weapon (any melee or ranged weapon), rare (requires installation)*

**Specs:** Variable, $$6,000,000

This weapon is littered with USB ports and lethal jacks, all primed with logic bombs, backtrace protocols, deanonymizing target-locks, and faraday cage user protection. You gain a +3 bonus to the attack rolls of this weapon, and it deals an extra 3d6 software damage on a hit.

When a target you hit with this weapon takes any amount of software damage as a result of the attack, you can choose to apply a penalty to their next attack roll or saving throw equal to this weapon’s rarity bonus.

Additionally, when you hit a creature with this weapon, you can choose to assign them to a small hit list programmed into the weapon. You know the general direction of any hit listed creature, and instantly know any easily observable or web searchable information about them and if they are currently disguised or otherwise not in their true form. If you are within 30 feet of a hit listed target, or within your weapon’s first range increment, whichever is larger, you know which square they occupy. You can hit list an amount of creatures equal to this weapon’s rarity bonus, and gain no hit list benefits against a creature that can ignore telecommunications and other such mind reading.

***Blades.*** A blade is any melee BEA:B/B. Whenever you roll a 20 on your attack roll with a blade, each creature of your choice within 5 feet of the target takes 5 damage of the type dealt by a rare BEA:B/B, 10 damage of the type dealt by a very rare BEA:B/B, and 15 damage of the type dealt by a legendary BEA:B/B.

***Blasters.*** A blaster is any ranged BEA:B/B. Whenever you make an attack roll with a blaster, the ammunition supplies itself from a collection of near-surging nanites. You may still provide your own ammo to benefit from the bonuses of specific ammo, and it is consumed as normal.
