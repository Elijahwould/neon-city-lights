A strong hand on the scruff like a lost kitten, a lasso of nanites, any manner of snare summed up as: you got got!

- A grappled creature's speed becomes 0 and it can't benefit from bonuses to movement speeds.
- The condition ends if the grappler is [incapacitated](/con:incapacitated).
- The condition also ends if an effect removes the grappled creature from the reach of the grappler or grappling effect, such as when a creature is hurled away by the *[shockwave](/tech:shockwave)* tech.
