*Cybernetic enhancement, executive*

Choose a class: Influencer, Ordinator, Shut-In, Source Coder, Streamer, or Techwizard. Choose one 2nd level tech to learn from that same class' list. You always have that tech prepared. Your techcasting ability for this trick depends on the class you chose: Charisma for Influencer, Source Coder, or Streamer; Wisdom for Ordinator or Shut-In; or Intelligence for Techwizard.

You can use cast this tech in this way a number of times equal to half your Proficiency bonus (rounded up), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest. You can also cast the tech using any spell slots that you have.
