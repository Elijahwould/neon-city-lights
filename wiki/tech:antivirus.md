Source: Player's Handbook (Remove Curse)

*3rd-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

At your touch, all viruses affecting one creature or object end. If the object is a infected technical item, its virus remains, but the tech breaks its owner’s attunement to the object so it can be removed or discarded.
