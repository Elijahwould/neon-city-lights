**Corporation Name.** Enchanting Subservience

**CEO.** Unknown

**Years in business.** New

**Industry.** Pleasure, insurance, legal consultation

**Motto.** "*Your future is safer in the hands of your betters.*"

**Impact.** Subsidiary ([Ignorance](/corp:ignorance))

Operating primarily through anonymous consensus between shareholders and multiple outreach programs, it had quickly become a necessity for Ignorance to form a legal consultant team second to none. Given the intentional lack of a face, however, it was inevitable that said legal team would carve out a place of their own. Thus was the conception of the Enchanting Subservience team, sometimes jokingly known as FEM-Corp in break room gossip sessions, for despite their draconian—occasionally predatory—business acumen, they are no strangers to exchanging favors of the flesh.

The primary purpose of ES is to serve as Ignorance's chief legal advisor and touchstone. Contracts with other companies are drafted and approved by ES. Budgets are allocated and debts are collected by the hands of ES. Their loyalty may largely belong to their parent corporation—not that they don't indulge in the occasional bout of backstabbery as all corporations eventually do—but they are willing to offer their aid to others, for the right price and deal, of course. If nothing else, Enchanting Subservience is entirely beholden to their word, that's why their word is often carefully crafted and curated by hundreds of individuals, wielded like a scalpel.

Excellent legal work and insurance policies are not Enchanting Subservience's only claims to fame, they bare two other legacies. The first is ES's internal hierarchy, forged by their own word, and exceeding strict, topped entirely by women who have earned the title of Enchantress, perfected wielders of legalese and positional power. The second is that of their Servitors, members with no specific place on the hierarchy who instead report directly to whoever has been appointed their superior, operating as taxmen, debt collectors, boytoys, bodyguards, and the occasional bounty hunter when clients run afoul of their obligations.
