## "Lots of places for you to lose your mind in Neon City, aren't there?"

Welcome to Neon City.

Neon City is a den of criminal activity, overlooked by corporate overlords that constantly vie for power over one another, and have no qualms about hiring mercenaries. These mercenaries, dubbed *runners* or *street jukes* by those in the local scene, come from many different paths of life and in all shapes and sizes.

Neon City itself is home to all shapes of life, with humans and splicers being the most predominant. The city itself also has a decent concentration of Gobbos, Nayalings, Kaijuborn, Jerboans, Elfs, Artificiates, Netnibblers, Trash Pandas… just to name a few.

The corporate sex dystopia is a product of people finding relics of the old world back in the 1300s, after the Fall. Humans, irresponsible as they are with things they don't quite understand, went back to and unlocked many things to degrees that were, ultimately, immediately damaging. Now, netsuckers have infested society, beings of dubious morals and intention stalk the streets, and an alarming amount of the populace crowd government-funded, corporately controlled mega brothels. Much of the general populace is kept complacent with excessive amounts of sex and pleasure, all over their media, advertising, entertainment, food, cybernetics and just about every other facet of their otherwise empty lives.

You, the player, will be playing a runner, mercenary or street juke—someone who has enough of the current system, or maybe someone who wants a little bit more out of life than just ease. You may not have any qualms working with corporations to achieve some of your goals. Maybe you'll stop at nothing to see one—or all—of them burn.

You are joining the ranks of misfit streamers, street samurai, machinists, auditors and more. People who live on the fringes of the law.

Whatever your story, it is yours to tell.
