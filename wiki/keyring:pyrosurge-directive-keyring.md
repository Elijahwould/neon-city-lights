##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Ring of Fire Elemental Command)

*Keyring, legendary (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$2,600,000

This ring is linked to the plasma state of matter.

While wearing this ring, you have advantage on attack rolls against surges from the linked plane, and they have disadvantage on attack rolls against you. In addition, you have access to the following properties.

The ring has 5 charges. It regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. Spells cast from the ring have a save DC of 17.

You can expend a 2 of the ring's charges to cast [ACCESS_ROOT(Target)](/tech:access-root-target) on a plasma surge. In addition, you have resistance to fire damage.

If you help slay a plasma surge while attuned to the ring, you gain access to the following additional properties:

- You are immune to fire damage.
- You can cast the following techs from the ring, expending the necessary number of charges: [Flame Vent](/tech:flame-vent) (1 charge), [Incendiary Blast](/tech:incendiary-blast) (2 charges), and [Pyrobarricade](/tech:pyrobarricade) (3 charges).
