# Levels and Ranks

As you advance and gain levels, each level has a title attached to it. Depending on the title, the MP cost of gaining a level will increase, and thus the amount of MP you require to reach the next rank will also increase. Below is a chart outlining character progression.

Example: if you're a level 13 Streetwise character, it will cost you 4 MP to reach level 14. At level 14, you become a Shadowrunner, and your next level will cost 5 MP.

The following is a proposed adjustment to the leveling curve for Neon City Nights. This includes starting at level 3!
