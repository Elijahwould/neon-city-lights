**Name.** Blanc Damarion

**Lineage.** Executive Elf

**Age.** 568

**Height and Weight.** 7'2", 160 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Affiliation.** [The Damarion Clan](/org:damarion-clan)

**Features.** Short, white hair, gaunt, borderline-skeletal frame, pure black eyes, housed in a skeletal machine body with four long, tendril-like arms

**Status.** Deceased (KIA)

One of the older members of the Damarion Clan Blanc was a specialist in the use of ancient technologies and forbidden relics of old. In one such venture through the deserts to the east, her exploration group encountered corrupted technology that she adapted to her own person to amplify her powers at the cost of her youth. She specialized in the use of mental torture and often was the source of discipline among the ranks of the family to ensure loyalty above all else was enforced.

She was last seen being telekinetically shoved out of a bullet train into the desert while moving at alarmingly high speeds, and held by some sort of unseeable force, causing her to be dragged across the desert like an object being pressed into a belt sander. The video was streamed live on glitch.tv to thousands of viewers, and still does numbers on the glitch.tv video service to this day.
