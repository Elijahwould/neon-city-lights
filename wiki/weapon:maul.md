*2d6 bludgeoning, 10 lb., heavy, two-handed*

A maul—historically called a war hammer—is a weapon that was used by both foot soldiers and cavalry. It is a very old weapon and gave its name, owing to its constant use, to Judah Maccabee, a 2nd-century BC Jewish rebel, and to Charles Martel, one of the rulers of France. In the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries before the [the Great Collapse](/the-great-collapse), the maul became an elaborately decorated and handsome weapon.

It was a popular weapon in the late medieval period. It became somewhat of a necessity in combat when armor became so strong that swords and axes were no longer able to pierce and ricocheted upon impact. The maul could inflict significant damage on the enemy through their heavy impact, without the need to pierce the armor.

### Features

- ***Knocking the Wind Out.*** Whenever you roll a 20 on an attack with this weapon, you may have your target suffer the stunned condition until the end of their next turn.
