##### Source: Neon City NightsThanks to ActFast231#8365 for their design submission!

Live Action Roleplay allows one to live out their epic fantasies, immerse themselves in history or a fantasy world, or just hang out with friends. You have spent many years participating in such events, slinging spells and saving princesses from peril. The skills and connections gained from the LARPing scene prove shockingly useful as a runner.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** History, Performance
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Disguise kit, Tailor's Tools
- **Equipment.** LARPers start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A disguise kit
  - A set of tailor's tools
  - A renfaire costume
  - A short sword (which counts as a machete)
  - A knight's standard

### Feature: Crafty Adventurer

You have advantage on Intelligence (History) and Intelligence (Culture) checks related to medieval lore and fantasy.

Additionally, you can fashion any improvised object you find around the area, within reason, into a makeshift, make believe weapon of your choice. As a bonus action, you can produce a single, mundane object that closely resembles a basic, close-quarters civilian or military weapon of your choice. This makeshift weapon shares the original weapon's weapon features and requires the same proficiency to use it. The makeshift weapon breaks the next time initiative ends, and you must finish a long rest before you can produce another makeshift weapon with this feature again.
