**Name.** Nyx King

**Lineage.** Netnibbler, ex-Splicer

**Age.** Unknown, physically locked at 27

**Height and Weight.** 5'7", 120 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/they

**Classes/Subclasses.** Mechanist (Field Mechanic) 20

**Features.** Black cat ears, long and restless black tail, vibrant blue hair; balance between thin, fit and curvy, with blue-and-pink eyes with a golden hue at certain angles

**Status.** Retired

The life Nyx led before becoming a runner lies dead and buried deep enough that no one but her has much of any knowledge about it, and she does not share it easily.

As soon as she woke up in Neon City, running came as natural to her as biting or pulling the trigger. But she had a purpose—a mission—and in so following it, the blue bomber made quite the name for herself. She kickstarted gang wars and ended some. She explored (and exploded) ancient safes of forgotten knowledge and terrors, put on shows gunning down people in live death matches and went to the moon twice—allegedly, anyway. She has infiltrated impossible places and hacked impossible things, some of which no one will never hear about. She fixed her broken heart, only to break it again and then put it back together once more.

Nowadays, Nyx is trying her best to settle down and build something good that will last. Using her accumulated knowledge and resources, she's been fixing jobs and working hard on yet another exciting project.

Excitable, intense, mercurial, trigger happy, horny, loud, boisterous… the list goes on. She is always hungry for something, owing both to her natural personality and Netnibbler nature. Her anxious brain is always going a million miles an hour, working on the next plan, design or meal… or simply distracted by all the fantasies, easy escapes and interesting people the city offers.
