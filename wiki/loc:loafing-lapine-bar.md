**Location Name.** The Loafing Lapine Bar

**Owner/Leader.** [Shylyss "The Ara-Ara Arsonist" Arnou](/legend:shylyss)

**Location Type.** Business (Front; base for [Red Light Rabbits](/org:red-light-rabbits))

**Years in existence.** <1 year

**Type.** Bar/Lounge

**Area.** [Red Light District](/district:red-light-district)

**Impact.** Minor

The Loafing Lapine Bar is a high-end establishment recently constructed on the outskirts between gang turf and corporate-held territory. Within this "no-man's-land" of odds and ends business lay a brick-and-mortar lounge that has begun to grow to have a devoted customer base. Between the expert mixologists, entertainment in the form of bunny-suit-dressed wait staff (and pole-dancers), dance floor, billiards tables, and food made to-order, there's little want for more if you're looking for a good time off-strip.

Behind closed doors, however, the newly-forged Red Light Rabbits work. There is a basement area that's kept as a general-use base of operations for the gang, consisting of beds, showers, entertainment of almost every form, and an arsenal of weaponry. Should the facility become a target of a raid, a panic button locks down every entrance and exit to the bar, prohibiting - or at least slowing - intruders' access.

The second floor houses the manager's office and staff break room that sees out over the dance floor along with a private VIP lounge.
