##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings)

You've lived a hundred lifetimes submerged in the virtual world (and green goo) of the Sense-Tanks, where a day of real time feels like a year in the Tanks. For as long as you can remember you've done nothing but play videogames and engage in online orgies. But now… something has forced you back to the real world.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Medicine, History
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Hacker's kit
- **Equipment.** Sense-tank addicts start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A list of names of a few of your fellow gamers
  - A bath towel
  - A credchip worth $$1,000

### Feature: VR Vagrant

When conversing with a fellow sense-tank user, you have advantage on Persuasion and Insight checks against them.
