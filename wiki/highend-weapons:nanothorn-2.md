##### Source: Neon City Nights

*Weapon (any melee or ranged weapon), very rare (requires installation)*

**Specs:** Variable, $$3,000,000

This weapon encases a powerful nanite control colony which, after surrounding the weapon, can vibrate with such microscopic precision so as to sever molecular bonds. You gain a +2 bonus to the attack rolls of this weapon, and it deals an extra 2d8 entropic damage on a hit.

When a creature you hit with this weapon takes any amount of entropic damage from the attack, you can choose to enervate it. An enervated creature only regains half the hit points from any instance of healing they receive, and remain enervated for a number of their turns equal to this weapon’s rarity bonus. Once a creature recovers from enervation, they become immune to this weapon’s version of the effect for one hour.

Additionally, when a creature you hit with this weapon dies as a result of the attack, and took any amount of entropic damage from the attack, you can cause the weapon to break down a number of their vital organs or stand-ins for such to integrate into yourself. You regain a number of hit points equal to the number of hit dice the creature had. You can regain hit points with this ability a number of times equal to this weapon’s rarity bonus before you must take a short or long rest to recharge the nanite colony.

***Blades.*** A blade is any melee BEA:B/B. Whenever you roll a 20 on your attack roll with a blade, each creature of your choice within 5 feet of the target takes 5 damage of the type dealt by a rare BEA:B/B, 10 damage of the type dealt by a very rare BEA:B/B, and 15 damage of the type dealt by a legendary BEA:B/B.

***Blasters.*** A blaster is any ranged BEA:B/B. Whenever you make an attack roll with a blaster, the ammunition supplies itself from a collection of near-surging nanites. You may still provide your own ammo to benefit from the bonuses of specific ammo, and it is consumed as normal.
