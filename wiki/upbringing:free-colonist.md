##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings)

You're one of the lucky ones, your parents always said. Away from the doldrums of corpo-feudalist life, aside from the occasional run-ins with pirates or corpo-frontiersmen, you've lived a quiet, communal life with the rest of your colonial brethren. Still, something has drawn you to Neon City to seek something greater.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Athletics, Animal Handling
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Builder's tools, Cook's utensils
- **Equipment.** Free colonists start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A set of farmer's or merchant's attire
  - A set of cook's utensils
  - A bottle of mango wine
  - A small pet of your choosing

### Feature: Sweat Off Your Own Brow

Once a day you are able to scavenge up to $$1,000 of mundane items to help you survive, such as food, water, ammunition and clothing.
