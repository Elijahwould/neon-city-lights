##### Source: Player's Handbook (Guards and Wards)

*6th-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (oil, an alarm bell, a lock, and a small silver rod worth at least $$1,000)

**Duration:** 24 hours

You create a lockdown that protects up to 2,500 square feet of floor space (an area 50 feet square, or one hundred 5-foot squares or twenty-five 10-foot squares). The locked down area can be up to 20 feet tall, and shaped as you desire. You can lock down several stories of a stronghold by dividing the area among them, as long as you can walk into each contiguous area while you are casting the tech.

When you cast this tech, you can specify individuals that are unaffected by any or all of the effects that you choose. You can also specify a password that, when spoken aloud, makes the speaker immune to these effects.

Lockdown creates the following effects within the locked down area.

- **Corridors.** Fog fills all the warded corridors, making them heavily obscured. In addition, at each intersection or branching passage offering a choice of direction, there is a 50 percent chance that a creature other than you will believe it is going in the opposite direction from the one it chooses.

- **Doors.** All doors in the warded area are magically locked, as if sealed by an *[programmed lock](/tech:programmed-lock)* tech. In addition, you can cover up to ten doors with a Deception (equivalent to the deception object function of the *[hardlight chicanery](/tech:hardlight-chicanery)* tech) to make them appear as plain sections of wall.

- **Stairs.** Webs fill all stairs in the warded area from top to bottom, as the *[sticky stuff](/tech:sticky-stuff)* tech. These strands regrow in 10 minutes if they are burned or torn away while Lockdown lasts.

- **Other Tech Effect.** You can place your choice of one of the following technical effects within the locked down area of the stronghold.
  - Place *[glow bots](/tech:glow-bots)* in four corridors. You can designate a simple program that the lights repeat as long as Lockdown lasts.
  - Place *[voicemail](/tech:voicemail)* in two locations.
  - Place *[aerosol stink spray](/tech:aerosol-stink-spray)* in two locations. The vapors appear in the places you designate; they return within 10 minutes if dispersed by wind while Lockdown lasts.
  - Place a constant *[aeroblast](/tech:aeroblast)* in one corridor or room.
  - Place a *[subliminal message](/tech:subliminal-message)* in one location. You select an area of up to 5 feet square, and any creature that enters or passes through the area receives the *[subliminal message](/tech:subliminal-message)* mentally.

The whole warded area radiates tech. A *[localized emp](/tech:localized-emp)* cast on a specific effect, if successful, removes only that effect. You can create a permanently Locked Down structure by casting this tech there every day for one year.
