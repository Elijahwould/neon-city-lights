**Location Name.** Spatha's Den

**Owner/Leader.** Enrico Spatha

**Location Type.** Business

**Years in existence.** ~25

**Type.** Brothel

**Area.** [Neon City Red-Light District](/district:red-light-district)

**Impact.** Minor

Spatha's Den is not a place for the faint of heart. People often come to this premium brothel planning to spend weeks or even months here, enjoying waves upon waves of pleasure for absurd prices, often to the point of personal ruin. Indeed, some find themselves going crazy or mindless over time, with old rumors persisting that lost causes were often sold to Bliss Co. for parts.

Many of those rumors were found to be unsubstantiated once the major leak of Bliss Co. data went live across Neon City, but that has not helped Spatha's make its image seem any less intimidating. Strangely, they seem to count on that—the kind of clientele that Spatha's attracts usually consists of requires a level of, for lack of any better word, depravity. And depravity abounds here.
