##### Source: Player's Handbook (Barkskin)

*2nd-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a handful of ceramic dust and a shard of iron)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You touch a willing creature. Until the tech ends, the target’s skin has a rough, bark-like appearance, and the target’s AC can’t be less than 16, regardless of what kind of armor it is wearing.
