##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Create Bonfire)

*Manifestation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You accelerate particles on the ground that you can see within range. Until the tech ends, a technical firepit fills a 5-foot cube. Any creature in the firepit's space when you cast the tech must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d8 fire damage. A creature must also make the saving throw when it moves into the firepit's space for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there.

The firepit ignites flammable objects in its area that aren't being worn or carried.

The tech's damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).
