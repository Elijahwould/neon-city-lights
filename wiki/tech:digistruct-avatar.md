##### Source: Unearthed Arcana (Conjure Knowbot)

*4th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** 10 minutes

You touch a single computerized device or computer system to conjure an avatar—a partially sentient piece of software imprinted with vestiges of your own skills and computer abilities. For the duration of the tech, you can use a bonus action to have the avatar execute a computer related task that would normally require an action. The avatar makes Intelligence ability checks using your ability score and proficiency bonuses including your proficiency with hacking tools, if applicable.

You have a limited telepathic bond with the avatar, out to a range of 500 feet from the device or system where the avatar was conjured. If you move beyond this range, the avatar disappears in 2d4 rounds, as if the duration of the tech had expired. Moving within range again immediately reestablishes the bond.

The avatar is bound to the system in which it was created, and it stays there until it is dismissed or the tech's duration expires.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 5th level or higher, the tech's duration increases to 1 hour. Additionally, your telepathic bond with the avatar is effective out to a range of 1,000 feet, and if you leave the range of the bond, the avatar continues performing its last directed task until the tech expires.
