**Name.** Laiton Damarion

**Lineage.** Remake (formerly Executive Elf)

**Age.** 252

**Height and Weight.** 9'7", 365 lbs

**Pronouns.** he/him

**Affiliation.** [The Damarion Clan](/org:damarion-clan)

**Features.** Large golden frame that looks like clockwork, steampunk machinery

**Status.** Alive

Little is known of Laiton by the public. He serves as the primary source of contact for nearly any and all contract work as well as recruitment. None so far have yet to meet him in person, instead interacting telecommunically or through the use of his faithful botler.

Like almost all Damarions, he is an amoral killer that shows no qualms over the extreme methods that his house employs and shows little regard even towards the deaths of his own family members.
