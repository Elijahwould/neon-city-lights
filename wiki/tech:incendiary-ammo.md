##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Flame Arrows)

*3rd-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You touch a magazine containing bullets or another type of ammunition. When a target is hit by a ranged weapon attack using a piece of ammunition drawn from the magazine, the target takes an extra 1d6 fire damage. The tech's effect ends on the piece of ammunition when it hits or misses, and the spell ends when twelve pieces of ammunition have been used from the magazine.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the number of pieces of ammunition you can affect with this spell increases by two for each slot level above 3rd.
