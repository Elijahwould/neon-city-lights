**Corporation Name.** Neon City Archaeological Procurement Society AKA NCAPS

**Leader.** N/A

**Organization Type.** Collaborative Collective

**Years in existence.** ~150+

**Goals.** Acquisition, recovery, conservation and liberation of old-world artifacts, technology, media and knowledge

**Creed.** *"~"*

**Impact.** Major

The Neon City Archaeological Procurement Society (or NCAPS for short) is a collective of like-minded individuals who seek to recover old-world technology, cultural relics, and all sorts of strange and interesting artifacts. In many ways, while their primary goal is ostensibly aligned with that of The Reliquary—the retrieval of old-world artifacts—they are their exact opposite in every other regard: they are more interested in the free dissemination of knowledge, information and benefits that are obtained as a result of the discoveries of these artifacts than they are in withholding them from Neon City (and, ostensibly, the world) at large.
