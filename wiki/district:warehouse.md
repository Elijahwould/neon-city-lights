**District.** Warehouse District

**Population.** ?

**Type.** Industrial

The Warehouse District sits between the docks on the southwest and the [South Side Slums](/district:south-side-slums). While it is colloquially known as the Warehouse District and primarily serves as such, a large number of businesses do exist that operate at all times of day, making it a great place for upstart businesses looking for cheap property to start their offices up.
