## “Your pixel army can’t save you now. My finger’s on the kill switch.”

The wildest dreams of Old World 3D-printing technology could never have matched the reality of digitization. By analyzing objects at the molecular level and creating an engram of that object, fabricators can effectively “summon” digital copies of objects, creatures, and even people. Their mastery of the global engram network also allows them to warp physical space, tapping into the same system that delivery services use for “next-minute” packages.

##### Source: Player's Handbook (School of Conjuration)

### Manifestation Expert

Beginning when you select this specialty at 2nd level, the cash and time you must spend to copy a Manifestation tech into your cyberdeck is halved.

### Minor Fabrication

Starting at 2nd level when you select this specialty, you can use your action to create an inanimate object in your hand or on the ground in an unoccupied space that you can see within 10 feet of you. This object can be no larger than 3 feet on a side and weigh no more than 10 pounds, and its form must be that of a mundane object that you have seen. The object is visibly cobbled-together, made from spare parts, junk, and 3D-printed components.

The object falls apart into useless scraps after 1 hour, when you use this feature again, or if it takes any damage.

### Expedited Shipping

Starting at 6th level, you can use your action to teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space that you can see. Alternatively, you can choose a space within range that is occupied by a Small or Medium creature. If that creature is willing, you both teleport, swapping places.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest or you cast a Manifestation tech of 1st level or higher.

### Focused Manifestation

Beginning at 10th level, while you are concentrating on a Manifestation tech, your concentration can’t be broken as a result of taking damage.

### Durable Fabrication

Starting at 14th level, any creature or object that you summon or create with a Manifestation tech has 30 temporary hit points.
