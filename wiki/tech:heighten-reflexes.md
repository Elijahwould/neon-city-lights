##### Source: Explorer's Guide to Wildemount (Gift of Alacrity)

*1st-level observation*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** 8 hours

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target can add 1d8 to its initiative rolls.
