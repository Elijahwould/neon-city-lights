**Corporation Name.** Bloody Knuckles, Inc.

**CEO.** Absentia Solo

**Years in business.** ~24

**Industry.** Security training, private security contracting, general problem solving through firepower

**Motto.** *"Making a name for yourself in a city full of killers and scoundrels ain’t easy—let us help you!"*

**Impact.** Minor

Deep in the heart of the city, an abandoned office building has finally reached the last stages of production and has officially opened up to the public. What was once just a gym has now become a part of a much bigger setting in Neon City—a partnership with a redeemed—and skeptically so—cult, that specializes in All Things Violent. With enough support, they have officially breached the limits into a recognizable corporation.

Primarily ran by renegades, it’s no surprise that when calls are made to runners over jobs here, they tend to be more, favorable towards those that won’t shy away towards leaving behind messes. Gangs in the Undercity have become rampant and have posed a threat to several high-end Corporations. Thus, Bloody Knuckles was officially born. And they’re always willing to hire…

While their main functions are scheduling jobs for runners with a more… violent edge of conducting business, they have reserved the rights to a large coliseum on the outskirts of Neon City and having completed a trial run involving several runners already, having deemed it a great success. They look forward to scheduling further slaughter fests for Neon City’s entertainment, with fierce competition set to meet those brave enough to apply… and promising rewards.
