##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Telekinetic)

You’ve attained a mastery over the Hovering Helping Hands, granting you the following benefits:

- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You learn the [hovering helping hand](http://neoncitylights.wikidot.com/tech:hovering-helping-hand) trick. You can cast it without verbal or somatic components, and you can make the helping hand invisible. If you already know this tech, its range increases by 30 feet when you cast it. Its techcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.
- As a bonus action, you can try to shove one creature you can see with the helping hand within 30 feet of you. When you do so, the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + the ability modifier of the score increased by this feat) or be moved 5 feet toward or away from you. A creature can willingly fail this save.
