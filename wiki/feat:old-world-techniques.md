##### Source: Neon City Nights

Either by choice or necessity, you have managed to really learn the ins and outs of weapons that some might consider… antiquated. That will be their final mistake. Your training grants you the following benefits:

- When making your first attack on your turn with a hunting bow, you can replace its Bleeding Wounds property with the Crippling Shot property until the beginning of your next turn.
  - ***Crippling Shot*.** Once per turn per target, hitting a target reduces their movement speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.
- You ignore the loading property of hunting bows.
- Your ranged attacks made with a slingshot ignore half and three-quarters cover.
- You can roll one additional weapon damage die when determining the extra damage for a critical hit made with a hunting bow or slingshot.
