## "In A.D. 2101, war was beginning…"

In the early 1300s, in the 13th century of a world after the Fall, explorers found traces of the old world. The old world was a civilization that was hyper-focused on media, memes, and so self-centered enough that it eventually burnt itself out waging nuclear war with each other, blasting it to hell and covering the planet in nuclear winter and deserts.

The civilization, strange as it was, had incredibly advanced technology. Upon its discovery, that technology slingshot parts of the world several centuries—if not millennia—into the future. For better or for worse, the political spheres of the world, such as they were, were not ready for these discoveries. Several wars broke out.

In the 1300s, there were many kingdoms and nations, somewhat stuck in the Middle Ages. What remains now are small havens and megacities, bastions of hyper-advanced civilization, each governed by several different methods of government—many of which have ultimately given way to full-blown corporatism.

Neon City itself is mostly a corporate oligarchy, paradoxically trapped somewhere in between a functional socialist utopia and a hyper capitalist nightmare dystopia. The vast majority of corporations understand that keeping the people of their city relatively safe, hale and healthy is incredibly important to their bottom line. It also allows them to exert a large level of control over them and ensures that there will always be people to exploit.

### A Recording

*A recorded lecture of the late professor Veronica Ramirez, about the history of our world, part two "The Relic Wars".*

"Good day, students. Today we shall be continuing our discussion of one of the most tumultuous periods in the history of our world, the Relic Wars. Last time we talked about the collapse and the few things we know about the dark times, today we shall touch upon the actual war. Which may have destroyed civilization as we know it, but paradoxically, turned out to be its greatest boon.

As you all know, the war occurred 300 years ago, or 1,300 years after the collapse of the old world. In a time when explorers rediscovered the remnants of an old, advanced civilization hyper-focused on media and memes, society eventually burnt itself out waging nuclear war with each other.

This fascinating rediscovery of the old world's technology catapulted parts of the known world centuries, if not millennia, into the future. However, the political powers of the time were not ready for such advancements, and as a result, several wars broke out. Everyone thrusting their greedy hands towards something so… unknowable, so uncontrollable.

In the early 1300s, most of the world was still stuck in a state similar to what the old world called, the middle ages. Even Neon City was but a humble fishing settlement during most of that time. But as you all know, it would soon find itself in the position it is in now… But we shall touch upon that another time.

The discovery of the old world technology led to the consolidation of most old duchies and kingdoms, into bastions of hyper-advanced civilization. As they grew larger and larger, they stopped calling themselves nations and rather switched to a more fitting term, Megacities. Each advanced its militaristic capabilities at an alarming rate. Constant border skirmishes, drone strikes, or full-on conflicts erupted between the many fledgling cities.

Of course, the discovery of old-world tech also brought with it, the discovery of old-world ideology. The greed of people wanting to get their hands on every bit of tech they could, only accelerated the formation of giant corporations, who at first, acted as advisors and sponsors to the Kings and Queens they were serving. The newly built military-industrial complexes being one of the only industries at the time, suited them perfectly. But they quickly outpaced the would-be rulers, growing so large in power, they didn't even have to topple the regimes they were serving under. They… simply took on the role of rulers themselves. No one batted an eye at the new coat of paint their oppressors styled themselves with.

After a short, but bloody conflict, which destroyed most of the still-young megacities. The few corporations who called themselves the 'Transcendental Intelligence and Technology Society', came together to sign a truce. The war hurting everyone's efforts of scavenging for more tech and, obviously, their profit margins, too much to be acceptable. None of the original companies who had signed the peace still remain, having been dissolved or taken over, long ago.

The Relic Wars changed the course of our history, and even today, 300 years later, we continue to feel their impact. The corporations had learned, that even in times of war, a good lay keeps the population happy…

I see some of you haven't fallen asleep yet, a better percentage than last time, I'm proud. Please prepare the material I have sent you regarding Neon City, we shall continue next week…"

*Recording end.*
