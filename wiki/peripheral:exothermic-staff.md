##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Staff of Fire)

*Peripheral, very rare (requires installation by a [shut-in](/shutin), [source coder](/sourcecoder), [streamer](/streamer), or [techwizard](/techwizard))*

**Specs:** 4 lbs., $$1,600,000

You have resistance to fire damage while you hold this staff.

The staff has 10 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC: [Flame Vent](/tech:flame-vent) (1 charge), [Incendiary Blast](/tech:incendiary-blast)(3 charges), or [Pyrobarricade](/tech:pyrobarricade) (4 charges).

The staff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the containment systems within the staff fail: the staff blackens, crumbles into cinders, and is destroyed.
