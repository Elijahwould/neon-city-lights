##### Source: Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide (Booming Blade)

*Altercation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 5 feet

**Components:** V, M (a weapon)

**Duration:** 1 round

As part of the action used to cast this tech, you must make a melee attack with a weapon against one creature within the tech's range, otherwise the tech fails. On a hit, the target suffers the attack's normal effects, and it becomes sheathed in high-frequency vibrations until the start of your next turn. If the target willingly moves before then, it immediately takes 1d8 thunder damage, and the tech ends.

This tech's damage increases when you reach higher levels. At 5th level, the melee attack deals an extra 1d8 thunder damage to the target, and the damage the target takes for moving increases to 2d8. Both damage rolls increase by 1d8 at 11th level and 17th level.
