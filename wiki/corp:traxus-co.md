**Corporation Name.** Traxus Co.

**CEO.** Sarran Helliner

**Years in Business.** ~7

**Industry.** Care and beauty products, with specialty products for runners and mercenaries

**Motto.** "*Look your best, feel your best—run your best.*"

**Impact.** Minor

A relatively new player on the scene, the Traxus Co. founder is a nayaling woman by the name of Sarran Helliner who sought to fill a particular niche she saw in Neon City. Runners, mercenaries, and the various individuals that take on dangerous jobs that kept the city from catching heavy fire from gangs and other corporations alike, often come out of these jobs smeared in blood, mud and more than one number of bodily fluids, all paired with the possibility of suffering multiple disfigurements. There were care products on the market that could address a number of these, but nothing that was properly made and advertised for hardcore runners. Thus, Traxus Co. was born.

The mission of the company was to create beauty and care products that would complement a runner's lifestyle and work ethic, leaving them fresh and gorgeous even in the worst of times. These body washes are specifically designed to carry their scent for longer durations, even with the messiest of stains. Special formulas regarding face and bodywash were created to deal with the tougher smears such as blood, and special formulations of make-up crafted to give guaranteed, long-lasting results! There are even specialty kits to fit those with unusual biology, major scars, and physical disfigurements. Holographic tattoos, unique colors, and concealing blush are just some of their many methods to help you look your absolute best.

All this and more, done with a runner's best interests at heart, so that they can look their best, feel their best, and run their best, handling threats of all kinds!
