##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/custom-lineages)

Invariably, there will always be others…

Despite the most common variants between the different people of the new world, they are all still originally human, and interbreeding is possible. Many times, this results in a child who is predominantly one parent's race, perhaps with superficial features resembling the other. Less often, but not unheard of, the child will be a true mixed-race child, carrying forward abilities and traits from both parents. Other times parents might seek out special retroviral treatments for an unborn child, instilling in them traits to help them thrive.

Stable treatments to change the race of a person after they are born are exceedingly rare, the most famous of which are the unstable Splicer treatments, which do not tend to be passed onto children (though it is not entirely unheard of), leading to "half splicers" in some communities.

# Custom Lineage Features

- **Creature Type.** You are a humanoid. You determine your appearance, and whether you resemble any of your kin.
- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, or increase two different scores by 1.
- **Age.** Your age range is equal to the average of the ranges from where you get your two inherited traits.
- **Size.** You are Medium or Small, your choice.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Feat.** You gain one Feat of your choice.
- **Inherited Traits.** You gain two racial traits of your choice from up to two other races.
