*1d4 varies*, 1 lb., light, unarmed*

Brass knuckles are "fist-load weapons" used in hand-to-hand combat. They are usually pieces of metal shaped to fit around the knuckles. Despite their name, they are often made from other metals, plastics or carbon fibers. Designed to preserve and concentrate a punch's force by directing it toward a harder and smaller contact area, they result in increased tissue disruption, including an increased likelihood of fracturing the intended target's bones on impact. The extended and rounded palm grip also spreads the counter force across the attacker's palm, which would otherwise have been absorbed primarily by the attacker's fingers.

While many are likely familiar with the traditional shape of brass knuckles, many varieties exist. The most popular ones among runners are modifiable, giving their users the opportunity flexibility in terms of the kind of damage they wish to inflict.

### Features

- ***Adjustable Weapon.*** You can spend a minute to change any attachments on your knuckles. Doing so changes the damage type. You may pick between Push Knives (piercing), Bagh Nakh (slashing), or Knuckle Dusters (bludgeoning).
