##### Source: Icewind Dale - Rime of the Frostmaiden (Create Magen)

*7th-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 hour

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a vial of quicksilver worth $$50,000 credits and a life-sized human doll, both of which the tech consumes, and an intricate crystal rod worth at least $$150,000 credits that is not consumed)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

While casting the tech, you place a vial of quicksilver in the chest of a life-sized human doll stuffed with ash or dust. You then stitch up the doll and drip your blood on it. At the end of the casting, you tap the doll with a crystal rod, transforming it into a mecharionette clothed in whatever the doll was wearing. The type of mecharionette is chosen by you during the casting of the tech.

When the mecharionette appears, your hit point maximum decreases by an amount equal to the mecharionette's challenge rating (minimum reduction of 1). Only an *[absolute source code manipulation](/tech:absolute-source-code-manipulation)* tech can undo this reduction to your hit point maximum.

Any magen you create with this spell obeys your commands without question.
