**Location Name.** Barley's Bar & Burger Barn

**Owner/Leader**. [Malcolm Barley](/npc:malcolm-barley)

**Location Type.** Restaurant

**Years in existence.** ~15

**Type.** Giant burgers and seriously great shakes

**Area.** [Neon City Downtown Residential District](/district:downtown-residential)

**Impact.** Minor

Barley's Bar & Burger Barn is a large restaurant smack dab in the middle of the Spark End district of Neon City, not too far from Poncy's. It is a place that specializes in huge burgers and shakes, as well as novelty alcoholic cocktails. The location is a testament to how good food doesn't need a massive price tag, or to come from some big-name company. It can be delicious and affordable to the general public. Sure, a lot of it is deep fried, made with butter, and practically slathered in grease, but you'll be hard-pressed to find a better, cheaper meal at one of those corpo-run "restaurants".

The place is mostly served by people in quaint, brown aprons and little hats, and the portions are absolutely massive.

Famous menu items include:

- **Big Bad Breakfast Burger.** An enormously heavy burger, loaded with bacon, egg, crispy onions and a humongous beef or turkey patty.
- **Barley Burger.** The standard by which all others are measured, a huge beef burger topped with lettuce, onions, tomato and pickles, coming with or without cheese, and topped with Barley's Secret Sauce.
- **Double Barley Burger.** Make your Barley Burger trouble, then make it double. The same toppings, but even more loaded and with double the meat. Share this with eleven other friends.
- **Bitchin' Kitchen Burger.** Take a Barley Burger patty and deep fry it, while the toppings for your regular burger are changed for caramelized onions, lettuce and a mixture of Barley's Chipotle Kicker and Barley's Secret Sauce.
- **Thiccc Cheeseshake.** A variety of shakes that have entire slices of cheesecake blended into them. Chocolate, strawberry, vanilla, rocky road swirl, pecan, classic and other varieties are served here
- **Barley's Big Bad Fries.** Barley's doesn't serve traditional fry baskets. Imagine instead a picnic basket full of fries. You're welcome.
