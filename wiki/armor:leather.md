*Medium armor, 1 lb., AC = 12 + Dex modifier (max 2)*

Everybody loves a nice leather jacket, and not just for the looks. Authentic leather jackets tend to offer a surprising amount of protection thanks to the durability of the leather itself, while still maintaining some level of mobility in them. Still, leather's a bit stiffer than most synthetic materials, which does mean that it hangs a bit heavier on the frame, even if it's not quite heavier than some alternatives.
