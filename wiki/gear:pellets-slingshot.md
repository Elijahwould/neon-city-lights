A slingshot pellet is basically an oversized ball bearing that's been polished smooth and made of a relatively heavier material to ensure additional damage upon impact. They're still much lighter to carry than regular ammo, owing to the lack of cartridges or powder. This is a pack of 20 non-technical pellets.
