##### Source: Strixhaven: A Curriculum of Chaos (Borrowed Knowledge)

*2nd-level observation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S, M (a modular dataslate that costs at least $$2500)

**Duration:** 1 hour

You draw upon knowledge from the many, many, many video tutorials of the past. Choose one skill in which you lack proficiency. For the tech's duration, you have proficiency in the chosen skill. The tech ends early if you cast it again.
