##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Weapon of Warning)

*Weapon (any), uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** Variable, $$700,000

This technical weapon warns you of danger. While the weapon is on your person, you have advantage on initiative rolls. In addition, you and any of your companions within 30 feet of you can't be surprised, except when incapacitated by something other than nontechnical sleep. The weapon immediately awakens you and your companions within range by technical means if any of you are sleeping naturally when combat begins.
