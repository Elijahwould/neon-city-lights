##### Source: Player's Handbook (Eldritch Blast)

*Altercation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A hyper-charged bullet streaks toward a creature within range, leaving a trail of energy in its wake. Make a ranged tech attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 kinetic damage.

The tech fires more than one shot when you reach higher levels: two shots at 5th level, three shots at 11th level, and four shots at 17th level. You can direct the shots at the same target or at different ones. Make a separate attack roll for each shot.
