This modification allows a vehicle to disable the self-drive mechanism and ignore packets from the SDN. The vehicle will remain invisible to the SDN grid, which will produce "valid" traversal records that match up with the optimization algorithms of the network. This will allow a runner—or criminal—to remain digitally invisible to some forms of tracking and law enforcement. Or pursuers.

A driver or passenger who is in a vehicle with this mechanism installed can spend an action to manually enable or disable the self-driving mechanism, which will automatically adjust as to whether the spoofer is enabled or not. A hacker or system administrator on the network can make an Intelligence check while using a set of hacker’s tools with a DC 15 to identify the presence of a spoofed vehicle within a mile of the initial scan. Succeeding on the check by more than 5 allows the person scanning the network to locate the vehicle precisely, but not gather any additional information.
