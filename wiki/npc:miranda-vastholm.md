**Name.** Miranda Vastholm

**Lineage.** Assumed human, unknown

**Age.** Unknown

**Height and Weight.** 6'1", 162lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Affiliation.** V.V. Incorporated

**Features.** Hires runners frequently for wet work and other jobs; secretes addictive saliva and other substances

**Status.** Alive

Miranda Vastholm's origins are unknown to the general masses, though she appeared a long time ago in underground tales, applying her trade in the sex industry and succeeding in taking over many small businesses on her path.

Tales of a woman like her have been around for many years, and most speak fondly of their interactions with her. She seems to have a soft spot for the general populace, and despite being a business runner and fixer herself, despises general corporate life. Her primary trades are sex and drugs, with some information brokering too. She's mostly in isolation with her partner [Talia Venk](/npc:talia-venk).

What her main goals are remains to be seen. Still, she slowly creeps up in power without treading on many toes, currently holding the cure to a virus that she's slowly spreading information on as discreetly as she can.
