##### Source: Player's Handbook (Pass Without Trace)

*2nd-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S, M (a vial of nanofog microdrones)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

A veil of cloaking nanofog radiates from you, masking you and your companions from detection. For the duration, each creature you choose within 30 feet of you (including you) has a +10 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) checks and can’t be tracked except by magical means. A creature that receives this bonus leaves behind no tracks or other traces of its passage.
