##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Dragon Slayer)

*Weapon (any sword or [machete](/weapon:machete)), rare*

**Specs:** Variable, $$300,000

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this technical weapon.

When you hit a kaiju with this weapon, the kaiju takes an extra 3d6 damage of the weapon's type. For the purpose of this weapon, "kaiju" refers to any creature with the Kaiju type.
