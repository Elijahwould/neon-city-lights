##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Lightning Lure)

*Altercation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self (15-foot radius)

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a hook of electrical energy that strikes at one creature of your choice that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 10 feet in a straight line toward you and then take 1d8 lightning damage if it is within 5 feet of you.

This tech's damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).
