**Name.** Elizabeth Anne Cummings

**Lineage.** Wukong

**Age.** 31

**Height and Weight.** 3'1", 145 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Affiliation.** [Propagation](/org:propagation)

**Features.** White fur, usually has her hair up in a ponytail

**Status.** Active

Beth-Anne is a lithe Wukong, though above average in height. She can usually be seen wearing comfortable tank tops and pants packing ton of pockets to hold all of her random tools and instruments.

She is vibrant and full of energy, eager to take full advantage of the new lease on life she's been given. Having lived with an invisible countdown timer hanging over her before she was engrammized, the freedom she now owns is tantamount to a miracle in her eyes.

Nonetheless, she's not one to rest on her laurels—while her brain chemistry may now be different, she still is intent on working diligently to find out all she can about the sentient brain—though with her main drive having essentially been cured centuries ago? Her responsibilities feel much lighter.
