*2d8 piercing, 1 lb., explosive*

Grenades can be set to detonate on impact or with a timer that causes them to explode on initiative count 20 (losing all initiative ties). As an action, you can prime and throw a grenade at a point you can see within 30 feet + your Strength modifier x 5.

### Features

- ***Fragmentation.*** When thrown, launched or the grenade explodes, each creature within 10 feet must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 2d8 piercing damage on a failed save, or half as much as on a successful one.
