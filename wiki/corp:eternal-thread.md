**Corporation Name.** Eternal Thread, Inc.

**CEO.** Blake Berry

**Years in business.** ~24

**Industry.** Creation and distribution of fashion and cosmetics

**Motto.** *"Reap what you sew."*

**Impact.** Subsidiary ([Heartpulse](/corp:heartpulse))

Originally known as 4Ever, Eternal Thread is a brand that was built upon the idea that your mods and cybernetics shouldn't be fashionable. In a world drenched by sexuality and powerful tech, it was a no brainer. The company makes body augments, cybernetic implants, limbs, clothing, and lingerie.

The company is well known for hosting fashion shows that quickly turn to full on orgies.

Exported out after being bought out by [Heartpulse, Inc.](/corp:heartpulse), the company soon upstarted as a partner company after several executives agreed to helm the mantle for the organization. Its de facto CEO, Blake Berry, was not new to the fashion industry as he worked for 4Ever as a model in his early career for the corporation.

"Being your better self" is what the company lives by, with "reap what you sew" as its catchier tagline. The clothes from Eternal Thread match high comfort, luxury, and trendsetting standards by the public, though some skeptics claim that the brand has begun changing its customers' mindsets…
