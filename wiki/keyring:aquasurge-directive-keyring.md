##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Ring of Water Elemental Command)

*Keyring, legendary (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$2,000,000

This ring is linked to the liquid state of matter.

While wearing this ring, you have advantage on attack rolls against surges from the linked state of matter, and they have disadvantage on attack rolls against you. In addition, you have access to the following properties.

The ring has 5 charges. It regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. Spells cast from the ring have a save DC of 17.

You can expend 2 of the ring's charges to cast [ACCESS_ROOT(Target)](/tech:access-root-target) on an liquid surge. In addition, you can stand on and walk across liquid surfaces as if they were solid ground.

If you help slay a liquid Surge while attuned to the ring, you gain access to the following additional properties:

- You can breathe underwater and have a swimming speed equal to your walking speed.
- You can cast the following techs from the ring, expending the necessary number of charges: [(De)Construct Water](/tech:deconstruct-water) (1 charge), [Hydrokinesis](/tech:hydrokinesis) (3 charges), [Dry Ice Bombardment](/tech:dry-ice-bombardment) (2 charges), or [Cryobarricade](/tech:cryobarricade) (3 charges).
