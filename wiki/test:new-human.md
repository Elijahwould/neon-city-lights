## "The only constant thing in life is change. Might as well embrace it."

##### Source: Neon City NightsThanks to reny#8234 for seeing the beauty in what humanity can truly become (and also helping us get rid of Custom Lineage)!

![Skylar Velvetine-Madison (Sky High), a new human bimbo ronin (character and art by reny)](https://neoncitylights.wdfiles.com/local--files/test:new-human/Sky.High.png)

The last four hundred years have destroyed what we once traditionally knew as humanity. The loss of most of the Old World has inspired curiosity, rapid developments and rediscoveries of sufficiently advanced tech have inspired risk taking, and cruel executive overlordship has eliminated notions of blind patriotism. New Humanity exists on a constant razor's edge, nudged by fear and mind-melting ads towards a corpo-approved living standard, waiting for the chance to be in the wrong place at the right time to escape into the perilous fortunes of a runner's life.

While new humans might make up the largest portions of the population in Neon City, that fact mightn't be one's first guess. There is no homogeneity to be found amongst mankind beyond a basic humanoid shape that is shared by other lineages. Genetic autonomy, generational specialization, and general adaptability have allowed humans to be spotted anywhere while looking less and less traditionally human. From the desert to ocean edges, to the farms, up the highest spires and down the subways, there will always be a new human hanging about somewhere… Because why not?

While life to the city's standards is debaucherous complacency and life on the run is a lethal game of opportunism, there forever remains a glimmer of kindness in humanity. In those odd camps at the edge of sandy, condemned buildings, in those abandoned undercity railways, where you find a new human, while they may sneer and smart about it, they will invite you to their fire. They will offer you a cigarette or a drink. In losing a static shape, they are no more perfect of heralds for common people.

### Roleplaying a New Human

When roleplaying a New Human, comfort, community, customization, or some combination of are likely your primary motivators. And all of these things are much more easily achieved when you can simply throw credits at your issues, leading many a New Human to hold some position in hotel bars, nightclubs, niche storefront odd jobs, or the oldest profession for quick creds when they aren't perusing bounty boards. Comfort is often found indoors, lacking the endurance of Gobbos or Nayalings, so what kind of home do you dream about? A tiny building with only the necessities? A dingy one that you can put on the road to escape corpo-cops? A fortified penthouse that can house any and every friend? These ideals feed into the community you could foster, and how they in turn could help grant a sense of autonomy to an otherwise railroaded life. When it comes down to it, your friends'll be worth more than any lick of wealth… Right?

### New Human Naming Conventions

New Humans have a wide variety of personal and family names stemming from Old World cultures becoming mish-mashed after the Great Relocation. Many New Humans, especially in adventuring circles, use nicknames and aliases for a number of reasons, primarily as a way of keeping their personal and professional lives separate.

For example, Scooter Fabuta, Cathenna Babineaux, Bhavika MacDonald, Diego Koroveshi, Mercedes Rojas, Kitty Kitsune, Driss Rasmussen, Ajulo Ivanovic, etc.

# New Human Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, or two different scores by 1.
- **Age.** Humans reach maturity at 18. Humans used to live to just under a century at max, but New Humans are always pushing that boundary.
- **Size.** Your size is Small or Medium, selected when you choose this lineage. Humans used to be between 5 and 7 feet tall and vary wildly in build, but modern genetics extremifies that variety to anywhere from 3 to 8 feet in height.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Old Skills.** You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice, one tool of your choice, and one Old World language of your choice (such as Spanish, Korean, French, Japanese, Mandarin, etc.).
- **Ancestral Diversity.** Countless ages of natural selection and specialized development combined with the current standard of personal physiological control produces a stunning variety of New Humanity. Choose one of the following options when you select this lineage:
  - **Specialized Predisposition.** Advanced pattern recognition, hinge joints designed for maximum propulsive force, and endurance to rival the stamina bursts of megafauna. You gain one Feat of your choice. If the selected feat does not increase one of your ability scores, you may take one [Minor Splice](/race:splicer).
  - **Natural Biodiversity.** What looks human has drastically changed, the common individual may have horns, a tail, unique ear designs, odd pigmentations to their skin or eyes. You gain two Traits of your choice from up to two other lineages. You can not target Traits from lineages possessing the Legacy Programming Trait, nor Traits with identical effects even if they have different names.
  - **Hyper Adaptability.** New Humanity carries on the old legacy of looking at the most terrifying horrors of incomprehensibility and seeing the fuckable. Improvise, adapt, overcome. Increase one ability score by 2 or two different scores by 1, targeting ability scores that have not yet been modified by your Ability Score Increase lineage trait. When you finish a long rest, you can change which scores this feature is targeting for increase.
