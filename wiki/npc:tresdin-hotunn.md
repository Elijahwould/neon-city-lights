**Name.** Tresdin Hotunn

**Lineage.** Human

**Age.** 25

**Height and Weight.** 6'6", 150 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Affiliation.** [The Red Suns](/org:red-suns)

**Features.** Chocolate brown hair, athletic bulk, notable clan tattoo under right eye. Stoic and detached.

**Status.** Alive

Life in the Undercity was never a walk in the park. Many know this. Seeing tomorrow was always a hope for these dwellers, never a promise. You do what you can to take that loaf of bread. Hardly a face that stood out of the masses, Tresdin was simply an exceptional fighter—while most were, down under, as it was the ticket to survival and to a better life… if you performed well.

Tresdin did well enough to make it in Neon City, but she was nothing more than a scrapper—a rebel in hiding from the corps, day in, day out. Her early years in the big city were unkind.

Some accept their fate—most have, truly, seeing the haze that clouds the minds of many. Why be anything more than a pit fighter, a bar brawler, with drinks and partners aplenty? The perfect life is already set for most, and in a perfect world? That ought to be good enough.

But… this is not a perfect world.

That is why she is who she is today: the leader of an up-and-coming gang known primarily as the Red Suns. She can always use the additional help. She stands as a Fixer for those who find themselves to be involved in more… rebellious activities. Still, whether you be a rebel, or simply in it for the money? Skills of all nature are welcomed.
