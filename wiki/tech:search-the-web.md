##### Source: Player's Handbook (Commune)

*5th-level observation (script)*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S, M (a hacked computer and a one-use USB dongle)

**Duration:** 1 minute

You contact members of a forum you frequent, or an AI you trust and ask up to three questions that can be answered with a yes or no. You must ask your questions before the tech ends. You receive a correct answer for each question.

Beings of the NET aren’t necessarily omniscient, so you might receive “unclear” as an answer if a question pertains to information that lies beyond their knowledge. In a case where a one-word answer could be misleading or contrary to the consultant's interests, the fixer might offer a short phrase as an answer instead.

If you cast the tech two or more times before finishing your next long rest, there is a cumulative 25 percent chance for each casting after the first that you get no answer. The fixer makes this roll in secret.
