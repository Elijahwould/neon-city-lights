**Name.** Noire Damarion

**Lineage.** Executive Elf

**Age.** 58

**Height and Weight.** 4'6", 105 lbs

**Pronouns.** he/him

**Affiliation.** Ex-[Damarion](/org:damarion-clan)

**Features.** Young, reedy, short white hair and sunken eyes

**Status.** Alive (the title of Noire revoked after failing his trial by combat)

The youngest and last legitimate heir of the Damarion name, Noire showed an extreme sense of cognitive awareness and hyper-reflexes at an early stage of his life. However, unlike his siblings, he proved physically frail. To make up for this, he was entombed within the body of an old suit of power armor from the days of the Relic Wars. However, even despite this boon, Noire showed a disinterest in the crueler tasks often left for him to undertake in the family’s efforts of converting him into a cold blooded predator.

As such, he was often left under the watch of his great aunt, Blanc Damarion, who would often enforce the Damarion decree, should Noire refuse. He was last seen being rescued on a live stream from his entombed power armor body by a group of runners.
