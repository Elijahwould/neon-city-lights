## “Ain’t I a stinker?”

Throughout history, there have been those who teach and guide through deception, those who gain by taking from others, and those who live by their cunning rather than their strength. The Trickster holds secrets in trust and deceives others to show the wisdom of humility. The Trickster must gain by their wits that which cannot be had by either erudition or the sword. The ordinators they bond are often more con-men or thieves than mystics, but they possess a strange wisdom nonetheless.

##### Source: Player's Handbook (Trickery)

### Duplicitous Training

When you choose this synergy at 1st level, you gain proficiency in the Deception or Stealth skill (your choice).

### Stealth Module

Starting when you choose this synergy at 1st level, you can use your action to touch a willing creature other than yourself to give it advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. This Buffing lasts for 1 hour or until you use this feature again.

### A.I. Assistance: Invoke Duplicity

Starting at 2nd level, you can use your A.I. Assistance to create an illusory duplicate of yourself.

As an action, you create a perfect illusion of yourself that lasts for 1 minute, or until you lose your concentration (as if you were concentrating on a tech). The illusion appears in an unoccupied space that you can see within 30 feet of you. As a bonus action on your turn, you can move the illusion up to 30 feet to a space you can see, but it must remain within 120 feet of you.

For the duration, you can cast techs as though you were in the illusion’s space, but you must use your own senses. Additionally, when both you and your illusion are within 5 feet of a creature that can see the illusion, you have advantage on attack rolls against that creature, given how distracting the illusion is to the target.

### A.I. Assistance: Distortion Field

Starting at 6th level, you can use your A.I. Assistance to vanish. As an action, you become invisible until the end of your next turn. You become visible if you attack or cast a tech.

### Insidious Strike

At 8th level, you gain the ability to infuse your weapon strikes with lethal toxins. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can cause the attack to deal an extra 1d8 poison damage to the target. When you reach 14th level, the extra damage increases to 2d8.

### Improved Duplicity

At 17th level, you can create up to four duplicates of yourself, instead of one, when you use Invoke Duplicity. As a bonus action on your turn, you can move any number of them up to 30 feet, to a maximum range of 120 feet.
