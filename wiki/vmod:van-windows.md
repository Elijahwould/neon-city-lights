*Requirements: must be installed on a [van](/vehicle:van)*

This modification installs windows in the back of a van. This changes the cover provided by the van to the rear passengers to half cover but allows the people in the back of the van to see and establish lines of sight to things outside the van. A van with this modification can add polycarbonate shielding and mounted gun ports to the back of the van.
