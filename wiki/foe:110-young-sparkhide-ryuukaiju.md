The eager and starving hunter of the sands, the young sparkhide scours the desert that surrounds its buried feeding ground, awaiting the chance that it might claim a greater meal in the form of some hapless traveler. Like a broken circuit, the opportunistic kaiju of sand, storm, and shell will await the path of least resistance before springing from the sand with all the speed of a lightning bolt. While oft spotted alone, where one young sparkhide has survived, yet more grow older deep beneath the dunes.

A sparkhide ryuukaiju is recognized by the dramatic frilled limbs crowning its head and the massive ridged horn atop its blunt upper jaw. Rows of spikes extend back from its nostrils to line its brow, and cluster on its over extended lower jaw, breaking into three lined segments across the body, one to each side of the underbelly and along the back. Its body is segmented, like that of an oversized worm, the friction between its plates and the material of sand generating a static charge that allows it to glide through sand as easily as a bird would the air.

***Terror of the Sands.*** The sparkhide possess many an adaptation tailored to its environment, namely retractable face plates that cover its nostrils and deep-set eye slits, the same retraction allowing a split in the jaw so it might unhinge into a radial array of segmented sets of teeth, and a set of burrow-limbs that serve the function of a cobra's hood, fins, and claws. Some fearful rumors would suggest the burrow-limbs are just as effective in the air or ocean as the sand.

***Ravenous Dynamo.*** It's uncertain if the sparkhide's diet is the cause or the result of its electrodynamic properties, but it is possessed of an all-consuming hunger. A sparkhide ryuukaiju spends most of its waking hours on the hunt, gorging itself on every spark of life it can catch or guzzling vast quantities of underground water. They make their feeding grounds between networks of buried bunker generators, supplementing their energy with direct current when food is lacking.

***Long Term Hunter.*** Sparkhides are not cowards, but do not relish physical exchanges, for they are a loss of energy. They prefer, instead, to act as ambush predators from afar, picking off singular prey or leaving biker caravans too injured to survive the night before returning for a meal. Even when forced into melee, sparkhides are dispassionate, serving only their appetite, typically retreating whenever there is nothing to be gained from further battle.

***Young Ryuukaiju.*** The true monsters, the ryuukaiju, are reminders of an Old World gone wrong. Their young are the oxymoronic promise of self-perpetuating scars and grand new life in one. Nascent ryuukaiju clutches can number up to fifty, but seasonal counts of the young tend only to number ten to five, sometimes even less. This is a stage in the ryuukaiju life cycle where they build confidence, shifting from the pure survivalist practice of their infancy to some level of forward action.
