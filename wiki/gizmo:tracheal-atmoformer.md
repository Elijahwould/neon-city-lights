##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Necklace of Adaptation)

*Gizmo, uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** 1 lb., $$150,000

While this device is attached to your throat, you can breathe normally in any environment, and you have advantage on saving throws made against harmful gases and vapors (such as *[riot control agent](/tech:riot-control-agent)* and *[aerosol stink spray](/tech:aerosol-stink-spray)* effects, inhaled poisons, and the breath weapons of some kaijus).
