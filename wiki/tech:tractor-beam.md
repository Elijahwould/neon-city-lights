##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Earthbind)

*2nd-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 300 feet

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose one creature you can see within range. A cloud of nanites in the form of yellow rings press down against the creature. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or its flying speed (if any) is reduced to 0 feet for the tech’s duration. An airborne creature affected by this tech descends at 60 feet per round until it reaches the ground or the tech ends.
