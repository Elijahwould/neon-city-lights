Source: Fizban's Treasury of Dragons (Nathair's Mischief)

*2nd-level deception*

**Casting Time:** 1 Action

**Range:** 60ft

**Components:** S, M (a digital folder filled with dank memes)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You fill a 20-foot cube you can see within range with the most dankest memes imaginable. Roll on the Meme Surge table to determine the effect produced, and roll again at the start of each of your turns until the tech ends. You can move the cube up to 10 feet before you roll.

*Meme Surge Table*

| d4 | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Uh-oh! You've misclicked, and leaked some… tasteful content. The entire cube gets filled with quite explicit imagery from your own "personal work folder" or the ads centering around that business. Each creature in the cube must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become charmed by you until the start of your next turn. |
| 2 | Deep-fried memes appear all around, and each creature in the cube must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or be blinded until the start of your next turn as the pixelization of the memes render them unable to see. |
| 3 | Each creature in the cube must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or begin giggling until the start of your next turn due to the absurd memes. A giggling creature is incapacitated and uses all its movement to move in a random direction. |
| 4 | Buffering ads, pop-ups, and a specific crusty yellow watermark appear and hover in the cube, turning it into difficult terrain until the start of your next turn. |
