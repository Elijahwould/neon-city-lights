Cybernetic augmentation is common practice in the world of Neon City—from minor neural chips that enhance a front-of-store employee’s charisma, to vicious mounted wrist blades and subdermal armor linkages to better a mercenary’s killing potential. Runners can visit a ripperdoc clinic in the Grey Market to purchase Augments that further empower and customize their abilities in combat.

Runners are able to have as many cybernetic augments as their Proficiency Bonus. As these modifiers increase, the number of cybernetic augments they can handle increases alongside them.

Runners are unable to gain the benefit of 2 effects if they share a name, thus stacking one type of augment will not give a stacking buff. Additionally, stacking augments of different rarities will result in the higher rarity augment of the two overriding the lesser. Commercial Augments can affect stats up to a maximum score of 20. Mercenary Augments can affect stats up to a maximum score of 22. Executive Augments can affect stats up to a maximum of 24. Any stat increases past these thresholds are disregarded.
