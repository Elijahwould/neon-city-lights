##### Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting Reborn (Spelldriver)

*Prerequisites: Character level 11 or higher; techcasting or gifted tech feature*

Through intense focus, training, and dedication—or cybernetic enhancement—you’ve expanded your mind, allowing yourself to cast more strong techs in a shorter amount of time. When you use your bonus action to cast a tech of 1st level or higher, you can also use your action to cast another tech of 1st level or higher. However, if you cast two or more tech in a single turn, only one of them can be 3rd level or higher.
