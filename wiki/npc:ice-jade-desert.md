**Name.** Ice Jade Desert

**Lineage.** Remake (previously Baphometic Nayaling)

**Age.** 25 + 1

**Height and Weight.** 7'0", 290 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/them

**Affiliation.** Unknown

**Features.** Ice blue polysteel, sleek polyplastic and steel tail with spikes, large curved horns, chrome joints, non-hooved legs; acerbic; cynical and quite sarcastic.

**Status.** Alive

Once upon a time, Ice Jade Desert was a tall blue nayaling with navy blue hair and pale blue skin, standing at almost seven feet tall. Curled, almost demonic-looking bone white horns broke the monotony of her cropped haircut, and her lips were painted a darker shade of blue. Her arms were sinewy and strong, her muscles corded and lean. She was a bear of a woman, her upper body and mid-section relatively stocky—no agility to be found, only imposing brawn, with large fists like bricks, shaped by either hard labor, or by their use in repeated instances of violence, directly applied from her fists. She had a distorted view about life and those who live in it, and was not particularly fond of advanced nanotechnology, even as she understood it was necessary.

She was killed in action during a job, in which Rose Kell caught her unawares, killing her to protect a netsucker by the name of Anthe Féval-Schwartz from her potential assault—in an event streamed live to thousands. After Anthe walked away with Jade's body, she was not seen again for a long time—until she was seen by Rose Kell once more, delivering unto her the body of Lucina Sylfaren's killer in a full remake body. Sightings place her now in the Undercity, occasionally performing jobs for an unknown employer.

She speaks in heavy Russian accent, hinting to some extent to her nature.
