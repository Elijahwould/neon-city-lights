### Expanded Tech List

### Rapid Responses

Starting at 1st level, you gain the *[quick text](/tech:quick-text)* trick. This trick can be cast as a bonus action and without any components. It counts as a streamer trick, and does not count against your total tricks known.

### Looking Over My Shoulder

Also at 1st level, your benefactor is always watching. When you fail a Perception, Insight, or Investigation check as well as a Dexterity Saving Throw, you can add your Charisma to the result. This can change the result into a success. Once you turn two failures into successes, you cannot use this feature again until you take a short or long rest.

- At 6th level you can apply this feature to all Intelligence skill checks.

### Slippery Advertising

At 6th level you can make it hard to be focused upon. Using a bonus action, your form is distorted for 1 minute or until your concentration is broken (as if you are concentrating on a tech) as you slip in and out of a frenzied digital advertising spree. Any creature that targets you with a tech or attack must make a Wisdom Saving Throw against your Streamer Save DC. On a failure, they must target a different creature, or the tech or attack is lost. Creatures immune to Software Damage are immune to this feature.

- Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you take a short or long rest.

### Counterargument

At 10th level, when you take software damage, make a Wisdom or Intelligence Saving throw, or are sent a telepathic message, you can use your reaction to deal software damage equal to your streamer levels. This can break a creature's concentration on a tech.

### Aggressive Marketing

At 14th level, you give into the whims of your benefactor. As an action, you begin spouting off advertisement jargon in rapid fashion. You create an aura of 20 feet around you that can be dismissed with a bonus action. While using this feature, you cannot speak, and can only use tricks or techs from the Marketing Firm expanded tech list.

Any creature that enters into the Aura or ends its turn there must make a Wisdom Saving throw against your Streamer DC, or be *[charmed](/con:charmed)* for one minute. If a creature is immune to charm, or succeeds this saving throw, they take software damage equal to your Charisma Modifier. If a creature fails, they may make the save again at the end of their turn.

- A creature charmed this way will actively defend the streamer. It will use its movement and actions to attack the streamer’s enemies until the charm is ended, either by successful save, or after the minute passes.
