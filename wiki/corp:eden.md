**Organization Name.** Eden Labs

**CEO.** Eden

**Years in business.** 30+, ~10 in the public eye

**Industry.** Security and entertainment

**Motto.** *"Intimacy redefined, lust redesigned."*

**Impact.** Minor

Eden Labs is a private science foundation which became known to the public roughly six years ago. They have garnered little attention to themselves, and it has worked wonders in their favor. They are recognized as a corporation, but as for recognition, scant others recognize and care for their name—a key one being Ignorance.

Prior to the AI takeover of the corporation, the company was more private and withheld a lot of its experiments. More recently, thanks to the new CEO, a core named Eden who was created by the company, Eden Labs has pushed more of its experiments to consumers, selling products in not only entertainment, but security as well. However, due to some unforeseen consequences, Eden Labs has had to fight against members of a cult who worship the new CEO, as well as a rebel group who has been laying low since the takeover. Fortunately, that hasn't stopped the growth of the company from reaching high status among Neon City.
