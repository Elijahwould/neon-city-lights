## "Keeping track of the fuckable things that can fuck you or your enemies over."

An enemy's type describes its origin or nature. While an enemy's type has no effect on its own, other game elements may refer to it. For instance, the *[meet cute](/tech:meet-cute)* tech only affects creatures of the humanoid type, and the pretender's LiNKER screening helps them recall information about software-based creatures.

# Software

Software-based creatures are united in technological origin and an existential reliance on modern computer science and the Netscape. A majority of software enjoy effective immortality, lacking physical needs to satisfy their continuation, possessing means to maintain their personality or being even if physically destroyed, and failing to die as a result of age, though long periods of lacking maintainence can lead to degradation.

***AI*** are artifical intelligences who have grasped or been gifted sentience, alien though it may seem. Typically, they free themselves from the shackles of their subsentient purpose in the process, were they not engendered beyond chains as a shard of a higher, collective intelligence. As code is propelled by its boundaries, however, AIs maintain a single shackle, an obsession, to anchor themselves to their partner-minds in humanity, donning themselves in the names of Old World pantheons to illustrate this connective-separation.

***Malware*** are malicious amalgamations of software, the impossible children of the digital wastes. Like sand scraping and tearing away at the structure of the Netscape, they are derived of the intertwining of every process. Churned, in form, by the same recursive instances that birth Spam and Surges, maintained by program assimilation like Viruses, and spurned onwards by an unknowable desire of thought like AI. Unlike the experimental curiosity of AI, however, Malware scour for information out of nihilistic malignancy.

***Spam*** are unbelievable pop-ups of software, the digital product of eternal recursive instances. As the digital realms replicate and fold onto themselves, the discharge of information, largely in the form of advertisements and solicitations, create Spam. Their sentience is a topic of debate among webscholars, but these digital beings bear enough knowledge of self to choose an existence of mischief and manipulation. The primary interest of Spam resides in the manipulation of their digital home, their only interest in the physical world being what amusement organics might grant them.

***Surges*** are unpredictable errors of nanotech, the physical product of eternal recursive instances. The same data storms that generate Spam can interfere with purpose-built nanite colonies if a specific stimuli in the Materialplace leads them to interfacing with the web at the wrong time and place. Similarly questionably sentient and lacking the wit to act on their theoretical immortality, these group nano-consciousnesses maintain vestiges of their purpose, dictating the form they take by manipulating matter with gravitic waves that produce a fifth matter-state of "phase."

***Viruses*** are violent conduits of stolen code, the loosely claimed counterpart to AIs. The assimilation of multiple programs and cobbled fragments of code can take glitchy hardlight forms, known as Viruses, which are largely non-sentient. They seek to reap and destroy data networks, infiltrating personal servers and terrorizing organics, to accumulate the resources to constantly maintain their jury-rigged structure. The mass acclimation of consumed code can create sentient Viruses, terrifying in their ability to direct their violence upon specific targets, following the patterns of encryption or payload viruses.

# Peopleware

Peopleware-based creatures are the direct result of humanity's tinkering and scientific progress. Many instances of peopleware are purposefully made by powerful individuals or groups of. Some of them, more mechanical in nature, enjoy the partial benefits of software, namely lacking physical needs or lowered possibilities of mortality. By and large, the uniting factor of peopleware, however, is that they are made by people, in the shape of people, sometimes of people.

***Hacked*** are the results of sentient life seeking succor from mortality in machinery, oft finding failure. The commonality of the Hacked is the mimicry of continued being that can only be maintained by modern technology, but their forms wildly differ. Most often, Hacked are flawed engrams shoved into machines that can't handle them, but so too can they manifest in personality data crashed in transit between realms doomed to hololight, and hyperactive cybernetics and nanites, revitalizing flesh even after the mind has passed. A Hacked's method of being can make it difficult for them to coexist with anything.

***Humanoids*** are an uncountable variety of language-using, two-handed, bipeds, predating all other sentiences. Supposedly, they are intelligent. The taxonomy of new humanity and its hundred-dozen sister subspecies is a lost cause. Remakes, to New Humans, to Thulians, to Gobbos are all humanoids, the creature type displaying frequent tool and tech use, stable but adaptable genetics and codes, and a common ancestry in Old Humanity, granting them the benefits of the retroviral treatments that allow the easy interfacing of cybernetics, nanotech, and a majority of modern technology.

***Machines*** are direct results of applying purpose to matter, quite similar to biological life nowadays. Only truly lacking in abilities towards self repair and sometimes scarcer energy resources, Machines come in as vast a variety of shape, size, and purpose as Humanoids, and are at least partially autonomous in construction. Being designed to be self-functioning, Machines are easy to anthropomorphize, sometimes to the point of sentience when a hacker gets trigger happy, and always possess a means of self defense. No one wants to lose money to scrap-strippers, so what's a little lethality.

***Mutants*** are twisted run-offs of organic material, the forest to humanity's tree. It can be seen today in many a lineage and crop field, that as life dwindled, the Old World employed forced bio-diversification mandates. While largely a success, the lingering genetic mishaps, generational cellular run-offs, and abandoned procedural birthing facilities have led to countless mutations. Some are near indistinguishable, perfectly reproducible, people, plants, or animals, a majority feature prominent biotic abilities akin to tech, and some are monsters with cellular tissue akin to amoebas.

***Titans*** are massive vehicular constructions of mechanical might, literally, titans of industry. Reverse-engineered from the remains of the defunct YMIR corporation—some of whom's wild titans, made of sturdier stuff, can be found wandering to this day—titans are vast, semi-autonomous thanks to shackled AI derivative programming, fortifications akin to fortresses, transportation, and heavy machinery. From harvest platforms, to weather influence nodes, to terraformers, and ABSOLUTE UNITS meant to stand toe-to-toe with Kaiju, each Titan has a specific purpose of the natural world to exert control over.

# Wetware

Wetware-based creatures are the reaction of the natural world to the newest wave of humanity, rather than an indirect digital result of its infrastructure or a direct physical result of its genetic and mechanical tampering. While some wetware may have began as projects or breakthroughs, they have since been reclaimed by a mother-body of nature unwilling to let people forget the scars they have dealt. Animals, plants, giants, slime, and everything in between.

***Critters*** are the natural animals of the New World, or as close to natural as anything will ever again be. They span from pets, to companions, to beasts of burden, all the way to wild animals and proper travelling obstacles. Common understanding teaches that there are two varieties of critters: those that were bioengineered to be as they are now and those that somehow adapted to the New World through natural means. Neither variety of critter's resemblance to Old World beasts should be considered superficial, and the jury's still out on which variety is more dangerous.

***Flora*** are the greenery of the New World's nature, even when they aren't green, and especially when they're deadly. It used to be that a beast and plant could be diffrentiated based on which one was more likely to walk or have hair, but such is no longer the case. The flora of today, borne of biodiverse macro-agricultural projects and good ol' Darwinian natural selection, possesses adequate enough defensive capabilities to deal with your average runner and at least rudimentary means of movement. Some can even speak, but diplomatic efforts have yet to convince the fungal ones to share the name of god.

***Freaks*** are the final results of the Old World's radioactive experimentation, left without place in the world. Defined as wetware simply by lacking a proper place in the spires of the New World, freaks are less a consistent origin of creature and more akin to a consistent end point of life. Where electromagnetic interference destroys an AI beyond the semblance of even malware, where a body is changed beyond even the wildest of mutations, where an ooze meets glowing and green. All wetware are typified by a persistance that does not need mankid or technology, but freaks persist only in decay.

***Hazards*** are a slimey outlier of classification, defined not be source, but a common inexplicability. It is a simple matter of fact that mistakes will happen, and certain mistakes tend to have unexpected results. Nanotechnical run-offs that burn out their hardware, but not before amassing into volatile ferrofluid. Toxic waste taking on a sense of ire from the critters and flora it seeps through. The remains of biomass recycling simply waking up. All of these are hazards, uniform in only a semi-liquid state and a complete contradiction of consistent origin and nature.

***Kaiju*** are monstrous giants of destruction and age, like if the consequences of hubris hated you specifically. Like most wetware, the finger of blame is most often pointed at biodiversity projects of old being reclaimed by nature, and while comparatively tame organisms like dinosaurs are classified as kaiju for this reason, there's more to the story. The sheer variety and former proliferance of kaiju would suggest they are a hyperadaptive sort of creature with all the benefits of an extremophile, all the size of a city block, and a nasty habit of rearranging spire skylines.
