In the middle of an expanse of blasted black glass stand the spindly rusting remains of a huge chunk of the Bezosphere. It is suspected the smooth black glass expands quite far under the dunes, and more is being buried with every sandstorm.

The spire of rusted panels and sprawling beams bending in the wind is suspected to be some kind of former communications array.

Many Jovian Thulians have been spotted near the area, lending credence to the theory that a colony of them exists in the area.
