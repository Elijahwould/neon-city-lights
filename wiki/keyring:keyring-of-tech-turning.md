##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Ring of Spell Turning)

*Keyring, legendary (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$800,000

While wearing this ring, you have advantage on saving throws against any tech that targets only you (not in an area of effect). In addition, if you roll a 20 for the save and the tech is 7th level or lower, the spell has no effect on you and instead targets the techcaster, using the slot level, tech save DC, attack bonus, and techcasting ability of the caster.
