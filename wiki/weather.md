The weather is a major part of everyday life, but one that often doesn't make it into games. The following is a resource provide small mechanical impacts for common weather types, as well as a resource for determining the weather. You can either select the appropriate weather for the day in your season, locale, and climate, or you can use the provided tables to generate a generic random result.

You can change weather as often or as little as you would like for your game, but I'd recommend once per day, rolling at the start of the day and using that weather throughout the day to keep things nice and simple. Rolling two, three, or even four times a day can produce more varied and realistic results.

The following weather effects are just the baseline suggestion for how these weather effects impact your game. They may have wider reaching or more specific consequences depending on your setting and activities—they may interact differently if you are traveling by ship or airship for example.

Take what is listed here as a starting point for how you want to handle weather.

## List of Weather Effects

### Clear Skies/Light Clouds

This is the game as you normally play it. Clear bright light during day time, view of the stars and moon at night. No modifiers are added to play.

### Heavy Clouds

The sky is blocked. High flying aerial creatures have total cover, and outdoor light does not count as sunlight (for the purposes of sunlight sensitivity and similar traits). Checks using Navigation Tools to determine your location based on celestial observation are made with disadvantage. Solar powered devices may not work as well unless they are running on battery power, and there's not enough sun to meet the requirement for things such as a Gobbo's photosynthesis.

### Rain

Unpleasant to travel in. Ground vehicles have their cruising speed reduced by half on the road. If you attempt to take a long rest without cover, you must make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw to gain the benefits for a long rest.

- Fire damage rolls have a –2 penalty, to a minimum of 1 fire damage. This also has the effect of Heavy Clouds.

### Heavy Rain

Same as rain, but the DC becomes 16 to benefit from a long rest without shelter. If heavy rain occurs two days in a row, ground vehicle travel becomes impossible except for well-paved roads that aren't flooded (ie. until one day without rain occurs); otherwise, their cruising speed is still reduced by half). Of course, heavy rain may cause flooding.

- Fire damage rolls have a –4 penalty, to a minimum of 1 fire damage.
- Lightning and cold damage rolls gain a +2 bonus.
- This also has the effect of Heavy Clouds.

### Freezing Cold

If you attempt to take a long rest without cover and heat, you must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw gain the benefits of a long rest. If you fail by 5 or more, you gain one level of exhaustion.

- Cold damage rolls have a +2 bonus.

### Sandstorm

Difficult to traverse. All travel speed and visibility is halved, and no aerial vehicle travel is possible through a sandstorm. If a sandstorm occurs for two days in row, all terrain is difficult terrain and ground vehicle travel is impossible until one day without a sandstorm passes, unless said vehicle is equipped to travel in such an environment. Also has the effect of Heavy Clouds and Scorching Heat.

- Lightning damage rolls have a +2 bonus.

### Scorching Heat

Blistering heat that is unpleasant to travel in. Creatures that attempt to travel during day light hours require twice the ration of water, and creature that travel for 4 or more hours or engage in heavy activity for 1 or more hour during the day and do not immediately take a short or long rest under cover must make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or gain a level of exhaustion.

- Cold damage rolls have a –2 penalty, to a minimum of 1 cold damage.
- Fire damage rolls have a +2 bonus.

### Snow

Unpleasant to travel in. All travel speed is halved. If snow occurs for two days in row, all terrain is difficult terrain and land vehicle travel is impossible until one day without snow passes, unless said vehicle is equipped to travel in such an environment.

- Has the effect of Heavy Clouds and Freezing Cold.
- Replace with Rain when in climates without snow.

### High Winds

Turbulent gusts sweep across the land. Select a wind direction based on locale or roll a d4 and consult the table. Flying creatures gain +10 movement speed when moving with the wind, and –10 movement speed when moving against it.

- Ranged weapon attacks have a –2 to attack rolls, and their range is reduced by half when shooting into the wind.

### Thunderstorm

Lightning flashes and thunder crashes. All creatures are lightly obscured if they are more than 20 feet from you. If you travel for 4 or more hours during a Thunderstorm, roll a d20. On a 1, you are struck by a lightning bolt dealing 3d12 lightning damage.

- Lightning and Thunder damage rolls have a +2 bonus.
- Also has the effect of Rain, High Winds and Heavy Clouds.

### Blizzard

At the end of every hour spend in a Blizzard, make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, you take 3d4 cold damage and gain one level of exhaustion. You make this check with advantage if you have proper gear. All creatures are heavily obscured if they are more than 20 feet from you. All terrain is difficult terrain.

- Also has the effect of Snow, High Winds, and Freezing Cold.
- Replace with Thunderstorm when in climates without snow.
