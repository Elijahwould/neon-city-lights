##### Source: [Feats for the Fearsome](https://www.patreon.com/posts/feats-for-16156471) (Gifted Spellcaster)

*Prerequisite: Techcasting feature, techcasting ability 13*

Thanks to long hours of study—or perhaps just stubborn repetition—you have expanded your technical potential. You learn 4 techs of your choice of 7th level or lower from your class' tech list. These techs are always considered prepared for you. These techs must be for a level for which you have tech slots.
