##### Source: Player's Handbook (Hunger of Hadar)

*3rd-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 150 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a pickled octopus tentacle)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You open a gateway to an unknown, unspeakable digital sub-realm, one filled with horrors and perversions alike. A 20-foot-radius sphere of darkness and sickly cold appears, centered on a point with range and lasting for the duration. This void is filled with a cacophony of soft whispers and slurping noises that can be heard up to 30 feet away. No light, technical or otherwise, can illuminate the area, and creatures fully within the area are blinded.

The digital void creates a warp in the fabric of space, and the area is difficult terrain. Any creature that starts its turn in the area takes 2d6 cold damage. Any creature that ends its turn in the area must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 2d6 acid damage as milky, otherwordly tentacles rub against it.
