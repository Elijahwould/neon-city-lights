**Corporation Name.** Armata Tactical Solutions, Inc.

**CEO.** Zachariah Ashton

**Years in business.** ~24

**Industry.** Security training, private security contracting, general problem solving through firepower

**Motto.** *"Through fire and flames, Armata is there."*

**Impact.** Major

Armata was formed when Zachariah Ashton found themself left afloat after the corporation they worked security for went under. Still recovering from a traumatic leg injury at the time, they decided to keep pushing themself. At first, they operated as a ronin, favoring providing solutions at a rather…high calibre.

After gaining notoriety and slowly taking more Ronin under their wing, they decided to retire as a runner, and instead enable the next generation of hardened warriors.

While Armata began as an edgerunner focused group, it's now grown into a far larger entity. As a whole, their MO is still to hire and send the minimum amount of talent needed for a job - sometimes dropping one ronin into a job to handle it. With the training they offer, some indeed are up to the challenge.

Armata also has begun a simulated 'death by combat' competition they run every two to three months, usually with a maximum of thirty to forty applicants, and closed admission to the public. Those who win are offered a full contract for the company.
