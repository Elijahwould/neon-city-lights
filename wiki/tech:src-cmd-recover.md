##### Source: Player's Handbook (Power Word: Heal)

*9th-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You issue a command through the source code to push a wave of healing energy through a creature you touch. The target regains all its hit points. If the creature is charmed, frightened, paralyzed, or stunned, the condition ends. If the creature is prone, it can use its reaction to stand up. This tech has no effect on hacked or machines.
