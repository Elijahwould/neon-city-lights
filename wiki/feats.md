## "Sometimes, you choose what you know. Sometimes, it chooses you."

Players in Neon City Nights have access to a number of feats. Some of these are included in the base Carbon Pink system, some of them are unique to Neon City. For all intents and purposes, when playing in Neon City Nights, you should refer to this list when looking up feats for your characters!

### [Actor](/feat:actor)

##### Source: Player's Handbook

Skilled at mimicry and dramatics, you gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You have an advantage on Charisma (Deception) and Charisma (Performance) checks when trying to pass yourself off as a different person.
- You can mimic the speech of another person or the sounds made by other creatures. You must have heard the person speaking, or heard the creature make the sound, for at least 1 minute. A successful Wisdom (Insight) check contested by your Charisma (Deception) check allows a listener to determine that the effect is faked.

### [Alert](/feat:alert)

##### Source: Player's Handbook

Always on the lookout for danger, you gain the following benefits:

- You can’t be surprised while you are conscious.
- You gain a +5 bonus to initiative.
- Other creatures don’t gain advantage on attack rolls against you as a result of being unseen by you.

### [Battle Couple](/feat:battle-couple)

##### Source: [Tumblr user amatuerdiydungeon](https://www.tumblr.com/amatuerdiydungeon/190459540242/i-love-watching-two-people-who-can-just-fight)

*Prerequisite: Two characters must take this feat at the same time, designating each other as their partner*

You've had so much experience fighting side by side with your companion, that you can practically guess what they are going to do before they do it. As a result of this synergy, you gain the following:

- Your two characters can fight from the same space and gain a +1 bonus to attack and AC while doing so.
- Your two characters can end their turn on the same space.
- When you are within 10 ft of your partner and they successfully make an attack on their turn, you can use your reaction to make an attack against an enemy within their range.
- When you are within 10 ft of your partner and are both required to make a saving throw against the same area of effect and you pass the saving throw, you can have your partner automatically pass the saving throw as well.
  - Both of you share the same pool of uses for this ability. Either of you can use this feature twice, and you regain your expended uses when you finish a long rest.

### [Battle Hacker](/feat:battle-hacker)

##### Source: Player's Handbook (War Caster)

*Prerequisite: The ability to cast at least one tech*

You have practiced casting spells in the midst of combat, learning techniques that grant you the following benefits:

- You have advantage on Constitution saving throws that you make to maintain your concentration on a tech when you take damage.
- You can perform the somatic components of techs even when you have weapons or a shield in one or both hands.
- When a hostile creature's movement provokes an opportunity attack from you, you can use your reaction to cast a tech at the creature, rather than making an opportunity attack. The tech must have a casting time of 1 action and must target only that creature.

### [Bike Ganger](/feat:bike-ganger)

##### Source: Player's Handbook (Mounted Combatant)

You are a dangerous foe to face while mounted—a modern-day cavalier, riding atop a steed of metal and burning rubber. While you are mounted and aren't incapacitated, you gain the following benefits:

- You have advantage on melee attack rolls against any unmounted creature that is smaller than your mount or vehicle.
- You can force an attack targeted at your mount or vehicle to target you instead.
- If your mount or vehicle is subjected to an effect that allows it to make Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, it instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if it fails.

### [Boosted WiFi](/feat:boosted-wifi)

##### Source: Player's Handbook (Spell Sniper)

*Prerequisite: The ability to cast at least one tech*

You have learned techniques to enhance your attacks with certain kinds of techs, gaining the following benefits:

- When you cast a tech that requires you to make an attack roll, the tech's range is doubled.
- Your ranged tech attacks ignore half cover and three-quarters cover.
- You learn one trick that requires an attack roll. Choose the trick from the Influencer, Ordinator, Shut-In, Source Coder, Streamer, or Techwizard tech list. Your techcasting ability for this cantrip depends on the tech list you chose from: Charisma for Influencer, Source Coder, and Streamer; Wisdom for Ordinator or Shut-In; or Intelligence for Techwizard.

### [Bunker Braver](/feat:bunker-braver)

##### Source: Player's Handbook (Dungeon Delver)

Alert to the hidden traps and secret doors found in many dungeons, you gain the following benefits:

- You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Intelligence (Investigation) checks made to detect the presence of secret doors.
- You have advantage on saving throws made to avoid or resist traps.
- You have resistance to the damage dealt by traps.
- Travelling at a fast pace doesn't impose the normal -5 penalty on your passive Wisdom (Perception) score.
- You can search for traps while traveling at a normal pace, instead of only at a slow pace.

### [Charger](/feat:charger)

##### Source: Player's Handbook

When you use your action to Dash, you can use a bonus action to make one melee weapon attack or to shove a creature. If you move at least 10 feet in a straight line immediately before taking this bonus action, you either gain a +5 bonus to the attack’s damage roll (if you chose to make a melee attack and hit) or push the target up to 10 feet away from you (if you chose to shove and you succeed).

### [Code Cracker](/feat:code-cracker)

##### Source: Player's Handbook (Linguist)

You have studied cyphers and codes, gaining the following benefits:

- Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You can ably create written ciphers. Others can't decipher a code you create unless you teach them, they succeed on an Intelligence check (DC equal to your Intelligence score + your proficiency bonus), or they use tech to decipher it.
- You learn three old world languages of your choice. This would allow you to understand texts that might not be immediately familiar to you when found perhaps buried in ruins, such as the operating manuals for old world equipment written entirely in Japanese.

### [CQC Firearm Training](/feat:cqc-firearm-training)

##### Source: Neon City Nights

Over a long time, you've managed to really drill in the basics of CQC into your training, granting you the following benefits:

- Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain proficiency with revolvers and submachine guns.
- You ignore the loading property of revolvers.
- Being within 5 feet of a hostile creature doesn’t impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls.

### [Cruelty](/feat:cruelty)

##### Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting Reborn (Cruel)

Neon City is a harsh place. The challenges and struggles you've faced throughout your life in it have twisted you, leading you to delight in inflicting pain and anguish upon others. You gain a number of cruelty dice equal to your proficiency bonus. As you level up, the amount of cruelty dice also increase with your proficiency bonus. Your cruelty dice are d6s. You can spend only one cruelty die per turn.

You can roll a cruelty die under any of the following circumstances:

- When you deal damage to a creature, you can spend one cruelty die to roll additional bonus damage equal to your cruelty die. The damage type is the same as the damage type of the source (slashing from a sword attack, fire from a fireball, etc.).
- When you score a critical hit, you can spend one cruelty die to gain temporary hit points equal to the roll. Starting at level 11, the amount of temporary hit points you gain is equal to twice the result of the roll, instead.
- When you make a Charisma or Strength (Intimidation) check, you can spend one cruelty die and add the roll to your check.

You regain all spent cruelty dice when you finish a long rest.

### [Crusher](/feat:crusher)

##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

You are practiced in the art of crushing your enemies, granting you the following benefits:

- Increase your Strength or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an attack that deals bludgeoning damage, you can move it 5 feet to an unoccupied space, provided the target is no more than one size larger than you.
- When you score a critical hit that deals bludgeoning damage to a creature, attack rolls against that creature are made with advantage until the start of your next turn.

### [Deadly Marksmanship](/feat:deadly-marksmanship)

##### Source: Neon City Nights

You've spent a long time honing your aim with rifles and other powerful ranged weapons, granting you the following benefits:

- Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You ignore the loading property of all two-handed ranged weapons.
- You may increase the total range of a two-handed ranged weapon you are currently holding by one increment of the weapon's short range.

### [Defensive Duelist](/feat:defensive-duelist)

##### Source: Player's Handbook

*Prerequisite: Dexterity 13 or higher*

When you are wielding a finesse weapon with which you are proficient and another creature hits you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to add your proficiency bonus to your AC for that attack, potentially causing the attack to miss you.

### [Demolitions Expert](/feat:demolitions-expert)

##### Source: Neon City NightsThanks to Celica#1988 for this one!

You're practically an expert when it comes to explosives. You gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- Your usage of grenades is more effective. The save for your grenades is equal to 8 + your Dexterity modifier + your proficiency bonus + any bonus granted by a grenade launcher (if any), or the base save of the grenade, whichever is higher. Additionally, you may designate any number of allies who would be affected by a grenade; those allies have advantage on their saving throw against the effects of your grenade.
- You may purchase grenades at 75% cost.

### [Detective](/feat:detective)

##### Source: [Feats for the Fearsome](https://www.patreon.com/posts/feats-for-16156471) (Detective)

Your keen eye for details and mechanical acuity give you a wide set of tools to unravel any mystery that presents itself.

You gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- By spending 1 minute examining a corpse that has been dead for no longer a month, you can automatically determine its cause of death.
- Whenever you make a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check, you gain a +2 bonus on subsequent Intelligence (Investigation) checks you make that relate to the same topic (for example, examining multiple pieces of evidence to a single murder.) These bonuses stack with multiple successes (up to a maximum of +10), but a failed check reverts your bonus to +0.
- By spending 1 minute studying a humanoid you can see, you can learn 1 thing, chosen by the fixer: the target's alignment (corporate, freelancer, rebel), one of the target's bonds, or one of its flaws.

### [Doc](/feat:doc)

##### Source: Player's Handbook (Healer)

You are an able physician, allowing you to mend wounds quickly and get your allies back in the fight. You gain the following benefits:

- When you use a healer's kit to stabilize a dying creature, that creature also regains 1 hit point.
- As an action. you can spend one use of a first aid kit to tend to a creature and restore 1d6 + 4 hit points to it, plus additional hit points equal to the creature's maximum number of Hit Dice. The creature can't regain hit points from this feat again until it finishes a short or long rest.

### [Dual Wielder](/feat:dual-wielder)

##### Source: Player's Handbook

You master fighting with two weapons, gaining the following benefits:

- You gain a +1 bonus to AC while wielding a weapon in each hand, and at least one of the weapons is a melee weapon.
- You can use two-weapon fighting even when the one-handed weapons you are wielding aren’t light.
- You can draw or stow two one-handed weapons when you would normally be able to draw or stow only one.

### [Durable](/feat:durable)

##### Source: Player's Handbook

Hardy and resilient, you gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- When you roll a Hit Die to regain hit points, the minimum number of hit points you regain from the roll equals twice your Constitution modifier (minimum of 2).

### [Engram Addict](/feat:engram-addict)

##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Fey Touched)

Your exposure to bizarre engram technology has changed you, granting you the following benefits:

- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You learn the [digital dash](http://neoncitylights.wikidot.com/tech:digital-dash) tech and one 1st-level tech of your choice. The 1st-level tech must be from the Observation or Conception school of tech. You can cast each of these techs without expending a tech slot. Once you cast either of these techs in this way, you can’t cast that tech in this way again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast these techs using tech slots you have of the appropriate level. The techs’ techcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.
