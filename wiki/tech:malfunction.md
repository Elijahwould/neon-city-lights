##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Negative Energy Flood)

*5th-level biochemistry*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, M (a broken bone and a square of black silk)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You send ribbons of entropic energy at one creature you can see within range. Unless the target is hacked, it must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 5d12 entropic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A target killed by this damage rises up as a hacked at the start of your next turn. The hacked pursues whatever creature it can see that is closest to it. Statistics for the hacked are in the Monster Manual. If you target a hacked with this tech, the target doesn’t make a saving throw. Instead, roll 5d12. The target gains half the total as temporary hit points.
