**Corporation Name.** Gothic Hoard, LLC

**CEO.** Miss Lilith

**Years in business.** ~40+

**Industry.** Goth fashion; investigation into old-world subcultures and practices

**Motto.** *"~"*

**Impact.** Minor

Gothic Hoard is a Goth Culture based corporation that specializes in the distribution and creation of edgy and gloomy fashion goths are known for, as well as information on the sub-cultures and old practices. They position themselves as an almost religious order and are one of the few companies to have a Face and a CEO as separate people. While the CEO is currently unknown beyond her name, the face of the company is a Splicer woman named Caroline Eve, who appears in several commercials involving the company and at this stage is synonymous with the brand.

They are organizationally split into two major branches. The Living is considered the lesser of two evils, being more focused on manufacturing and design of clothes and accessories offered by the corporation, and putting funding towards merchandise and advertisements, such as Streamers, or specially made Cyberdecks.

The Undead, however, are Gothic Hoard's darker and more prominent faction, with unknown intentions. Their experimentation with hacked technology has caused widespread damage to the city's underworld. This faction regularly conflicts with [The Reliquary](/corp:reliquary) over the occasional new tech, though it is unknown why they hoard so much themselves when their R&D is more geared towards creating perfected types of Hacked.
