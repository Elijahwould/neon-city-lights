##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings), Neon City Nights

Foto Finish televises their events all throughout, drawing crowds and eyes from all levels of Neon City, and you were one of its contenders. Depending on what medal you took home, you've got a fan base that knows your face, as well as every other part of you. But the sponsorships are drying up, and it's time to reach higher. Or deeper.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Acrobatics, Athletics
- **Tool Proficiencies.** None
- **Equipment.** Spicy athletes start their careers with the following equipment:
  - The shorts and top of your team
  - A half-gallon bottle of lube
  - The names of your prior teammates
  - A credchip worth $$3,000

### Feature: Hometown Hero

The Denizens of Neon City recognize you as a paragon of athletic prowess. When recognized, gain advantage on the first charisma (Persuasion, Deception, Intimidation) you make against that creature. This benefit is retained for as long as they respect you.
