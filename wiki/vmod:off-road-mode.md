*Requirements: vehicle with City Driver feature*

This modification allows a driver to, as an action, switch a vehicle from road mode to off-road mode. The modded vehicle will adjust the shock weight and use advanced nanites that will deploy to then cover the wheels. This effectively reduces the modded vehicle’s speed (calculated by halving the vehicle's speed, then adding 15 ft), but disables the *City Driver* feature until the system is deactivated. While in Off-Road Mode, the modded vehicle gains the following feature:

- **Superior Traction.** If this vehicle moves at least 25 feet in a straight line, it can climb a distance of up to 5 feet up a nearly vertical plane, or 15 feet of an incredibly steep incline. It does so without needing to pass a check and without costing any additional movement—but it must spend that movement to make the climb.
