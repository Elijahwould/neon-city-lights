##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Blade of Disaster)

*9th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You manifest a huge katana made of nanites which is about 3 feet long in an unoccupied space you can see within range. The blade lasts for the duration. When you cast this tech, you can make up to two melee tech attacks with the blade, each one against a creature, loose object, or structure within 5 feet of the blade. On a hit, the target takes 4d12 kinetic damage. This attack scores a critical hit if the number on the d20 is 18 or higher. On a critical hit, the blade deals an extra 8d12 kinetic damage (for a total of 12d12 kinetic damage).

As a bonus action on your turn, you can move the blade up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see and then make up to two melee tech attacks with it again.

The blade can harmlessly pass through any barrier, including a *[dynabarricade](/tech:dynabarricade)*.
