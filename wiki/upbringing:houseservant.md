##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings) + Neon City Nights

Estates need looking after, but the high-octane world of executive procrastination leaves little time for familial affairs, so you were one of the small army of young men and women employed to look after the estates of the upper class.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Persuasion, Sleight of Hand
- **Tool Proficiencies.** First aid kit, Cook's utensils
- **Equipment.** Houseservants start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A maid or butler's outfit
  - A first aid kit
  - A monofilament kitchen knife
  - A feather duster
  - A broken toy
  - A spare set of your prior employer's car keys

### Feature: Commanding Presence

Your time managing an estate has afforded you a commanding presence. You have advantage on any and all Persuasion, Intimidation and Deception checks against humanoids at least 15 years younger than you.
