Source: Player's Handbook (Beacon of Hope)

*3rd-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

This tech gives the freshest of vibes. It also deploys health-boosting nanites to creatures of your choosing. Choose any number of creatures within range. For the duration, each target has advantage on Wisdom saving throws and death saving throws, and regains the maximum number of hit points possible from any healing.
