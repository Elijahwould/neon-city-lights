It is believed that kaiju were far more prominent closer to the Great Collapse than they are today, harbingers of the near-end gifted to the world made manifest. The commonly accepted theory for the existence of the Kaijuborn is that they were in some manner created to combat or aid in surviving the onslaught of the very first kaiju, taking their names from the Old World media that now acted as a retroactive warning to humanity's hubris.

Unlike Jerboans, which became a point of reference in genetic engineering, Kaijuborn are assumed to have been designed for the sole purpose of safeguarding the remnants of humanity and die out after the threat of everyday kaiju attacks died down. Yet, they remain, as a simple truth of love and life rather than labs or factories.

Kaijuborn are a proud lineage of people, descended from the first bastions of days long past and the iconic names they took on. Beyond the obvious physical changes, without the assurance of RNA markers or any kind of project funding, it is unclear how any one Kaijuborn traces back their name as far as they do, but they manage it. Curiously, this historical pride seems to engender in Kaijuborn certain bonds—and rivalries—with other variety of Kaijuborn, even if their goals might otherwise align.

### Roleplaying a Kaijuborn

When roleplaying a Kaijuborn, remember that most—if not all—of your actions are inspired by an understated pride. It is not the haughty, self-assured nature of the Elf, but rather a feeling of responsibility to those who came before you and how far they were willing to go for the world you've inherited from them. In Neon City, most Kaijuborn live in a Little Big Town borough called Kaiju Alley, where large, clan-like, familial units preserve the history, alliances, and rivalries of their ancestors through spoken tradition and occasional road-wrecking displays of force. Think about how your actions will reflect upon your clan and greater dynasty. Are you willing to make a sacrifice that would empower your descendants if it gives a storied enemy dynasty an upper hand today? Are you venturing solo because you lack the full support of your clan or are you their spearhead towards reclaimed greatness?

### Kaijuborn Naming Conventions

Within Kaijuborn clans, the name of a dynasty is placed like a mantle onto the acting clan head in lieu of a family name. Other members of the same clan attribute a hyphenated numerical distinction to their name to denote how many kin lie between them and the clan leader, however this secondary name is often excluded in mixed company to avoid confusion. Kaijuborn first names tend to be physical descriptions of size, power, or other such characteristics.

Kaijuborn names include Earth Gojira, Spikebound third-Anguirus, Longfang eleventh-Barugon, Everbud sixth-Biollante, Behemoth second-Gojira, Wall Rumbling third-Ghidorah, Lover tenth-Mothra, etc.

# Kaijuborn Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1, or increase three different scores by 1.
- **Age.** Kaijuborn reach maturity at 18. While some Kaijuborn have been rumored to survive well into their third century, most pass on sometime before their second. The oldest possible age for Kaijuborn is yet unknown.
- **Size.** Your size is Medium. Kaijuborn are usually well over 7 feet in height and usually top out at 10 feet, although some rare exceptions exist. They weigh anything from 250 to 650+ pounds.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Strange Beast.** For the purposes of observation techs and creature type detection abilities, you read as kaiju in addition to humanoid.
- **Massive Build.** You count as one size larger for the purposes of grappling and shoving, as well as when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.
- **Monstrous Proof.** When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of your attacks with a powerful emission of destructive energy, the shape of which is determined by your Kaiju Dynasty. Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier). On a failed save, the creature takes 1d10 damage of the type associated with your Kaiju Dynasty. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage.
  - This damage increases by 1d10 when you reach 5th level (2d10), 11th level (3d10), and 17th level (4d10). You can use your Monstrous Proof a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

## Kaijuborn Dynasties

### Anguirus

Rumor places Anguirus Kaijuborn as the second variety to ever appear, immediately after the king of monsters variety. While the clans of today tell tale of a fierce, near-lethal rivalry between the Gojira and the ankylosaurus-like Kaijuborn, today the two clans are the most close-knit of any other pairing, their long fights having sparked a deep respect and understanding. While this challenging nature sometimes puts an Anguirus Kaijuborn on the wrong side of a misunderstanding, a stubbornness the Baragora Kaijuborn can sympathize with, it makes them trustworthy friends, relentless fighters, and stalwart defenders.

- **Ultrasonic Roar.** Your Monstrous Proof is a 10-foot radius around you and deals thunder damage.
- **Rotating Disc.** Once per turn, as part of an Attack action, you can roll 5 feet in a direction and attempt to hit a creature with a mighty slam. Make an unarmed attack roll. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 + your Strength modifier piercing damage, and you gain temporary hit points (minimum of 1) equal to your Constitution modifier. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
- **Spiked Carapace.** You can curl up into your spined hide as an action. Until you emerge, you gain a +4 bonus to your AC, and you have advantage on Strength and Constitution saving throws. While in your shell, you are prone, your speed is 0 and can't increase, you have disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws, you can't take reactions, and the only actions you can take are the bonus action to uncurl or the Attack action, with each attack being replaced with your Rotating Disc attack, which do not suffer disadvantage from the prone condition. In this way, you can use the Rotating Disc attack multiple times a turn, but still expend uses of it as normal.

### Baragora

The life of a Baragora Kaijuborn is oft a lonely one when compared to many other Kaijuborn, every clan being able to site some rumored or ancient instance of the Baragora dynasty initiating some fight with another or standing idle when they could have formed an alliance in action by offering aid, having most spurned the Gojira dynasty. There is reason to the isolationistic behavior though, ancient Baragora having taken their role as protector and guardian most literally, to the exclusion of other bonds, leaving the modern subterranean reptile Kaijuborn a people dedicated to their homes, if nothing else.

- **Heat Ray.** Your Monstrous Proof is a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide and deals fire damage.
- **Eruptive Burrow.** You gain a burrowing speed equal to one third of your base walking speed (rounded down). When you burrow, you leave behind a tunnel that is traversable by any creature that is your size or smaller, and that collapses behind you one minute after its creation. As an action, if you occupy a space that is no more than 20 feet below a creature, you can target it with your Monstrous Proof, creating a tunnel through the space your Monstrous Proof passes through, and causing the target to fall into a space adjacent to you on a failed save.
- **Molten Stance.** As long as you are conscious, tech or tech effects cannot move you against your will.

### Barugon

Oral history places Barugon Kaijuborn as the final dynasty to emerge from the last days of the Great Collapse. This is both a benefit and a detriment, as the chameleon-like Kaijuborn lack a great deal of the storied history that would bind them to other clans, but it also lessens the burden of expectation on their shoulders. This leads some Kaijuborn to dislike the Barugon Kaijuborn, finding them snippy, disrespectful, or icy, though conversely, some other lineages find a faster friend in them than some other dynasties, friendships to which Barugon Kaijuborn are fiercely loyal.

- **Chilling Liquid.** Your Monstrous Proof is a 10-foot radius around you and deals cold damage.
- **Extendable Tongue.** As a bonus action you may shoot your sticky tongue out, targeting a surface, object or creature within 20 feet. If the target is Small or smaller, you can make a Strength (Athletics) check to pull it to you and grapple it (automatically succeeding against objects that are not being worn or carried unless the DM sets a difficulty for a particularly complicated scenario).
  - Alternatively, if the target is Medium or larger, you can choose to be pulled to it. This automatically succeeds, but this does not grapple it, though if it is a surface or large object, you can choose to hold onto it at the point you grappled if there is something to grab onto. Attacks of opportunity generated by this movement have disadvantage.
- **Rainbow Death Ray.** As a reaction, when you are hit by a ranged attack, you can roll 1d6 and add the number rolled to your AC against the triggering attack, possibly causing it to miss as you intercept with multicolored beams. Starting at 5th level, when you use this reaction, you can choose to immediately regain your reaction for the round. You must finish a long rest before you can regain your reaction with this trait again.

### Battra

Tracing their dynasty back to a joint formation with the Mothra dynasty, Battra Kaijuborn were similarly entrusted to lead the remnants of humanity, but becoming aware of what was supposed to be their finite lineage in the world, they developed a misanthropic persona, their clan placing value on only self-interest and the sadistic glee of destruction. In rare moments, for the purpose of continuing a never-ending debate and rivalry with the Mothra dynasty, they can be convinced to aid Mothra Kaijuborn, but are otherwise considered an enemy or annoyance at best by other Kaijuborn.

- **Prism Beams.** Your Monstrous Proof is a 15-foot cone and deals lightning damage.
- **Limited Flight.** Having betrayed their own pride, Battra Kaijuborn have sought out modern genetic editing, adding bat-like bone structures to support their otherwise vestigial moth wings. You have a flying speed of 30 feet. To use this speed, you cannot be wearing medium or heavy armor. You must land at the end of each of your turns. If you do not, you begin falling slowly at a rate of 60 feet per round at the end of your turn, taking no fall damage.
- **Powder Projection.** You know the *[incendiary shot](/tech:incendiary-shot)* trick. You can cast the *[emergency barrier](/tech:emergency-barrier)* tech using this trait. Casting this tech with this trait doesn’t require material components. Once you cast the tech with this trait, you can't cast the tech with it again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast this tech using tech slots you have of the appropriate level. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your techcasting ability for these techs when you cast them with this trait (choose when you select this lineage).

### Biollante

After the success of the original Gojira Kaijuborn as a divergent of humanity, there was an attempt made to foster a plant borne of kaiju. The attempts were widely regarded as a failure, save for those samples that parasitically latched onto a Gojira Kaijuborn, forming the first of the Biollante dynasty. Despite many attempts at sacrificial reconciliation over the years, the Biollante dynastic clan has always maintained a tense relationship with that of Gojira. For the modern Biollante Kaijuborns soulful consideration of others, though, they often find friendship among the Mothra Kaijuborn.

- **Acidic Sap.** Your Monstrous Proof is a 15-foot cone and deals acid damage.
- **Limb Extension.** Your melee attacks' reach is increased by 5 feet. You suffer disadvantage to any attack you make against a target within 5 feet to you.
- **Golden Spores.** When you are reduced to a number of hit points less than half your hit point maximum, you can, as a reaction, teleport up to 10 feet to a space you can see. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest. Starting at 5th level, when you use this reaction, you can modify it in one of the following ways:
  - You can immediately expend any additional number of uses of this trait, regaining 1d6 + your Constitution modifier hit points for each use expended.
  - You can immediately expend all remaining uses of this trait. For a number of rounds equal to the number of uses expended, you deal an additional 1d4 acid damage with all your attacks and damaging techs.

### Caesar

Following the dynasties of Anguirus, Battra, and Biollante, the dynasty of the canid-lion Kaijuborn, Caesar, were introduced as something of a counter to the Gojira dynasties' mounting frustration. In the dynastic clan of Caesar Kaijuborn was found many a wise soul, benevolent music lovers, and surprisingly agile trainers. The Caesar have never forgiven the Battra for being unable to turn over a new leaf and forge new bonds like the Anguirus did. Despite the mystical mediator trope foisted onto Caesar Kaijuborn, their modern counterparts are more than happy to waste a day away on a comfortable rock catching desert rays.

- **Prism Eye.** Your Monstrous Proof is a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide and deals neon damage.
- **Flying Kick.** As a bonus action, immediately before you take the Attack action on your turn, you can choose a target that is within three times your jumping distance. Make a Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check against the target's AC. If you succeed, you jump into a spot adjacent to the target and have advantage on your first attack against them. If you fail, you only clear half the distance, taking no fall damage if you fall as a result.
- **Tackle Break.** The clawed tips of your fingers are natural weapons, which you can use to make precise strikes. Your unarmed strikes deal piercing damage equal to 1d4 + your Dexterity modifier and have the finesse modifier.

### Ghidorah

In the time of the Great Collapse, unlike Battra who grew misanthropic but still supposedly participated in the Kaijuborn's role as protector to some degree, upon learning of the planned short-lived nature of the Kaijuborn, the original of the Ghidorah dynasty turned full against the other Kaijuborn and their wards as a subjugator. While many today of the Ghidorah dynastic clan lack the exceedingly rare mutation that granted the founder of the mantle such an advantage in multiple heads and fully formed minds, they still tend to possess bifurcated or trifurcated cerebral growths, leading to superiority complexes and paranoia that make them a point of ire for all other Kaijuborn.

- **Gravity Beam.** Your Monstrous Proof is a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide and deals kinetic damage.
- **Void Dragon.** You do not need to breathe, and you can speak telecommunicatively to any creature you can see, provided the creature is within a number of feet of you equal to 10 times your level. The creature can respond in kind as long as it is capable of any manner of speech.
- **Tryannical Minds.** You know the *[hovering helping hand](/tech:hovering-helping-hand)* trick, and the hand is invisible when you cast the trick with this trait. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the *[hydraulic boost](/tech:hydraulic-boost)* tech with this trait. When you reach 5th level, you can cast the *[neurotap](/tech:neurotap)* tech with this trait. Casting these techs with this trait doesn't require material components. Once you cast either of these techs with this trait, you can’t cast that tech with it again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast these techs using tech slots you have of the appropriate level. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your techcasting ability for these techs when you cast them with this trait (choose when you select this lineage.)

### Gojira

The first and most powerful of the kaiju were a result of radiation explosively altering the DNA of the creatures that weren't outright eradicated. Finding purchase in deep sea reptilian life, the resulting genetic alterations led to a unique life form that could alter itself on the fly, which ultimately culminated in a self-sustaining organism with all the advantages of a reptile and mammal that independently mutated cellular walls and other plant-like features. In the Gojira dynasty, these features present themselves as thick, commonly dorsal, scales, thick bodies and tails, and an ever-burning atomic energy. They are the mightiest and proudest of Kaijuborn.

- **Atomic Breath.** Your Monstrous Proof is a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide and deals entropic damage.
- **Amphibious.** You can breathe air and water. You have a swimming speed of 30 feet.
- **King of the Monsters.** You are renowned for your ability to endure the worst of blows against your durable scales. Natural Biodiversity cannot inherit this trait. Your hit point maximum increases by 1, and it increases by 1 every time you gain a level. When you take damage, you can use your reaction to roll a d12 and reduce the damage by that amount. You can use the damage reduction of this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

### Hedorah

Barely even humanoid in shape, the ancestor of the Hedorah dynastic clan mutated through multiple different forms, incorporating biological divergences so quickly so as to become unrecognizable to the science of the time as anything beyond a virile pollutant of sludge. While this made no friends in the staunch and proud Gojira, Mothra, or Anguirus Kaijuborn, it did create an odd kinship with the Barugon Kaijuborn, who understood an unchosen isolation well and were no faithful fans of typical Kaijuborn manners. Modern Hedorah Kaijuborn have devolved a bit, modern drug resilience leaving their toxic nature to seep more into trashy personalities.

- **Polluting Mist.** Your Monstrous Proof is a 10-foot radius around you and deals poison damage.
- **Alien Pollution.** You have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned and you have resistance to poison damage.
- **Acidic Sludge.** As a bonus action, you can cause the poisonous sludge seeping from your very being to become caustic in nature. At the start of each of your turns while this trait is active, you deal 1d12 poison damage to any creature grappling you or any creature grappled by you. If a creature takes no poison damage from this trait, they instead take half the number you rolled (minimum 1) as acid damage.

### Mothra

Originally entrusted with leading the future of humanity along with their partner in the Battra Kaijuborn, the Mothra Kaijuborn were the most mindful and kindest caregiver of them all, regarding the lives that they were entrusted with as their very brood, even while that same brood sometimes found them difficult to understand, like a serene mystery. Along with the Caesar Kaijuborn, and the Anguirus Kaijuborn they later developed a fondness for, Mothra Kaijuborn were the most important in softening the Gojira up to the emotional needs of their wards. Modern Mothra Kaijuborn are much the same, sometimes even honorably called the Queens of Monsters.

- **Antenna Beams.** Your Monstrous Proof is a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide and deals software damage.
- **Peaceful Cries.** As a bonus action, you can sacrifice some of your health to mend another creature's injuries. You reduce your current hit points by 2d6 and one creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet of you regains a number of hit points equal to the amount you lost. If this hit point reduction reduces you to 0 hit points or less, the amount the target restores is doubled. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
- **Silken Scales.** As a reaction to an attack targeting you, you can create a 10-foot-radius sphere of dense yellow scales and pollen strung out on silk centered on yourself. The sphere spreads around corners, and its area is heavily obscured. It lasts until the start of your next turn or until a wind of moderate or greater speed (at least 10 miles per hour) disperses it. In addition, you can immediately flutter your otherwise vestigial wings to move up to half your movement speed without provoking opportunity attacks. You can use this trait once, and regain it after a short or long rest.
