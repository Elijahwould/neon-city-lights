##### Source: Player's Handbook (Geas)

*5th-level conception*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V

**Duration:** 30 days

You place a technical command on a creature that you can see within range, forcing it to carry out some service or refrain from some action or course of activity as you decide.

If the creature can understand you, it must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become charmed by you for the duration. While the creature is charmed by you, it takes 5d10 software damage each time it acts in a manner directly counter to your instructions, but no more than once each day. A creature that can’t understand you is unaffected by the tech.

You can issue any command you choose, short of an activity that would result in certain death. Should you issue a suicidal command, the tech ends. You can end the tech early by using an action to dismiss it. A *[antivirus](/tech:antivirus)*, *[primary system flush](/tech:primary-system-flush)*, or *[absolute source code manipulation](/tech:absolute-source-code-manipulation)* tech also ends it.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 7th or 8th level, the duration is 1 year. When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 9th level, the tech lasts until it is ended by one of the techs mentioned above.
