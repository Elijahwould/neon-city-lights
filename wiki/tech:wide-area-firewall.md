##### Source: Player's Handbook (Forbiddance)

*6th-level protection (script)*

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (powdered ruby worth at least $$100,000)

**Duration:** 1 day

You create a ward against magical travel that protects up to 40,000 square feet of floor space to a height of 30 feet above the floor. For the duration, creatures can’t teleport into the area or use portals, such as those created by the *[pierce the realms](/tech:pierce-the-realms)* tech, to enter the area. The tech proofs the area against all teleportation, and therefore prevents creatures from accessing the area by way of the *[net shift](/tech:net-shift)* tech.

In addition, the tech damages types of creatures that you choose when you cast it. Choose one or more of the following: AIs, surges, spam, viruses, and hacked. When a chosen creature enters the tech’s area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, the creature takes 5d10 neon or entropic damage (your choice when you cast this tech).

When you cast this tech, you can designate a password. A creature that speaks the password as it enters the area takes no damage from the tech.

This tech’s area can’t overlap with the area of another *wide area firewall* tech. If you cast *wide area firewall* every day for 30 days in the same location, the tech lasts until it is dissipated, and the material components are consumed on the last casting.
