## "Honorable men die hard, but they still die. It’s the real bastards who survive and conquer. Fighting isn’t about who’s right—it’s about who’s left."

A warlord is the ultimate survivor, a brutal warrior who relies on raw power and deadly precision to carry the day. They combine rigorous training with physical excellence to deal devastating blows. Where other ronin use fancy techniques, augment themselves with tech, dally in body modification, or rely on their allies, a warlord lets their skill speak for itself.

### Improved Critical

Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, your weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

### Improved Fighting Style

Additionally at 3rd level, your selected Ronin fighting style is further refined. You gain the following the bonus based on your Fighting Style:

- **Blind Fighting.** Your blindsight increases to a range of 15 feet.

- **Defense.** Select one damage type from the following: bludgeoning, piercing, slashing. You gain resistance to that damage type when benefiting from this fighting style.

- **Dueling.** You gain a +1 to attack rolls you make when benefiting from this fighting style.

- **Great Weapon Fighting.** You can add half of your Strength modifier (rounded down) to damage while benefiting from this fighting style.

- **Gun-Fu.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls you make when benefiting from this fighting style.

- **Interception.** You gain a second reaction which can only be used on this fighting style's reaction (allowing you to use it and one other reaction, or use it twice).

- **Mariner.** As long as you are not wearing heavy armor or using a shield, you also ignore the effects of non-technical difficult terrain.

- **Marksmanship.** You gain a +1 to damage rolls you make when benefiting from this fighting style.

- **Protection.** You gain a second reaction which can only be used on this fighting style's reaction (allowing you to use it and one other reaction, or use it twice).

- **Pugilism.** You gain a +1 to attack rolls you make when benefiting from this fighting style.

- **Riot Fighting.** You gain a second reaction which can only be used on this fighting style's reaction (allowing you to use it and one other reaction, or use it twice).

- **Thrown Weapon Fighting.** You gain a +1 to attack rolls you make when benefiting from this fighting style.

- **Two-Weapon Fighting.** Attacking with your second weapon no longer requires your bonus action, but you can make that attack only once per turn.

### Remarkable Athlete

Starting at 7th level, you gain proficiency in Athletics. If you already have proficiency in Athletics, you can add twice your Proficiency bonus to your Athletics checks.

Additionally, you gain a climbing and swimming speed equal to your walking speed, and your jumping distance is doubled.

### Superior Critical

Starting at 7th level, your weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 18-20.

### Additional Fighting Style

At 10th level, you can choose a second option from the Fighting Style class feature.

### Determination

Starting at 15th level, you can attempt to use physical prowess to break through technical effects that are otherwise physically impassable. As an action, you can make a Strength (Athletics) check against the tech save DC of the techcaster to attempt to force your way through technical barriers or effects. On a success, you can move up through the effect, spending four feet of movement for each foot moved through the effect.

### Ultimate Critical

Starting at 15th level, your weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 17-20.

### Survivor

At 18th level, you attain the pinnacle of resilience in battle. At the start of each of your turns, you regain hit points equal to 5 + your Constitution modifier if you have no more than half of your hit points left. You don’t gain this benefit if you have 0 hit points.

### Fighting Mastery

Additionally at 18th level, you gain the benefits of Improved Fighting Style for the fighting style you gain from your 10th level feature.
