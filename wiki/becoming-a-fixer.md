If you're interested in becoming a fixer, we'd love to have you! Running games can be tough work.

First of all, you should make sure you're familiar with the [rules and other general information regarding fixers](/fixerinfo). Applying is as easy as going to our [#request-support](https://ptb.discord.com/channels/931248195105869905/1150842510579605636/1151955160185196544) channel and clicking on the Fixer Application button. Once you've opened an application, you should copy the format beneath and fill out the form with the relevant information, so we have an idea of your level of experience, general availability, so on and so forth.

```
**NAME / USERNAME:** You don't have to give a legal name, but you should give us the name you want to be called.
**AGE:** Self-explanatory, we'd hope!
**TTRPG EXPERIENCE:** Usually in years. Tell us about all of the different games you've run!
**5E FAMILIARITY:** How much specifically have you run 5E, and how intimately familiar are you with it?
**WM/LIVING WORLD EXPERIENCE:** Have you ever played or run games in a living world, in-person or online? If so, how much? Adventurer's League and similar things work, too!
**ONLINE PBP/CHAT EXPERIENCE:** You'd be surprised how many people are way better at voice games than pseudo-play by post. Have you played or run a lot of games that are primarily done play by post or by chat?
**DM STYLE:** What kind of games do you prefer to run—social-heavy, arena-style combats, mixed, somewhat longer-form, mini-campaign deals?
**TIME AVAILABILITY:** How much time are you able to dedicate to running games on the server? We don't have hard and fast requirements, but we'd prefer Mini Fixers run their three games in ~6 weeks, and for full fixers to remain at least active enough to run one game every two to three weeks.
**ADDITIONAL COMMENTS:** Tell us more about yourself so that we can get to know you!
```
