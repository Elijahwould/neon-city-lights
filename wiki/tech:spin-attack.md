##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything + Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide (Sword Burst)

*Manifestation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self (5-foot radius)

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

With a meme-ridden cry, you cause a hardlight blade to rapidly swivel around you. Each creature within range, other than you, must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 kinetic damage.

This spell's damage increases by 1d6 when you reach 5th level (2d6), 11th level (3d6), and 17th level (4d6).
