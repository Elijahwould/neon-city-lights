## List of Gizmos

### [Anti Cloaking Emitter](/gizmo:anti-cloaking-emitter)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Lantern of Revealing)

*Gizmo, uncommon*

**Specs:** 2 lbs., $$450,000

While on, this device runs for 6 hours on 1 small battery, shedding bright light in a 30-foot radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet. [Cloaked](/tech:physical-cloaking) creatures and objects are visible as long as they are in the emitter's bright light. You can use an action to reduce the light, reducing the light to dim light in a 5-foot radius.

### [Aqua Pack](/gizmo:aqua-pack)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Cloak of the Manta Ray)

*Gizmo, uncommon*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$300,000

This trendy little backpack, featuring a wave pattern design, expands into a nanotech cloak, which can easily cover your whole body. While wearing this cloak with its hood up, you can breathe underwater, and you have a swimming speed of 60 feet. Pulling the hood up or down requires an action.

### [Autocord](/gizmo:autocord)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Rope of Climbing)

*Gizmo, uncommon*

**Specs:** 3 lbs., $$125,000

This 60-foot length of rubbery cord weighs 3 pounds and can hold up to 3,000 pounds. If you hold one end of the cord and use an action to speak the command word, the cord activates. As a bonus action, you can command the other end to move toward a destination you choose. That end moves 10 feet on your turn when you first command it and 10 feet on each of your turns until reaching its destination, up to its maximum length away, or until you tell it to stop. You can also tell the cord to fasten itself securely to an object or to unfasten itself, to knot or unknot itself, or to coil itself for carrying.

If you tell the cord to knot, large knots appear at 1- foot intervals along the cord. While knotted, the cord shortens to a 50-foot length and grants advantage on checks made to climb it.

The cord has AC 20 and 20 hit points. It regains 1 hit point every 5 minutes as long as it has at least 1 hit point. If the cord drops to 0 hit points, it is destroyed.

### [Ballistic Cloak](/gizmo:ballistic-cloak)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Cloak of Protection)

*Gizmo, uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$800,000

You gain a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws while you wear this cloak.

### [Bubble Helmet](/gizmo:bubble-helmet)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Cap of Water Breathing)

*Gizmo, uncommon*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$100,000

This item looks like a small disk of metal that sticks on the back of a wearer's neck. When the wearer submerges, a mental command causes it to unfurl a hyperglass helmet around the wearer's head. The disk recycles the wearer's exhalations and extracts oxygen from the water, ensuring a steady supply of breathable air. The helmet automatically retracts when the disc is not submerged.

### [Creepy Ass Theremin](/gizmo:creepy-ass-theremin)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Pipes of Haunting)

*Gizmo, uncommon*

**Specs:** 2 lbs., $$500,000

You must be proficient with wind instruments or electronic instruments to use this theremin. They have 3 charges. You can use an action to play them and expend 1 charge to create an eerie, ooky-spooky sort of tune. Each creature within 30 feet of you that hears you play must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of you for 1 minute. If you wish, all creatures in the area that aren't hostile toward you automatically succeed on the saving throw. A creature that fails the saving throw can repeat it at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. A creature that succeeds on its saving throw is immune to the effect of this theremin for 24 hours. The theremin regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

If you have proficiency with a given musical instrument, you can add your proficiency bonus to any ability checks you make to play music with the instrument. An influencer can use a musical instrument as a techcasting focus. Each type of musical instrument requires a separate proficiency.

### [Deceitful Masque, +1, +2, or +3](/gizmo:deceitful-masque)

*Gizmo, uncommon (+1), rare (+2), or very rare (+3), (requires installation by a pretender)*

**Specs:** .5 lbs., $$1,500,000 (uncommon), $$3,000,000 (rare), $$6,000,000 (very rare)

While wearing this masque, which comes in the style of any facial covering from goggles to scarfs, you gain a bonus to the saving throw DCs of your Nanophage features. You gain an identical bonus to tech attack rolls and the saving throw DCs of techs gained from the pretender class. The bonus is determined by the gizmo's rarity.

While wearing this masque, you gain an additional Nanophage Die which you can utilize without expending one from your class. Once this property is used, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.

### [Discreet Neuroscanner](/gizmo:discreet-neuroscanner)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Medallion of Thoughts)

*Gizmo, uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** 1 lb., $$55,000

The medallion has 3 charges. While wearing it, you can use an action and expend 1 charge to cast the *[neurotap](/tech:neurotap)* tech (save DC 13) from it. The medallion regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

### [Encrypted Neuromanipulator](/gizmo:encrypted-neuromanipulator)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Helm of Telepathy)

*Gizmo, uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$300,000

While wearing this headset, you can use an action to cast the *[neurotap](/tech:neurotap)* tech (save DC 13) from it. As long as you maintain concentration on the tech, you can use a bonus action to mentally send a message to a creature you are focused on. It can reply, using a bonus action to do so while your focus on it continues.

While focusing on a creature with *[neurotap](/tech:neurotap)*, you can use an action to cast the *[subliminal message](/tech:subliminal-message)* tech (save DC 13) from the headset on that creature. Once used, the *[subliminal message](/tech:subliminal-message)* property can't be used again until the next dawn.

### [Engrammatic Armory](/gizmo:engrammatic-armory)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Quiver of Ehlonna)

*Gizmo, uncommon*

**Specs:** 2 lbs., $$100,000

This harness has three compartments, each of which connects to a miniature digital space that allows it to hold numerous items while never weighing more than 2 pounds.

-The outer compartment can hold up to sixty rounds of ammunition, such as bullets or shotshells (or similar objects).

-The middle compartment pouch holds up to eighteen one-handed weapons such as machetes or pistols (or similar objects).

-The inner compartment holds up to six two-handed weapons, such as rifles or polearms (or similar objects).

You can draw any item the armory contains as if doing so from a regular harness or holster.

### [Engrammatic Cube](/gizmo:engrammatic-cube)

##### Source: Player's Handbook (Bag of Holding)

*Gizmo, uncommon*

**Specs:** 15 lbs., $$160,000

This metallic, yet flexible, cube contains a miniature digital space considerably larger than its outside dimensions, roughly 2 feet in diameter at the mouth and 4 feet deep. The cube can engrammatically store up to 500 pounds, not exceeding a volume of 64 cubic feet. The cube weighs 15 pounds, regardless of its contents. Retrieving an item from the cube requires an action.

If the cube is overloaded, punctured, or cut, it shatters and is destroyed, and its contents are scattered in the Digital Plane. The cube can be inverted, causing its contents spill forth, unharmed, but the cube must be put right before it can be used again. Breathing creatures inside the cube can survive up to a number of minutes equal to 10 divided by the number of creatures (minimum 1 minute), after which time they begin to suffocate.

Placing an engrammatic cube inside a digital space created by another Engrammatic Cube, or similar item instantly destroys both items and opens a gate to the Digital Plane. The gate originates where the one item was placed inside the other. Any creature within 10 feet of the gate is sucked through it to a random location on the Digital Plane. The gate then closes. The gate is one-way only and can’t be reopened.

### [Firewall Shackles](/gizmo:firewall-shackles)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Dimensional Shackles)

*Gizmo, rare*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$200,000

You can use an action to place these shackles on an incapacitated creature. The shackles adjust to fit a creature of Small to Large size. In addition to serving as mundane manacles, the shackles prevent a creature bound by them from using any method of extradimensional movement, including teleportation or travel to a different plane of existence. They don't prevent the creature from passing-through an interdimensional portal.

You and any creature you designate when you use the shackles can use an action to remove them. Once every 30 days, the bound creature can make a DC 30 Strength (Athletics) check. On a success, the creature breaks free and destroys the shackles.

### [Force Ablation Module](/gizmo:force-ablation-module)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Brooch of Shielding)

*Gizmo, uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$600,000

While wearing this brooch, you have resistance to kinetic damage, and you have immunity to damage from the *[micro seeker](/tech:micro-seeker)* tech.

### [Hardlight Armament Generator HAG](/gizmo:hardlight-armament-generator)

##### Source: Neon City Nights (Yash)

*Gizmo, common*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$200,000

This pair of sleek fingerless gloves come with integrated, programmable hardlight projectors.

By spending 1 minute, you may create a bond with a weapon with the Thrown property you are holding, which is then consumed and held in an internal stasis until you choose to end the bond. While using these gloves, you may create a projection of the consumed weapon when you take the attack action.

These projections gain the Returning feature (as seen in the Mechanist's widgets), disappearing immediately after being used to make a ranged attack and reappearing in your hand.

### [HeadCamo](/gizmo:headcamo)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Hat of Disguise)

*Gizmo, uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$145,000

While wearing this hat, you can use an action to cast the *[palette swap](/tech:palette-swap)* tech from it at will. The tech ends if the hat is removed.

### [Heatsink Sandals](/gizmo:heatsink-sandals)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Boots of the Winterlands)

*Gizmo, uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$600,000

These airy sandals are light and cool to the touch. While you wear them, you gain the following benefits:

-You have resistance to fire damage.

-You ignore difficult terrain created by sand. You can tolerate temperatures as high as 80 degrees Fahrenheit without any additional protection. If you wear light clothes, you can tolerate temperatures as high as 160 degrees Fahrenheit.

### [Hovercalves](/gizmo:hovercalves)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Winged Boots)

*Gizmo, uncommon*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$1,600,000

These anti-grav pods hook around your calves, allowing you to fly at a speed equal to your walking speed. The batteries in the hovercalves are good for up to 4 hours, all at once or in several shorter flights, each using a minimum of 1 minute from the duration. If you are flying when the duration expires, you descend at a rate of 30 feet per round until you land.

The hovercalves' batteries recharge 2 hours of flying capability for every 12 hours they aren't in use.

### [Hoversoles](/gizmo:hoversoles)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Boots of Levitation)

*Gizmo, rare (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$400,000

While you wear these boots, you can use an action to cast the *[boost bots](/tech:boost-bots)* tech on yourself at will.

### [Hydraulic Arm-Braces](/gizmo:hydraulic-arm-braces)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Gauntlets of Ogre Power)

*Gizmo, uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$450,000

Your Strength score is 19 while you wear these gauntlets. They have no effect on you if your Strength is 19 or higher without them.

### [Interception Gloves](/gizmo:interception-gloves)

##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Gloves of Missile Snaring)

*Gizmo, uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$150,000

These gloves seem to almost meld into your hands when you don them. When a ranged weapon attack hits you while you're wearing them, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage by 1d10 + your Dexterity modifier, provided that you have a free hand. If you reduce the damage to 0, you can catch the projectile if it is small enough for you to hold in that hand.
