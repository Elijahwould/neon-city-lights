##### Source: Player's Handbook (Mage Slayer)

You have practised techniques in melee combat against techcasters, gaining the following benefits:

- When a creature within 5 feet of you casts a tech, you can use your reaction to make a melee weapon attack against that creature.
- When you damage a creature that is concentrating on a tech, that creature has disadvantage on the saving throw it makes to maintain its concentration.
- You have advantage on saving throws against techs cast by creatures within 5 feet of you.
