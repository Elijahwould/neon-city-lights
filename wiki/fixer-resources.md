Below are a bunch of things that we think would be useful to fixers who would like to run jobs.

# Useful Links

## Virtual Tabletops

- **[Tableplop.](https://new.tableplop.com/)** Tableplop is a free virtual tabletop that you can use to quickly put down maps for your players. You can invite players to it to play directly or stream the view for other players while running the game in Discord text.
- **[Owlbear Rodeo.](https://www.owlbear.rodeo/)** Owlbear Rodeo is another a free virtual tabletop that you can use to quickly put down maps for your players. Some people might have better ways to differentiate between it and Tableplop, but we recommend you use what you like best. (Celica likes Tableplop more!)
- **[Roll20.](https://roll20.net/)** You know it, you love it: Roll20 is another free virtual tabletop, one that many people are already familiar with.

## DM Advice

- **[On Balancing Encounters.](https://slyflourish.com/the_lazy_encounter_benchmark.html)** Having trouble with encounter balance? Use this page for help.
- **[Scripting the Game.](https://rtalsoriangames.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/RTG-ScriptingtheGamev1.2.pdf)** A good way to help you effectively creative a game's narrative structure when planning to run one.
- **[The Ultimate Guide to 5 Room Dungeons.](https://www.roleplayingtips.com/5-room-dungeons/)** Having trouble with pacing and planning in your game? Introduce yourself to the concept of the 5 Room Dungeon!

## Tools

- **[Discord Timestamps.](https://r.3v.fi/discord-timestamps/)** A way for you to create Discord timestamps that automatically translate to the local time for other people when they see it in Discord.
- **[Hammertime.](https://hammertime.cyou/)** Like Discord Timestamps, another way to create Discord timestamps that automatically translate to local times. This one seems easier to use now that the UI got updated!
- **[Donjon.](https://donjon.bin.sh/)** A suite of tools for Dungeon Masters. Generate random names, dungeons, treasure, towns, cities, worlds, etc.! It also helps you calculate encounter size, has a useful initiative tracker, and more.
- **[5ETools.](https://5e.tools/index.html)** A suite of tools for D&D5E. A great place to look for inspiration for monsters, or to find the original text of things.
- **[D&D 5th Edition Community Wiki.](http://dnd5e.wikidot.com/)** The original and most useful 5E Wikidot. Great for referencing original 5E things, in the event we somehow don't cover it here. We owe a lot of our layout decisions to them!
- **[Five Room Dungeon Generator.](https://perchance.org/5dungeon)** An easy way to create a general outline for a 5 Room Dungeon. It's a great way to give yourself ideas for a one-shot gig.
