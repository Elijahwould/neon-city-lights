##### Source: Player's Handbook (Conjure Elemental)

*5th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute

**Range:** 90 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (spray paint can for gas, chunk of brick for solid, magnesium powder for plasma, or a filled spray bottle for liquid)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You digistruct a surge to serve your needs. Choose an area of air, earth, fire, or water that fills a 10-foot cube within range. A surge of challenge rating 5 or lower appropriate to the area you chose appears in an unoccupied space within 10 feet of it. For example, a plasma surge emerges from a bonfire, and a solid surge rises up from the ground. The surge disappears when it drops to 0 hit points or when the tech ends.

The surge is friendly to you and your companions for the duration. Roll initiative for the surge, which has its own turns. It obeys any verbal commands that you issue to it (no action required by you). If you don’t issue any commands to the surge, it defends itself from hostile creatures but otherwise takes no actions.

If your concentration is broken, the surge doesn’t disappear. Instead, you lose control of the surge, it becomes hostile toward you and your companions, and it might attack. An uncontrolled surge can’t be dismissed by you, and it disappears 1 hour after you summoned it. The fixer has the surge's statistics.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 6th level or higher, the challenge rating increases by 1 for each slot level above 5th.
