##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

*Altercation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 5 feet

**Components:** V, M (a glass tube of argon and mercury)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You spit such a hot take you ignite the fluoresence of a broken light bulb. Each creature of your choice that you can see within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 1d6 neon damage.

The tech's damage increases by 1d6 when you reach 5th level (2d6), 11th level (3d6), and 17th level (4d6).
