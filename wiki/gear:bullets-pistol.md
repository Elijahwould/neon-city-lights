A bullet is a kinetic projectile, a component of firearm ammunition that is shot from a gun barrel. These are primarily made of steel and lead. Pistol rounds are a lower caliber, usually, such as 9mm or .380. They also generally contain less powder. This is a pack of 20 non-technical bullets.
