*Medium armor, 4 lb., AC = 15 + Dex modifier (max 2), disadvantage on stealth checks*

Building on the concepts put forth by newtonium armor, composite armor aims to do the same but starting from a nanoprene base instead of a synthweave one. A skin-tight nanoprene suit is fitted with additional ceramiron plates to protect vital areas, and while it is still just as mobile as a standard suit of newtonium or nanoprene might be, the nanoprene base makes it just as loud as base nanoprene, making it more of a good option for people who need the protection more than they need the ability to skulk around.
