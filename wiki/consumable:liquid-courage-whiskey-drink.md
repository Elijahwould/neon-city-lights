*Consumable (soda), rare*

Liquid Courage™ was one of the purview distilleries before Rhyton presented breweries their proper competition. Now, it's a legacy name nosing its way into the soda industry. Lightly carbonated, and too low proof to legally qualify as liquor, the shockingly blue Liquid Courage™ Whiskey Drink is a dry affair, sweet, with an aftertaste of tart fruit and a tinge of scotch. It's frequently used as a mixer for a taste of whiskey in virgin drinks.

It's difficult to tell if the whiskey's smoky taste is a quality of the drink itself or a result of the way it seems to boil in its decorative glass.

You gain 10 temporary hit points and fall under the effect of the *[buff](/tech:buff)* tech (no concentration) for 1 hour after drinking this Whiskey.
