*Vehicle, civilian*

It's a Motorhome, bro.

*Motorhome · *Gargantuan vehicle (heavy transport) (10,000 lb, 2x6 squares)* · **Creature Capacity** Up to 10 Medium creatures · **Cargo Capacity** 3,000 lbs. · **Armor Class** 18 · **Hit Points** 200 (damage threshold 10, mishap threshold 25) · **Speed** 80 ft. · STR — DEX — CON — INT — WIS — CHA · 20 (+5) — 8 (-1) — 20 (+5) — 0 (—) — 0 (—) — 0 (—) · **Saving Throws** (—) · **Damage Immunities** poison, software · **Condition Immunities** blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, stunned, unconscious · ***Hit and Run.*** A motorhome can move through the space of any Medium or smaller creature. When it does, the creature must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or take 21 (4d10) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone. If the creature was already prone, it takes an extra 21 (4d10) bludgeoning damage. This trait can’t be used against a particular creature more than once each turn. · ***Prone Deficiency.*** If a vehicle rolls over and falls prone, it can’t right itself and is incapacitated until flipped upright. · ***Creature Comforts.*** A motorhome has many features within it that allow for many to comfortably reside inside of it. Any creature can take a short or long rest inside a motorhome. Additionally, due to onboard batteries, any creature that requires UV either for power or photosynthesis can receive the benefits of UV power while taking said long rest due to installed UV lamps.*
