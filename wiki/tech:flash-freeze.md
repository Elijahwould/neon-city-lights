##### Source: Fizban's Treasury of Dragons

*2nd-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 1 Action

**Range:** Self (30-foot cone)

**Components:** S, M (a vial of meltwater)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A burst of cold nitrocoolant emanates from you in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 3d8 cold damage and is hindered by ice formations for 1 minute, or until it or another creature within reach of it uses an action to break away the ice. A creature hindered by ice has its speed reduced to 0. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn’t hindered by ice.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 3rd level or higher, increase the cold damage by 1d8 for each slot level above 2nd.
