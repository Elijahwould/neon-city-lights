##### Source: Strixhaven: A Curriculum of Chaos (Silvery Barbs)

*1st-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 reaction, which you take when a creature you can see within 30 feet of yourself rolls an attack roll, an ability check, or a saving throw

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

When someone attempts to do something, and you think they could use some encouragement—or to get dragged in the dirt for even trying—you know just the right words to say. The triggering creature must reroll the d20 and uses the higher or lower roll (your choice).
