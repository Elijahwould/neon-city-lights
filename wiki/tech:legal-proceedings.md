##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Ceremony)

*1st-level protection (script)*

**Casting Time:** 1 hour

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M ($$2,500 credits worth of powdered silver, which the tech consumes)

**Duration:** Instantaneous (see below)

You perform one of several legal proceedings. When you cast the tech, choose one of the following ceremonies, the target of which must be within 10 feet of you throughout the casting.

***Atonement.*** You touch one willing creature whose alignment has changed, and you make a DC 20 Wisdom (Insight) check. On a successful check, you restore the target to its original alignment.

***Coming of Age.*** You touch one humanoid who is a young adult. For the next 24 hours, whenever the target makes an ability check, it can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to the ability check. A creature can benefit from this proceeding only once.

***Dedication.*** You touch one humanoid who wishes to be dedicated to your entity's service. For the next 24 hours, whenever the target makes a saving throw, it can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to the save. A creature can benefit from this proceeding only once.

***Funeral Proceeding.*** You touch one corpse, and for the next 7 days, the target can’t become hacked by any means short of a Wish tech.

***Investiture.*** You touch one willing humanoid. Choose one 1st-level tech you have prepared and expend a tech slot and any material components as if you were casting that tech. The tech has no effect. Instead, the target can cast this tech once without having to expend a tech slot or use material components. If the target doesn’t cast the tech within 1 hour, the invested tech is lost.

***Wedding.*** You touch adult humanoids willing to be bonded together in marriage. For the next 7 days, each target gains a +2 bonus to AC while they are within 30 feet of each other. A creature can benefit from this proceeding again only if widowed.
