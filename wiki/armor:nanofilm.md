*Light armor, 1 lb., AC = 11 + Dex modifier*

Nanofilm armor tends to be skin-tight and made of high-end materials. It offers the bare minimum protection, but it is quiet and form-fitting—and a lot cheaper than synthweave, even if it's a little heavier than micromesh.
