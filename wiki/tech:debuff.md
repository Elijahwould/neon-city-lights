##### Source: Player's Handbook (Bane)

*1st-level conception*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of blood)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Up to three creatures of your choice that you can see within range must make Charisma saving throws. Whenever a target that fails this saving throw makes an attack roll or a saving throw before the Tech ends, the target must roll a d4 and subtract the number rolled from the attack roll or saving throw.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this Tech using a Tech slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st.
