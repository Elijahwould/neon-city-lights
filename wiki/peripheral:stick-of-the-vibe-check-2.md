##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Rod of the Pact Keeper +2)

*Peripheral, very rare (requires installation by a [streamer](/streamer))*

**Specs:** 1 lb., $$3,000,000

While holding this rod, you gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls and to the saving throw DCs of your streamer techs.

In addition, you can regain one streamer spell slot as an action while holding the rod. You can't use this property again until you finish a long rest.
