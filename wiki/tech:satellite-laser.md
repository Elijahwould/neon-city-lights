##### Source: Player's Handbook (Flame Strike)

*5th-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (pinch of sulfur or a can of aerosol spray, a lighter, and a sizable satellite figurine)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A vertical column of a very literal orbital laser roars down from the stars to a location you specify. Each creature in a 10-foot radius, 40-foot-high cylinder centered on a point within range must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 4d6 fire damage and 4d6 neon damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 6th level or higher, the fire damage or the radiant damage (your choice) increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 5th.
