**Name.** Camilla Holstein Alecto

**Lineage.** Splicer (cow) Eldritch Abomination

**Age.** 28

**Height and Weight.** 6'1", unknown

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Classes/Subclasses.** Influencer (Channel of the Tech Geek) 14, Ordinator (Industry Synergy) 6

**Features.** Bountiful chest, digitigrade legs with hooves, a tanned complexion complimented by chocolate-colored spots all around, pink-purple, friendly glowing eyes

**Status.** Dead

Camilla, or Cammy, was a simple girl from simple origins. The Undercity had that effect on everyone: you either escaped or survived. And escape often meant selling just enough of your soul to make it. For Cammy, it was Big Beef and the life of corporate sponsored splicing campaigns that saw her become one of many poster children for the company. Who else could one trust if not for someone that looked almost exactly like the CEO, bovine, bountiful, and cheery? Even that, however, led her down some interesting paths in life, particularly her life outside of the company once she'd paid off her sponsorship debt through streaming.

Her passion for the Old World, its relics, and the things one can do with them was the shining star to her universe. For her, it was everything. Making do with what little you have was something her father would espouse daily through the frugal means of their day-to-day living. And she sought to perform her running as a means of finding and exploring the New World, and everything it could contain. For her audience, naturally. Ultimately, she was adored by many for her impossibly endless mirth and innocence.

That innocence would find its end at the bottom of the ocean. A dark place saw her changed. A change that pushed her into a sudden paradigm shift. She appeared tired, pallid, her mind slowly twisting as her thoughts were interrupted and flooded. Her dreams were filled with the horrors of what she witnessed.

Then, one day, she simply vanished. Her streams stopped. Her friends could not reach her.

Some think she found her fate at the end of a gun in a gig. Maybe she simply had a change of heart? Perhaps Big Beef came back to collect after so much time had passed? Rumors abound that she walked to the shoreline and kept going and some believe she still walks amongst the populace, hooded and incognito.

Whatever the case may be, Camilla Holstein is dead.

Long live the cow.
