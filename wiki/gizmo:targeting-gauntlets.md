##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Bracers of Archery)

*Gizmo, uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$150,000

While wearing these bracers, you have proficiency with the hunting bow and hunting rifle, and you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls on ranged attacks made with such weapons.
