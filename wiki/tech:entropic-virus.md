##### Source: Player's Handbook (Chill Touch)

*Biochemistry trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** 1 round

You unleash a disintegrating hardlight virus in the space of a creature within range. Make a ranged tech attack against the creature to assail it with vicious hardlight microshards. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 entropic damage, and it can't regain hit points until the start of your next turn. Until then, the virus clings to the target.

If you hit a hacked target, it also has disadvantage on attack rolls against you until the end of your next turn.

This tech's damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).
