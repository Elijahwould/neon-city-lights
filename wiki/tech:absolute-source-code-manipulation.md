##### Source: Player's Handbook (Wish)

*9th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Absolute Source Code Manipulation—A.S.C.M.—is the mightiest tech a runner can cast. By simply speaking aloud, you can alter the very foundations of reality in accord with your desires, altering the source code of reality at a fundamental level.

**Basic Access.** The basic use of this tech is to duplicate any other tech of 8th level or lower. You don’t need to meet any requirements in that tech, including costly components. The tech simply takes effect.

**Admin Access.** Alternatively, you can create one of the following effects of your choice:

- You create one object of up to $$2,500,000 credits in value that isn’t a high-end item. The object can be no more than 300 feet in any dimension, and it appears in an unoccupied space you can see on the ground.
  - Any item created in this way cannot be sold on the grey market.
- You allow up to twenty creatures that you can see to regain all hit points, and you end all effects on them described in the greater restoration tech.
- You grant up to ten creatures that you can see resistance to a damage type you choose.
  - A creature can only benefit from one variation of this effect at a time. If a creature were to be granted a new resistance through this method, the creature chooses which resistance to keep; the other is immediately discarded.
- You grant up to ten creatures you can see immunity to a single tech or other technical effect for 8 hours. For instance, you could make yourself and all your companions immune to a technolich’s life drain attack.
- You undo a single recent event by forcing a reroll of any roll made within the last round (including your last turn). Reality reshapes itself to accommodate the new result. For example, a usage of ASCM could undo an opponent’s successful save, a foe’s critical hit, or a friend’s failed save. You can force the reroll to be made with advantage or disadvantage, and you can choose whether to use the reroll or the original roll.

**Full Root Access.** You might be able to achieve something beyond the scope of the above examples by inputting a command directly into the source itself.

State your command to the DM as precisely as possible. The DM has great latitude in ruling what occurs in such an instance; the greater the stated command, the greater the likelihood that something goes wrong. This tech might simply fail, the effect you desire might only be partly achieved, or you might suffer some unforeseen consequence as a result of how you worded your command. For example, editing the source code such that a villain is dead might propel you forward in time to a period when that villain is no longer alive, effectively removing you from the game. Similarly, issuing a command for an incredibly powerful item or artifact might instantly transport you to the presence of the item's current owner.

The stress of casting this tech via admin or full root access weakens you. After enduring that stress, each time you cast a tech until you finish a long rest, you take 1d10 entropic damage per level of that tech. This damage can’t be reduced or prevented in any way. In addition, your Strength drops to 3, if it isn’t 3 or lower already, for 2d4 days. For each of those days that you spend resting and doing nothing more than light activity, your remaining recovery time decreases by 2 days.

Finally, there is a 33 percent chance that you are locked out from casting ASCM if you suffer this stress. In such a case, it is possible to undergo Source Manipulation Therapy by spending Downtime to allow oneself to recover their ability to access the tech again.
