**Corporation Name.** Trinity Television Broadcasting & Streaming Inc.

**CEO.** Abigail "Abby" Aberdeen

**Years in business.** ~120

**Industry.** Broadcasting, Television, Streaming

**Motto.** *"The Trifecta of Entertainment: News, Talk, and Gossip!"*

**Impact.** Moderate

They primarily concern themselves with things that will gain the most views, most clicks, most eyeballs on their work. News and athlete-related events are easy buy-ins, especially those surrounding the stars of Foto Finish and amateurs alike, but gossip and rumor-milling is lucrative, and they will roll with this.

Most of the news they offer are in small blurbs that involve interviewing specific faces and newly designed products that a corpo will send in a sponsorship to get the word out and perform "live fire" demonstrations. This can range from your typical hand-held devices from NANiTE to exotic inventions from a start-up that are "guaranteed to make your night" to straight up firearms. Definitely the "turn your brain off" type of thing.

Their CEO, Abigail "Abby" Aberdeen, is a splicer with a particularly catchy name and a catchier figure. With the fall of Bliss Co., she jests about being the next best thing to take the crown—ajoke that some consider a serious bid.
