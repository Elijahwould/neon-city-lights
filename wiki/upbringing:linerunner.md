##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings)

You’re employed to keep the power running, climbing up the sides of mountainous megacity spires to repair and maintain the lines and wires running from their solar banks and batteries to every habi-cube, boardroom, office and storefront in your home skyscraper. But since you’re used to the constant threat of danger, you might as well get paid better.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Acrobatics, Technology
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Electrician's tools, Trailblazer's kit
- **Equipment.** Linerunners start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A set of climbing hooks
  - Rappel gear
  - 100 ft of rope
  - A work polo shirt
  - A set of electrician's tools
  - A credchip worth $$1,500

### Feature: Hear the Singing in the Wires

Electric power has its own music. The sixty-cycle hum is the part most people hear, but there are overtones and rhythms that only someone who has worked with them can discern. You are one of them. Not only can you hear this music, but you can draw meaning from it.

If you can get within ten feet of a generator or transmission line that feeds power to a building, fortification, or complex, you can learn about one piece of major high-tech equipment that is powered by that system, and its approximate location within the building, as if the *[object sensor](/tech:object-sensor)* tech had been cast. As long as you stay within ten feet of the powerlines running through the building, you can continue the effect.

Some examples of high-tech equipment that can be located using this feature: clone vats, sense-tanks, cryopods, teleporters, security systems, etc.
