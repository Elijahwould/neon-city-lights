##### Source: Player's Handbook (Scorching Ray)

*2nd-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create three rays of fire and hurl them at targets within range. You can hurl them at one target or several. Make a ranged tech attack for each ray. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 fire damage.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 3rd level or higher, you create one additional ray for each slot level above 2nd.
