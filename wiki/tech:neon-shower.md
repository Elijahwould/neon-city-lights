##### Source: Player's Handbook (Sacred Flame)

*Altercation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Neon light bursts around a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d8 neon damage. The target gains no benefit from cover for this saving throw.

The tech's damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).
