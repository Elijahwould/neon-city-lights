**Name.** Lieutenant Harley "Howitzer" Harrison

**Lineage.** Remake

**Age.** Unknown

**Height and Weight.** 6'3", 470 lbs (8'9", 625 lbs when in artillery)

**Pronouns.** they/them

**Affiliation.** [The Jingos](/org:jingos)

**Features.** A modified heavy loader chassis bristling with high-end military tech; wields a modified 75mm cannon, short temper

**Status.** Alive

Referred to affectionately as ‘Howie’ by close associates and second in command; Harley is in charge of operations that occur outside the city limits (most often due to their weapon of choice posing severe collateral damage risks). Much like their leader, Harley is a veteran of the corporate wars with a specialty in heavy weaponry and explosives.

Quick to anger and quicker on the draw, Harley is very much a shoot first, ask questions later type of individual.
