##### Source: Player's Handbook (Longstrider)

*1st-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of dirt)

**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a creature. The target's speed increases by 10 feet until the tech ends.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st.
