##### Source: Player's Handbook (Cloud of Daggers)

*2nd-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a knife and a stopwatch)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You fill the air with hardlight spinning shivs in a cube 5 feet on each side, centered on a point you choose within range. A creature takes 4d4 slashing damage when it enters the tech’s area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 2d4 for each slot level above 2nd.
