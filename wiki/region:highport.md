Further up the Rum Veins is the quaint and overly secured town of Highport. Where Gobbo Beach is the vacation destination of the masses, Highport is for the upper classes. A place where the streets are clean, the restaurants are many and ALL the food is real—none of this food is from genetically engineered, spliced origins.

Ranches and plantations with more turrets than a Maximum-security prison provide all that is needed to wine and dine any CEO or executive. Stretching views of the carefully manicured countryside are to be seen from the manor windows. For the spicier clientele, an overcharged monowire electric fence where they can gentlemanly bet watching mutants and scavengers fry.

Unlike Gobbo Beach down the river, business doesn't really slow down in Highport, as much effort is made by the corps to keep it quite cozy year-round. In addition, they provide year-round boat tours of the Rum Veins all the way up into the majesty of the leaking vale… which has hidden turrets everywhere, and speakers broadcasting chirps in place of the birds that were wiped out by said turrets.

The brochure doesn’t mention the occasional suited body floating downriver, or all the espionage and kidnappings that happen behind the scenes.
