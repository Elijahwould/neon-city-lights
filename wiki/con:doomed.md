You've run afoul of the wrong CEO, an esoteric ~ATH code, or even your own body. You aren't long for this world.

- A doomed creature dies after 13 (2d12) hours have passed after gaining the condition, or at a greater amount of time as determined by the fixer.
- A doomed creature continues to be doomed even after it dies. Effects equivalent to a 7th-level or higher tech can remove the doomed condition (such as *[repair](/tech:repair)* cast on a living creature, *[remodel](/tech:remodel)*, *[total recreation](/tech:total-recreation)*, or *[absolute source code manipulation](/tech:absolute-source-code-manipulation)*).
