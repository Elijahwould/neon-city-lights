##### Source: Player's Handbook (Hellish Rebuke)

*1st-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 1 reaction, which you take when you are damaged by a creature within 60 feet of you that you can see

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You point your finger, and the creature that damaged you is momentarily surrounded by hellish flames. The creature must make a Dexterity saving throw. It takes 2d10 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this Tech using a Tech slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 1st.
