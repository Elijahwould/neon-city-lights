Source: Player's Handbook (Bestow Curse)

*3rd-level biochemistry*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch a creature, and that creature must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become infected for the duration of the tech. When you cast this tech, choose the nature of the virus from the following options:

- Choose one ability score. While infected, the target has disadvantage on ability checks and saving throws made with that ability score.
- While infected, the target has disadvantage on attack rolls against you.
- While infected, the target must make a Wisdom saving throw at the start of each of its turns. If it fails, it wastes its action that turn doing nothing.
- While the target is infected, your attacks and techs deal an extra 1d8 necrotic damage to the target.

A *[antivirus](/tech:antivirus)* tech ends this effect. At the DM's option, you may choose an alternative virus effect, but it should be no more powerful than those described above. The DM has final say on such a virus's effect.

***At Higher Levels.*** If you cast this tech using a tech slot of 4th level or higher, the duration is concentration, up to 10 minutes. If you use a tech slot of 5th level or higher, the duration is 8 hours. If you use a tech slot of 7th level or higher, the duration is 24 hours. If you use a 9th level tech slot, the tech lasts until it is dissipated. Using a tech slot of 5th level or higher grants a duration that doesn't require concentration.
