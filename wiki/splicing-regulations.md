As we all know, splicing is one of the newer and more dangerous advancements in the world, allowing people to potentially become their fursona or emulate certain animalistic traits.

Along with this advancement also came the discovery that some DNA is more compatible with human DNA then others, often allowing for a full conversion of a human into something new. With that discovery, however, came the realization that some DNA is outright dangerous. Because of this, there are regulations that splicer surgeons follow when splicing an individual—specifically regarding what they can add and change.

The following is a list in descending order of how extreme splicing can get before it becomes fairly dangerous:

- **Primates.** Full conversions are possible, though these are hardly splicers anymore (see: [the Wukong](/race:wukong))
- **Non-Primate Mammals.** Heavy splicing
- **Birds and Reptiles.** Middling splicing
- **Insects and Fish.** Minor splicing

Of course, outliers exist on both ends of the spectrum. Many either go through extreme splicing levels with any animal, or none at all. Unfortunately, most surgeons will ignore these guidelines for any number of reasons.
