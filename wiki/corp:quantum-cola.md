**Corporation Name.** Quantum Cola Co.

**CEO.** The Dude

**Years in business.** ~98

**Industry.** Consumer-grade and high-end electronics

**Motto.** *"With or without: always refreshing."*

**Impact.** Minor

Quantum Cola is a company that came into existence after a factory from ages past was discovered by explorers. Upon accidentally turning the place on, they were met by a dormant VI, calling itself "The Dude".

As soon as the VI came online, it reopened several facilities around the area that would eventually become Neon City and begun production of Quantum Cola's many brands of beverages.

There are whisperings of experimentation on workers, but these days that's par for the course for any corporation.
