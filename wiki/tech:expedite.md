*6th-level observation*

**Casting Time:** 1 reaction, which you take when a creature within range casts a tech with additional effects when casted over consecutive days

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

This tech allows you to impart onto another, or yourself, the exact workings of the local digital realms in relation to the near future of the triggering tech. Such information allows the initial casting to be altered in line with these algorithmic predictions of the future to bring the process about more efficiently. The triggering tech is considered to have been cast a number of days equal to your techcasting ability modifier in addition to today's triggering casting. A creature can not benefit from this tech again by any means, until 24 hours have passed.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 7th or higher, the number of days added to the long term casting of the triggering tech is increased by your techcasting ability modifier an additional time for each level about 6th.
