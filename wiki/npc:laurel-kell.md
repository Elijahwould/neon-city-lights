**Name.** Dr. Laurel Kell

**Lineage.** Executive Elf

**Age.** 286

**Height and Weight.** 6'0", 210 lbs

**Pronouns.** he/him

**Affiliation.** [Armata Solutions](/corp:armata)

**Features.** Jet black hair and crystal blue eyes

**Status.** Active

When looking at Dr. Kell, many have described him as having an aura of charisma. He carries himself with confidence, but not with a swagger. Adding to this is a perpetual knowing smirk backed by piercing blue eyes. All in all, he is one of the best in his business, but also in appearance… if you ask him, of course.

Doctor Laurel Kell is a respected name in the sphere of corporate security psychology—a rising star in the field. With awards for going above and beyond while in the field, it's no wonder he's made it to the top of the food chain in one of the many departments that focus on corporate force protection, though he does not always stay in his lane…
