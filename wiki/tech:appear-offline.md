##### Source: Player's Handbook (Mind Blank)

*8th-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** 24 hours

Until the tech ends, one willing creature you touch is immune to software damage, any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, observation techs, and the charmed condition. The tech even foils the *[absolute source code manipulation](/tech:absolute-source-code-manipulation)* tech and techs or effects of similar power used to affect the target's mind, or to gain information about the target.
