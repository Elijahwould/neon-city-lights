You are among the "elite" within the world of fast-food chains, one of the few thousand that manually flipped those synthetic protein slabs in the kitchens. Whether by corporate laziness or simply your flawless ability as head chef, you've worked in terrible conditions and served SCOP you wouldn't even give to your landlord (or maybe you would). But, hey! Anything is better than a dead-ender or staying unemployed.

- **Skill Proficiencies:** Culture, Sleight of Hand
- **Tool Proficiencies:** Cook's utensils, gaming set (video game)
- **Equipment:** Burger flippers start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A set of cook's utensils
  - A set of kitchen clothes
  - A shirt with a local burger chain's logo
  - An unemployment notice
  - A credit chip worth $$500

### Feature: Fast Food Connoisseur

With your knowledge of the vilest yet nutritious pieces of synthfood, you can always feed yourself on whatever you happen to pick up in the streets. So as long as you have a set of cook's utensils on your person, anything you find is capable of becoming edible.
