##### Source: Player's Handbook, [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/the-human-race)

The last four hundred years have changed humanity. The loss of old world knowledge has made humanity curious and inquisitive, advances in technology and medicine has made them daring risk takers and Corporate overlordship has erased any notions of blind patriotism. Fear and propaganda are the primary tools keeping people working for corporations, and all it takes to tip them to a life of adventure, peril and fortune in the undercities is being in the wrong place at the right time.

### Roleplaying a Human

Unlike the photosynthetic Gobbos, the robotic Artificiates and Remakes, and the wild and rambunctious Wukong, humans greatly prefer the comfort of living indoors. As such, the primary motivation of human adventurers is accruing wealth.

Hotel bars, nightclubs, corporate bounty boards, and even odd jobs from the innumerable niche storefronts are a great way to earn some quick Creds.

### Human Naming Conventions

Humans have a wide variety of personal and family names stemming from Old World cultures becoming mish-mashed after the Great Relocation. Many humans, especially in adventuring circles, use nicknames and aliases for a number of reasons, primarily as a way of keeping their personal and professional lives separate.

For example, Scooter Fabuta, Cathenna Babineaux, Bhavika MacDonald, Diego Koroveshi, Mercedes Rojas, Kitty Kitsune, Driss Rasmussen, Ajulo Ivanovic, etc.

# Human Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Two different ability scores of your choice increase by 1.
- **Age.** Humans reach adulthood in their late teens and live less than a century.
- **Size.** Your size is Medium. Humans are between 5 and 8 feet tall and vary wildly in build.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Skills.** You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.
- **Feat.** You gain one Feat of your choice.
