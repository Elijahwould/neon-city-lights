##### Source: Critical Role ([Twitter](https://twitter.com/VoiceOfOBrien/status/1233524328517586949)) (Widogast's Transmogrification)

*6th-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 hour

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a sense-tank filled, with a special nanite-infused advanced modification and splicing solution worth $$15,000, which the tech consumes)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You permanently alter the physical body of a humanoid creature into another type of humanoid body. After submerging the target in the sense-tank, you run a series of scripts and commands that manipulate the flesh and function of the target's body into that of another humanoid lineage.

They lose their previous lineage traits and instead gain the traits of another humanoid lineage of the target's choice, aside from languages known (which remain unchanged). Should the target wish to alter their gender along with or instead of their lineage, they can choose to do so as part of the tech. When the process is completed, you and the target each suffer one level of exhaustion.

Once a creature's lineage or gender has been changed via this tech, they cannot be affected again by this tech for 1 year.
