##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Primal Savagery)

*Manipulation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You sculpt a custom dose of SLUDGE into sharp claws or fangs around your hand or mouth, ready to deliver a gooey attack. Make a melee tech attack against one creature within 5 feet of you. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 acid damage. After you make the attack, your hand or mouth returns to normal.

The tech's damage increases by 1d10 when you reach 5th level (2d10), 11th level (3d10), and 17th level (4d10).
