*S* - Scripted techs can be cast without expending a tech slot by adding 10 minutes to their casting time.

*C* - Consumed materials are those that are destroyed or otherwise made inert by the casting of their tech and must be replaced.

*P* - Periphery materials are those that must be acquired separately and can not be replaced by a tech focus or component toolbox. Most typically have a price associated.
