**Name.** Captain Judas Von Heitz

**Lineage.** Human

**Age.** Unknown

**Height and Weight.** 7'9", 430 lbs

**Pronouns.** they/them

**Affiliation.** [The Jingos](/org:jingos)

**Features.** Wears a bright yellow jacket, heavily augmented body, wields a tri barreled tomgirl in place of his right arm

**Status.** Alive

Judas is the primary point of contact for standard operations. Of the three leaders of the Jingos, Judas is the one most commonly seen in active duties. He is a softspoken man with a thinly veiled hatred towards [Armata](/corp:armata), though his reasons for it remain unknown and simply "doesn’t like 'em," as he puts it.
