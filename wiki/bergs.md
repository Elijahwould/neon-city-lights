## "Neon City? Oh, no, that's just the tip of the iceberg. Literally."

Similar to icebergs that float in the ocean, these massive columns of frost and rime are an oasis in the Undercity.

People can choose to harvest the ice that forms from the nitrogen cooling systems that circulate through the column's surface in lieu of getting well water from the local stream. Some more entrepreneurial businesses can be found, such as shaved ice stands at the base or even rerouting some of the cooling systems to make “seasonal” ice rinks. One Corp in particular at the cavern town of [Glitterhaven](/sector:glitterhaven) has its frost used to make an endless Winter paradise for year-round merriment and profit upon the entertainment hosted within its Wonderland.
