##### Source: Player's Handbook (Creation)

*5th-level deception*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute

**Range:** 30 ft

**Components:** V, S, M (a sample fragment of the item you plan to create)

**Duration:** Special

You coalesce and command nanites from the surrounding area to create a nonliving object of vegetable matter within range: soft goods, rope, wood, or something similar. You can also use this tech to create mineral objects such as stone, crystal, or metal. The object created must be no larger than a 5-foot cube, and the object must be of a form and material that you have seen before.

The duration depends on the object’s material. If the object is composed of multiple materials, use the shortest duration.

| Material | Duration |
| --- | --- |
| Vegetable matter | 1 day |
| Stone, crystal | 12 hours |
| Precious metals | 1 hour |
| Gems | 10 minutes |
| Expensive Alloys, Theoretical Minerals | 1 minute |

Using any material created by this tech as another tech's material component causes that tech to fail.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 6th level or higher, the cube increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 5th.
