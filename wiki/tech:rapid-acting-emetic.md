##### Source: Player's Handbook (Ray of Sickness)

*1st-level biochemistry*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A ray of sickening greenish energy lashes out toward a creature within range. Make a ranged tech attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 poison damage and must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it is also poisoned until the end of your next turn.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.
