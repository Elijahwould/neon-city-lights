##### Source: Player's Handbook (Crusader's Mantle)

*3rd-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Bright, empowering nanites swarm around you in an area with a 30-foot radius, infusing neon tech in your allies' weapons. Until the tech ends, the area moves with you, centered on you. While in the area, each non-hostile creature in the area (including you) deals an extra 1d4 neon damage when it hits with a weapon attack.
