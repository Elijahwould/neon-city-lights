##### Source: Neon City NightsThanks to lanayla#4358 for this submission!

Out in the wastes you'll occasionally stumble upon an area where the vegetation's mutated and survived due to an overabundance of shade and water, protected from the wasteland's deserts like a toxic oasis. The people that thrive here are survivalists through and through, and they protect their swamps with surveillance and trapping.

Still, we all eventually need to make our ways to the city if we want to make it in life.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Survival, Nature
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Trailblazer's kit, Cooking utensils
- **Equipment.** Swamplanders start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A trailblazer's kit
  - A set of cooking utensils
  - A fishing pole with line and hook
  - A strangely woven outfit

### Feature: What are you doing in my swamp?

You suffer no difficult terrain penalty when in Swampland. As you're so at home here, you've also learned to let out a noise that disrupts modern lighting and old to give you the chance to dissuade those from trespassing on your land, though the skill appears to still have utility when in the city.

When in an area lit by artificial sources, you can let out a tech-enhanced roar as an action that extinguishes and temporarily disrupts all lighting in a 30-foot area centered on your position. Any permanent artificial light sources of light (such as lamps, streetlights, etc.) will relight at the beginning of your next turn. They otherwise remain extinguished. You must take a short rest before you can use this feature again.
