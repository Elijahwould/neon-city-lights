##### Source: Player's Handbook (Ensnaring Strike)

*1st-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

The next time you hit a creature with a weapon attack before this tech ends, a writhing mass of thorny vines appears at the point of impact, and the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be restrained by the technical vines until the tech ends. A Large or larger creature has advantage on this saving throw. If the target succeeds on the save, the vines shrivel away.

While restrained by this tech, the target takes 1d6 piercing damage at the start of each of its turns. A creature restrained by the vines or one that can touch the creature can use its action to make a Strength check against your tech save DC. On a success, the target is freed.

***At Higher Levels.*** If you cast this tech using a tech slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st.
