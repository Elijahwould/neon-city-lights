A bullet is a kinetic projectile, a component of firearm ammunition that is shot from a gun barrel. These are primarily made of steel and lead. Rifle rounds are a higher caliber, usually, such as the still-in-use 7.62x39mm (or 7.62mm Soviet), or the 5.56x45mm NATO round. They also generally contain more powder. This is a pack of 20 non-technical bullets.
