##### Source: Player's Handbook (Swift Quiver)

*5th-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a magazine, clip, arrow or other type of non tech ammunition)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You use nanites to transmute your quiver, clip or magazine so it produces an endless supply of non technical ammunition, which seems to leap into your hand or weapon when you reach for it or load it.

On each of your turns until the tech ends, you can use a bonus action to make two attacks with a weapon that uses ammunition from the quiver, clip or magazine. Each time you make such a ranged attack, nanites in your quiver, clip or magazine automatically replace the piece of ammunition you used with a similar piece of nontechnical ammunition. Any pieces of ammunition created by this tech disintegrate when the tech ends. If the quiver, clip or magazine leaves your possession, the tech ends.
