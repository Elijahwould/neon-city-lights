Strictly speaking, there is little truly known about Vermillion Damarion (Vermil for short) beyond their work. No fingerprints, medical records, nothing. The name Vermillion is, in of itself, an alias for a long generational line of assassins trained from a young age to take up that very mantle. Because of this, Vermillion's reputation as a marksman supersedes that of their own extensive age; building upon a singular legacy of infamy as a cold calculated killer amongst a family reviled for their cruelty and inhuman apathy.

For those that know Vermillion, she is a softspoken individual, often limiting her conversation to only a few sentences at most and letting her more boisterous companions (or her rifle) do most of the talking for her.

For several decades, there was a period of inactivity as the family tore itself apart from the inside with many simply vanishing and quickly being forgotten by the city at large. It is only within recent years that they have begun to resurface upon hushed lips and with them, the Damarion's huntress. However, just who is predator and who is prey? That remains to be seen. Pray that it isn't you.
