**Corporation Name.** Bright Future, Inc.

**CEO.** Mr. Brightside

**Years in business.** ~70

**Industry.** Renewable energy, power across Neon City and nearby areas

**Motto.** *"We do everything we can to light up your Bright Future."*

**Impact.** Major

Bright Future is the company that manufactures most of the city's renewable energy sources, as well as offering replacement parts for when they break.

They were originally a subsidiary of Osaka Technologies but, after the collapse of the main building, the company broke off and under the leadership of Mr. Brightside, the man who bought the company with his own money and made it into the energy powerhouse it is today.

They had several deals with Bliss Co. for ownership of a large collection of the outer farms on the fringes of Neon City's limits. Now, without Bliss Co. in the way, they have begun to lay claim to more of the of those farms.
