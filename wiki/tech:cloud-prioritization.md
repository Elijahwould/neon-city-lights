##### Source: Player's Handbook (Wind Walk)

*6th-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (fire and water)

**Duration:** 8 hours

You and up to ten willing creatures you can see within range assume a gaseous surge for the duration, appearing as wisps of cloud. While in this cloud form, a creature has a flying speed of 300 feet and has resistance to damage from nonmagical weapons. The only actions a creature can take in this form are the Dash action or to revert to its normal form.

Reverting takes 1 minute, during which time a creature is incapacitated and can’t move. Until the tech ends, a creature can revert to surge form, which also requires the 1-minute transformation.

If a creature is in surge form and flying when the effect ends, the creature descends 60 feet per round for 1 minute until it lands, which it does safely. If it can’t land after 1 minute, the creature falls the remaining distance.
