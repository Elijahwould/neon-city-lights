## "Anywhere. Anytime."

Transmat Drivers are frighteningly efficient generalists, assigned to backtrace their marks through every channel they access, be it digital or physical, with the aid of a transmat beacon; what is essentially a high-power miniature digitization gate keyed to their person. Both the money needed to back a Transmat Driver and the scope of their hunts makes them the most likely variety of Auditor to show up on a corpo payroll.

##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Horizon Walker)

### Transmaterial Techs

Starting at 3rd level, you learn an additional tech when you reach certain levels in this class, as shown in the Transmaterial Tech table. The tech counts as an Auditor tech for you, but it doesn't count against the number of Auditor techs you know.

### GateNav

At 3rd level, you can perform an area ping with your transmat beacon to locate mysdata hotspots. As an action, you detect the distance and direction to the closest operable, full-scale digitization gate within 1 mile of you.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Upload Admin

At 3rd level, you can leave tethers of your data in other realms, the conversion of energy as they snap back to you empowering your strikes.

As a bonus action, choose one creature you can see within 30 feet of you. The next time you hit that creature on this turn with a weapon attack, all damage dealt by the attack becomes kinetic damage, and the creature takes an extra 1d8 kinetic damage from the attack. When you reach 11th level in this class, the extra damage increases to 2d8.

### Surf the Net

At 7th level, you learn to briefly step through the digital realms. As a bonus action on your turn, you can cast the *[etherealnetwork](/tech:etherealnetwork)* tech with this feature, without expending a tech slot, but the tech ends at the end of the current turn.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Download Admin

At 11th level, you gain the ability to project your mysdata from your beacon in a forward download packet. When you use the Attack action, you can transmit up to 10 feet before each attack to an unoccupied space you can see.

If you attack at least two different creatures with the action, you can make one additional attack with it against a third creature.

### Signal Interference

At 15th level, your mastery over your transmat beacon allows you to assume a state between digital and physical, interacting with inputs as you so choose to diminish their impact. When you take damage from an attack, you can use your reaction to give yourself resistance to all of that attack's damage this turn.
