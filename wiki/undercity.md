## "For every prohibition above, there will be an underground that supplies."

The Undercity is a vast tunneling system beneath the city, both connecting a few surface level hubs of activity via railway transit and serving as a whole underworld to its own. There are underground cafes, shopping malls, hot-spots, tourist traps, and even whole townships just under the streets of Neon City. Much like the city itself some may not see the surface world and thanks to the complex system of mirrors and tubes in place to funnel natural light from the surface most may never need to.

The Undercity, being host to most of the city's servers and electrical components, needs to maintain a constant cool temperature to avoid the cable and servers from degrading or for the citizenry to experience summertime temperatures year-round. As such, Atmos™ cooling systems are in place to keep areas constantly cold—with some actually icing over from lack of maintenance or idle neglect. These areas, known as bergs, tend to remain in forgotten or abandoned sections of housing and isolated caves away from the public. A select few bergs are epicenters of trade and traffic within the Undercity, with notable examples being the APEX and Bliss Co. personal storage areas as well as Puzzle Quest Inc.'s “Winter Wonderland”. It's debatable as to how accessible these specific bergs are to the public without specific corp access, but the surrounding township from the bergs more than makes up for that.

Deeper caves do exist, however, discovered by greedy corporate ventures, mammalian curiosity, the occasional recovery team for lost tech, or salvage experts looking for a big score. These areas are far within the labyrinthine tunnel systems of the depths. The lost tech and abandoned laboratories, mine shafts, housing districts, server banks and bergs are perfect breeding grounds for some of the vilest creatures imaginable: Data Miners, Hacked, Husks, Rampant Hazards, Wild Critters and Kaiju, all of which call these deeper shadows home.
