**Sector.** Glitterhaven

**Population.** ?

**Type.** Economic / Entertainment Sector

An obnoxiously bright and cheerful cavern, Glitterhaven is located deep under the south of Neon City's Undercity. This is where all those holistic self-care routines are dreamt up, trendy diet routines adopted, adapted, then abandoned. It is also *the* gayest of gay districts/sectors since the cavern itself is lit up like a certain Festive Holiday with bioluminescent fungi, gemstones, and people walking around. If you're ever in need of tourist attraction quality jewelry, weird niche cultural decorations, or just a place to trip without taking a hit then this is the spot for you.

The people here are friendly enough, though every 20 to 30 feet there's a gem-stall selling similar products as each other, or a collectibles and antique store with cool knives, canes, and replica weapons from pop-culture media. It is known for its festive culture and for being a staging ground for the more colorful scenes in modern film.

Puzzle Quest got its start here and has grown in popularity and influence enough to have it expanding into other sections and media outlets from escape rooms, to film and music festivals, live theater, Shadowcast stunt shows, to full-blown theme parks of the gothic "Ravens Loft," the film-reference heavy “Hollywood Highgrounds,” and “Winter’s Wonderland,” the ever-popular yearlong festival. Ticket prices skyrocket during the summer months for obvious reasons.
