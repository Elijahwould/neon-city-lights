##### Source: Player's Handbook (Message)

*Manipulation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a short telescoping antenna)

**Duration:** 1 round

You point your finger toward a creature within range and text a message. The target (and only the target) sees and hears the message and can reply in a text that only you can see and hear.

You can cast this tech through solid objects if you are familiar with the target and know it is beyond the barrier. Technical silence, 1 foot of concrete, 1 inch of common metal, a thin sheet of lead, or 3 feet of polysteel or synthetic soil blocks the tech. The tech doesn't have to follow a straight line and can travel freely around corners or through openings.
