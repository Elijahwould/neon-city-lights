##### Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting Reborn (Cruel)

Neon City is a harsh place. The challenges and struggles you've faced throughout your life in it have twisted you, leading you to delight in inflicting pain and anguish upon others. You gain a number of cruelty dice equal to your proficiency bonus. As you level up, the amount of cruelty dice also increase with your proficiency bonus. Your cruelty dice are d6s. You can spend only one cruelty die per turn.

You can roll a cruelty die under any of the following circumstances:

- When you deal damage to a creature, you can spend one cruelty die to roll additional bonus damage equal to your cruelty die. The damage type is the same as the damage type of the source (slashing from a sword attack, fire from a fireball, etc.).
- When you score a critical hit, you can spend one cruelty die to gain temporary hit points equal to the roll. Starting at level 11, the amount of temporary hit points you gain is equal to twice the result of the roll, instead.
- When you make a Charisma or Strength (Intimidation) check, you can spend one cruelty die and add the roll to your check.

You regain all spent cruelty dice when you finish a long rest.
