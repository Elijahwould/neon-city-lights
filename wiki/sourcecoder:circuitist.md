## "Your enemies might rest but rust never sleeps."

Some people have a natural gift for the weave and flow of modern technology's constant evolution, those individuals that see the building blocks that make up even the most base of augments, from cybernetics to nanotech runes. They see the circuits traced in design from the first days of the Old 'Net, the rare earth and silicon veins that sculpt the very heart of tech and the very mind of AIs. They see it, and think, well what's the difference, really? The circuitist is the kind of specific brand of insane that would use their own body as a circuit board and breaker, thus having to spend the rest of their life dodging the profiteering of those all too happy to use them as such; a heavy price for quite a direct gift.

##### Source: Tal'Dorei Reborn (Runechild)

### Circuitboard Tech

You learn additional techs when you reach certain levels in this class, as shown on the Circuitboard Techs table. Each tech counts as a source coder tech for you, but it doesn't count against the number of source coder techs you know.

When you gain a level in this class, you can replace one tech you gained from this feature with another tech of the same level. The new tech must be a manipulation or protection tech from the source coder, streamer, or techwizard tech list.

### Source Code Circuits

At 1st level, you've turned your body into an antenna and circuit platform for the fragments of the Old 'Net that composite the source code. You start with 1 source code circuit, and gain an additional circuit whenever you gain a level in this class. These circuits can manifest anywhere on the body, though the first is usually soldered into the veins near the heart or brain. Your source code circuits are invisible when [INERT].

At the end of a turn during which you spent one or more source points, a number of your source code circuits equal to the number of source points you spent begin to glow with activity, becoming [ACTIVE] circuits. You use these [ACTIVE] circuits to power your circuitist subclass features. Also, as a bonus action, you may spend a source point to convert two [INERT] circuits into two [ACTIVE] circuits. Such expenditure does not activate circuits at the end of your turn.

If you have 5 or more [ACTIVE] circuits, you emit bright light in a 5-foot radius and dim light for an additional 5 feet.

If you expend an [ACTIVE] circuit to use one of your circuitist features, it returns to being an [INERT] circuit. Any [ACTIVE] circuits also revert to [INERT] circuits after you finish a long rest.

### Circuit Unbroken

Starting at 1st level, you can release the stored energy within your circuits to absorb or deflect threatening attacks. Whenever you take damage, you can expend any number of [ACTIVE] circuits as a reaction. Roll a number of d6s equal to the number of circuits you expended, and reduce the damage by the total.

When you reach 6th level, you can touch a creature as an action and expend up to 3 [ACTIVE] circuits to transfer your warding energy to it for up to 1 hour. The next time that creature takes damage within the duration, it rolls 1d6 per [ACTIVE] circuit spent and reduces the damage by the total. You can't transfer this energy to a creature already under the effect of Circuit Unbroken.

Your Circuit Unbroken die becomes a d8 at 14th level.

### The Flesh is Weak

At 6th level, you can redirect your circuits to flow through your muscles rather than your nanites and blood. When you make a Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution ability check or saving throw, you can expend an [ACTIVE] circuit as a reaction to gain advantage on the roll. Once you use this feature to grant yourself advantage on a saving throw, you can't use this feature that way again until you complete a long rest.

### The Code is Willing

Also at 6th level, you can reveal hidden nanotech runes and technical barriers that surround you. As an action, you expend 1 [ACTIVE] circuit to reveal hidden or invisible technical traps, marks, nanotech runes, setups, firewalls, or sensors within 60 feet of you. They glow with dim light in a 5-foot radius for 1 minute.

You have advantage on Tech checks to discern the nature of any tech revealed in this way for the duration. If the marks you reveal mean something in a language you can't read, you can understand them while they are glowing as if you knew that language.

### Short Circuit

Upon reaching 14th level, you can redirect and funnel energy to overpower even the most insulated of barriers. When you cast a tech, you can expend 2 [ACTIVE] circuits to cause the tech to deal kinetic damage instead of its usual damage types.

Additionally, all creatures targeted by the tech or within the tech's area must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your tech save DC or be knocked prone or pushed up to 15 feet away from the tech's point of origin (your choice).

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you complete a short or long rest, unless you spend 3 source points to use it again. Such expenditure does not activate circuits at the end of your turn.

Starting at 18th level, you can use a bonus action and expend an [ACTIVE] circuit to sublimate your physical vessel across local nanites, transmission signals, and your own circuits. While partially discorporated in such a way, you gain the following benefits:

- You have a flying speed of 60 feet.
- Creatures have disadvantage on saving throws against your source coder techs.
- You have resistance to damage dealt by techs.
- Whenever you cast a tech of 1st level or higher, you regain hit points equal to the tech's level.

Your form remains partially discorporated until the end of your turn. However, you can expend an [ACTIVE] circuit at the end of your turn, no action required, to extend the duration until the end of your next turn. When this feature's duration runs out and you recorporate, you are stunned until the end of your next turn.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you complete a long rest.
