A combination mechanical and electronic lock that is synced to both basic biometric data and a physical key. Without both of the normal ways of accessing the lock, a creature proficient with both hacker’s kits and locksmith's tools can pick this lock with both a successful DC 17 Intelligence check and a successful DC 17 Dexterity check, made within two rounds of each other.
