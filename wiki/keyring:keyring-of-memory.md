##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Ring of Spell Storing)

*Keyring, rare (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lb., $$1,000,000

This ring stores techs cast into it, holding them until the wearer who installed this ring uses them. The ring can store up to 5 levels worth of techs at a time. When found, it contain 1d6 - 1 levels of stored spells chosen by the Fixer.

Any creature can cast a tech of 1st through 5th level into the ring by touching the ring as the tech is cast. The spell has no effect, other than to be stored in the ring. If the ring can't hold the tech, the tech is expended without effect. The level of the slot used to cast the tech determines how much space it uses.

While wearing this ring, you can cast any tech stored in it. The tech uses the slot level, tech save DC, spell attack bonus, and techcasting ability of the original techcaster, but is otherwise treated as if you cast the tech. The tech cast from the ring is no longer stored in it, freeing up space.
