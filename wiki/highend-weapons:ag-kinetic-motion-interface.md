##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Mace of Disruption)

*Weapon ([baseball bat](/weapon:baseball-bat) or [baton](/weapon:baton)), rare (requires installation)*

**Specs:** Variable, $$900,000

Reputed to be one of many in the possession of Almond Gemsplash, host of Almond Gemsplash's Audacious Arena, the Interfaces are his stick to ensure that the show goes on.

When you hit a virus, malware or a hacked with this technical weapon, that creature takes an extra 2d6 neon damage. If the target has 25 hit points or fewer after taking this damage, it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be destroyed. On a successful save, the creature becomes frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

While you hold this weapon, it sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet.
