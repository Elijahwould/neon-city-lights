*2d6 piercing, 20 lb., ammunition R (range 80/320), burst fire, strength 19, heavy, finesse, two-handed*

A machine gun is a fully automatic, rifled autoloading firearm designed for sustained direct fire with rifle cartridges. Other automatic firearms such as automatic shotguns and automatic rifles (including assault rifles and battle rifles) are typically designed more for firing short bursts rather than continuous firepower and are not considered true machine guns.

As a class of military kinetic projectile weapon, machine guns are designed to be mainly used as infantry support weapons and generally used when attached to a bipod or tripod, a fixed mount or a heavy weapons platform for stability against recoils. Many machine guns also use belt feeding and open bolt operation, features not normally found on other infantry firearms.

The most common variety of machine gun used by runners is the M134 Tomgirl, manufactured and sold by [Armata](/corp:armata).

### Features

- ***Hail of Bullets.*** It costs 3 times as much ammo to fire this gun as normal. When using the burst fire property, you may target a 15' cube rather than a 10' cube. Doing so costs 45 ammo instead of 30.
- ***Deployable.*** Before firing this weapon, you must deploy it using an action unless you meet its strength requirement. While deployed in this way, you treat this weapon's strength requirement as four points lower, your speed is reduced to 0, and you cannot fire this weapon at long range. You can collapse this weapon to cancel this feature as an action or bonus action.
- ***Focus Fire.*** Once per turn, if you hit a target, you gain a +1 cumulative bonus to hit and damage against that target, to a maximum of +3. This bonus resets whenever you change targets to another target.
