**Name.** Gillian Mason

**Lineage.** Human

**Age.** 36

**Height and Weight.** 5'10", 180 lbs

**Pronouns.** he/him

**Affiliation.** [Poncy's Bar](/loc:poncys-bar)

**Features.** Competent, affable, detached jokester with shaggy black hair, long nose, relaxed eyes and small soul patch

**Status.** Alive

One of the bartenders on rotation at Poncy's. Tends to pick up shifts for others. Is generally agreed to be a nice guy and competent at mixing a drink and waiting on a table. Is mildly concerned that so many of his coworkers appear to be getting involved in running—one of his fears is that he'll form a deep personal connection with one of his coworkers and then have to find out that they're never coming back.

Was almost killed and then brought back from the brink in the terrorist attack at Poncy's.

**Owner.** Celica#1988

**Creator.** Celica#1988

**Fixer Use.** Open.
