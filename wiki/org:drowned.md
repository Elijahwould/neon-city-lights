**Organization Name.** The Drowned Below

**Leader.** Alecto (de facto)

**Organization Type.** "Contracted" Assassins

**Years in existence.** ~1,500+

**Goals.** To rid Neon City of the true evil that lies within it, one target at a time

**Creed.** *"The deeper you sink, the less you die."*

**Impact.** Moderate

Long ago, there was a city of contracted killers that operated outside of the law—vigilantes, they called themselves, tasked with a list as old as time itself… or so the stories claim. No one knew how, nor why, but the one who held that damnable parchment controlled the fates of those etched on it in their very hands. Alas, even they were not immortal. Eventually lost to the tides, the original leader and the followers they once commanded became nothing more than a forgotten tale, evil settling its clutches into the world once more. Events leading up to the Relic Wars themselves, as well as the creation of Neon City, caused a once slumbering beast to stir once more.

In modern times, the Drowned are a nightmarish group once thought to be mere myth, having once again risen from their submerged grave in recent months, with a being known only as *Alecto* seated at the helm. Their claimed existence was long before the construction of Neon City, making them one of the oldest organizations in the world to date. With their original goal held strong, and an uptick in followers springing up and around in the Undercity, their existence has spread throughout, with numerous runners now under its employ.

It is said that traveling the docks at night may result in an encounter with one of them. Covered head-to-toe in the sea's embrace and masked by the scent of saltwater, members often have fully unanchored themselves of their past, embracing the perfect form they could not achieve without doing so. There is little reason to be afraid, however—they are more than interested in embracing another future follower of the Ocean itself, with one only needing to take the plunge and to let the water in.

While many may consider it a noble—if endless—pursuit to attempt to clean Neon City of its many sins, the Drowned are often at odds with the heavy-hitters and power brokers of the city proper. Their stated goal being what it is, it is easy to see that the days of any individual member of this organization become numbered the moment they join their folds. Still, many find their true calling in letting the water in, and those who do take the plunge make for dogged, nigh-unstoppable adversaries.

The following are personally associated with the Drowned:

- Alecto
- Drowned Unit 01
- "Maria" & "Lillian"
- Captain Fortune
- Bonnie Reed
- The Nameless One
