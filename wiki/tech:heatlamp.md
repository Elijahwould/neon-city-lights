##### Source: Player's Handbook (Produce Flame)

*Manifestation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** 10 minutes

A flickering hardlight flame, produced by a heatlamp application, appears at the end of your finger. The lampflame remains there for the duration and harms neither you nor your equipment. The lampflame sheds bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet. The tech ends if you dismiss it as an action or if you cast it again.

You can also attack with the lampflame, although doing so ends the tech. When you cast this tech, or as an action on a later turn, you can hurl the lampflame at a creature within 30 feet of you, shattering the flickering hardlight bulge and releasing the photothermal energy. Make a ranged tech attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 fire damage.

This tech's damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).
