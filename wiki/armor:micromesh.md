*Light armor, AC = 11 + Dex modifier, disadvantage on stealth checks*

Micromesh armor is a material that's exceedingly lightweight, if a bit noisy. The materials used to make micromesh tend to make crinkly noises when worn and offer the bare minimum protection possible.
