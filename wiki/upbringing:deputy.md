##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings)

You’re a peacekeeper out in the slums or out in the forgotten corners of the corporate frontier, roaming either the streets or the wasteland with your six-shooter and pristine badge. Technically, your power comes from the consent of the people, unlike the tyrannical corpo-cops and executive security forces, but out here people need someone to call on when a roving horde of genetic mishaps or lunatic raiders comes knocking, and you’re the one they call.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Athletics, Survival
- **Tool Proficiencies.** None
- **Weapon Proficiencies.** Revolver
- **Equipment.** Deputies start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A deputy's badge and identification card
  - A revolver
  - 20 rounds of pistol ammunition
  - A Stetson hat
  - A radio tuned to your local sheriff's department or precinct
  - A credchip worth $$1,000

### Feature: A Personal Touch

The relationship between Corpo-Cops and the general populace is often less than amicable but working in a close-knit community taught you the value of showing people a friendly face. When meeting others in person, you can easily memorize and recall their name and face.

Additionally, when introducing yourself and others to new people, you are proficient in the first skill check you do to make a good first impression of yourself and your allies.
