##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Summon Shadowspawn)

*3rd-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 90 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (gamer tears or influencer bathwater inside of a crystalline designer vial worth $$30,000)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You call forth a voidwalker. It manifests in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. This corporeal form uses the Voidwalker stat block. When you cast the tech, choose an emotion: Fury, Despair, or Fear. The creature resembles a misshapen biped marked by the chosen emotion, which determines certain traits in its stat block. The creature disappears when it drop to 0 hit points or when the tech ends.

The creature is an ally to you and your companions. In combat, the creature shares your initiative count, but it takes its turn immediately after your. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don’t issue any, it takes the Dodge action and it uses its move to avoid danger.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast the tech using a tech slot of 4th level or higher, use the higher level wherever the tech’s level appears on the stat block.

***Tech Lists.*** [Streamer](/techs:streamer), [Techwizard](/techs:techwizard)
