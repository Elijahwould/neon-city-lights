**Status.** *Retired*

At a young age, Katarina—or Kat to her friends and loved ones—learned exactly how cold and unforgiving the world could be after seeing her parents gunned down in gang crossfire at the age of 5. The next several years were a blur of moving, finding safe spaces, learning how to hack to steal creds, running from the corpos she'd stolen from, grabbing whatever food she could, and just living day to day. At least that was it, until the day she decided to tag in a certain neighborhood and met her first wife.

After that fateful moment, running became a safer means of earning creds and not bringing trouble back home. Running was, and still is, an exciting life, and it was while running that she met her second wife. She considers herself a loyal friend but also tends to keep to herself—aside from the once-a-week potluck of high skilled runners. She knows one day she'll shut her deck off, moving on to dedicate time to her family. But until then, when she's needed, she's just a text away.
