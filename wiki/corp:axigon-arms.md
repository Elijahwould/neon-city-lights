**Corporation Name.** Axigon Arms Dealing

**CEO.** [Samael Axigon](/npc:samael-axigon)

**Years in Business.** ~30

**Industry.** Security, Weapons Manufacturing and Robots

**Motto.** "*In a city competing for mere table scraps, why settle for anything less than the best?*"

**Impact.** Moderate

The military complex has always been a profitable endeavor and of the many that sought to dip their hands into the industry, Axigon proved itself to be one of the premiere companies. Producing high-end military grade weaponry and armor such as the Justiciar Edition Polysteel Bodysuit, the AXM-8 Autocannon and the AXM-Legacy line of blasters and BEA B/B Executioner weapons.

Always in an arms race against rival corporations like APEX and Armata Solutions, they are rumored to have testing facilities out in the wastes where they test new and experimental weapons. Where they get their technology is unknown, as are many secrets behind their development that led to the (relatively) sudden emergence of the corporation as a powerhouse within the weapons industry.
