##### Source: Fizban's Treasury of Dragons (Raulothim's Psychic Lance)

*4th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a code of software from your device at a creature that you can see within range. Alternatively, you can utter a creature’s name. If the named target is within range, it becomes the tech's target even if you can’t see it. If the named target isn’t within range, the code vanishes without effect.

The target must make an Intelligence saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 7d6 psychic damage and is incapacitated until the start of your next turn. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn’t incapacitated.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 5th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 4th.
