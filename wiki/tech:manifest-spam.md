##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Summon Fey)

*3rd-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 90 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a small figurine of a gilded flower worth at least $$30,000)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You call forth a hardlight spam projection. It manifests in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. This corporeal form uses the Spam Projection stat block. When you cast the tech, choose a mood. Hatemail, Memey, or Trolling. The creature resembles a spam creature of your choice marked by the chosen mood, which determines one of the traits in its stat block. The creature disappears when it drops to 0 hit points or when the tech ends.

The creature is an ally to you and your companions. In combat, the creature shares your initiative count, but it takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don’t issue any, it takes the Dodge action and uses its move to avoid danger.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 4th level or higher, use the higher level wherever the tech’s level appears in the stat block.

***Tech Lists.*** [Auditor](/techs:auditor), [Shut-In](/techs:shutin), [Streamer](/techs:streamer), [Techwizard](/techs:techwizard)
