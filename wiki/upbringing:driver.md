You've lived most of your life on the road—literally. You are most at home on the back of a motorcycle, or behind the wheel of a car. Whatever your vehicle of choice may be, you know one major truth: you *gotta* go fast.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Technology
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Mechanic's Tools
- **Vehicle Proficiency.** One land vehicle of your choice
- **Equipment.** Drivers start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A pair of sunglasses
  - A set of casual clothing
  - A mechanic's tool kit
  - A driver's license
  - A credchip worth $$1,500

### Feature: Punch It

You’re used to getting in and out of sticky situations—and you know when it's time to put the pedal to the metal.

When you move on your turn in a vehicle during combat, you can double your vehicle’s movement speed until the end of the turn. Additionally, when you move in this manner, all attacks directed at the vehicle and its passengers suffer from disadvantage until the start of your next turn.

You may use this feature a number of times equal to half your proficiency bonus (rounded down), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
