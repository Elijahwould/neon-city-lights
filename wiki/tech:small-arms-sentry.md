##### Source: Player's Handbook

*2nd-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 5 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (four or more bullets or similar ammunition)

**Duration:** 8 hours

You plant four pieces of nontechnical ammunition – bullets, most likely – in the ground within range and lay nanites upon them to protect an area. Until the tech ends, whenever a creature other than you comes within 30 feet of the ammunition for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there, one piece of ammunition flies up to strike it. The creature must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 piercing damage. The piece of ammunition is then destroyed. The tech ends when no ammunition remains.

When you cast this tech, you can designate any creatures you choose, and the tech ignores them.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 3rd level or higher, the amount of ammunition that can be affected increases by two for each slot level above 2nd.
