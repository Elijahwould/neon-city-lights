##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings)

You've been an undercity gutter runner your whole life, pilfering and scavenging to survive. You know how to blend in with the crowd, duck and weave through alleys and over rooftops, and generally keep yourself a nuisance instead of a threat.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Acrobatics, Deception
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Locksmith’s kit, Hacker's kit
- **Equipment.** Street kids start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A dark hoodie
  - Climbing hooks (wrist augments or tools)
  - A locksmith's kit
  - A hacker's kit
  - A credchip worth $$1,500

### Feature: I Know A Guy

You know a guy. For just about everything, you've got someone. Whenever you 'need a guy' for something, you'll have one. Work together with your GM to find this solution. They will typically help you with things, so long as your requests are reasonable (Combat is not a reasonable request.) Once you use this, you cannot make a request of 'another guy' until you finish a short or long rest.
