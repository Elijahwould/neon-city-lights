##### Source: Player's Handbook (Heal)

*6th-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a creature that you can see within range. A surge of healing nanites washes through the creature, causing it to regain 70 hit points. The tech also ends blindness, deafness, and any diseases affecting the target. This tech has no effect on constructs or hacked.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 7th level or higher, the amount of healing increases by 10 for each slot level above 6th.
