##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Steel Wind Strike)

*5th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** S, M (a melee weapon worth at least 10 credits)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You flourish the weapon used in the casting and then vanish to strike like the wind. Choose up to five creatures you can see within range. Make a melee tech attack against each target. On a hit, a target takes 6d10 force damage.

You can then teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within 5 feet of one of the targets you hit or missed.
