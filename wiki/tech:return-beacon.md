##### Source: Player's Handbook (Word of Recall)

*6th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 5 feet

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You and up to five willing creatures within 5 feet of you instantly teleport to a previously designated location. You and any creatures that teleport with you appear in the nearest unoccupied space to the spot you designated when you prepared your sanctuary (see below). If you cast this tech without first preparing a sanctuary, the tech has no effect.

You must designate a sanctuary by casting this tech within a location, such as a temple, dedicated to or strongly linked to your deity. If you attempt to cast the tech in this manner in an area that isn’t dedicated to your deity, the tech has no effect.
