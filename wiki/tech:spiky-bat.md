##### Source: Player's Handbook (Shillelagh)

*Manipulation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a nanobot vial and a baton, bat, or quarterstaff)

**Duration:** 1 minute

The wood of a baton, bat, or quarterstaff you are holding is imbued with nanobot spikes. For the duration, you can use your techcasting ability instead of Strength for the attack and damage rolls of melee attacks using that weapon, and the weapon's damage die becomes a d8. The weapon also becomes technical, if it isn't already. The tech ends if you cast it again or if you let go of the weapon.
