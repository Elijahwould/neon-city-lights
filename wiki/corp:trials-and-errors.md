**Corporation Name.** Trials and Errors

**CEO.** Thomas A. Edwards

**Years in business.** ~15

**Industry.** Entertainment, Escape Rooms

**Motto.** *"Everything in life is a trial, and we all make little errors. And sometimes we need a little break from it all!"*

**Impact.** Minor

Trials and Errors is a smaller corporation that leans on the benevolent side. They mostly make escape rooms, an old-world type of entertainment where people come to try to solve puzzles and escape a room.

It is known for wanting to give back to the running community, with many of the gigs they seek to hire runners for being just trial runs of their newest rooms. They tend to be easier jobs, with the worst part about it being the feedback forum at the end. But it often comes with a drink, and some good food. So, who can really complain?

Several newer escape room designs—primarily for runner engagement—are being made more family-friendly for the average consumer. Rooms so far include things like a murder mystery, stopping a drug crazed drug lord, as well as assaulting a Netsucker's keep to free a friend!

Thomas isn't always present at every gig or every location—he is only one moth, after all. To that end, he employs several assistants to help maintain each location. His best assistant is a young woman by the name of Bonnie Bunsalot, a smaller rabbit splicer woman with a hell of a good eye for creating interesting and fun puzzles.

They have had a 98% satisfaction rate since opening nearly 15 years ago. Which is pretty great, considering how upstanding the company has always seemed to be!
