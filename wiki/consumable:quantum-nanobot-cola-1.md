*Consumable (soda), common*

Quantum Cola's™ signature Nanobot Cola, shorthanded as Nano-Cola on their packaging, is your titular coke. It's a classic brown concoction of smooth, carbonated, sugary goodness and fortifying, restorative nanobots that comes in round bottles, or more often, cans, with a red styling and whitish silver accents. Some people claim it tastes vaguely medicinal when flat.

Nano-Cola bubbles with a faint red static when agitated that makes it perfect for chugging, or for long sipping experiences.

You regain 2d4+2 hit points when you drink this Nano-Cola, which you can personally perform as a bonus action. When self-administered as an action, you regain the maximum value of hit points (10).
