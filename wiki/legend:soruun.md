**Name.** Soruun Azureburn

**Lineage.** Kaijuborn (Gojira)

**Age.** 44

**Height and Weight.** 7'8", 447 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Classes/Subclasses.** Enforcer (Loyal) 20

**Features.** Sky blue scales, grey eyes, sharp draconic facial features, digitigrade legs, long tail, a heavy and powerful build. Appears fat to the casual observer but bears a lot of muscle, no cyberware or biomods present anywhere on the body

**Status.** Alive

Soruun Azureburn is something of an oddity in Neon City. A woman that fashions herself as a knight, straight from a fantasy tale, speaking in "Ye Ol' Common", and weaving technical effects like they were spells. Indeed, she carries herself less like a traditional enforcer, and more like the paladins common in roleplaying games. She doesn't simply look the part, either. She seems genuinely committed to battling corruption and improving the lives for people around Neon City.

Born in a smaller community in the Undercity, she was raised on stories of chivalry and heroism. As she tells it, her family comes from a line of knights in some pre-Relic Wars order. She dreamed of reviving the practice and tradition, of being a big shining hero, so she started training early. Combat, physical conditioning, tech use, the whole nine yards. Even landed a deputy position in her community for a time, before she was old enough to come into Neon City proper.

She spent some time with LARPers, the Knights of Sweets, a couple of runners, and here we are now. A full-blown, self declared "Knight of Neon City". That someone as seemingly righteous and holy has made it this far without being put six feet under is a feat in and of itself. Soruun remains committed wholly to fighting evil and helping the innocent, be it as a runner or whatever form it takes (if the slik-tok videos about healthy living and self defense are anything to go by).
