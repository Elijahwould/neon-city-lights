##### Source: Player's Handbook (Clairvoyance)

*3rd-level observation*

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes

**Range:** 1 mile

**Components:** V, S, M (a high-tech display with access to the internet and a drone worth $$10,000)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You create an invisible drone within range in a location familiar to you (a place you have visited or seen before) or in an obvious location that is unfamiliar to you (such as behind a door, around a corner, or in a grove of trees). The drone remains in place for the duration, and it can’t be attacked or otherwise interacted with.

When you cast the tech, you choose seeing or hearing. You can use the chosen sense through the drone as if you were in its space. As your action, you can switch between seeing and hearing. A creature that can see the drone (such as a creature benefitting from see invisibility or truesight) sees a hardlight, intangible drone about the size of your fist.
