A compact but powerful personal computer. The typical cyberdeck looks like a black cube or rectangle about 6 to 12 inches in length. It can be accessed wirelessly by a user’s peripherals, but serious users utilize a physical connection to their datajack for improved speeds. Techwizards need a cyberdeck to store their tech programs.
