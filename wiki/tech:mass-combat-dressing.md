##### Source: Player's Handbook (Mass Cure Wounds)

*5th-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A swarm of healing nanites fly out from a point of your choice within range. Choose up to six creatures in a 30-foot-radius sphere centered on that point. Each target regains hit points equal to 3d8 + your techcasting ability modifier. This tech has no effect on hacked or machines.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 6th level or higher, the healing increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 5th.
