##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Staff of Striking)

*Peripheral, very rare (requires installation)*

**Specs:** 4 lbs., $$4,800,000

This implement of reigning in the lust-filled population of Neon City can be wielded as a technical [quarterstaff](/weapon:quarterstaff) that grants a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it.

The quarterstaff has 10 charges. When you hit with a melee attack using it, you can expend up to 3 of its charges. For each charge you expend, the target takes an extra 1d6 kinetic damage. The quarterstaff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the anti-horni within is finally overcome by the horniness without and succumbs, becoming a nontechnical quarterstaff.
