## "Life is a publicity stunt. A shill. You've been had."

Those who don the Mask of the PACT Shill intimately know the limits of nanophage, a biological pursuit that means nothing to beings of pure digital manifestations and kingdoms of influence and monetary obligation. The body is just a statistic to software and market boardrooms alike. They have sold their personas, risking their truest selves, to the Partnered Autonomous Commercial Titles that control the razor edge of technological advancement and public opinion through their streamers, deeming a simple PACT, at the cost of pride, the lesser of two evils. Know, when you have been shadowbanned, it was a shill who swung the hammer.

### Sponsored Specialist

When you reach 3rd level, you strike a bargain with a commercial sponsor of your choice detailed by the [streamer](/streamer) class. Your choice augments some of your mask features. In addition, you learn both 1st and 2nd level techs from your sponsor's expanded tech list. PACT Shills sponsored by VPN Corp gain the expanded techs of the appropriate level from one column of your choice in addition to the phasic column, this distinction applies to your future mask features.

### Gifted Tech

Also at 3rd level, your sponsor allocates a product-image defense budget, granting you access to techs and the [streamer tech list](/techs:streamer).

##### Tricks

You know two tricks of your choice from the [streamer tech list](/techs:streamer). You learn an additional streamer trick of your choice at 10th level, and again at 17th level, as shown in the Tricks Known column of the PACT Shill table.

##### Tech Slots

The PACT Shill table shows how many tech slots you have to cast your techs of 1st through 4th level. The table also shows what the level of those slots is; all of your tech slots are the same level. To cast one of your streamer techs of 1st level or higher, you must expend a tech slot. You regain all expended tech slots when you finish a short or long rest.

##### Techs Known of 1st Level and Higher

At 3rd level, you know two 1st-level techs of your choice from the streamer tech list.

The Techs Known column of the PACT Shill table shows when you learn more streamer techs of your choice of 1st level or higher. A tech you choose must be of a level no higher than what's shown in the table's Slot Level column for your level.

Additionally, when you gain a level in this class and mask, you can choose one of the streamer techs you know and replace it with another tech from the [streamer tech list](/techs:streamer), which also must be of a level for which you have tech slots.

##### Techcasting Ability

Your nanophage modifier is your techcasting ability for your streamer techs, so you use your nanophage modifier whenever a tech refers to your techcasting ability. In addition, you use your nanophage modifier when setting the saving throw DC for a streamer tech you cast and when making an attack roll with one.

**Tech save DC** = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Nanophage modifier

**Tech attack modifier** = your proficiency bonus + your Nanophage modifier

##### Techcasting Focus

You can use a techcasting focus for your streamer techs.

### Crash Report

Beginning at 3rd level, your biotechnical imprint acts as a foothold for your sponsor's range of influence. While you have an active Lethal Error, you can use the hyperlinked weapon as a techcasting focus for your streamer techs, and when you cast a trick you deal additional damage equal to your Lethal Error. You deal this additional damage once per casting of a trick.

In addition, you gain a specific benefit based on your chosen sponsor:

##### Charity Foundation

When you use a Sequence Breach, you can use your own suffering as a springboard into a discussion of healthy coping mechanisms, healing a creature within 60 feet of you an amount of health equal to your Nanophage Potency + your Nanophage modifier (minimum of +1). If the Sequence Breach was cannibalized, you double the amount of hit points restored after rolling.

##### E-Sports Team

Whenever you target a creature with a Sequence Breach, you casually inform it that it might have less difficulty fighting you if it got gud, lol, your next attack against the creature deals additional damage equal to your proficiency bonus. If the Sequence Breach was cannibalized, you exploit their skill issue, the additional damage equaling twice your proficiency bonus.

##### Gacha Game

While using the TempRiseErr.OVERHEAT, if you roll a 1 or 2 on your lethal error damage die, you can reroll the die and choose which roll to use. First try, gamers. The lethal error damage die is instead maximized if the creature you are attacking is currently under the effects of one of your Sequence Breaches or if you cannibalized a Sequence Breach on your last turn. Check this ultra-diamond-rare pull!

##### Goth Topic

When you deal lethal error damage to a creature, it cannot regain hit points until the start of your next turn as you drain it of pep, color, and nanite signal strength. If the creature is hacked, the kinship of the macabre grants it disadvantage on attack rolls against you for the same duration.

Whenever you cannibalize a Sequence Breach, you can roll the Nanophage die twice and use the result of your choice. Angst abides by your schedule.

##### HentaiHub

When you target a creature with a Sequence Breach, you can expend and roll an additional Nanophage die to manifest a number of designer, tentacular products, forcing the target to take kinetic damage equal to the Nanophage roll and make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, the creature is restrained until the beginning of your next turn. If the Sequence Breach was cannibalized, the tentacles make themselves at home, the creature being restrained until the end of your next turn instead.

##### Survivalist

Whenever you reduce a hostile creature to 0 hit points, you further reinforce that only the fittest shall survive and regain a number of hit points equal to one roll of your Nanophage Potency.

If the creature was under the effects of one of your Sequence Breaches, you can immediately apply the same Sequence Breach to another creature within range, because you'll sooner drink your piss than waste a resource. You still spend a Nanophage die for this application and choose if you want to cannibalize it or not.

##### Telecom Provider

When you target a creature with a Sequence Breach, you can expend a tech slot to cast *[command prompt](/tech:command-prompt)* at the target as part of the same action. (Be wired in.) The target immediately fulfils the command, losing the requisite action economy of its next turn, if it fails the saving throw. (No one is free.) If the Sequence Breach was cannibalized, you can choose to affect a second creature within range, or have the target make the Wisdom saving throw at disadvantage. (Just try to cancel a cable package.)

##### VPN Corp

When you target a creature with a Sequence Breach, you can cause your Nanophage to teeter on the edge of surging in a Variable Protective Network around yourself, gaining a bonus to your AC equal to half your proficiency bonus until the start of your next turn. If the Sequence Breach was cannibalized, an allied creature of your choice within 20 feet of you also gains this AC bonus, the matter barrier appearing in two places at once as if through superposition.

##### Wellness Brand

When you deal lethal error damage to a creature, it glimmers with a surprise coating of cream that really brings out that youthful glow until the end of your next turn. For the duration, the creature cannot benefit from half cover, three-quarters cover, or being [invisible](/con:invisible).

If you had used a Sequence Breach on your last turn, the next attack against the creature has advantage.

### Safe-Class Promotional Tech

At 7th level, your sponsor has analyzed you as more benefit than cost and granted you confidential access to tech usually reservered for their stars. You learn both the 3rd level techs from your sponsor's expanded tech list and may cast each tech once without expending a tech slot, regaining the ability to do so with this feature on a long rest.

### Battle Souvenir

Starting at 7th level, when you use your action to cast a trick, you can immediately make one weapon attack as a bonus action.

When you reach 15th level, when you use your action to cast a tech, you can immediately make one weapon attack as a bonus action.

### Monitor Chip Upgrade: Shadowban

At 11th level, your Retributive Monitor Chip can isolate an individual nanite colony's Inherent Protocol address and secretly mark it for isolation and technical destruction. A creature chipped by you has disadvantage on its saving throws against your streamer techs.

### Euclid-Class Promotional Tech

At 15th level, your sponsor has deemed you an asset of equivalent value to the typical livestream staff team and granted you restricted tech access. You learn both the 4th level techs from your sponsor's expanded tech list and may cast each tech once without expending a tech slot, regaining the ability to do so with this feature on a long rest.

### Keter-Class Promotional Tech

At 18th level, your sponsor reveals that your work up until now has merely been a test of loyalty and granted your secret access to techs that elude even some of their streamers. You learn both the 5th level techs from your sponsor's expanded tech list and may cast each tech once without expending a tech slot, regaining the ability to do so with this feature on a long rest.

### Absolute Capitasync

At 18th level, the mask has become the face, you can manipulate your persona as finely as your sponsor, having realized the truth that all modern profit is built on a back of suffering and making it look pretty for the camera. Instead of spending a tech slot to cast a tech, you can instead spend and roll a number of hit dice equal to twice the level of slot you would spend for the level you intend to cast the tech. The tech is then cast at that level, and you lose hit points equal to the total rolled. You can cast techs with this feature a number of times equal to your Nanophage modifier (minimum of once), regaining all uses when you finish a long rest.
