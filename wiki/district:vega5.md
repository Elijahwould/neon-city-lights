**District.** The VEGA5 Strip

**Population.** ?

**Type.** Economic / Entertainment Sector

The VEGA5 strip looms large in the off-center of Neon City, bustling with activity. Neon signs and holoprojections fill every line of sight and large incredibly designed structures loom provocatively from both sides of the strip. Bliss Co with their Sex Casino: The Cream Palace (Now under the operation of Hentai Hub), and an assortment of hotels. Armata with their Colosseum-inspired Casino and Arena: Roll of Champions. Helping to provide the lead on security there, helping to safeguard the daily cash flow, with backup from local gangs. Autobae provide luxury services to transport those around in style, and if the odd client disappears in the nights of Neon City, well… who are we to ask?

Every single CEO worth their creds in this city—at least the ones that enjoy money, sex and thrill-seeking—has a stake here, and will act to protect it and their interests. Serving mostly as a neutral ground for corporations—both the big players, as well as up-and comers, and even other organizations and gangs—to engage each other in, Casinos continue to crop up as space permits as more hands clamor for wealth and the lure of new money and land keeps the stakes and tensions high. Still, all of these disparate entities fly their banners high, laying stake and claim to their plot of earth here, draining their customers dry in any way they can. A capitalist's dream.

All manners of provocative characters litter the streets enticing people into the casinos, brothels and bars for gambling, sex, myriad vices and even more. Drinks and drugs flow freely and, though almost every establishment is well protected, petty crimes are still abundant in the back alleys. Word on the street is if you've got enough money, you can find your dreams here.

The consortium of corporations, organizations and others cooperate fully to keep the streets clean of gutter trash and the clientele rich and well protected… and preferably broke by the time they leave. Few are those that beat the odds, and fewer still are those that leave to tell their tale about it. Bit players clamor, trying to muscle in on the bits of turf the Corps haven't swallowed up already, but even they try to keep their fights outside the strip's limits.

The strip takes its name from the old destroyed Vegas strip and the five founding corporations that brought it into existence: Armata, Bliss, Autobae, SenseCorp and Heartpulse Industries.
