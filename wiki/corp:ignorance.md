**Corporation Name.** Wishful Ignorance: Trust, Concerns, and Holdings

**CEO.** Unknown

**Years in business.** Brand new

**Industry.** Pleasure, entertainment, former Bliss Co. asset security and redistribution

**Motto.** *"A lack of knowledge need not be a lack of enjoyment. Close the curtain."*

**Impact.** Major

The tower has fallen and the former peasants raise themselves as demiurge feasting on the corpse of god. It was inevitable that the collapse of Bliss Corporations would lead to massive changes in the city, and one of those twists of inevitability was the rapid obtainment and securing of Bliss's most proprietary assets and most handsome of coffers. Thus introduces Wishful Ignorance, a trust, concerns, and holdings corporation standing in the shadow of a fallen titan and boldly claiming to reach the same heights. While the stranglehold once placed on the city is impossible to reapply, the tiny logo of WI has stretched its touch to an uncomfortable number of ads and public dealings.

Ignorance primarily operates under a board of directors obscured behind bureaucracy and anonymity, hiding the identity of any true CEO in accordance with their cred, that the internal operations need be a known unknown to further their goals. They offer refuge to otherwise unsupported subsidiaries, like those of Orgasmic Artifice and Enchanting Subservience, keep adult product and servers alike maintained, all while proudly hosting a much tighter leash on their own resources and their public image. While Ignorance undoubtedly contributes to a nightmare they offer relief from much like their predecessors, a fact they near-openly admit, their practice of inscrutability leaves no target for enmity save the corporation as a whole, which they redirect and diffuse with many an outreach program.

As runners have proven time and time again, the best defense is offensive allies, and so Ignorance, despite its secretive nature, is happy to share its resources. Indulging in the zero-competition on threat of hostile takeover model of practice like [Toncheva Healthcare](/corp:toncheva) and [NordCorp](/corp:nordcorp), Ignorance very much capitalizes on the open cornerstone of lewd endeavors, multiple partnerships and sponsor programs funding a plethora of mysterious in-house projects. Almost as if in a rush to achieve advancement for the sheer sake of it, as far as the average conspiracy theorist or rebel can tell.
