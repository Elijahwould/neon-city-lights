##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings)

The worst thing that could possibly happen to you has happened: you've been blacklisted from elite society. You were born into privilege, you know there's no working your way back in. It looks like you're going to have to use your boardroom skill set to rebuild your life.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Deception, Persuasion
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Microlabs
- **Equipment.** Ex-executives start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A black suit
  - A microlab
  - A white shirt or blouse
  - A data pad with notable members of your corporation
  - A credchip worth $$5,000

### Feature: Inside Informant

You have connections to your previous employer or other groups you dealt with during your previous employment. You can communicate with your contacts, gaining information at the fixer/DM's discretion.
