##### Source: Neon City Nights

*Weapon (any melee or ranged weapon), rare (requires installation)*

**Specs:** Variable, $$1,500,000

This weapon doubles as a multiform irregular transducer, approximating motion and touch into sound from a series of powerful microtized speakers. You gain a +1 bonus to the attack rolls of this weapon, and it deals an extra 1d8 thunder damage on a hit.

When you hit a target with this weapon you can cause a number of additional targets equal to this weapon’s rarity bonus within 5 feet of the original target to take half as much thunder damage as you rolled for the original attack. Additionally, once per round, you can cause a creature that hits you with a melee attack to take thunder damage, as if struck by this weapon.

The audio systems that power this weapon are fully accessible. You can attach it to a device as a speaker or use it as a mic, boosting audio input or your voice up to three times as loud, and you can save samples to a soundboard and create instantaneous sounds from a point of your choice within 30 feet, such as the crackle of bass-boosted distortion or ominous neon resonance.

***Blades.*** A blade is any melee BEA:B/B. Whenever you roll a 20 on your attack roll with a blade, each creature of your choice within 5 feet of the target takes 5 damage of the type dealt by a rare BEA:B/B, 10 damage of the type dealt by a very rare BEA:B/B, and 15 damage of the type dealt by a legendary BEA:B/B.

***Blasters.*** A blaster is any ranged BEA:B/B. Whenever you make an attack roll with a blaster, the ammunition supplies itself from a collection of near-surging nanites. You may still provide your own ammo to benefit from the bonuses of specific ammo, and it is consumed as normal.
