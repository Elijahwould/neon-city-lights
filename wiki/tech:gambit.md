##### Source: Player's Handbook (Contingency)

*6th-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S, M (a statuette of yourself carved from matte plastic and decorated with materials worth at least $$150,000)

**Duration:** 10 days

Choose a tech of 5th level or lower that you can cast, that has a casting time of 1 action, and that can target you. You cast that tech—called the gambit tech—as part of casting *gambit*, expending tech slots for both, but the gambit tech doesn’t come into effect. Instead, it takes effect when a certain circumstance occurs. You describe that circumstance when you cast the two techs. For example, a *gambit* cast with *[oxygen filter](/tech:oxygen-filter)* might stipulate that *oxygen filter* comes into effect when you are engulfed in water or a similar liquid.

The gambit tech takes effect immediately after the circumstance is met for the first time, whether or not you want it to, and then *gambit* ends.

The gambit tech takes effect only on you, even if it can normally target others. You can use only one gambit tech at a time. If you cast this tech again, the effect of another *gambit* tech on you ends. Also, *gambit* ends on you if its material component is ever not on your person.
