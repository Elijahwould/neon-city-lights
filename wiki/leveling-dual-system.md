Characters can follow two different leveling progression tracks in Neon City, depending on the kind of character they have. Track selection is made at character creation and **cannot be changed**.

The **Standard Track** starts at level 3 and goes through to level 20. Characters that go through the Standard Track will reach level 20 with higher amounts of credits overall and will have higher downtime points to spend throughout their careers. This is the track every character is assumed to be by default when on the server.

- Any amount of a player's current characters can be on the Standard Track.

The **Fast Track** starts at level 5 and goes through to level 20. Characters that go through the Fast Track will reach level 20 with lower credits overall but reach this level much faster as they require less MP to reach these levels. These characters are usually supplemented with notoriety bonuses and are common for players who have less time to play in jobs.

- Only one of a player's current characters at any given time can be on the Fast Track.
  - Full Fixers can have up to two separate characters on the Fast Track. Fixers usually have less time to play in games, since they spend time running them!

# Levels and Ranks

As you advance and gain levels, each level has a title attached to it. Depending on the title, the MP cost of gaining a level will increase, and thus the amount of MP you require to reach the next rank will also increase. Below is a chart outlining character progression.

Example: If you're a level 13 Streetwise character on the standard track, it will cost you 4 MP to reach level 14. However, if you're on the fast track, it will cost you 3 MP to reach level 14. At level 14, you become a Shadowrunner, and your cost to level up increases.
