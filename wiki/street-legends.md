## "Fear the old ones in a profession where people die young."

Legends made are legends known. The city is never in short supply of a starry-eyed runner in hopes of being known and celebrated. A hero of the common people or an idol of the upper echelon, the following list are regular people that rose to stardom through one method or another. They have earned their place among the heavenly host of degenerates this city is wont to produce, but these degenerates are almost as well-known as corporate jingles. One can assume the city is aware of their presence and spread their stories like paper to wind.█
