##### Source: Player's Handbook (Glibness)

*8th-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V

**Duration:** 1 hour

Until the tech ends, when you make a Charisma check, you can replace the number you roll with a 15. Additionally, no matter what you say, tech that would determine if you are telling the truth indicates that you are being truthful.
