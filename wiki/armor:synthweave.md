*Light armor, 1 lb., AC = 12 + Dex modifier*

Synthweave is the standard for all runners who want to maximize both the amount of protection and mobility from their protective gear while keeping the weight as low as possible. It is significantly more expensive than other classes of light armor, but that extra bit of protection is often appreciated in the heat of battle.
