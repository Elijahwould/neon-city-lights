##### Source: Player's Handbook (Shield)

*1st-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 reaction, which you take when you are hit by an attack or targeted by the [Micro Seeker](/tech:micro-seeker) tech

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** 1 round

An invisible barrier of technical force appears and protects you. Until the start of your next turn, you have a +5 bonus to AC, including against the triggering attack, and you take no damage from micro seeker.
