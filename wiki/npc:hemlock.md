**Name.** Hemlock / Rachel Zimmerman

**Lineage.** Remake

**Age.** Unknown

**Height and Weight.** 5'10", 280 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Affiliation.** N/A

**Features.** Ornate engravings and addons to her chassis.

**Status.** Active

Hemlock does not wear clothing or gear, opting instead for armored yet elegant addons for her chassis. Many are etched or engraved with swirling emblems such as the Fleur de Lis and similar. Her head has no visible face, with her optic and acoustic systems being cleverly hidden within the addons on her neck and head.

Hemlock is a relatively new player in the ring of Neon City fixers, but she has already proved her mettle… to a degree. When questioned on her origin, she will not answer. However, she seems to have fairly exacting knowledge of certain past events. Rumors persist that she is far older than she admits, but these are often quickly squashed.
