##### Source: Player's Handbook (Goodberry)

*1st-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a small piece of wax paper, for wrapping burgers)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Up to ten smashburger sliders, individually wrapped, appear in your hand and are infused with energy for the duration. A creature can use its action to eat one slider. Eating a slider restores 1 hit point, and the slider provides enough nourishment to sustain a creature for one day. The sliders lose their potency if they have not been consumed within 24 hours of the casting of this tech.
