**Name.** Diamant Damarion

**Lineage.** Executive Elf

**Age.** 242

**Height and Weight.** 9'10", 385 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Affiliation.** [The Damarion Clan](/org:damarion-clan)

**Features.** Diamond blue hair, immense bulk, childlike demeanor and unsettling smile

**Status.** Alive

The muscle of the Damarion Clan, Diamant serves as one of the family’s more active field agents. Where most other Damarions bolster their forms in ancient armor and brass covered metal, Diamant showcases little to no cybernetic augmentation in favor of what can only be inferred to as biogenic alterations instead which bolster her abilities well beyond that of the average individual.
