##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Create Homunculus)

*6th-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 hour

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a 3-D printed creature figurine that looks like the form you wish your biomachine to take, and ash, both of which the tech consumes, and a strange jewel-encrusted novelty dagger worth at least $$100,000)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

While speaking an intricate incantation that you found on a weird website, you cut yourself with a jewel-encrusted dagger, taking 2d4 piercing damage that can’t be reduced in any way. You then drip your blood on the tech’s other components and touch them, transforming them into a special construct called a biomachine. It is your faithful companion, and it dies if you die. Whenever you finish a long rest, you can spend up to half your Hit Dice if the biomachine is on the same plane of existence as you. When you do so, roll each die and add your Constitution modifier to it. Your hit point maximum is reduced by the total, and the biomachine’s hit point maximum and current hit points are both increased by it. This process can reduce you to no lower than 1 hit point. and the change to your and the biomachine’s hit points ends when you finish your next long rest. The reduction to your hit point maximum can’t be removed by any means before then, except by the biomachine‘s death. You can have only one biomachine at a time. If you cast this tech while your biomachine lives, the tech fails.
