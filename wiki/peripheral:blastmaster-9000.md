##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Wand of Fireballs)

*Peripheral, rare (requires installation by a techcaster)*

**Specs:** 1 lb., $$2,800,000

This mil-spec wand-like device has 7 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast the [Incendiary Blast](/tech:incendiary-blast) tech (save DC 15) from it. For 1 charge, you cast the 3rd-level version of the tech. You can increase the tech slot level by one for each additional charge you expend.

The wand regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the wand's last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the wand crumbles into ashes and is destroyed.
