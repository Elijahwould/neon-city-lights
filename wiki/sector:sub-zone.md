**Sector.** The Sub Zone

**Population.** ?

**Type.** Corporate

*"If I had a credchip for every time someone made a joke about this name…"*

Not long after the bombing of Bliss Co., those that worked down below fled in droves from the incoming storm of the brave and foolhardy alike. Picking up bits and pieces of the once mighty fortress that was BlissCo. was no easy feat… and when rumor broke of the data released to the public being riddled with malware and viruses, it made mining for even a single byte an incredibly difficult task. Many gave up on extracting more than a few files, leaving the majority of the treasure to those with the intellect to navigate their way through one of the most difficult minefields to date—and hungrier for a prize.

Even now, chunks of unknown, heavily encrypted files float through the digital realm, ripe for the picking.
