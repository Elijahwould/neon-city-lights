Typically achieved through light refraction or extreme environmental mimicry. Either way, *poof*, gone.

- An invisible creature is impossible to see without the aid of tech or specific senses.
- An invisible creature is heavily obscured for the purpose of hiding.
- An invisible creature's location can be detected by any noise it makes or tracks it leaves.
- Attack rolls made by an invisible creature are made with advantage, unless the target can see through the invisibility.
- Attack rolls against an invisible creature are made with disadvantage, unless the attacker can see through the invisibility.
