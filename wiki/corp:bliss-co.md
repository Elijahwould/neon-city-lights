**Corporation Name.** Bliss Corporation

**CEO.** *None*

**Years in business.** ~200

**Industry.** Pleasure, entertainment

**Motto.** *"~"*

**Impact.** Defunct

A company that has reached out and grabbed much of Neon City, its subcompanies and ads can be felt everywhere. They offer most of the distractions from the oppression of the capitalist nightmare that Neon City is rife with—all the while also being one of the major sources of said nightmares. They sell sex toys and related paraphernalia, host massive sexual roleplay servers on the net, and sell mods to push for a far more sexual—and distracted—populace.

The CEO of the company was a woman simply named Ecstasy. Having lived such a long time, perhaps even since the founding of the corporation itself, she has survived many, many corporate assassination attempts. However, recent events have raised the question: where is the CEO of the premier Corporation in Neon City today?

As a result, many of their current properties and claims to the city are in disarray, and a power vacuum now stands in its place—waiting for someone to claim it.

**Owner.** *Unclaimed.*

**Creator.** Ambi Antagonist

**CEO Use.** Closed.
