##### Source: Neon City Nights

You've spent a long time honing your aim with rifles and other powerful ranged weapons, granting you the following benefits:

- Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You ignore the loading property of all two-handed ranged weapons.
- You may increase the total range of a two-handed ranged weapon you are currently holding by one increment of the weapon's short range.
