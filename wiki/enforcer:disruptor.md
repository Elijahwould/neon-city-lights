## "Say what again! I dare you—I double dare you, motherfucker!"

Disruptors are dedicated primarily to enforcing careful use of tech. Primarily aligned with corporate interests, and even more often in the service of the Reliquary, they are primarily concerned with keeping the secrets of the large corporations. To accomplish that goal, they are primarily armed with tech sensors, hacking and suppressant features, and other abilities that allow them to disrupt others who find themselves too dependent on tech.

### Bonus Proficiencies

When you choose this clause at 3rd level, you gain proficiency in the Tech skill if you don’t already have it.

### Clause Perks

When you take this Clause at 3rd level, you gain the following two Clause Perks options.

- **Chaff Strike.** When a creature within 30ft of you that you can see casts a tech, as a reaction, you may spend your Clause Perk to teleport to any unoccupied space adjacent to the creature and then make a melee attack against them. On a hit, the target suffers the weapon attack's normal effects; the creature's tech fails and has no effect, the tech wasted.
- **Static Interference.** Immediately after you deal damage to a creature with the ability to cast techs with your Neon Smite, you can use your Clause Perk as a bonus action and mark them with static interference. For the next minute, any time the creature inflicted with this mark casts a tech, they must make a Constitution saving throw or take 1d6 software damage per level of the tech cast.

### Hostile Tech Firewall

Starting at 7th level, you create a field around you that weakens foreign, hostile techs while you're not [Incapacitated](/con:incapacitated). You and any creature of your choice within 10 feet of you gains advantage on all saving throws against techs.

When you reach 18th level in this class, the range of the aura increases to 30 feet.

### Disabling Surge

Starting at 15th level, whenever you cast *[counter EMP](/tech:counter-emp)* or *[localized EMP](/tech:localized-emp)*, you may add your proficiency bonus on the ability check to defeat a tech of higher level.

### Tech Eradicator

At 20th level, you can unleash the full brunt of your hostile tech firewall, making you a force to be reckoned against those who would use tech. You can use your action to gain the following benefits for 1 minute:

- When you activate this ability, and at the start of your round, you regain your Clause Perk.
- You may activate Chaff Strike against any creature within 45 feet of you that you can see.
- When using Chaff Strike, if your attack hits, you immediately recover your reaction this turn.
- Any creature targeted by Static Interference makes their saving throws against it at disadvantage.

This effect ends early if you are incapacitated or die. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
