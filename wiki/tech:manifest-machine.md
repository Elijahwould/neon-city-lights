##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Summon Construct)

*4th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 90 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (an ornate stone and metal lockbox worth at least $$40,000 credits)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You manifest a hardlight copy of a machine. It forms in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. This form uses the Construct Spirit stat block. When you cast the tech, choose a material: Clay, Metal, or Stone. The creature resembles a golem or a modron (your choice) made of the chosen material, which determines certain traits in its stat block. The creature disappears when it drops to 0 hit points or when the tech ends.

The creature is an ally to you and your companions. In combat, the creature shares your initiative count, but it takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don’t issue any, it takes the Dodge action and uses its move to avoid danger.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 4th level or higher, use the higher level wherever the tech's level appears in the stat block.
