Turn all the brains and computers off for a bit, mm, okay, night-night…

- An unconscious creature is [incapacitated](/con:incapacitated), can’t move or speak, and is unaware of its surroundings
- An unconscious creature drops whatever it’s holding and falls [prone](/con:prone).
- An unconscious creature automatically fails Strength and Dexterity saving throws.
- Attack rolls against an unconscious creature have advantage.
- Any attack that hits the creature is a critical hit if the attacker is within 5 feet of the creature.
