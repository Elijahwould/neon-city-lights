**Name.** Dorée Damarion

**Lineage.** Controlled Surge (formerly Executive Elf)

**Age.** 247

**Height and Weight.** 11'6"

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Affiliation.** [The Damarion Clan](/org:damarion-clan)

**Features.** Golden-blonde hair, soft spoken voice, warming presence, hums a distinct musical tune when corporeal

**Status.** Converted into a controlled surge in Vermil Damarion's service

A quiet and soft-spoken individual, Dorée has only been seen by one person in Neon City in this recent century. Little is actually known about this member of the family beyond her interest in music, housing a plethora of old records and disks within her home amidst the high-rises.

There's been speculation of her involvement with the downfall of [Bliss Co.](/corp:bliss-co), particularly given her most recent disappearance, but no one is around to lend credence to those rumors. In truth, she had resurfaced as an AI responsible for the destruction of Bliss Co. HQ using an advanced singularity device. After her apparent destruction, what scattered remnants of her could be salvaged within cyberspace were recovered and uploaded to an engrammatic device under Vermil's watch.
