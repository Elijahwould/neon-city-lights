##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Gloves of Thievery)

*Gizmo, uncommon*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$250,000

These gloves are invisible while worn. While wearing them, you gain a +5 bonus to Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks and Dexterity checks made to pick locks, electronic and mundane.
