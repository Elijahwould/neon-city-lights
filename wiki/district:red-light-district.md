**District.** Red-Light District

**Population.** ?

**Type.** Economic

Many markets exist in Neon City to tend to whatever needs the people of the city have. However, tucked away a bit to the east of the [VEGA5 Strip](/district:vega5) is Neon City's infamous Red-Light District. Whatever it is that you are looking for in the city can be found here—so long as what you want is incredibly salacious. The District famously houses the old headquarters for both HentaiHub and Gothic Hoard, and originally had several brothels set up by Bliss Co.—now bought out since Bliss Co.'s fall.
