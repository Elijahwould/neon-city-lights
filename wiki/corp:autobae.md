**Corporation Name.** Autobae Inc.

**CEO.** Aneona

**Years in business.** ~120

**Industry.** Transporation and infrastructure

**Motto.** *"~"*

**Impact.** Major

Autobae is one of, if not the most widely used form of transportation in the city, providing cars, helicopters, trains, and boats at a fair and balanced price to its customers. However a common misconception is that it's all AI run. In truth Autobae's CEO is the primary AI, but there are a few organic employees who do what the AI can't, such as locate lost vehicles and bots, perform maintenance in spaces too inconvenient for the AI to reach, and to model personalities and hear about possibly new demographics that don't have an online presence yet.

Aneona, the CEO AI, has a fascination with those she employs and sells to, leaving temporary fragments of herself in each Autobae to watch and learn from the city itself. In recent years, rumors have spread that Autobae has planned to introduce a line of organic clones in the near future. But before then, Autobae seems to be slowly transitioning to handling other important infrastructure in the city.

Either way, a lot more cars in the slum lately.
