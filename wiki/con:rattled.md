Imagine everything that sucks about being [stunned](/con:stunned) and now imagine it isn't actually that bad.

- A rattled creature can’t take reactions.
- A rattled creature suffers a penalty equal to half their proficiency bonus to any check that adds their proficiency bonus. Effects that allow a rattled creature to add their proficiency bonus twice suffer the penalty twice.
- Any effect that would cure the [stunned](/con:stunned) condition cures rattled as well.
- Any effect that grants advantage against or immunity to the [stunned](/con:stunned) condition applies to rattled as well.
