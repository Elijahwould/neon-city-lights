*1d8 piercing, 2 lb., ammunition P (range 80/320)*

A pistol is a handgun, more specifically one with the chamber integral to its gun barrel, though in common usage the two terms are often used interchangeably. The term is often used to describe any type of handgun, in this case excepting revolvers (which have a single barrel and a separate cylinder housing multiple chambers).

A magnum cartridge is a firearm cartridge with a larger case size than, or derived from, a similar cartridge of the same projectile (bullet) caliber and case shoulder shape. The term derives from the .357 Magnum, the original cartridge with this designation. A heavy pistol therefore is a pistol that fires a larger firearm cartridge—and with the benefit of additional firepower and the damage it deals, also come the downsides of additional recoil and slightly more limited magazine capacity.

### Features

- ***Crippling Shot.*** Once per turn per target, hitting a target reduces their movement speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.
