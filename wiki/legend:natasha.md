**Name.** Natasha Viszla

**Lineage.** Splicer (wolf)

**Age.** 28

**Height and Weight.** 8'6", 280 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Classes/Subclasses.** Renegade (Path of the Juggernaut) 18, Ronin 2

**Features.** Vivid purple hair with a white ombre, fluffy wolf's tail, incredibly lean and muscular body, long vertical scar over her left eye, sleeve tattoos on her arms

**Status.** Alive

Born the runt into a nameless family in the Undercity with little more than scraps, the once-human was forced to quickly learn the nature of fighting for survival when food became more valuable than lives. Under the false protection of her father, a small time CEO of a company specializing in biochemistry, the reason behind the bond between Alpha and Omega was uncovered much too late—and a true wolf was born through experimentation, unwillingly or otherwise- it was never disclosed.

Some twenty years have passed since then, with Natasha finding her way into the upper city—taking a job at Poncy’s and working the shifts she could grab..but it wasn't until when the woman picked up her first real job as a runner did she become accustomed to a new lifestyle. Months have passed since her first day Topside, and she is almost unrecognizable from those early stages, now proudly wearing the scars she's earned along the way, as well as the bonds she's picked up—ones she never believed would find her.

Nowadays she carries many jobs outside of just being a runner: she works as a shift manager at [Poncy’s](/loc:poncys-bar), as a head manager at [The Arcanum](/loc:arcanum), and is the proud of owner of Nat's Gym. It's not hard to catch her eye, and she especially has a love for fellow splicers.

The first meeting with this wolf can prove to be intimidating—but those that truly know her would say otherwise. Well… outside of a job, at least.

Married to [Rose "Carmilla" Viszla](/legend:rose).
