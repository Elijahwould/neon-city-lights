## "Listen, Gobbos are how you *know* that Goblin Slayer got it wrong."

##### Source: Mordenkainen's Monsters of the Multiverse, [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/the-gobbo-race), [All Along the Watchtower](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-NEJcj_BQBH6Z7ftvs1g), Neon City NightsThanks to reny#8234 for all of her work towards making Gobbo a more interesting lineage!

Gobbos are the result of the G.O.B.B.O. project, a last-ditch experiment by Old World scientists to develop a type of human capable of Generating Oxygen By Biosynthesizing Oxides. Intended to supplement the natural facets of the world as plants were mutated, forests lost to freak storms, and fertile land lost to desertification, the Gobbos are photosynthetic humanoids with chlorophyll-skin that allows them to absorb sunlight and breathe carbon dioxide for energy.

Needing far less food and preferring to live outdoors, it is incredibly rare for Gobbos to find long-term employment under any variety of corporation, instead leading lives dictated be pure flights of fancy, whether that fancy be mischievous and hedonistic, lackadaisical and half-awake, or causing violent problems on purpose. Pop-up outskirt towns, neighborhoods edging the desert or coast, and the titular Gobbo Beach are usually littered with hungover Gobbos basking in the morning sun, any cloudiness or inclement weather making them groggy and irritable.

Since the original program's conception and ceasation, the purpose of Gobbos has changed. The need for renewable energy and the rapid boom of the Gobbo lineage—accounting for near 20% of the new world's population—has taken the burden off of the natural world, allowing it the chance to spring forth, made new, as humanity has. Gobbos easily live in tandem with their distant cousins, having now since deviated into socioecological niches to support their easy-going but every-wanting habits to avoid conflict with new flora, thriving still.

### Roleplaying a Gobbo

When roleplaying a Gobbo, your primary motivations should likely be focused on your own comforts and enjoyments, as shaped by your biodeviation and upbringing. When an action would not result in the instant gratification of satisfying said motivations, your decision might be influenced by one of two very simple questions; What would make you the most creds, and what would make the most trouble for your enemies. Your variable—but statistically lacking—size and simple bodily needs allow you to enjoy life more fully than most. So, do you live it up, drowning every moment in sunshine, drinks, pills, stall-bought food, and endless casual relations? Have you found your simpler pleasures in the unexpected, such as a bloody fight or sappy movies? Do you resist your simple, plantborn, nature out of spite and denial?

### Gobbo Naming Conventions

Gobbos, considering themselves still mostly human, have human-like names and family names, but their culture of sunbathing, simple pleasures, and disregard for mass public wants have edged them towards using informal nicknames.

Gobbo nicknames include Shifty, Sketchy, Sleepy, Boozy, Rummy, Sneaky, Socks-On, Muffin, Git, Bozo, Tiddies, Slim, Wobbles, Shaggy, Cuddles, Peanut, Gorgeous, Bae, Amigo, Munchkin, etc.

# Gobbo Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1, or increase three different scores by 1.
- **Age.** Gobbos reach maturity at 18. The long-term tax of mammalian and plant-based physiological needs leads Gobbos to struggle to make it just under a century.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Photosynthetic.** You need to eat and drink half as much as a human. When you take a long rest, you must do so either in sunlight, under a UV light, or for an additional 2 hours, or suffer -1 to your Dexterity Score.
- **Motormouth.** You gain proficiency in your choice of either Deception, Intimidation, or Persuasion. Additionally, any creature of your choice within 30 feet of you can always understand simple and straightforward concepts from your speech.
- **Simple Pleasures.** You have advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the charmed condition on yourself.

## Generating Oxygen By Biosynthesizing Oxides Biodeviations

### Oxygen Rich

Deviating the least from the original Gobbo flavor, oxygen rich Gobbos have suffered a further decrease in height, but not to the detriment of their overall mass, being quite developed or plump in other ways. Of course, this is considered by most an advantage that can be applied to help maintain an easy life, making the oxygen rich the most avoidant of combat, and the least respectful once forced into it.

- **Size.** Your size is Small. Oxygen Rich Gobbos are between 3 and 4 feet tall.
- **Cup Check.** When you damage a creature with an attack or tech and that creature is a larger size than you, you can cause the attack or tech to deal extra damage equating to your proficiency bonus. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all uses on a long rest.
- **Tricky Little Git.** You can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of your turns.

### Semi Aquatic

The semi aquatic Gobbo is set apart by tints of cool colors in their skin and a level of lankiness, for a Gobbo anyway, like the reaching roots of a lily pad. Having adapted to the many aqueducts, water-filtration plants, and hydroelectric facilities of the city to free up terrestrial space for nature's revival, the semi aquatic Gobbo is happy to waste a day away face-down in a pool, resistant to the impurities of their submerged domiciles.

- **Size.** Your size is Small or Medium, selected when you choose this lineage. Semi Aquatic Gobbos are between 3 and a half and 5 and a half feet tall.
- **Biofiltration.** You have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned and you have resistance to poison damage. When you are suffering from the poisoned condition, if there was an initial save against it, once per turn, you can make the initial save again if you are fully submerged underwater.
- **Oxidize Hydrogen Bonds.** You can breathe air and water, and gain a swimming speed equal to your base walking speed.

### Fragrant Shrub

Often seen covered in a stain of hardier plant life, sometimes in warmer shades of old autumnal colors, the fragrant shrub Gobbo are naturally bigger than their counterparts, typically in all ways. This difference is attributed to their heightened chance of symbiosis and woody cell walls, believed to be a result of backwards compatible cross-contamination with the Organic Rainforest Carnivorous Conifer project.

- **Size.** Your size is Medium. Fragrant Shrub Gobbos are between 5 and 7 feet tall.
- **Overgrowth.** You have advantage on Stealth checks made in areas with a high amount of plant-life. Additionally, while you can not explicitly communicate with plant-life, you can convey certain ideas to it like you might an animal. You can use the Animal Handling and Nature skills interchangeably when dealing with plant-based phenomena.
- **Lumber Limbs.** When you make a melee attack on your turn, your reach for it is 5 feet greater than normal.
