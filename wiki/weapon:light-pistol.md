*1d6 piercing, 2 lb., ammunition P (range 30/120), light*

A pistol is a handgun, more specifically one with the chamber integral to its gun barrel, though in common usage the two terms are often used interchangeably. The term is often used to describe any type of handgun, in this case excepting revolvers (which have a single barrel and a separate cylinder housing multiple chambers).

The most common type of pistol used in the contemporary era is the semi-automatic pistol, which is the kind of pistol this is.

### Features

- ***Double Tap.*** Once per turn, when you miss with an attack against a target while using a light pistol, your next attack against the same target with the same light pistol is made with advantage.
