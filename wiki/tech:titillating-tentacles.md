##### Source: Player's Handbook (Arms of Hadar)

*1st-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self (10-foot radius)

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You invoke the power of the titillating tentacles. Tendrils erupt from you and batter all creatures within 10 feet of you. Each creature in that area must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 2d6 necrotic damage and can’t take reactions until its next turn. On a successful save, the creature takes half damage, but suffers no other effect.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st.
