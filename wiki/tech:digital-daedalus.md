##### Source: Player's Handbook (Maze)

*8th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You banish a creature that you can see within range into a labyrinthine subrealm. The target remains there for the duration or until it escapes the maze.

The target can use its action to attempt to escape. When it does so, it makes a DC 20 Intelligence check. If it succeeds, it escapes, and the tech ends (a minotaur or goristro demon automatically succeeds).

When the tech ends, the target reappears in the space it left or, if that space is occupied, in the nearest unoccupied space.
