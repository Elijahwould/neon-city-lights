##### Source: Player's Handbook (Keen Mind), Neon City NightsThanks to Jenny Jinxy#5892 for this one!

You’re smart enough to not need to wear a watch. Through convenient means, your brain has an evolved or nanite-enhanced internal clock and precise metabolism control. You can track time, direction, and you can remember details with uncanny precision for up to 30 days, limited to your sense's capacity of absorbing information. You gain the following benefits.

- Increase your Intelligence or Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You always know directions.
- You need to sleep, drink and eat only the half amount for your race.
- You always know the time left before the next sunrise or sunset or astronomical equivalent for where you are.
- You can perfectly recall anything you have perceived in the last 30 days.
