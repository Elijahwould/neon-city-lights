**Organization Name.** Raynor & Co. Acquisition and Investigative Services

**Leader.** [Maximillian Raynor](/npc:max-raynor)

**Organization Type.** Private Investigation and Research

**Years in existence.** <1

**Goals.** Provides investigative services

**Creed.** *"If you need it, we can find it."*

**Impact.** Minor

After being ousted from the Reliquary's CEO position after ~200 years (being the second to the name), for the apparent crime of deciding that the way the Reliquary does business could use change, Maximillian Raynor Tark took his middle name, made into his new family name, and hired away several trusted associates of his from the Reliquary.

Soon after, he established Raynor & Co. AIS, a company established primarily to continue doing what he does best: information brokerage, investigation and research. Of course, starting up smaller does mean that he has constant need of runners, and he most certainly has the money to pay them.

Raynor & Co. has also been heavily involved in both funding research and exploration for a vaccine for the ongoing DeVir Virus that's been plaguing both the Undercity and Neon City proper… with mixed results.
