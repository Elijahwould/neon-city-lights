##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Eyes of Charming)

*Gizmo, uncommon (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$40,000

These flickering contact lenses fit onto the eyeballs. They have 3 charges. While wearing them, you can expend 1 charge as an action to cast the *[meet cute](/tech:meet-cute)* tech (save DC 13) on a humanoid within 30 feet of you, provided that you and the target can see each other. The contacts regain all expended charges daily at dawn.
