The Mass Utility Labor Engine is a multi-legged robotic lockbox capable of carrying the spoils of any freelancer’s hoard while they are busy. The M.U.L.E. has a carrying capacity of 500 pounds and can follow simple directions, either verbally or by an app on the owner’s personal device. It cannot fight but will run if attacked.
