##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings)

You know what they say about sand. And in your experience, it really does get everywhere. You’ve spent your life fixing up clogged vehicles, cobbling together and jury-rigging makeshift solutions that get bikes, buggies and merchant walkers "at least to the next town over". But unless you wanna stare at the same horizon for the rest of your life, it’s time to get a move on.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Technology, Survival
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Mechanic's tools, Basic toolkit
- **Equipment.** Desert mechanics start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A box of mechanic's tools
  - A basic toolkit
  - A set of stained denim overalls
  - An oversized wrench (which counts as a baseball bat)
  - A knapsack of spare parts, bolts, screws and wires

### Feature: Jury Rig

If there's anywhere you can't be lugging around a bunch of different tools, it's a desert. So, you learned to do what you can with what you can carry.

If you try to repair a broken piece of technology but don't have an appropriate set of technician's tools, you can make a technology check with any other set of tools that you are proficient in instead. If you fail this check, you further damage the device, making it impossible to repair without the proper equipment.

You have advantage on repair checks related to all vehicles.
