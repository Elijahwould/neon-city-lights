##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Dwarven Thrower)

*Weapon ([sledgehammer](/weapon:sledgehammer)), very rare (requires installation by a [jerboan](/race:jerboan))*

**Specs:** 5 lb., $$5,000,000

You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this technical weapon. It has the thrown property with a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet. When you hit with a ranged attack using this weapon, it deals an extra 1d8 damage or, if the target is a giant, 2d8 damage. Immediately after the attack, the weapon flies back to your hand.
