##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide (Green-Flame Blade)

*Altercation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self (5 ft radius)

**Components:** S, M (a weapon)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

As part of the action used to cast this tech, you must make a melee attack with a weapon against one creature within the tech's range, otherwise the tech fails. On a hit, the target suffers the attack's normal effects, and an RGB lighting array overheats and vents towards a different creature of your choice that you can see within 5 feet of the target. The second creature takes fire damage equal to your techcasting ability modifier.

This tech's damage increases when you reach higher levels. At 5th level, the melee attack deals an extra 1d8 fire damage to the target, and the fire damage to the second creature increases to 1d8 + your techcasting ability modifier. Both damage rolls increase by 1d8 at 11th level and 17th level.
