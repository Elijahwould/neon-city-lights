##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Holy Avenger)

*Weapon (any sword or [machete](/weapon:machete)), legendary (requires installation by an [enforcer](/enforcer))*

**Specs:** Variable, $$7,000,000

You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this technical weapon. When you hit a malware, virus or a hacked with it, that creature takes an extra 2d10 neon damage.

While you hold the drawn sword, it creates an aura in a 10-foot radius around you. You and all creatures friendly to you in the aura have advantage on saving throws against techs and other technical effects. If you have 17 or more levels in the enforcer class, the radius of the aura increases to 30 feet.
