##### Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting Reborn (Freedom of the Waves)

*3rd-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (an escape key and a bottle of water)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You conjure a deluge of digital seawater in a 15-foot-radius, 10-foot-tall cylinder centered on a point within range. This water takes the form of a tidal wave, a whirlpool, a waterspout, or another form of your choice. Each creature in the area must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your tech save DC or take 2d8 bludgeoning damage and fall prone. You can choose a number of creatures equal to your techcasting modifier (minimum of 1) to automatically succeed on this saving throw.

If you are within the tech’s area, as part of the action you use to cast the tech, you can vanish into the deluge and teleport to an unoccupied space that you can see within the tech’s area.
