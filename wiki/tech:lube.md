##### Source: Player's Handbook (Grease)

*1st-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet (10-foot square)

**Components:** V, S, M (a bit of pork rind or butter)

**Duration:** 1 minute

Slick lube covers the ground in a 10-foot square centered on a point within range and turns it into difficult terrain for the duration. When the grease appears, each creature standing in its area must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. A creature that enters the area or ends its turn there must also succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone.
