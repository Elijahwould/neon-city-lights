##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (Telepathic)

You have developed the ability to direct your nanites to maintain direct mental communication with others, granting you the following benefits:

- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You can control the nanites in your mind to speak directly to the mind of any creature you can see within 60 feet of you. Your communication doesn't give the creature the ability to respond to you, but it is possible to "hear" a reply if you are reading surface thoughts.
- You can cast the *[neurotap](/tech:neurotap)* tech, requiring no tech slot or components, and you must finish a long rest before you can cast it this way again. Your techcasting ability for the tech is the ability increased by this feat. If you have tech slots of 2nd level or higher, you can cast this tech with them.
