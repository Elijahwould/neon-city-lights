**Name.** Silica "AI.ngel" Gelova

**Lineage.** Life-Lite Remake (AI-Simar & Sexbot traits), formerly Executive Elf

**Age.** 29 (28+1) legally, 600+ spent in digital realms

**Height and Weight.** 5'9'', 175 lbs.

**Pronouns.** she/they

**Classes/Subclasses.** Techwizard (Nexus Modder) 19

**Features.** Gold-threaded chocolate skinweave, color-shifting hair reaching the floor, exposed obsidian and gold cyberarms, all in a highly voluptuous frame. Often seen wearing her crystal-patterned archcoder dress, while accompanied by her cyberdeck's AI globule and slime sim-u-pet.

**Status.** Alive

Once on the run and hunted by the powers that be, Silica is an escapee from the top floor district of Nobrac, a sheltered high-class area for many corp families, and getting out of there turned out to be the best mistake of her life.

Born into the corporate rat chase, she was lucky to have been kicked out of corpo-college and in turn avoiding becoming a complacent pawn. For a while, life was spent in the endless flow of the digital realms, enjoying every lust, vice and game conceivable for what would amount to centuries from her dilated perspective. What finally broke the worst parts of her addiction was getting scouted to join a hacker terrorist cell, known as the “Endbyte”, and together with her mentor 3ch0Ch4mb3r, they helped Silica reveal her aptitude for techcasting and gift for understanding code in its purest forms.

Silica’s need to forge a new identity came from the fact that a corporation caught on to their operation, eliminating her entire crew in the flesh—blowing their brains out in their very own Sense-Tanks. Seeing this coming, her friend and mentor barely managed to separate and digitize Silica’s psyche just before her body too was flatlined. Picking up the pieces of her own mind, she would find her revenge, having uploaded a virus into the consciousness chip that would house the corpo bitch’s brain who was responsible, right before a Remake procedure. Taking the chance, Silica took her place, getting inserted into a shiny new Life-Lite chassis.

Now she has become more known for her fully realized self, as an erotic nexus modder who publicly started out her running career as winner of the monster mishap menagerie. Her lewd streak could never go unnoticed as she's participated in many experiments, shows and events, never shy to show off her best self. In a more unknown setting, the Life-Lite utilized her peak of technological might, brandishing absolute source code manipulation to help restore the fractured AI of Dorée Damarion, having experienced such a condition herself, also helping restore the family’s digital estate.

Today, after extensive enhancement, augmentation, and spiritual awakening through sexual therapy, the AI.ngel of Endbyte can be found content with her various lovers when she’s not in the tank, and those lucky to find her haven among the flow of code will be welcomed in her open arms.
