**Organization Name.** The Jingos

**Leader.** [Red Sun](/npc:red-sun)

**Organization Type.** Paramilitary Group

**Years in existence.** ~30

**Goals.** Involve itself in combat, security, gun running, and anything that may or may not require violence

**Creed.** *"Money ain't got no smell."*

**Impact.** Moderate

Give war a chance! Sometimes corporations want a job done with plausible deniability, be it sabotaging their competition, needing some extra muscle on a security detail or wanting someone to go away for a long time (like forever) without worry of their names being tied to the action. That is where the Jingos come in.

Created from various formerly retired corporate war veterans, the Jingos are a paramilitary organization stationed around the outskirts of Neon City. In the wake of Bliss' destruction, many of their contracts became void. As a result, they have now started to branch out across various different organizations for work. They sell their services to anyone and everyone willing to pay them for their services which has led them to possessing a reputation as the junkyard dogs of Neon City and their brutish nature has had them viewed as little more than heavily armed thugs by most.

### Operation

The Jingos' main base of operations is on an abandoned oil rig not far off the coast of Neon City. It’s here that recruits are brought for initiation, promotions and other "ceremonies". In the case of high value jobs or large-scale operations, they will gather squadrons here for debriefing and deployment.

For all other assignments and daily work, the majority of Jingo forces are gathered around the various warehouses topside where "Basecamp" is located. Here, operatives can accept approved contracts, rearm and resupply and form individual squads for their own jobs.

### Ranking

Much as their namesake indicates, Jingos are militaristic and while formally, high ranking figures still utilize their ranks from their service years; the company as a whole operates on a similar, albeit less detailed system.
