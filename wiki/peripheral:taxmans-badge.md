##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Wand of Fear)

*Peripheral, rare (requires installation*

**Specs:** 1 lb., $$850,000

Made in the image of the institution and profession that all money-earning citizenry fear, this badge has 7 charges for the following properties. It regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the badge's last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the badge implodes and is destroyed.

**Command Prompt**. While holding the wand, you can use an action to expend 1 charge and command another creature to flee or grovel, as with the [Command Prompt](/tech:command-prompt) tech (save DC 15).

**Cone of Fear.** While holding the badge, you can use an action to expend 2 charges, causing the badge's embedded holoprojector to emit a 60-foot cone of amber light, showing each creature an image of their tax returns being rejected. Each creature in the cone must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of you for 1 minute. While it is frightened in this way, a creature must spend its turns trying to move as far away from you as it can, and it can't willingly move to a space within 30 feet of you. It also can't take reactions. For its action, it can use only the Dash action or try to escape from an effect that prevents it from moving. If it has nowhere it can move, the creature can use the Dodge action. At the end of each of its turns, a creature can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.
