There's a pressure around your head that makes thinking things through… harder.

- A creature suffering a headache subtracts half its proficiency bonus from any check or save using its Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score.
- Any effect that would cure the [poisoned](/con:poisoned) condition cures a headache as well.
- Any effect that grants advantage against or immunity to the [poisoned](/con:poisoned) condition does not apply to a headache.
