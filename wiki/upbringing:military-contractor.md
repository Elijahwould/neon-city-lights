##### Source: Player SubmissionThanks to @jessicashtebs for the submission!

Neon City is a place awash in scum and villainy. As a result, private military corps are always hiring. And, for reasons only you know, you answered the call. Whether for [Armata](/corp:armata), [the Jingos](/org:jingos), or even just one of the smaller corps' response teams, you plied the time-honored trade of being a gun for hire. And hell, you got pretty decent at it too. But for some reason, you've found yourself solo. Though, as long as you've got your knowhow and trusty service weapon, you'll be fine. Right?

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Athletics, Intimidation
- **Tool Proficiencies.** None
- **Weapon Proficiencies.** Combat rifle
- **Equipment.** Military contractors start their careers with the following equipment:
  - An Armata Arms M8 combat rifle
  - A set of comfortable tactical clothes
  - A set of dog tags

### Feature: Tactical Acumen

Years of being hyper-aware of the environment means you have a knack for being more prepared than most for hostilities to break out. When rolling initiative, you may choose to add half your proficiency bonus to your roll. Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest.
