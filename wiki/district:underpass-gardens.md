**District.** Underpass Gardens

**Population.** Roughly ~440,000 overall

**Type.** Residential and Commercial

Tucked underneath the mega highway that connects the majority of the city, the Underpass Gardens actually consist of a small district in Neon City that separates the Downtown Residential area from Downtown proper. It lies not too far from the old Osaka HQ building. It is home to a large number of the city's homeless—people who have slipped in between the cracks of the system in Neon City despite its advances—and is mostly untouched by the NCPD or corporate influence. The gardens are called so because they exist almost like a co-op or commune, an odd piece of untouched greenery lying strangely untouched by the city's technology.

Many sections of the Underpass Gardens have connections that lead directly to the Undercity—enormous spiraling staircases that are a challenge to go down that usually spiral around the massive elevator-containing pillars. These are occasionally littered with apartments and small shops built into the rock at their sides a good couple of hundred feet under the city proper, and their proximity to metro movers and subway stops for both the city and Undercity proper make them prized locations for runner dwellings.
