*"Arcanum - where magic happens."*

Founded by Ser Maur Stoker-Lucienne in 1591, The @rcanum soon became known as one of the most upscale clubs in the Undercity—while also attracting a reputation for people seeming to disappear. Nonetheless, the club remained in business, and even thrived. Eventually, the owner entered a partnership with one Renita Besson, who allowed him to expand his operations.

Thus it continued until Besson's death at the hands of a handful of runners, and then, three months later, Lucienne himself met a similar end. An upheaval in ownership resulted, but now has begun to settle as a woman calling herself Carmilla Viszla has seized the reins.

Multiple hires occurred not long after the acquisition, to help edge the @rcanum into a new era. You might find some familiar faces pouring you a drink, one such being a certain Wolfgirl from Poncy's, the new Head Manager here..
