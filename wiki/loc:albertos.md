**Location Name.** Alberto's Ristorante

**Owner/Leader.** Alberto Accetta

**Location Type.** Business

**Years in existence.** ~19

**Type.** Restaurant

**Area.** [Neon City Downtown Residential District](/district:downtown-residential)

**Impact.** Minor

Alberto's is a small local joint in the Downtown Residential district that serves Italian-American classics that survived through the Great Collapse. They're known for having a huge multi-pizza brick oven that can make pretty good, large, thin pies.

There is a basement that usually serves as a den for gambling at night, but also is a good place for runners to meet up and exchange information.
