## "Big things really do come in small packages. Except that one; their package is huge."

##### Source: Player's Handbook, [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/the-jerboan-race), [All Along the Watchtower](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-NEJcj_BQBH6Z7ftvs1g), Neon City NightsThanks to reny#8234 and Celica#1988 for their lore and mechanical contributions!

![Yulia Crusher, a female Jerboan (by Spliff)](https://neoncitylights.wdfiles.com/local--files/race:jerboan/Yulia.Crusher.png)

During the final days of the Old World, many former nations resorted to bizarre and dangerous experimentation on their populace, in a desperate attempt to outpace or survive the encroaching desert. Rapid, frenzied advances in genetic therapy created mostly mutants and monsters that plague the fringes of the New World to this day, but one of the more successful attempts became the Jerboans. Half man, half desert rodent, Jerboans set the benchmark for what, 400 years later, became cheap and affordable genetic manipulation, and are considered the progenitors of Gobbos, Nayalings, Elfs and Splicers.

Jerboans were created to survive the harsh, rapidly forming desert during the trek north during the Great Relocation, and quickly developed a culture founded on nomadic, communal principles and a rebellious attitude towards the corporation dominated New World. Preferring to remain at the edges of corporate civilization, Jerboans roam the New World in tight, biker gang-like cliques, revering their ancestors by maintaining their punk rock, outsider approach to the state of the world.

### Roleplaying a Jerboan

A Jerboan that resorts to finding work outside his gang’s usual activities of holding up merchant caravans on the edge of the desert, is usually either an outcast or the last of their tribe. Jerboans are a suspicious lot, bound by the traditions of their forefathers to keep civilization and those that freely engage in it at arm’s length. That said, those same traits make Jerboans a stalwart ally and a close friend when they finally begin to open up.

### Jerboan Naming Conventions

Jerboans name their children in reference to their noble punk and biker traditions. Jerboan family names are added to frequently by the feats and deeds of members of the family, though these are often shortened to the deeds to the individual when speaking with outsiders. Deeds of the individual are attached to the end of the name. Many Jerboan families are known as Deedless, having done nothing spectacular within their families.

For Example, the name "Spike Tamperer, Mutantender, Hogrider, Bloodstauncher, Cutter, Deedswiper, Legloser, Sandsweeper" would be shortened to Spike Sandsweeper.

Other shortened name examples are Guts Grimglower, Ziggy Sawbones, Mud Madman, Toastie Oneshot, Crud Breakknuckle, Cruise Drifter, Feral Bloodguts, Toothy Jawbreaker, Scab Fixer, etc.

# Jerboan Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, or increase two different scores by 1.
- **Age.** Jerboans reach adulthood at 16 and live about a century.
- **Size.** Your size is Medium. Jerboans are between 4 and 5 feet tall.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 25 feet. Your speed is not reduced by wearing heavy armor.
- **Genetic Resilience.** You have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned and you have resistance to poison damage.
- **Hard Hitter.** You have proficiency with the axe, sledgehammer and maul.
- **Desert Mechanic.** You gain proficiency with the artisan’s tools of your choice: mechanic’s tools, electrician’s tools, or builder’s tools.
- **Stuffed Tough.** During a long rest, you can choose to eat $$1,000 worth of food and drink to gain +2 AC until your next long rest. Natural Biodiversity cannot inherit this trait.

## Jerboan Subgenres

### Punk Rock Jerboan

Punk Rock Jerboans pride themselves on their wild, untamed spirits and rebellion against the corporate society that dominates the world. They channel that spirit through their music, creating fast and furious anthems that speak to their experiences on the fringe of civilization. They cover themselves in piercings and tattoos and style their hair into wild spiky mohawks or shave it completely, to cover their head in colorful tattoos. All of this is a symbol of rebellion against the corporate order.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 1.
- **Live While You Can.** Your hit point maximum increases by 1, and it increases by 1 every time you gain a level.
- **I Wanna Be Sedated.** You have advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the frightened condition or diseases on yourself.

### Adult Alternative Jerboan

Adult Alternative Jerboans have found a home in the darker corners of the New World, their music a mix of melancholy and contemplation with a dash of rebellion. Their general laid-back attitude mixes well with their preference for introspective lyrics and melodic tunes over the raw energy of punk rock. They're often seen wearing vintage, old-world clothing and sporting messy, unkempt hair.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2.
- **So We Can Stay Alive.** You have proficiency with light and medium armor.
- **Tubthumping.** At the start of your turn, you can choose to gain 1 hit points, even if you are unconscious. Once you use this trait you cannot use it again until you complete a long rest.

### Death Metal Jerboan

Long ago, one Jerboan found themself frustrated to no end by the endless grind of capitalism and found themselves drowned in despair. Through that despair, however, they found enlightenment through rage. Deff Metal Jerboans follow the path of this legend, surrendering to their emotions and gaining power from the soul of metal.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 1.
- **Nightvision.** You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
- **Master of Puppets.** You have advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the charmed or paralyzed condition on yourself.
- **Moon Above, Sun Below.** When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the *[digital resizing](/tech:digital-resizing)* tech on yourself once with this trait, using only the tech's enlarge option. When you reach 5th level, you can cast the *[physical cloaking](/tech:physical-cloaking)* tech on yourself once with this trait. You don't need material components for either tech. You regain the ability to cast these techs with this trait when you finish a long rest. You may instead expend a tech slot of the appropriate level to cast them. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your techcasting ability for these techs, chosen at character creation.

### Industrial Rock Jerboan

Industrial Rock Jerboans are a rare breed, taking the raw energy and angst of punk rock and channeling it through the mechanical and electronic sounds of the post-apocalyptic world. Their music is abrasive and chaotic, reflecting the harsh reality of life on the edges of civilization. Their fashion reflects their preference for the raw mechanical sound of their music, they incorporate lots of leather and metal accessories into their clothes.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 1.
- **Various Methods of Escape.** You gain proficiency in your choice of either locksmith's kits or hacker's kits. When you make an ability check involving either a locksmith's kit or hacker's hit, you can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to the ability check.
- **Lights in the Sky.** When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the *[threat detector](/tech:threat-detector)* tech once with this trait. When you reach 5th level, you can cast the *[thermovision](/tech:thermovision)* tech on yourself once with this trait. You don't need material components for either tech. You regain the ability to cast these techs with this trait when you finish a long rest. You may instead expend a tech slot of the appropriate level to cast them. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your techcasting ability for these techs, chosen at character creation.

### Visual Kei Jerboan

Visual Kei Jerboans have embraced the art and fashion of old-world Japanese Kei culture, creating a unique style that combines punk rock with flamboyant and theatrical flair. Their music is just as visually stunning, with over-the-top performances and a sound that blends punk rock with elements of glam and goth. Not wanting to be outdone by others, they constantly try and create more creative costumes and clothes.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 1.
- **Babymetal.** Your size is Small, instead of Medium, but you do not have disadvantage on attack rolls made with Heavy weapons due to your size. In fact, you can use the weight and momentum to your benefit, gaining a +2 bonus to the damage rolls of Heavy weapons while under the benefits of Stuffed Tough.
- **L'Arc-en-Ciel.** Visual Kei Jerboans live for the chance to shock and awe. If you miss with an attack roll or fail a saving throw, you can gain a bonus to the roll equal to the number of allies who can see you within 30 feet of you, to a maximum of your proficiency bonus, possibly causing you to succeed. You can use this trait a number of times equal to half your proficiency bonus, regaining all uses on a long rest.

### Nu Metal Jerboan

Nu Metal Jerboans have taken the hard-hitting sound of metal and fused it with the energy and attitude of punk rock and splashed in a little dash of hip-hop. Their music is loud and aggressive, with a sound that reflects the intensity of their Jerboan spirit, and their fight against the corporations.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 1.
- **Stealing Society.** You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice, one tool of your choice, and one old world language of your choice (such as Spanish, Korean, French, Japanese, Mandarin, etc.).
- **When Worlds Collide.** Jerboans were the first breakthrough in what is now modern and easy genetic alterations that later went on to be foundational to the development of Gobbos, Nayalings, Elfs, and Splicers, and the rare few Nu Metal find that these throughlines make things backwards compatible. Choose one of the following options when you select this lineage:
  - **Bring Me To Life.** You need to eat and drink half as much as a human. When you take a long rest, you must do so either in sunlight, under a UV light, or for an additional 2 hours, or suffer -1 to your Dexterity Score. Additionally, you only need $$750 worth of food and drink to proc Stuffed Tough.
  - **Animal I Have Become.** Your horns are natural melee weapons, which you can use to make unarmed strikes. If you hit with them, you deal piercing damage equal to 1d6 + your strength modifier, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.
  - **Awake.** Your walking speed is increased by 5 feet, you can't be put to sleep by tech, and you only require four hours of light sleep.
  - **Down With the Sickness.** You can choose one trait from the Major Splicer Traits list. Natural Biodiversity cannot inherit this trait. This major trait adds a destabilization risk to your DNA, meaning you cannot gain any Minor Splicer Traits in the future.
