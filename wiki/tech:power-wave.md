##### Source: Player's Handbook (Destructive Wave)

*5th-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self (30 ft radius)

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You strike the ground, creating a shockwave of nanite-driven hypersonic energy that ripples outward from you. Each creature you choose within 30 feet of you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 5d6 sonic damage, as well as 5d6 neon or entropic damage (your choice), and be knocked prone. A creature that succeeds on its saving throw takes half as much damage and isn’t knocked prone.
