**Name.** Emmi Remington

**Lineage.** Splicer (Wolf)

**Age.** Uncertain!

**Height and Weight.** Uncertain!

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Affiliation.** [Poncy's Bar](/loc:poncys-bar)

**Features.** Dusty red fur

**Status.** Alive

Emmi Remington carries a light pack and a toolbox, lightly decorated with stickers, as well as a wrench slung over her shoulder. She's dressed in a loose grey jumpsuit, with the stylized letters 'HGI' inside a test tube on the back—the logo of Happsworth Global Innovations. Around her neck is a soft red woolen scarf, distinctly hand-made. At her side is a small machine with a ball-shaped core and four stubby legs.

The wolf is currently under employ by Poncy's as their machine mechanic. Even with the new machines procured at Poncy's, they still need regular maintenance—and who better than Emmi, who has worked there for so long, to provide it?

**Owner.** *Unplayed. This is a retired player's PC, and is retained historically as Poncy's oldest employee.*
