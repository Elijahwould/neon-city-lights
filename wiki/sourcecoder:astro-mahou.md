## “In the name of the Moon, I will punish you!”

The moon, the stars, the sun, and other celestial bodies have long been a part of humanity's curiosity. While space travel and fallout of the Old World has quashed most of this line of questioning, few still carry that same spark of awe when thinking about what's above. The movements of comets and the orbits of asteroids have all been mapped out to be predictable down to the decimal, and the belief that fate can be changed due to their positions has long been a hypothesis. For a source coder, that becomes their reality, as using the exact position of certain X, Y, and Z-axis values of objects allow them to manipulate users' codes in ways unheard of up until now. Whether this was an intended function of the source code is anyone's guess.

##### Source: Dragonlance: Shadow of the Dragon Queen (Lunar Sorcery)

### Celestial Embodiment

At 1st level you learn additional techs when you reach certain levels in this class, as shown on the Astronomy Techs table. Each tech counts as a source coder tech for you, but it doesn’t count against the number of source coder techs you know. These techs can’t be replaced when you gain a level in this class.

Whenever you finish a long rest, you can choose what astral position manifests its power through your tech: X-Star, Y-Asteroid, or Z-Moon. While in the chosen phase, you can cast one 1st-level tech of the associated phase in the Astronomy Techs table once without expending a tech slot. Once you have cast a tech in this way, you can't do so again until you finished a long rest.

### Shine Down Sparkle

Starting at 1st level, you can call down the radiant light of the nighttime skybox on command. You learn the *[neon shower](/tech:neon-shower)* tech, which doesn't count against the number of source coder tricks you know. When you cast the tech, you can target one creature as normal or target two creatures within range that are within 5 feet of each other.

### Placement of Good Fortune

Beginning at 6th level, the current position of your Celestial Embodiment can affect your Metatech feature. Each Celestial Embodiment position is associated with certain sectors of tech, as shown here:

- **X-Star.** Protection and observation techs
- **Y-Asteroid.** Altercation and biochemistry techs
- **Z-Moon.** Observation and manipulation techs

Whenever you use Metatech on a tech of a sector of tech associated with your current Celestial Embodiment position, you can reduce the source points spent by 1 (minimum 0). You can reduce the source points spent for your Metatech a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.)

### Spatial Realignment

At 6th level, you gain greater control over the positions of your space-based technology. As a bonus action, you can spend 1 source point to change your current Celestial Embodiment phase to a different one.

You can now cast one 1st-level tech from each astral position of the Astronomy Techs table once without expending a tech slot, provided your current phase is the same as the astral position tech. Once you cast a astral position tech in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.

### The Horoscope's Empowerment

Beginning at 14th level, the power of a astral position saturates your being. While you are in a Celestial Embodiment phase, you also gain the following benefit associated with that position:

- **X-Star.** You can use a bonus action to shed bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet or to douse the light. In addition, you and creatures of your choice have advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) and Wisdom (Perception) checks while within the bright light you shed.
- **Y-Asteroid.** You have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. In addition, while you are entirely in darkness, attack rolls have disadvantage against you.
- **Z-Moon.** You have resistance to entropic and neon damage.

### Cosmic Power, The Final Frontier

At 18th level, as a bonus action, you can tap into a special power of your current Celestial Embodiment position. Alternatively, as part of the bonus action you take to change your astral position using the Spatial Realignment feature, you can immediately use the power of the astral position you are entering:

- **X-Star.** You radiate what seems to be starlight for a dazzling moment, blinding your foes and healing your allies. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your tech save DC or be blinded until the end of its next turn. In addition, one creature of your choice in that area regains 3d8 hit points.
- **Y-Asteroid.** You momentarily emanate the desolation of a rock in space, always there, but always cold. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw against your tech save DC or take 3d10 entropic damage and have its speed reduced to 0 until the end of its next turn. In addition, you become invisible until the end of your next turn, or until immediately after you make an attack roll or cast a tech.
- **Z-Moon.** You've fully tapped into the positional planes of your reality and can digitally teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within 60 feet of yourself. You can bring along one willing creature you can see within 5 feet of yourself. That creature teleports to an unoccupied space of your choice that you can see within 5 feet of your destination space. In addition, you and that creature gain resistance to all damage until the start of your next turn.

Once you use one of these bonus action benefits, you can't use that benefit again until you finish a long rest, unless you spend 5 source points to use it again.
