##### Source: Player's Handbook (Mass Heal)

*9th-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A flood of regenerative nanites spread out from you, injecting themselves into injured creatures around you and stitching their wounds closed. You restore up to 700 hit points, divided as you choose among any number of creatures that you can see within range. Creatures healed by this tech are also cured of all diseases and any effect making them blinded or deafened. This tech has no effect on hacked or machines.
