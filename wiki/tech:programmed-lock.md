##### Source: Player's Handbook (Arcane Lock)

*2nd-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (lockhacking software $$2,500, which the tech consumes)

**Duration:** Until dissipated

You touch a closed door, window, gate, chest, or other entryway, and it becomes locked for the duration.

You and the creatures you designate when you cast this tech can open the object normally. You can also set a password that, when spoken within 5 feet of the object, suppresses this tech for 1 minute. Otherwise, it is impassable until it is broken or the tech is dismissed or suppressed. Casting *[unlock](/tech:unlock)* on the object suppresses this tech for 10 minutes.

While affected by this tech, the object is more difficult to break or force open; the DC to break it or pick any locks on it increases by 10.
