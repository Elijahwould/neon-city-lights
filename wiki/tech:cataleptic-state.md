##### Source: Player's Handbook (Feign Death)

*3rd-level biochemistry (script)*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of graveyard dirt)

**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature and put it into a cataleptic state that is indistinguishable from death.

For the tech’s duration, or until you use an action to touch the target and dismiss the tech, the target appears dead to all outward inspection and to techs used to determine the target’s status. The target is blinded and incapacitated, and its speed drops to 0. The target has resistance to all damage except psychic damage. If the target is diseased or poisoned when you cast the tech, or becomes diseased or poisoned while under the tech’s effect, the disease and poison have no effect until the tech ends.
