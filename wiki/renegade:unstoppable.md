## "Ain't nothin'—ain't *nobody*—can beat me!"

It is the state of things that objects in motion tend to stay in motion. Just the same, a renegade in motion tends to stay that way, no matter what obstacle lies in their path. Ain't nobody higher on life, and then some, than a hyped up renegade on the path of the unstoppable. No telling what all ends up inside the body of the life of the party, but the unstoppable can feel the itch to stay ahead of the drop from that high, charging ahead to deliver the crash they're outrunning to whoever has the stupidest face in their vicinity.

##### Source: Tal'Dorei Reborn (Juggernaut)

### Outta The Way

Starting when you choose this path at 3rd level, your hype mingles with an unspeakable cocktail of aftereffects lingering inside you to push you and your enemies further. When you hit a creature with a melee attack while you're hyped up, you can push that creature up to 5 feet away from you in a direction of your choice. A creature that is Huge or larger makes a Strength saving throw with a DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier. On a success, the creature is not pushed.

Starting at 10th level, you can push a creature up to 10 feet when you hit it with a melee attack while hyped.

### Shift Your Weight

At 3rd level, you've figured out that standing still gets you killed. You never truly stop, bouncing around even when you're still. While you are hyped, you can't be knocked [prone](/con:prone) or moved along the ground against your will.

### Tear It Down

Starting at 6th level, your melee weapon attacks deal an extra 1d8 damage to machines, and deal double damage to objects and structures.

### Don't Let 'Em Catch You

Also at 6th level, you can lose yourself to your personal rhythm, like the ingrained beat of the last party you blacked out at. At the start of your turn, no action required, you can get jiggy with it until the start of your next turn. While defensively dancing, you can't be grappled, attack rolls against you have disadvantage, and your weapon attacks are made with disadvantage.

### Mosh Pit

Starting at 10th level, you've mastered shoving posers into the trampling of true fans. As a reaction when you push a creature at least 5 feet, you can then leap into an unoccupied space next to the creature. If you do so, the creature must succeed on a Strength saving throw with a DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier or be knocked prone. This leap costs no movement and does not provoke opportunity attacks.

Additionally, whenever you push a creature into a space within 5 feet of one of your allies, the ally can use its reaction to make a melee weapon attack against that creature.

### Can't Feel Nothin'

At 14th level, even running on fumes gives you that good buzz, numbing you to the fetters of life. While you're hyped, your speed cannot be reduced, and you are immune to the [frightened](/con:frightened), [paralyzed](/con:paralyzed), [prone](/con:prone), [rattled](/con:rattled) and [stunned](/con:stunned) conditions.

If you are [frightened](/con:frightened), [paralyzed](/con:paralyzed), [rattled](/con:rattled) or [stunned](/con:stunned), you can still use a bonus action to enter a hype, even if you can't otherwise take actions. You aren't affected by any of these conditions while hype.
