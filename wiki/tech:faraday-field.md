##### Source: Player's Handbook (Circle of Power)

*5th-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self (30 foot radius)

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Invisible protective nanites swirl around you, distorting and diffusing technical energy within 30 feet of you. Until the tech ends, the sphere moves with you, centered on you. For the duration, each friendly creature in the area (including you) has advantage on saving throws against tech and other technical effects.

Additionally, when an affected creature succeeds on a saving throw made against a technical or technical effect that allows it to make a saving throw to take only half damage, it instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throws.
