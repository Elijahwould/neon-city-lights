##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings)

You were born beyond the edge of the city, out in the endless deserts, searching for relics of the old world while hopping from oasis to oasis. You know how to work hungry and keep yourself hidden, from monsters and men alike, but something has drawn you to the megacities.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Survival, Stealth
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Trailblazer's kit, Weaponsmith's tools
- **Equipment.** Edge runners start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A tan cloak
  - A trailblazer's kit
  - A set of weaponsmith's tools
  - An air-filtering rebreather
  - A vellum map to a location out in the desert

### Feature: Storm Chaser

You’ve been given a supernatural sense of danger, giving advantage on perception checks focused on determining ambushes, traps, and changes in the weather.
