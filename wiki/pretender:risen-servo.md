## "As sure as prisons are the models for the city, I nurture my need for oil and electricity."

It has always been a curiosity, to some, to see how mechanical innovation has burgeoned into life all its own, counting among the vast population of Neon City, yet know that somewhere in the sand exists a line where emerge the hacked. The corruptive failures who reached too far. So reaching further are the Mask of the Risen Servo. They are hyperfixated upon death and all its variations and avoidances, picking apart at the existential dread painted as the foundation of modern tech; are you still you if you are a reconstruction of purely outward personality observations? Find out. Hack yourself. Don the dreaded name of "I."

### Humanoid-Hacked Firewall

When you don this mask at 3rd level, you sear a line in the sand of your self to maintain the distinction of what is and isn't hacked. You gain the following benefit:

- **MysOS Error Handler.** You can mitigate or intensify the signal interference of your nanophage to strengthen your errors or breaches respectively. You gain an additional Nanophage die. Additionally, you deal an additional Nanophage Potency of damage when utilizing the APOPTOSIS Lethal Error against hacked, and you have resistance to entropic damage while you hold the hyperlinked weapon, which sheds bright light out to a radius of 20 feet.

Additionally, your Lethal Error feature improves, and you learn the following error.

| Mask Error | Damage Type |
| --- | --- |
| ImmnDefnsErr.APOPTOSIS | Neon |

### Net Escape

Upon reaching 7th level, at start of your turn, if you aren't incapacitated, you can choose to technically escape between realms, using your early modwares as a digitization relay.

While escaped, you can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain, as well as see and affect creatures and objects on the etherealnet. You take 1d10 kinetic damage if you end your turn inside an object and are immediately shunted to the nearest unoccupied space. Once per turn while you are escaped, you can transmit up to 30 feet to a point you can see, requiring no action. This escaped state lasts for a number of rounds equal to your Nanophage modifier (minimum of 1) or until you end it earlier, requiring no action.

You can use this feature once. Beginning at 15th level, you can use this feature twice. You regain all expended uses when you finish a short or long rest.

### Monitor Chip Upgrade: Resistor

At 11th level, your Retributive Monitor Chip can act as a pull-up/pull-down signal interface that ensures a known state and strength, setting up targets for the same errors that fuel your weapons. Whenever you damage a chipped creature with your lethal error, your weapon deals an additional Nanophage Potency of the hyperlinked error's damage. In addition, the chipped creature cannot move through other creatures or objects through any means.

### LiNKER Receptor Modware

At 15th level, you've taken to outdated machine and cybernetic mods with illegal modifications that retroactively write your personality data into your LiNKER Screening results, warding it from hacked. You cannot be [charmed](/con:charmed) or [frightened](/con:frightened) by hacked, nor can you be possessed.

In addition, you can alter your screening results to include your modware, weaving nanophage through metal additions to your body. As a bonus action, you can spend and roll one Nanophage die, losing hit points equal to the result, to gain one of the following effects for the next hour:

- **Bio-Neuro Retro Cybernetics.** Your ability scores can never be reduced below 1 and if your ability scores would be reduced, you can use your reaction to half the amount they would be reduced by, rounding down to a minimum of 1.

- **Cerebrospinal Sandbox.** You cannot be effected by viral programming, such as trojans. If you are under the effects of a trojan when you activate this modware, the effects are suppressed for the duration.

- **Nimble 27 Seeker Lenses.** You can see through technical darkness up to 30 feet. You can also see [invisible](/con:invisible) creatures and objects within 30 feet of you.

- **Save/Get Intracircuitry.** Your maximum hit points cannot be reduced, and whenever you are subjected to an effect from a creature that would deal damage and reduce your maximum hit points at the same time, you can use your reaction to half the damage dealt.

You can have any number of effects active at the same time. You can spend multiple Nanophage die as part of the same action to activate multiple modwares at the same time.

### Absolute Datasync

At 18th level, the mask has become the face, you have made a mockery of the immortality of software and nested it in flesh and metal, as every hacked has failed to do, your very thoughts strung out between every byte and error. When you are reduced to 0 hit points while you wield a weapon with a hyperlinked lethal error, you can choose to end the hyperlinked lethal error to drop to 1 hit point instead. If you have multiple lethal errors hyperlinked on different weapons, you can choose which one ends.
