**Corporation Name.** BLAM! Co. Specialized Arms and Heavy Ordinace Dealing

**CEO.** Mr. BLAM!

**Years in business.** ~50

**Industry.** Weapons Manufacturing and Distributing

**Motto.** *"If it don't explode, it's not from BLAM Co.!"*

**Impact.** Minor

BLAM! Co. came about around 50 years ago, after a Jerboan named Jax Tikentoker noticed a distinct lack of heavy ordinance and explosives avaliable to the public, along with "less-lethal" weapons. Determined to fill these needs, he created BLAM! Co. and started producing heavy ballistic and laser weapons, explosives, and—inspired by how sex-filled the city was—the "Lewdacious" series of Anti-Riot, less-than-lethal weapons.

Their current CEO, Mr. BLAM, was a cute robotic mascot obsessed with explosives, but became sapient after enough time had passed, and learned all he could about business and worked his way up to CEO. Now the rat-shaped robot runs the company with rigorous vigor and open-hand policies and is one of the few CEOs that treats lesser employees with kindness. Don't ask—we don't know how, either.

BLAM Co. is known for their reliable weapons and their overkill design philosophy. "If it don't explode, it's not from BLAM Co.!" Rivals include [Axigon Arms Dealing](/corp:axigon-arms) and [Armata Solutions, Inc.](/corp:armata)
