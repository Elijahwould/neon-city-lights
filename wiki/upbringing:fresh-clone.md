##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings)

Some hyper-wealthy sonofabitch decided they wanted a mini-me around and cooked up some stem cells to make you. Maybe it was ego, maybe it was whim, maybe it was to have a fresh set of organs around in case they needed a transplant. You grew up in relative luxury, making yourself useful in small ways around the penthouse, but as you came of age, your usefulness began to decline. You've somehow managed to escape their influence, but their organization still haunts you.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Athletics, Medicine
- **Tool Proficiencies.** First aid kit, Cook's utensils
- **Equipment.** Fresh clones start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A cooking apron
  - A number of small tattoos across your person which read "SPARE PARTS"
  - A set of cook's utensils
  - A first aid kit

### Feature: Don't I Know You?

On character creation, choose an organization or corporation. Your originator is from there. People familiar with your originator can recognize your face. The age difference is a dead giveaway, but even so, it can have a subtle influence. You have advantage on the first Charisma (Persuasion) or Charisma (Deception) check made against someone who recognizes you (DM's discretion as to when that happens).
