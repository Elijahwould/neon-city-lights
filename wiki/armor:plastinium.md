*Medium armor, 2 lb., AC = 13 + Dex modifier (max 2)*

Plastinium armor basically consists of garments that have thin plates made of a compound of nanoplastics and aluminum which keep them somewhat flexible while offering just a bit more protection than slinging a leather jacket over your body. The benefit of it is that it's light enough as a result, but it is still more restrictive than light armor.
