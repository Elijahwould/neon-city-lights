##### Source: Explorer's Guide to Wildemount (Gravity Fissure)

*6th-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self (100-foot line)

**Components:** V, S, M (a fistful of iron filings)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You manifest a ravine of gravitational energy in a line originating from you that is 100 feet long and 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 8d8 kinetic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

Each creature within 10 feet of the line but not in it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 8d8 kinetic damage and be pulled toward the line until the creature is in its area.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 7th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 6th.
