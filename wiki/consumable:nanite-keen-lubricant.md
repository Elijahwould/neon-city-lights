*Consumable (substance), very rare*

(Insert Description Here.)

This clear, gelatinous lubricant sparkles with tiny, ultrathin obsidian shards. The oil can coat one slashing or piercing weapon or up to 5 pieces of slashing or piercing ammunition. Applying the oil takes 1 minute. For 1 hour, the coated item is technical and has a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls.
