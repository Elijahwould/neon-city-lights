##### Source: Player's Handbook (Magic Initiate), Neon City NightsThanks to Casters & Co.#6381 for this one!

Choose a class: Influencer, Ordinator, Shut-In, Source Coder, Streamer, or Techwizard. You learn two tricks of your choice from that class's tech list.

In addition, choose one 1st level tech to learn from that same list. You always have that tech prepared. You can cast it once without a tech slot at its lowest level, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a long rest. You can also cast the tech using any spell slots that you have.

Your techcasting ability for these techs depends on the class you chose: Charisma for Influencer, Source Coder, or Streamer; Wisdom for Ordinator or Shut-In; or Intelligence for Techwizard.
