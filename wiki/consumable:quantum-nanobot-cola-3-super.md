*Consumable (soda), rare*

As opposed to the health appeal offered by Nano-Cola+, Quantum Cola's™ Nanobot Cola SUPER is a drink pandered to a higher variety of customer. The brown concoction has brought its sugary taste back in full force, and it has faint notes of cinnamon and nutmeg that disguise a higher quality construction of restorative nanites. Packaging is black, red, and blue, and reuses the "+" design with a tacked-on "SUPER."

Nano-SUPER bubbles with a faint blue static when agitated that makes it perfect for chugging, or for long sipping experiences.

You regain 8d4+8 hit points when you drink this Nano-SUPER, which you can personally perform as a bonus action. When self-administered as an action, you regain the maximum value of hit points (40).
