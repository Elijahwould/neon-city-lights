**Location Type.** Business

**Years in existence.** < 1

**Type.** Tailor & Armorer

**Area.** [Neon City Downtown Residential District](/district:downtown-residential)

**Impact.** Minor

After having spent a few months in the running scene, Geo Albright had the idea to share his passion for practical fashion with the citizens of Neon City. His starting selections were rather basic, consisting of regular t-shirts and hoodies, but he later branched out into various other articles of clothing. In addition, he began to throw in techniques carried over from his running style to produce and sell all kinds of weapons and armor, even including some elemental weapons not unlike the ones utilized in his running gear. It was a step up to say the least since before he would simply throw in a free taser or can of pepper spray with the purchase of an item in hopes of keeping his customers safe on the streets. It's clear to say that lots of determination and experience comes a long way.

Eager customers can see what items are readily available for purchase as well as book appointments by navigating his website. There is also a Flutter account in Albright Armory's name, though one could come to the conclusion that it used to be the owner's personal account before it was reimagined for business. The screencaps and clips from numerous video games are a dead ringer.
