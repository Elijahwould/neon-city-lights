##### Source: Player's Handbook (Aura of Life)

*4th-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self (30-foot radius)

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Nanite-bots exit from you in an aura with a 30-foot radius. Until the tech ends, the aura moves with you, centered on you. Each non-hostile creature in the aura (including you) has resistance to entropic damage, and its hit point maximum can’t be reduced. In addition, a non-hostile, living creature regains 1 hit point when it starts its turn in the aura with 0 hit points.
