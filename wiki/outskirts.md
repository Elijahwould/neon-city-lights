## "There is life beyond the walls."

While there is far more world beyond the borders of the city, it is not the world of old. Blasted wastelands, unconquered forests, and burning sands live in places that once, long ago, had been cities, monuments, and natural wonders. Ancient ruins can be found that are teeming with monstrous beasts or sadistic technocults, but also often are filled to the brim with artefacts and technological wonders that have withstood the passage of time.

Settlements and outposts are scattered through this graveyard of steel and sand. Often, these are nowhere near matching the level of automation and standards of living that can be found in Neon City. These locations tend to act as resupply points for runners and nomadic desert dwellers, and as such are respected by those who walk the wastes.█
