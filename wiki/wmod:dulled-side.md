Most melee slashing weapons either have a single edge that is incredibly sharp or are balanced around having two of them that are equally so. Some people will purposefully dull one of those edges so they can have aim to have an option to break bones rather than rend the flesh. A slashing weapon with this modification can deal bludgeoning damage instead.
