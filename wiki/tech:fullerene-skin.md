##### Source: Player's Handbook (Stoneskin)

*4th-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

This tech gifts a willing creature you touch a skin of ceramic ablative armor. Until the tech ends, the target has resistance to nontechnical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
