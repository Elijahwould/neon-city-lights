##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Ring of Animal Influence)

*Keyring, rare*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$65,000

This ring has 3 charges, and it regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the ring, you can use an action to expend 1 of its charges to cast one of the following techs:

- [Pack Lead](/tech:pack-leader) (save DC 13)
- [Buck's Bad Trip](/tech:bucks-bad-trip) (save DC 13), targeting only critters that have an Intelligence of 3 or lower
- [Doolittle's Dialog](/tech:doolittles-dialog)
