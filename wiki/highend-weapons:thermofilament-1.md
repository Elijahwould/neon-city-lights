##### Source: Neon City Nights

*Weapon (any melee or ranged weapon), rare (requires installation)*

**Specs:** Variable, $$1,500,000

This weapon is centered around an extremely powerful heating element that vents throughout the entirety of the weapon, enshrined in conductive metals and plastics. You gain a +1 bonus to the attack rolls of this weapon, and it deals an extra 1d6 fire damage on a hit.

You can use a bonus action to release the safety limits of this weapon, causing it to erupt into flames. These flames shed bright light in a 40-foot radius and dim light for an additional 40 feet. While the weapon is ablaze, it deals an extra 1d6 fire damage to any target it hits. The flames last until you use a bonus action to reengage the safety, you stow the weapon, or begin your turn without the weapon in your hands.

While the weapon is ablaze, any target you hit with it gains a single charge of burn. The thermofilament weapon can only apply an amount of burn equal to double its rarity bonus to a single target. As a reaction, if a target you can see has any amount of burn, you can automatically reengage the weapon’s safety to superheat the debris left on the target and set them explosively aflame, dealing 1d6 fire damage for each charge of burn. This removes all burn from the target, even if it was applied by a different source.

***Blades.*** A blade is any melee BEA:B/B. Whenever you roll a 20 on your attack roll with a blade, each creature of your choice within 5 feet of the target takes 5 damage of the type dealt by a rare BEA:B/B, 10 damage of the type dealt by a very rare BEA:B/B, and 15 damage of the type dealt by a legendary BEA:B/B.

***Blasters.*** A blaster is any ranged BEA:B/B. Whenever you make an attack roll with a blaster, the ammunition supplies itself from a collection of near-surging nanites. You may still provide your own ammo to benefit from the bonuses of specific ammo, and it is consumed as normal.
