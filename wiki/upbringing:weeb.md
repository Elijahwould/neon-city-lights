##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings) + Neon City Nights

You’ve spent your whole life studying the blade. Well, not really. You’ve spent your whole life with a fascination of certain cultures that make a certain exotic blade, as well as the toys and media that those cultures reproduce. Still, you paid a lot of money for all that, and now it’s time to show it off.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Culture, History
- **Tool Proficiencies.** None
- **Weapon Proficiencies.** Katana
- **Equipment.** Weebs start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A graphic t-shirt with something bizarre and colorful printed on it
  - A pair of khaki shorts
  - A well-kept katana
  - A selection of plastic statuettes
  - A credchip worth $$1,000

### Feature: Otakubabble

Your historical knowledge of Ancient J.A.V. Lore can overload the minds of the non-initiated, allowing you to confound and perplex others. You can roll Intelligence (Culture) in place of Charisma (Deception) value. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
