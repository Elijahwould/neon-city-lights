##### Source: Player's Handbook (Vampiric Touch)

*3rd-level biochemistry*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

The touch of your nanite-wreathed hand can siphon vitality from others to heal your wounds. Make a melee tech attack against a creature within your reach. On a hit, the target takes 3d6 necrotic damage, and you regain hit points equal to half the amount of entropic damage dealt. Until the tech ends, you can make the attack again on each of your turns as an action.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd.
