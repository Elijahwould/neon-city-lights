##### Source: Player's Handbook (Thaumaturgy)

*Manipulation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** V

**Duration:** 1 minute

You manifest a digital coincedence, a sign of Artificial Interference, within range. You create one of the following technical effects within range:

- Your voice is boosted up to three times as loud as normal for 1 minute.
- You cause the light from a lightbulb, a flashlight, or a small camp oven to flicker, brighten, dim, or change color for 1 minute.
- You harmlessly shake the ground with hard bass for 1 minute.
- You create an instantaneous sound that originates from a point of your choice within range, such as the fizzle of neon signage, the squeak of city rats, or technocultist whispers.
- You instantaneously cause an unlocked door or window to fly open or slam shut.
- You alter the appearance of your eyes for 1 minute.

If you cast this tech multiple times, you can have up to three of its 1-minute effects active at a time, and you can dismiss such an effect as an action.
