##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Wand of Polymorph)

*Peripheral, very rare (requires installation by a techcaster)*

**Specs:** 1 lb., $$1,300,000

This wand has 7 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 of its charges to cast the [Reformat](/tech:reformat) tech (save DC 15) from it.

The wand regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the wand's last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the wand runs afoul of some obscure Corporate law and self-destructs.
