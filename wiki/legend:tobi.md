**Name.** Tobi Greene

**Lineage.** Heavy Loader Remake (formerly Street Elf)

**Age.** ~240

**Height and Weight.** 8'0", 500 lbs.

**Pronouns.** he/him

**Classes/Subclasses.** Enforcer (Loyal) 20

**Features.** Dark grey armored chassis, a complete lack of visible facial features poorly counteracted by a pair of cheap sunglasses held in place by a winter hat, massive dark green hoodie and pair of cargo pants

**Status.** Alive

Tobi Greene's past doesn't matter too much, as the details are hazy for him anyway. But what matters is that he's a former combat medic and bodyguard for Osaka, dying from a headshot protecting a VIP during an ambush by… someone bad, probably. It would appear that his loyalty was something someone thought should be rewarded, so they scooped up what was left of his brain and went through the engramization process, shoving him into a heavy remake body so that he may continue his service to the corporation.

At least, that was the plan.

Osaka collapsed on itself before his new body could be activated. Leaving him suspended and awaiting orders that would never arrive, he woke up decades later, stepping out of the ruins of Osaka tower and towards a life of freedom.

Still, you really can't teach an old dog new tricks, and he quickly found himself in the running business, proving himself time and time again to be a stalwart and incorruptible defender of his new friends and allies. Early on in his career, he brought food to a job in progress, and an unnamed runner complimented his cooking. He proceeded to bring food on every job he ever went to, eventually starting a food truck called ["Robit Sando Stando"](/loc:robit-sando-stando) with his fellow runner [Katze](/legend:katze), quickly becoming well loved by the people of the city. To this day, Tobi still runs jobs with his fellow runners, and the Sando Stando continues to be a popular place to pick up sandwiches of all kinds—with the fried chicken sandwich being a perennial favorite.
