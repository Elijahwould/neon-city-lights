**Name.** Ernest "Rucker" Beauregard

**Lineage.** Remake, half-Heavy Loader, half-Life Lite

**Age.** 64

**Height and Weight.** 6'6", 310 lbs.

**Pronouns.** he/they

**Affiliation.** [N.C.A.P.S.](/org:ncaps)

**Features.** Tall, seeming mix of effeminate upper body fitted with limbs that are a bit too big and strong, wide hips, short, fluffed white and orange hair that's been carefully side-trimmed

**Status.** Alive

Rucker is a bit of a conundrum. They should be pretty old, given the circumstances, but they look like a young, effeminate male who happens to be pretty tall and shapely. His bottom half is clearly that of a remake, a heavy loader with extremely powerful legs, though they are designed to have wide hips and a stocky build. His arms are the same: a mostly metal chassis with limited synthflesh, made of covered polysteel and plastic shieldings, mostly intended for the work of a heavy loader. What's unusual is that his torso and head are… they're more life-like than even a life-lite, which is the cause of some confusion. Are they part life-lite, or part artificiate?

Still, here they stand, a mix of the seemingly-organic with powerful polysteel appendages, somehow cutting an oddly androgynous—if not quite effeminate—frame. They stand in overalls, looking through what seems to be some sort of tablet deck, and are usually found with a bottle of cola in the other hand, from which they regularly seem to drink, further adding to the mystique as to what he really is. They have a mischievous set of eyes that will track the party’s every movement. When they speak, it's with a measured, focused intensity. They very clearly don't suffer anyone’s bullshit. They are the kind of person willing to sell many different things to whoever eventually decides to come by the Old Shit Shack—and they also protect that place, fiercely.
