## “What seems impossible today will one day become your warm-up.”

Personal trainers use their influence to show their followers how to be healthier—or at least how to seem healthier. They might teach yoga, tai chi, weightlifting, or athletic sexual positions, but their teachings are always intensely physical. Between all of their sweating and grunting, they tend to shill for various life-improvement products, ranging from protein drinks to jade eggs to shove up your ass.

##### From: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Swords)

### Bonus Proficiencies

When you join the Channel of the Personal Trainer at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with medium armor and the Athletics skill. If you were previously trained in Athletics, you gain expertise in the Athletics skill.

If you’re proficient with a civilian or military melee weapon, you can use it as a focus for your influencer techs.

### Fighting Style

At 3rd level, you adopt a particular style of fighting as your specialty. Choose one of the following options. You can’t take a Fighting Style option more than once, even if you later get to choose again.

- **Dueling.** When you are wielding a melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that weapon.

- **Pugilism.** Your unarmed strikes can deal bludgeoning damage equal to 1d6 + your Strength modifier on a hit. If you aren’t wielding any weapons or a shield when you make the attack roll, the d6 increases to d8.

- **Two-Weapon Fighting.** When you engage in two-weapon fighting, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of the second attack.

### Impressive Flourish

At 3rd level, you learn to conduct impressive displays of martial prowess and speed.

Whenever you take the Attack action on your turn, your walking speed increases by 10 feet until the end of the turn, and if a weapon attack that you make as part of this action hits a creature, you can use one of the following Impressive Flourish options of your choice. You can use only one Impressive Flourish option per turn.

***Defensive Flourish.*** You can expend one use of your Influencer Inspiration to cause the weapon to deal extra damage to the target you hit. The damage equals the number you roll on the Influencer Inspiration die. You also add the number rolled to your AC until the start of your next turn.

***Aggressive Flourish.*** You can expend one use of your Influencer Inspiration to cause the weapon to deal extra damage to the target you hit and to any other creature of your choice that you can see within 5 feet of you. The damage equals the number you roll on the Influencer Inspiration die.

***Mobile Flourish.*** You can expend one use of your Influencer Inspiration to cause the weapon to deal extra damage to the target you hit. The damage equals the number you roll on the Influencer Inspiration die. You can also push the target up to 5 feet away from you, plus a number of feet equal to the number you roll on that die. You can then immediately use your reaction to move up to your walking speed to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the target.

### Extra Attack

Starting at 6th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

### Masterful Flourish

Starting at 14th level, whenever you use an Impressive Flourish option, you can roll a d6 and use it instead of expending a Influencer Inspiration die.
