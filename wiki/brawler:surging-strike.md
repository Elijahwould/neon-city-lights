## "The laws of thermodynamics cannot be broken—but they are amenable to a bit of bending."

One of the strangest elements of modern technology is the usage of gravitic fields to rapidly shift matter between its five possible states—gaseous, liquid, solid, plasma, and phased. These rapid matter shifts, colloquially known as “surges,” allow tech to generate intense heat and cold, condense liquids from the air, and seemingly create solid objects from nothing. The Clan of the Surging Strike has mastered methods of allowing their CHI implant to manipulate surgetech, effectively permitting them to manipulate the state of matter by punching it real hard.

### Initiate of Xploits

When you choose this clan at 3rd level, you learn how to manipulate the four elements in subtle ways. You learn two of the following tricks: *[pyrocache](/tech:pyrocache)*, *[aerogrid](/tech:aerogrid)*, *[geomesh](/tech:geomesh)*, or *[hydroscript](/tech:hydroscript)*. You learn two additional tricks from this list at 6th level.

### Surge Disciple

When you choose this clan at 3rd level, you learn disciplines that harness the power of the four states of matter. A discipline requires you to spend CHI points each time you use it. You learn two surge disciplines of your choice, which are detailed in the surge disciplines sections below. You learn two additional surge disciplines of your choice at 6th, 11th, and 17th level. Whenever you learn a new surge discipline, you can also replace one surge discipline that you already know with a different discipline.

***Casting Surge Techs.*** Some surge disciplines allow you to cast techs. To cast one of these techs, you use its casting time and other rules, but you don’t need to provide material components for it. When you cast a tech with a discipline, it is cast at the tech's lowest level unless otherwise specified.

Once you reach 5th level in this class, you can spend additional CHI points to increase the level of an surge discipline tech that you cast, provided that the tech has an enhanced effect at a higher level, as *[flame vent](/tech:flame-vent)* does. The tech’s level increases by 1 for each additional CHI point you spend. For example, if you are a 5th-level brawler and use Plasma Jet to cast *[flame vent](/tech:flame-vent)*, you can spend 2 CHI points to cast it as a 2nd-level tech (the discipline’s base cost of 1 CHI point plus 1).

The maximum number of CHI points (its base CHI point cost plus any additional points) that you can spend on the spell is 2, unless a discipline specifies otherwise. This maximum increases to 3 at 9th level, 4 at 13th level, and 5 at 17th level.

### Surge Disciplines

#### Aqueous Whip

You summon a long, rubbery whip of pure water that you grip by one end. You can spend 1 CHI point as a bonus action to create a whip of water that shoves and pulls a creature to unbalance it. A creature that you can see that is within 30 feet of you must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes bludgeoning damage equal to your Martial Arts die + your Wisdom modifier, plus an extra 1d10 bludgeoning damage for each additional CHI point you spend, and you can either knock it prone or pull it up to 25 feet closer to you. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage, and you don’t pull it or knock it prone. You can’t use Aqueous Manipulation and cast a tech during the same turn.

#### ATP Absorption

You channel the incoming energy, and exploit it for your own ends. You can spend 1 CHI point to cast *[energy converter](/tech:energy-converter)*.

#### Blast Wave

You send a blast of air outward with a thrust of your open palm. You can spend 2 CHI points to cast *[aeroblast](/tech:aeroblast)*.

#### Condensation Manipulation

You can spend 1 CHI point to cast *[(de)construct water](/tech:deconstruct-water)*.

#### Cyclone Strike

You summon a swirling wind and concentrate it around your fist. When you make an unarmed attack on your turn, you can choose to strike out condensed bursts of air which stretch out beyond your normal reach. Your reach increases by 10 feet for that attack. If the attack hits you can spend 1 CHI point to force the target to make a Strength saving throw or be pushed back 10 feet and be knocked prone.

#### Effortless Step

Hydraulic technology helps you reach new heights as you jump. Your jump height and distance are doubled (quadrupled with Step of the Wind) and you gain advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks related to jumping.

#### Enduring Stance of Bedrock

Your CHI implants harden in response to trauma. When you use the Dodge action, you cannot be moved, pushed, grappled, frightened, or knocked prone against your will until the start of your next turn, even if technically compelled to do so. You must be standing on the ground to use this ability.

#### Frozen Bullet

You summon a shard of razorsharp ice and fling it at your foe. You can spend 1 CHI point to cast *[cryoshiv](/tech:cryoshiv)*.

#### Groundbreaker

You shake the ground, causing a tremor that knocks over your foes. You can spend 1 CHI point to cast *[jackhammer](/tech:jackhammer)*.

#### Ice Glide

Whenever you take the Dash action, until the end of your turn for any movement you make along a surface you can leave a trail of slippery ice. This ice counts as difficult terrain and lasts until the start of your next turn.

#### Mach One Punch

The force of your punch makes a vacuum of air, causing a thunderous boom that blows away everything around you. You can spend 1 CHI point to cast *[shockwave](/tech:shockwave)*.

#### Plasma Cutter

Your arms and legs become enveloped in roaring gouts of flame. When you make an unarmed attack on your turn, you can choose to strike out tendrils of flames which stretch out beyond your normal reach. Your reach increases by 5 feet for that attack, and it deals fire damage instead of bludgeoning damage. If the attack hits you can spend 1 CHI point to deal an extra 1d10 fire damage.

#### Plasma Jet

You can spend 1 CHI points to cast *[flame vent](/tech:flame-vent)*.

#### Badger-esque Excavation

Your appendages harden themselves for digging, as the floor becomes a mirror you can see through. You can spend 2 CHI points as a bonus action to gain tremorsense with a range of 30 feet and a burrow speed equal to half of your walking speed for up to 1 minute. Your movement leaves behind a tunnel that remains for as long as this ability is active, and then collapses.

#### Curtain of Ferocious Wind

Upgrades to your ventilation system can give you a personal edge in battle. You can spend 2 CHI points to cast *[dispersal field](/tech:dispersal-field)*.

#### Flamethrower Lash

You can spend 2 CHI points to cast *[flamethrower](/tech:flamethrower)*.

#### Flourish of the Furnace

You reach out with your CHI implant to manually hack metal, causing it to burst into flames. You can spend 2 CHI points to cast *[thermal runaway](/tech:thermal-runaway)*.

#### Fuck It, We Ball

Your CHI manifests as a roaring orb of fire. You can spend 2 CHI points to cast *[searing ball](/tech:searing-ball)*.

#### Grasp of the Ground

The power of the earth is in the palm of your hand. Literally! You can spend 2 CHI points to cast *[concrete clutch](/tech:concrete-clutch)*.

#### Imbedded Thrusters

Upgrades to your spinal vents allow for you to have greater control in the air. Your Slow Fall ability no longer requires a reaction to use, and instead you may use it automatically whenever you are conscious and not incapacitated, and it negates all falling damage regardless of distance. When you use the Slow Fall ability, you may also glide along the air to move 5 feet in any horizontal direction for every 5 feet that you fall.

#### Impenetrable Sudden Slab

You summon an concrete barrier to defend yourself or an ally. As a reaction, you may spend 2 CHI points to use your Deflect Missiles ability against an attack you can see within 30 feet of you. Any damage not prevented by this ability is taken by the original target of the attack. If you reduce the damage to 0, you can’t make an attack with it.

#### Kaiju's Volley

Rays of fire spring from your outstretched hand to sear your foes. You can spend 2 CHI points to cast *[incendiary salvo](/tech:incendiary-salvo)*.

#### Snowed-in Lockdown

No one really expects a brawler to have a snow cannon on standby, huh? You can spend 2 CHI points to cast *[snowball cannon](/tech:snowball-cannon)*.

#### Vibratory Strike

You can spend 2 CHI points to cast *[blowout](/tech:blowout)*.

#### Water Jet

You can spend 2 CHI points as an action to unleash a jet of water in a line that is 30 feet long and 5 feet wide. Each creature in the line must make a Strength saving throw, taking bludgeoning damage equal to your Martial Arts die + your Wisdom modifier on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. In addition, you can move each target that fails its saving throw to any unoccupied space touching the line.

#### Wind's Chokehold

You can spend 3 CHI points to cast *[seize person](/tech:seize-person)*.

#### Aerial Step

You can spend 4 CHI points to cast *[hover bots](/tech:hover-bots)*, targeting yourself, except your fly speed is equal to your movement speed.

#### Deep-Sea Evolution

Aquatic adaptations to your implant serve you in the unknown depths. You spend 2 CHI points as an action to gain underwater adaptations for 8 hours. While this ability is active, you can breath normally underwater, you gain a swim speed equal to your walking speed, and you gain blindsight with a radius of 60 feet while underwater.

#### Earthsplitter Strike

You can spend 3 CHI points to cast *[concrete geyser](/tech:concrete-geyser)*.

#### Flesh-Hardening Technique

You can spend 4 CHI points to cast *[fullerene skin](/tech:fullerene-skin)*, targeting yourself.

#### Fist of the Elements

The elements charge your weapon. You can spend 3 CHI points to cast *[energize weapon](/tech:energize-weapon)*, choosing from only the damage types cold, fire, or thunder. Your unarmed strikes count as a weapon for the purposes of this discipline.

#### Hydraulic Push

You conjure up a mighty wave of water that crashes down on your foes. You can spend 3 CHI to cast *[surf's up](/tech:surfs-up)*.

#### Hydrokinetic Manipulation

You touch the body of water with your CHI, coaxing it to change its form. You can spend 4 CHI points to cast *[hydrokinesis](/tech:hydrokinesis)*.

#### Plasma Burst

You can spend 3 CHI points to cast *[incendiary blast](/tech:incendiary-blast)*.

#### Plasma Wall

You can spend 4 CHI points to cast *[pyrobarricade](/tech:pyrobarricade)*.

#### Rain of Forgotten Glaciers

Letting out a burst of nitrocoolant, you cool the air in the skies above you, condensing the water droplets into freezing rain and sleet. You can spend 3 CHI points to cast *[coolant storm](/tech:coolant-storm)*.

#### Sand Wall

You throw pocket sand on the ground, summoning a wall of sand to serve as your barricade. You can spend 3 CHI to cast *[psammobarricade](/tech:psammobarricade)*.

#### Six Ways to Sunday

You summon six spheres of earth from the ground, suspend them in the air around you, and ignite them with the fire from your CHI implant. With a thought, you launch these blazing meteors at your foe. You can spend 3 CHI points to cast *[hot shots](/tech:hot-shots)*.

#### Surgefist Stance

You effortlessly flow between martial and surgetech assault. When you use your action to use an surge discipline, you can make one unarmed strike as a bonus action, or you can use your Flurry of Blows.

#### Vapor Self

You can spend 3 CHI points to cast *[cloud digitization](/tech:cloud-digitization)*, targeting yourself.

#### It's Time to Slam Now

You conjure up a sphere of water that engulfs and imprisons foes that touch its surface. You can spend 4 CHI to cast *[soaking ball](/tech:soaking-ball)*.

#### Water Wall

You can spend 3 CHI to cast *[hydrobarricade](/tech:hydrobarricade)*.

#### Wind Wall

A wall of strong wind rises from the ground, batting away anything in its path. You can spend 3 CHI points to cast *[aerobarricade](/tech:aerobarricade)*.

#### Aerokinetic Manipulation

You can spend 5 CHI points to cast *[aerokinesis](/tech:aerokinesis)*.

#### A.P.E.X. Avatar

As an ultimate display of your mastery of the elements, now have access to The Almighty Perpetual Element Xploit, also known as A.P.E.X Surgetech. You can spend 5 CHI points as an action to have the states of matter form a protective sphere around your body, gaining multiple benefits for 1 minute. While this ability is active, you have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, acid, cold, fire, lightning, and sonic damage. You also gain a burrow, fly, and swim speed equal to your movement speed. Lastly, you can use any of the following abilities as a bonus action:

- You create a small earthquake on the ground in a 15-foot radius around you. Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 3d8 bludgeoning damage and is knocked prone.

- You create a line of fire 15 feet long and 5 feet wide extending from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the line must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 5d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

- You create a 15-foot cube of swirling wind centered on a point you can see within 60 feet of you. Each creature in that area must make a Constitution saving throw. A creature takes 3d10 bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If a Large or smaller creature fails the save, that creature is also pushed up to 10 feet away from the center of the cube.

- You create a 15-foot cone of ice shards extending from your outstretched hand in a direction you choose. Each creature in the cone must make a Constitution save throw. A creature takes 3d8 cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature that fails its save against this effect has its speed halved until the start of your next turn.

#### Mold Like Clay

You master the many forms that earth may take. You can spend 5 CHI to cast *[(de)construct stone](/tech:deconstruct-stone)*.

#### Stone Wall

You can spend 5 CHI points to cast *[geobarricade](/tech:geobarricade)*.

#### The Depth's Revenge

You contact someone that shouldn't be contacted, as a swirling pool of water smashes and pulls your foes towards its center. You can spend 5 CHI points to cast *[Alecto's drowning call](/tech:alectos-drowning-call)*.

#### Visor Like Fire

Your foes may try to hide in darkness, but their body heat lights up like a torch to you. You may cast *[thermovision](/tech:thermovision)* without expending any CHI. In addition, while under the effects of *[thermovision](/tech:thermovision)*, you may spend 2 CHI points as a bonus action to gain truesight until the end of your next turn.

#### Winter’s Gale

You can spend 5 CHI points to cast *[flash freeze](/tech:flash-freeze)*.
