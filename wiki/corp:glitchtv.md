**Corporation Name.** glitch.tv streaming services, inc.

**CEO.** Alexia Williams

**Years in business.** ~14

**Industry.** Social media

**Motto.** *"your window into day-to-day life."*

**Impact.** Minor

Originally Streamline Entertainment, glitch.tv is an older enterprise, starting first on live-streaming concerts, before expanding into offering basically *every* kind of streamed content you can think of, up to—and including—adult content.

glitch.tv's reputation is a hot topic of debate. They are lauded for their fostering of new talent, being fairly generous with their contracts to up-and-coming streamers, particularly ones who focus on hot-button topics within the city. On the flip side, their practices have been decried as exploitative, prioritizing profit over their streamers' well-beings, along with the never-ending accusations of favoritism, stilted algorithms, and a lot of other problems you would've expected them to have learned from the mistakes of their long-gone ancestors.

The most damning criticism is their penchant for invasion of privacy—it is not uncommon to see glitch.tv cameras and microphones perched all along the city in all sorts of areas one would not expect, and a large amount of livestreams exist that focus on day to day street events. Equipment is often vandalized in rougher parts of the city and just as often replaced with newer and tougher models, but where many see a surveillance state, they see opportunity in equal measure.

Despite everything, glitch.tv dominates the streaming market, having established major partnerships with corporations all across the city. Alexia Williams, their CEO, is a media darling; a household name known for her magnetic personality, wit and unending charisma. Behind closed doors, she's better known for her cutthroat business tactics and unapologetic attitude. Ultimately, for many people, glitch.tv is an integral part of daily life in Neon City, providing endless hours of entertainment and distraction from the harsh realities of life in a crapsack world.
