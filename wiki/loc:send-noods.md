**Location Name.** Send Noods Noodle Cart

**Owner/Leader.** Yosuke Ito

**Location Type.** Business

**Years in existence.** ~12

**Type.** Food cart

**Area.** Right outside to the south of the [VEGA5 Strip](/district:vega5)

**Impact.** Minor

Yosuke Ito has made a living by parking his noodle cart a block or two away right outside the most active districts of the night life in Neon City. Opening late and running open even later, Yosuke serves small, medium and large bowls of his house noodles, made with a milky white pork bone broth and a salty soy-based tare. Bowls come topped with many things, which can include sliced pork belly, spicy ground pork, soft-boiled marinated egg, bamboo shoots, nori, corn, bean sprouts, and more. The house special does sliced pork belly, egg, bamboo shoots and nori. It's pretty much just ramen, but Yosuke himself has never much called it that.

The only other item on the menu is gyoza, which are pan fried and stuffed full of either veggies or a pork and veggie mixture. The most popular thing on his menu is the house noodle and gyoza combo with a cola, and lines can get a little crazy during the middle of the night when a lot of people are coming out to replenish themselves after a night of dancing… or other activities.

The location is a popular meet-up place for runners as well since it's out of the way from corporate control, and Yosuke's always been discreet.
