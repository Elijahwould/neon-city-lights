##### Source: Player's Handbook (Magic Weapon)

*2nd-level manipulation*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You touch a nontechnical weapon. Until the tech ends, that weapon becomes a technical weapon with a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 4th level or higher, the bonus increases to +2. When you use a tech slot of 6th level or higher, the bonus increases to +3.
