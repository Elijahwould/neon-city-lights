**Name.** Gregory Raynor Tark

**Lineage.** Human

**Age.** 24

**Height and Weight.** 5'7", 160 lbs

**Pronouns.** he/him

**Affiliation.** [Raynor & Co. Acquisition and Investigative Services](/org:raynor-and-co), ex-[Reliquary](/corp:reliquary)

**Features.** White hair, bright yellow eyes, nervous disposition

**Status.** Alive

A young man with a shock of white hair, bright yellow eyes hidden behind spectacles, and an incredibly nervous disposition, Gregory Raynor is a favorite of many who meet him. Incessantly helpful and quite positive (despite that nervous disposition), he is a stark contrast in many ways to his severe older brother [Maximillian](/npc:max-raynor), serving as both fixer and assistant to runner crews that he hires when on a task put forward by him.

He was a part of the Reliquary with his older brother (by a few hundred years), until the Reliquary decided to oust them due to… complex circumstances. He is particularly skilled in research, incredibly knowledgeable in many old-world subjects and has an encyclopedic knowledge of modern history.

He was recently rescued by a cadre of runners while he had gone missing. Rumors say he had been researching a vaccine for the disease running rampant in the [Undercity](/undercity), and that he was doing so perhaps a little *too* close to the source.
