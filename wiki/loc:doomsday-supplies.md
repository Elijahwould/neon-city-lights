**Location Name.** Doomsday Supplies

**Owner/Leader.** John Trent

**Location Type.** Small store

**Years in existence.** <1

**Type.** Store specializing in prepper and survivalist gear

**Area.** [Downtown](/district:downtown)

**Impact.** Minor

Doomsday Supplies is a store recently opened by John Trent, a man who has long foreseen the collapse of society and believes that the fall of Bliss CO. was a harbinger of impending doom. With an unwavering conviction that the end is nigh, Trent has dedicated his life to preparing for the apocalypse and helping others do the same.

Trent believes that the collapse of Bliss CO. was a wake-up call to the fragility of Neon City and that people need to take responsibility for their well-being. He has a general distrust of large corporations and believes that they are not to be relied on in times of crisis- instead, one has to look out for oneself and loved ones.

Doomsday Supplies specializes in providing prepper and survivalist gear, from long-lasting food and water filters to rugged camping gear and weaponry. With Trent at the helm, Doomsday Supplies has become a hub for like-minded individuals who share his fears and are determined to outlast the end of the world.

"*When the world ends again, we'll be ready. Will you?*" —John Trent, CEO
