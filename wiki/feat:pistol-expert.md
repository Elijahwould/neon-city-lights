##### Source: Player's Handbook (Crossbow Expert)

You have a quick hand and keen eye when employing handy firearms, granting you the following benefits:

- You ignore the loading property of firearms with which you are proficient.
- Being within 5 feet of a hostile creature doesn’t impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls with firearms.
- When you use the Attack action and attack with a one-handed weapon, you can use a bonus action to attack with a pistol or revolver you are holding.
