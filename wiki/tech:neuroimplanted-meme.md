##### Source: Player's Handbook (Phantasmal Force)

*2nd-level deception*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a holographic projector peripheral)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You craft an meme that takes root in the mind of a creature that you can see within range. The target must make an Intelligence saving throw. On a failed save, you create a holographic object, creature, or other visible phenomenon of your choice that is no larger than a 10-foot cube and that is perceivable only to the target for the duration. This tech has no effect on hacked or constructs.

The meme includes sound, temperature, and other stimuli, also evident only to the creature.

The target can use its action to examine the meme with an Intelligence (Investigation) check against your tech save DC. If the check succeeds, the target realizes that the meme is a deception, and the tech ends.

While a target is affected by the tech, the target treats the meme as if it were real. The target rationalizes any illogical outcomes from interacting with the meme. For example, a target attempting to walk across a holographic bridge that spans a chasm falls once it steps onto the bridge. If the target survives the fall, it still believes that the bridge exists and comes up with some other explanation for its fall; it was pushed, it slipped, or a strong wind might have knocked it off.

An affected target is so convinced of the meme’s reality that it can even take damage from the deception. A meme created to appear as a creature can attack the target. Similarly, a meme created to appear as fire, a pool of acid, or lava can burn the target. Each round on your turn, the meme can deal 1d6 software damage to the target if it is in the meme’s area or within 5 feet of the meme, provided that the illusion is of a creature or hazard that could logically deal damage, such as by attacking. The target perceives the damage as a type appropriate to the deception.
