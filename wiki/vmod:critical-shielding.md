*Requirements: vehicle with Weak Point feature*

This piece of equipment adds a set of polycarbonate shielding around the more sensitive parts of the vehicle's hover engine or similar weak point. When the vehicle receives a critical hit, you ignore the automatic mishap suffered by the Weak Point feature of the vehicle, and instead destroy the Critical Shielding.

You can only have one Critical Shielding mod on any vehicle at any given time.
