**District.** Everest Bay Docks

**Population.** ?

**Type.** Economic/Industrial

A bay at the southwest of Neon City, housing the majority of the city's major docks. This is, curiously, one of the city's centers of commerce, and all sorts of interesting business goes on behind the scenes here. The Reliquary have several warehouses here by the docks, and many a sea expedition is launched from here.

**Creator.** Celica#1988
