## "When you're a raccoon, sometimes the entire world is your dumpster."

##### Source: Volo's Guide to Monsters, [All Along the Watchtower](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-NEJcj_BQBH6Z7ftvs1g)

No one is quite sure where the Trash Gremlins come from. The most commonly accepted theory is that a raccoon got itself stuck in a Wukong birthing facility while they were still running and hopped out again. Now, Neon City and its surroundings have this small group of tribal humanoids infesting the crawl spaces and sewer systems of the spires and datacenters. They survive off of the uncountable masses of garbage all around, scavenging for parts, food, and clothing, using what others throw away. Trash Gremlins are often born in Neon City proper, though the nests they are raised in are deep enough in the twisting maintenance corridors of the undercity to be free of the depravity Neon City has to offer.

### Roleplaying a Trash Gremlin

Trash Gremlins are scrappy underdogs who work together, living as outcasts. Consider what such an upbringing so far away from the normal urban life is, and what it might do to someone. What did your character do to survive? Are they still influenced by that old way of life where they might not know where their next meal come from?

### Trash Gremlin Names

Trash Gremlins take whatever they first hear as their names, oftentimes the advertisements blaring in the megacity spires at all hours of day, right before they are taken to trash gremlin nests to safety, for fear of others in the city harming their children. They often learn the meaning of their names much, much later in life. Trash Gremlins don’t often take surnames, instead choosing to take epithets of notable locations in the areas they visit.

# Trash Gremlin Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1, or increase three different scores by 1.
- **Age.** Trash Gremlins reach adulthood at 20 years of age. It is currently unknown what the upper limit of Trash Gremlin age is.
- **Size.** You are Small. Trash Gremlins are rarely taller than 3 feet.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Nightvision.** You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light and in darkness as if it were dim light. You discern colors in that darkness only as shades of gray.

## Trash Gremlin Dumpsters

### Sewer Dweller

Sewer Dweller Trash Gremlins are born and raised in the labyrinthine underground tunnels and sewers of Neon City. They are skilled at navigating the dark and cramped spaces, and have a knack for finding useful items and technology that others may have discarded and forgotten about.

- **Cringe.** As an action on your turn, you can cower pathetically to distract nearby foes, whimpering and whining. Alternatively, you can produce an awful meme. Until the end of your next turn, your allies gain advantage on attack rolls against enemies within 10 feet of you that can see you. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
- **Pack Tactics.** You have advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of your allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

### Genetic Roulette

These Trash Gremlins are altered genetically, thanks to the large amount of time they spend skulking through various abandoned gene labs. The horrors they may happen upon, steeling their mind against others they may find in the outside world. The various sparkly electrical outlets they play with have altered their nanites, giving them a natural ability to control techs. While no Trash Gremlin should be underrated, these ones are particularly dangerous.
