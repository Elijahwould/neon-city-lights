##### Source: Player's Handbook (Grasping Vine)

*4th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

A cable manifests at your command in an unoccupied space of your choice that you can see within range. When you cast this tech, you can direct the cable to lash out at a creature within 30 feet of it that you can see. That creature must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or be pulled 20 feet directly toward the cable.

Until the tech ends, you can direct the cable to lash out at the same creature or another one as a bonus action on each of your turns.
