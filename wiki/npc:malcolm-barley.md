**Name.** Malcolm Barley

**Lineage.** Human

**Age.** 58

**Height and Weight.** 5'6", 210 lbs

**Pronouns.** he/him

**Affiliation.** [Barley's Bar & Burger Barn](/loc:barleys)

**Features.** Polite, professional, approachable and willing to listen to others; short and jacked, salt and pepper hair and goatee

**Status.** Active

Malcolm Barley was a fairly well-known runner back in the day. Man was a professional, got the job done, and with minimal casualties, if any at all. But once he got older, the corpos had fewer and fewer contracts for him. So he took his money and invested it.

Barley's Bar & Burger Barn is his legacy, and a testament to how good food doesn't need a massive price tag, or to come from some big-name company. It can be delicious and affordable to the general public. Sure, a lot of it is deep fried, made with butter, and practically slathered in grease, but you'll be hard-pressed to find a better, cheaper meal at one of those corpo-run "restaurants".

And Barley himself still manages the place to this day. Nearly thirty years of giving the people great deals and greater meals, all locally sourced for customers' convenience. Despite the business' lighthearted atmosphere, he and his crew take their jobs seriously. They can only smile because their customers do, and they strive to give everyone a truly enjoyable, one-of-a-kind experience.
