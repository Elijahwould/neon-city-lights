##### Source: Player's Handbook (Fire Bolt)

*Altercation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You fire an incendiary bullet at a creature or object within range. Make a ranged tech attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 fire damage. A flammable object hit by this tech ignites if it isn't being worn or carried.

This tech's damage increases by 1d10 when you reach 5th level (2d10), 11th level (3d10), and 17th level (4d10).
