##### Source: Fizban's Treasury of Dragons (Summon Draconic Spirit)

*5th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (an object with the image of a kaiju engraved on it, worth at least $$50,000 credits)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You call forth a kaiju spirit. It manifests in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. This corporeal form uses the Draconic Spirit stat block. When you cast this tech, choose a family of kaiju: chromatic, gem, or metallic. The creature resembles a kaiju of the chosen family, which determines certain traits in its stat block. The creature disappears when it drops to 0 hit points or when the tech ends.

The creature is an ally to you and your companions. In combat, the creature shares your initiative count, but it takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don’t issue any, it takes the Dodge action and uses its move to avoid danger.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 6th level or higher, use the higher level wherever the tech's level appears in the stat block.

*Kaiju Spirit · *Large Kaiju, Neutral* · **Armor Class** 14 + the level of the tech (natural armor) · **Hit Points** 50 + 10 for each tech level above 5th (the dragon has a number of hit dice [d10s] equal to the level of the tech) · **Speed** 30 ft., fly 60 ft., swim 30 ft. · STR — DEX — CON — INT — WIS — CHA · 19 (+4) — 14 (+2) — 17 (+3) — 10 (+0) — 14 (+2) — 14 (+2) · **Damage Resistances(Chromatic and Metallic Only)** acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison · **Damage Resistances(Gem Only)** kinetic, entropic, software, neon, thunder · **Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, poisoned · **Senses** blindsight 30 ft., darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12 · **Languages** Kaiju, understands the languages you speak · **Challenge** — **Proficiency Bonus (PB)** equals your bonus · **Shared Resistances.** When you summon the kaiju, choose one of its damage resistances. You have resistance to the chosen damage type until the tech ends*
