A bunch of questions that are fairly commonly asked when it comes to the server. As such, the following is a non-exhaustive list of the most common questions we get, and answers for them.

#### Q. Can I use regular D&D races and classes here?

No. We do not use stock Dungeons and Dragons 5th Edition content in Neon City Nights. In many cases, while technical items might be sold in the grey market and may refer to a magical item from a D&D source, those items are never ported one to one.

A major reason for that is that Neon City Nights is almost like an **extension** or rework of 5th Edition rather than a pure reskinning. A good example: we have more [conditions](/conditions) and we outline them separately. Additionally, we have different skills: Religion is replaced with Culture, while Arcana is replaced with Technology. They serve similar functions, ultimately, so in many cases they may also feel like just reskins, but it really gets into the weeds when you look at how some classes changed. Just look at [Auditor](/auditor) and compare it to base Ranger, or even the [Ronin](/ronin) subclass, [Warlord](/ronin:warlord), which can be compared to the Champion Fighter.

#### Q. What is the Avrae prefix on this server?

We use . instead of !, as ! is used by Tupper, and Tupper does not like sharing!

#### Q. What bots does the server use?

We have the following bots available for player interaction:

- **Avrae.** General game and character sheet handling, as well as dice rolling, etc. Allows for aliases and more. Read more [here](https://avrae.io/)!
- **Tupperbox.** Allows for posting as specific characters. More information [here](https://tupperbox.app/)!

#### Q. What are my best options for character sheets?

The best options are always ones that allow you to integrate with Avrae, but let you customize the content as best as possible.

- One option we recommend is [Dicecloud](https://v1.dicecloud.com/), along with its [formula cheat sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1RX8hgmMfyff1YA4ttGVrvtLBGKcqPug-Xj9EJUIOhzA/edit#gid=2049461169) for anyone who finds it useful.
- Another we recommend is the 2.1 GSheet, linked [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1HZeFpLm3ZSAn-U4P5nEcei1mgIYCZYaIyD1RKh9ho34/)! Make sure you **make a copy** and then edit it.

Once you're done with your character sheet, make sure you have made it viewable by all, and able to be edited by Avrae (you can use *.help import* in #bot-chat to get instructions on how to let Avrae access your sheet), and then you can .import the sheet with the appropriate link.

#### Q. Do you allow for Combo Weapons from Carbon Pink?

No.

#### Q. Will you ever allow for Combo Weapons from Carbon Pink?

Unlikely. We'd rather spend our time budget elsewhere!

#### Q. Can I purchase items for other characters?

Not exactly. Any non-consumable, high-end item you have on you can be brought with you on a mission and loaned to another player's character, but the item still belongs to you at the end of the day.
