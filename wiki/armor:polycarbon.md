*Heavy armor, 4 lb., AC = 14, disadvantage on stealth checks*

Polycarbon armor is very apparent, but also very light. It consists of several pieces of armor, including a vest, leg and arm guards, gauntlets and a light, visored helmet, all usually in full black or with a bit of a carbon fiber look to it. It's the lightest of the options for heavy armor, but it's not easy to move around in at all. Great for cops on a budget, which if you're the NCPD, is you.
