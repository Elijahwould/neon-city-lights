Transmission is the process of instant matter transfer and deconstruction, which allows individuals to physically transport themselves, others and objects from one location to another, be it within mysdata or into one of the countless digital realms. The process of transmission involves the complete deconstruction of an individual's physical and digital makeup, followed by the transfer of their data to a new location, where they are then recorporated. Transmission is made possible through a complex network of servers and data centers, which work together to facilitate the near-instantaneous transfer of data across vast distances.

The mechanics of transmission are complex, but the basic process involves the use of specialized techs, to deconstruct one's mysdata. Techcasters can use the nanites in one's body and the environment, in addition to their natural attunement to technology, to store a person's or object's current state, creating an engram (A digitized version of the object.) After commanding the nanites to deconstruct the relevant mysdata, the data is sent to the target location. Where nanites in the environment will work to reconstruct it from thin air. Or in the case of Transmitting engrams to the digital realms, a digital avatar is created instead.

There are, however, limitations to Transmission. The process is not perfect, and there is always the risk of data corruption or loss during transmission. Most average techcasters, require months or even years to learn the intricate details of Transmission techs and mysdata analytical deconstruction. But once mastered, they allow near limitless freedom when traversing the Physical and various Digital Realms. Additionally, the cost associated with Transmission can be prohibitively expensive for many individuals, particularly for long-distance travel or when transporting a large group of people.
