##### Source: Player's Handbook (Blade Ward)

*Protection trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** 1 round

You extend your hand and draw discarded scrap into a protective shell. Until the end of your next turn, you have resistance against bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage dealt by weapon attacks.
