## "Genius is another word for magic—and the point is that it *is* inexplicable.""

You may be used to worlds that use the game system of Dungeons & Dragons, where a higher power grants the ability for people to use magic. In Neon City, access to pseudo-magical abilities is achieved through what's colloquially known as Tech and those who can use it are called *Techcasters*. Techcasting involves the manipulation of nanites, both in one's system, and those that float around now ever-present in the atmosphere itself, to create advanced effects.

Some people come by this ability in different ways. Some do so through rigorous study and research on how to control them, such as [Techwizards](/techwizard), or [Technoblades](/ronin:technoblade). [Ordinators](/ordinator) are granted this ability by hyper-advanced AI that have piggy-backed along on the backs of another lifeform. Others yet are granted this ability simply by sheer force of will and personality, allowing [Influencers](/influencer) and [Streamers](/streamer) to control their nanites in this way.

Whatever the method, the ability to manipulate the very fabric around us is in the hands of few, and rumors abound as to how people come across this ability in the first place…
