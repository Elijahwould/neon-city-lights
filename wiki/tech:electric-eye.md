##### Source: Player's Handbook (True Seeing)

*6th-level observation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (an ointment for the eyes that costs $$2,500 credits; is made from mushroom powder, saffron, and fat; and is consumed by the tech)

**Duration:** 1 hour

The target gains true sight, able to see things others can't.
