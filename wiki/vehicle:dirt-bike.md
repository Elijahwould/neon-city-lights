*Vehicle, civilian*

A dirtbike is a type of motorcycle designed for off-road travel across long stretches of land. With much of its frame stripped of protective materials found in your typical motorcycle in exchange for its unparalleled maneuverability across the land, it is the vehicle of choice for those that enjoy riding out of the city and across areas where the self-driving systems disengage.

It is a simple design that can be mimicked with other materials for those on a budget. Undercity trap bikes are oft sought after due to their illegitimate, but powerful, design for something so slapdash. Its modularity is what makes the most enticing, some private sec firms going out of their way to 'quietly' acquire one. [Kazanari Automotive](/corp:kazanari-automotive) supplies their own line of legitimately designed dirtbikes for both private and commercial use, as well as motorcross and electric. KTM-TT R430 is the most popular and widely distributed of electric dirtbikes. For casual users and recreational bikers, the MLX300R model makes for the best cost-effective model. Those looking for that competitive edge without dipping their wallets for the KAI YCX line will seek the KAI CRF550RL motorcross dirtbike with its focus on stability and its powerful engine block that does not weigh down the entire machine, thanks in part to the company's proprietary engine block design.
