These high-strength shackles can bind a Small or Medium creature. Escaping the restraints requires a successful DC 20 Dexterity check. Breaking them requires a successful DC 20 Strength check. Each set of restraints comes with one key. Without the key, a creature proficient with locksmith’s tools can pick the restraints’ lock with a successful DC 15 Dexterity check. Restraints have 15 hit points.
