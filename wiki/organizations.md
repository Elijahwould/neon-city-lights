## "Staying out of corporate hands doesn't mean everything's sunshine and rainbows."

Organizations in Neon City brandish a variety of standards to further separate them from the megacorporations that much of the populace have come to know and love. Whereas a corp might have its logo and branding on a variety of goods, spaces, and even people, an organization does not carry that same network of recognition city-wide.

Decentralized from the whole, they operate more as independent legions of people that seek out niches corporate entities either cannot or will not dirty their hands with. From digging for relics to digging claws into skulls, organizations are bereft of the same safety nets offered to larger entities. It is not unheard of, however, that some organizations become the interest of the giants.█
