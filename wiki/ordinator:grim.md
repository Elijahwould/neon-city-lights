## “Always go to other people’s funerals, otherwise they won’t come to yours.”

Many A.Is harbor a fascination with entropy and the life and death cycles of organics; their purely digital existences providing the comfort and ennui of immortality. As such, they seek out exceedingly gloomy individuals to host them, becoming Grim Ordinators. Caked in mascara and black hair dye, Grim Ordinators coordinate with their A.I to harness the forces of entropy and look kinda hot doing it.

##### Source: Dungeon Master’s Guide (Death)

### Bonus Proficiency

When you choose this domain at 1st level, you gain proficiency with military weapons.

### Reaper

At 1st level, you learn one Biochemistry Trick of your choice from any Tech list. When you cast a Biochemistry Trick that normally targets only one creature, the Tech can instead target two creatures within range and within 5 feet of each other.

### A.I Assistance: Touch of Entropy

Starting at 2nd level, you can use A.I Assistance to destroy another creature's life force by touch. When you hit a creature with a melee attack, you can use A.I Assistance to deal extra entropic damage to the target. The damage equals 5 + twice your Ordinator level.

### The Call of The Void

Starting at 6th level, your ability to channel negative energy becomes more potent. Entropic damage dealt by your Ordinator Techs and A.I Assistance options ignores resistance to entropic damage.

### Entropic Strike

At 8th level, you gain the ability to infuse your weapon strikes with entropic energy. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can cause the attack to deal an a 1d8 entropic damage to the target. When you reach 14th level, the extra damage increases to 2d8.

### Improved Reaper

Starting at 17th level, when you cast a Biochemistry Tech of 1st through 5th level that targets only one creature, the Tech can instead target two creatures within range and within 5 feet of each other. If the Tech consumes its material components, you must provide them for each target.
