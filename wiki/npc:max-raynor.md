**Name.** Maximillian Raynor Tark

**Lineage.** Human…?

**Age.** Looks to be early 40s; actually around 250+

**Height and Weight.** 6'1", 198 lbs

**Pronouns.** he/him

**Affiliation.** [Raynor & Co. Acquisition and Investigative Services](/org:raynor-and-co), ex-[Reliquary](/corp:reliquary)

**Features.** Muddled grey eyes, pale skin and sharp cheekbones

**Status.** Alive

A man who has been alive for much longer than his body would indicate, Maximillian Raynor Tark was the CEO of the Reliquary for a large amount of his life. Having been involved in almost half of the Reliquary's existence means that he has both participated in and witnessed a large amount of the things that make the current organization what it is.

Historically considered to be a ruthless leader, the past 50 years have seen him significantly soften his position as he has become more involved with runners outside of his corporate influence. So much has his position changed that he was eventually ousted by the Reliquary's Board of Directors from his position as CEO and then forced to resign from the corporation entirely. Not one to be dissuaded from his own goals, however, he immediately established [Raynor & Co.](/org:raynor-and-co) with the express goal of sating his ever-growing curiosity and seeing what good he can do for the city that he calls his home. To that end, he can commonly be found hiring fixers and runners to help get more feet on the ground. He can often be found in the company of his little brother, [Gregory](/npc:gregory-raynor).

People who have had the chance to meet him in his new position think of him as having a bit of a dry charm—affable when engaging with people and exuding a sense of absolute calm behind his cum-colored pale complexion.

In his office, a little note is hanging from his computer monitor: stop hiring foul-mouthed Gobbos to do important jobs.
