**Location Name.** The Garden Food Dispensary

**Owner/Leader.** [Daría "Katze" Abernathy](/legend:katze)

**Location Type.** Open Communal Area

**Years in existence.** Under a year

**Type.** Food dispensary

**Area.** [Underpass Gardens](/district:underpass-gardens)

**Impact.** Minor

The Garden Food Dispensary sits in the center of the city, in the biggest section of the Underpass Gardens that stands closest to its massive communes. It is here that, every single day, starting at 8AM, a large contingent of semi-trucks, portable food trucks, impromptu outdoor kitchen and grill setups and more come together in droves. Funded in part by certain *players* of the city with an interest in its success and in the elevation of those the city's system has more openly failed, the Garden Food Dispensary makes thousands of meals daily in a large variety of configurations.

The Dispensary is a great way for corporations and the city's bevy of food businesses to earn easy goodwill with the city at large, with many establishments setting up their own pop-ups to work alongside the stalwart regulars to give out food to the disadvantaged—though the quantity they serve and the regularity with which they do it mean they are not often found at the location.

It is said that the Dispensary often is one of the few places that have people who both still remember—and will still make—the original recipe of Pizzazz! with any regularity. Given that a batches of it are made to be sold to the highest bidder, the exorbitant prices that the gimmick seems to command (sometimes in the several hundreds of thousands of credits, paid by high-ranking executives or particularly foolhardy runners) tend to make up for missed donations, thus maintaining the Dispensary's mission: feeding the hungry.
