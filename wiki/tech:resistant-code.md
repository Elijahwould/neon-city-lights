##### Source: Player's Handbook (Resistance)

*Protection trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a disposable dongle)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch one willing creature. Once before the tech ends, the target can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to one saving throw of its choice. It can roll the die before or after making the saving throw. The tech then ends.
