##### Source: Player's Handbook (Spare the Dying)

*Biochemistry trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a living creature that has 0 hit points. The creature becomes stable. This tech has no effect on hacked or machines.

***Tech Lists.*** [Mechanist](/techs:mechanist), [Ordinator](/techs:ordinator)
