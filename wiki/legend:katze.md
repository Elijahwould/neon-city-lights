**Name.** Daría "Katze" Abernathy neé Rojas

**Lineage.** Splicer (cat)

**Age.** 26

**Height and Weight.** 5'1", 145 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Classes/Subclasses.** Influencer (Orator) 19, Streamer (E-Sports) 1

**Features.** Black cat ears with a silver ombre instead of human ears, a long black cat tail, black-furred digitigrade legs

**Status.** Alive

Short and tan-skinned, this feline-looking individual seems to barely have it together at any given moment. A slightly tired expression rests atop amber feline eyes that flit to and fro with a practiced alertness. She has a mop of shoulder-length, somewhat fluffy black hair, with two adorable cat ears poking up to the sides of her head. Her lithe upper body is heavily contrasted by the heaving masses she has to contain in whatever manner of dress she needs.

She was originally the head bartender and head manager at [Poncy's Bar and Grill](/loc:poncys-bar) before quietly stepping aside recently. She was often seen in the company of other Street Legends, but no one's quite sure what she actually does.

A woman of similar stature and countenance has been spotted at the [Garden Food Dispensary](/loc:garden-food-dispensary), but no one has been able to wrangle her for any sort of interview about it.

Married to both [Ruby Stained Pitch Abernathy](/legend:rsp) and S4-PPH|R3.

**Player.** @celicaxs
