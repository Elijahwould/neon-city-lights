##### Source: Player's Handbook (Dispel Evil and Good)

*5th-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S, M (an ampule of powdered neodymium magnets)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Shimmering energy surrounds and protects you from spam, hacked, and creatures originating from the digital realm. For the duration, AI, surges, spam, viruses, and hacked have disadvantage on attack rolls against you. You can end the tech early by using either of the following special functions.

- **Break Programming.** As your action, you touch a creature you can reach that is charmed, frightened, or possessed by an AI, a surge, a spam, a virus, or a hacked. The creature you touch is no longer charmed, frightened, or possessed by such entities.

- **IP Blacklist.** As your action, make a melee tech attack against an AI, a surge, a spam, a virus, or a hacked you can reach. On a hit, you attempt to drive the entity back to its originating digital address. The entity must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or be sent back to its origin address (if it isn’t there already). If they are already at their origin address, hacked are sent to the Rust-aligned domain, and surges are dispersed into the local nanite network.
