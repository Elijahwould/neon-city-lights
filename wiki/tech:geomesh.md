##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Mold Earth)

*Manipulation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** S

**Duration:** Instantaneous or 1 hour

You choose a portion of synth-soil or concrete (or natural dirt or stone) that you can see within range and that fits within a 5-foot cube, injecting a mesh of hardlight influence. You manipulate it in one of the following ways:

- If you target an area of loose synth-soil, you can instantaneously excavate it, move it along the ground, and deposit it up to 5 feet away. This movement doesn't involve enough force to cause damage.
- You cause shapes, colors, or both to appear on the synth-soil or concrete, spelling out words, creating images, or shaping patterns. The changes last for 1 hour.
- If the synth-soil or concrete you target is on the ground, you cause it to become difficult terrain. Alternatively, you can cause the ground to become normal terrain if it is already difficult terrain. This change lasts for 1 hour.

If you cast this tech multiple times, you can have no more than two of its non-instantaneous effects active at a time, and you can dismiss such an effect as an action.
