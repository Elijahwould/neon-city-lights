##### Source: Guildmaster's Guide to Ravnica (Encode Thoughts)

*Conception trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** S

**Duration:** 8 hours

Putting a finger to your head, you pull a memory, an idea, or a message from your mind and transform it into a tangible micro-card of glowing data called a thought chit, which persists for the duration or until you cast this tech again. The thought chit appears in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you as a Tiny, weightless, semisolid object that can be held and stored like a disk. It is otherwise stationary.

If you cast this tech while concentrating on a tech or an ability that allows you to read or manipulate the thoughts of others (such as *[neurotap](/tech:neurotap)* or *[synaptic override](/tech:synaptic-override)*), you can transform the thoughts or memories you read, rather than your own, into a thought chit.

Casting this tech while holding a thought chit allows you to instantly receive whatever memory, idea, or message the thought chit contains. (Casting *[neurotap](/tech:neurotap)* on the chit has the same effect.)
