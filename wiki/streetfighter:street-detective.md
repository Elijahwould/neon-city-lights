## "Oh, you know. Here, there, chasing down dead ends. And guess what? They're not all dead."

##### Source: adapted from [Benjamin Huffman's Pugilist v3](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/184921/The-Pugilist-Class)

### Back Alley Investigator

When you choose this discipline at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with two of the following skills of your choice: Insight, Investigation, or Perception. In addition, when you make an Intelligence (Investigation), Wisdom (Insight), or Wisdom (Perception) check, you can spend 1 mettle point to gain advantage on the check.

### Hypervigilant

Also starting at 3rd level, you have advantage on initiative rolls. During the first round of combat, you have advantage on attack rolls against creatures who haven’t acted yet.

### Analytical Eye

Starting at 6th level, you can use a bonus action and expend 2 mettle points to hone in on the idiosyncrasies and bad habits of an enemy you can see within 30 feet.

When you do, you have advantage on weapon attacks against the creature and you add half your proficiency bonus (round up) to your AC against attacks made by that creature. These effects continue for 1 minute or until you use this feature again.

### Pulse of the Streets

Starting at 11th level, when you take a long rest in a specific district of Neon City, or a settlement outside of it, you can choose to become familiar with the location. When you use this feature again, you replace your previous familiar location with the current one. You gain the following benefits while in a familiar location:

- You cannot be surprised and you add your proficiency bonus to your initiative.
- You have darkvision to a range of 120 feet.
- When you make an ability check using the Insight, Investigation, or Perception skills that adds your proficiency bonus, add twice your proficiency bonus instead.
- You cannot be lost by any means.
- When you are not in combat, you can travel between any two points in the settlement twice as fast as your speed would normally allow.

### Unerring Eye

Starting at 17th level, you can use a bonus action and spend 1 mettle point to open your senses to your surroundings for 1 minute. During this time, you have advantage on saving throws against being blinded or deafened and have truesight out to a distance of 30 feet.
