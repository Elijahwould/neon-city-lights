## "That wolf is smart. And apparently on the internet, that wolf is very *sexy* to some people. That's OK."

LiNKER and Nanophage are a painful and scarring binary imbibement that leaves those that survive irrevocably changed, yet some found this physiological autonomy on the very precipice of death an exhilarating high, and snatched it from the jaws of sanity. This might sound familiar, as the community-owned mask seeking evolutionary perfection was a great one that has since split, one half being the mask of the snarling beast. Beast masked pretenders laugh in the face of the splicer safety mandates, ever-risking their minds to the throes of feral instincts, afforded the safety-net in nanophage that your typical beastman lacks.

### Pheromonal Markers

When you don this mask at 3rd level, you begin to exist in a state of twilight between critter and person, senses honed like that of a natural predator. You gain advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.

### Beast Mode Minor Hybridization

Upon donning this mask at 3rd level, you have hybridized your nanophage to respond to specific chemical sequences aligned with pseudolegal splicing procedures. As a bonus action, you can directly alter your phenotype to express these splices, assuming your Beast Mode for up to 1 hour. You can still speak, use equipment, and wear armor in this form. You can revert to your true form earlier as a bonus action, and do so automatically when falling [unconscious](/con:unconscious), dropping to 0 hit points, or when dead.

You can use this feature two times, regaining all uses when you finish a short or long rest.

While transformed into Beast Mode, you gain the following features:

- **Aggressive Build.** You gain a +1 to melee damage rolls. This bonus increases by 1 at 11th and 18th level. You also have advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.

- **Durable Hide.** You have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. While you are not wearing heavy armor or wielding a shield, you gain a +1 bonus to your AC.

- **Feral Claws.** You can use Dexterity instead of Strength for the attack and damage rolls of your unarmed strikes. Your unarmed strikes deal 1d6 slashing damage, increasing to 1d8 at 11th level, and 1d10 at 18th level. You can hyperlink your unarmed strikes with a Lethal Error as a single weapon, and can do so as part of activating Beast Mode. When you use the Attack action with an unarmed strike on your turn, you can make one unarmed strike as a bonus action.

### Instinctual Rush

At 7th level, the twilight state of your senses primes your body for the hunt, the grand everchase. Your speed increases by 10 feet. You also add 10 feet to your long jump distance and 3 feet to your high jump distance.

While transformed into Beast Mode, you gain the following feature:

- **Pack Instincts.** Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can tap into the primal pulse of mob mentality. The next attack roll made by an allied creature against the target has advantage.

### Monitor Chip Upgrade: Psychophagia

At 11th level, your Retributive Monitor Chip can signal your animal senses, ringing out marrow deep like a dinner bell, directing a deep and unending hunger to draw your savagery. While in Beast Mode, your attacks have advantage against a chipped target. Additionally, as the intensity of your hunger acts as an undercurrent to your force of presence, you gain proficiency in Charisma saving throws.

### Beast Mode Major Hybridization

Starting at 15th level, your nanophage-splice hybrid has mutated alignment with your genetic sequence and base code, keeping the beast within close at hand. You can activate your Beast Mode a third time between short or long rests.

While transformed into Beast Mode, you gain the following feature:

- **Ferocious Teeth.** When you use the Attack action with an unarmed strike on your turn while you are below half your maximum hit points, you can make two unarmed strikes as a bonus action, instead of one.

### Absolute Therosync

At 18th level, the mask has become the face, you have mastered your hybridization and married instinct with elevated human thought, the twilight of your urges becoming a constant invitation, be it night or day. When you have expended all uses of your Beast Mode activation, you can instead enter Beast Mode by spending and rolling two Nanophage Dice, losing hit points equal to the total.

While transformed into Beast Mode, you gain the following feature:

- **Feed the Everchase.** At the start of each of your turns, you regain hit points equal to your Nanophage modifier (minimum of 1) + your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1) if you have at least 1 hit point, but no more than half of your maximum hit points.
