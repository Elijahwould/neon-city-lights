## "The list is long and replete with names. There is work yet to be done."

Being chosen by the dwellers in the bottom of the ocean leaves one burdened with no simple task. Those whose visions have been invaded during moments of great peril may find an unusual dark passenger whispering in their minds. And those who pay attention to the voices may find themselves with renewed purpose—all they need to do is let the water in…

### Clause Perks

When you take this clause at 3rd level, you gain the following two clause perks.

- **Dreadful Fog.** As an action, you channel the depths to summon a thick cloud of fog that surrounds you for 20 feet in all directions. The fog moves with you, remaining centered on you and making its area heavily obscured. You and each creature you designate can instead treat the area as lightly obscured. This fog lasts for 10 minutes, spreads around corners, and cannot be dispersed unless you choose to end this effect (no action required).
- **Crushing Tides.** As a bonus action, you channel the might of the sea to bolster your attacks for 1 minute. When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can choose to push the target 10 feet away from you. If pushed into an obstacle or another creature, the target takes bludgeoning damage equal to your Charisma modifier.

### Aura of Storms

Starting at 7th level, you radiate an aura like that of a violent storm. While you’re not incapacitated, the area within 10 feet of you is considered difficult terrain for your enemies, and ranged attack rolls made against you or any allies within the aura are made at disadvantage.

When you reach 18th level in this class, the aura affects creatures within 30 feet of you.

### Wrath of the Seas

At 15th level, you can call on the force of crashing waters as a reaction whenever a creature moves into or out of your reach. The creature takes 1d12 bludgeoning damage and must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC or have their speed reduced to 0 until the start of their next turn and be knocked prone.

### Fury of the Drowned

At 20th level, you can choose to fully let the water in, channeling the fury of the Drowned all throughout your body. As an action, you embrace these dark voices to gain the following benefits for 1 minute:

- You have advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks and you gain climbing and swimming speeds equal to your walking speed. If you already have a climbing or swimming speed, it is doubled.
- If you are within 5 feet of a creature and no other creatures are within 5 feet of you, you have advantage on attack rolls against that creature.
- Allies within your Aura of Storms can use your Wrath of the Seas feature as though it were their own. Their activations of Wrath of the Seas use your spell save DC.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest.
