## "See, Icarus's problem with his invention was that he flew too close to the sun. That's why I'm sticking close. To the ground. Where I can get up close and personal with my enemies."

Whether one has obtained this weapon of mass destruction by corpo test runs, military conscription, or just plain making something they saw once in an Old World Anime, this is a mechanist that runs purely by this invention. A mechanist who specializes as a Mech Pilot modifies their mech into a war machine, whether that's blinding speed, a hull stronger than most tanks, or loaded with so many weapons it would make a Dirty Fighter renegade cream themselves. Not only that, the mech can even enhance the mechanist’s tech, and serve as cover for their allies. Be warned though: A Mech Pilot mechanist without a Mech is open season for peppering with lead.

### Pilot's Kit

When you adopt this specialization at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with two land vehicles of your choice.

### Custom Armor

Mech Suit

The Mech Suit acts as a Land Vehicle piloted by the Mechanist. While the Mechanist is within it, the Mechanist can have a beneficial tech effect just one of the two. In the case of a Healing tech being used on the Mechanist and its Suit, the healing is split in half between the two. The Mechanist can also cast techs originating from the Mech or targeting themself if they wish. Any touch based spells can only target the Mechanist or its Mech.

The mech can be piloted by its mechanist, however, another creature with at least intelligence of 9 can attempt to pilot the mech. However, every check is made with disadvantage. While within the Mech, the Pilot uses the Mech’s AC and HP instead of their own. When a Mech reaches 0 hit points it becomes inoperable and must be repaired before it can be used again. It can be repaired from breaking down during a Long Rest. During a short rest, a Mech Pilot can repair the mech using the Mechanist’s hit dice, spending them as per normal with expending hit dice to heal.

The Mech can act as partial cover during combat for allies standing behind it. The mech can use your strength or intelligence for attack and damage rolls. And uses your saves for its own.

During a Long Rest, you can swap your Mech and its attachments out. You can only have one active mech at a time.

Any Mech can have a weapon integrated into it, and any weapon integrated into it is considered Proficient while being used by the Pilot within the Mech.

A Mech can have two attachments equipped at 3rd level. And the Mechanist can only apply each of each type Attachment once, unless the attachment says otherwise.

Attachments

- **Weapon Integration (Any).** Integrate a Weapon into the Mech. This can be selected more than once.

- **Hellfire Cannon (Combat or Armored Only).** As an attack during the attack action, the Pilot can spew forth flames hitting anyone within a 15-cone, anyone caught in the attack must make a Dex Save against the Mechanist’s tech DC. This attack does 2d6 Fire damage at 3rd level, increasing by a d6 at levels 5, 9 and 15. A mechanist can use this a number of times equal to their Intelligence.

- **Shield (Combat and Armored Only).** The Pilot can integrate a shield into their mech, choosing a normal shield, or a technical one.

- **Subwoofer Blast (Agility and Combat Only).** As an attack during the attack action, the Pilot can have the mech fire a sonic blast of energy to try and shove an enemy. The Mechanist rolls an attack roll as normal, dealing 1d10+Intelligence Modifier Force damage on a hit, and then the target makes a Strength Save DC equal to the Mechanist DC, on a success, the Target is shoved five feet away from the Mech. On a failure, the target is shoved ten feet and knocked prone. Subwoofer’s range is 15/30

- **Grapple Hook (Agility and Armored only).** As an action, the Mech can fire a grappling hook up to sixty feet away. If this hits an enemy, the Mech can attempt to drag the enemy towards it, causing the enemy to make a Strength Save against the Pilot’s Tech DC. Moving the target ten feet on failure, or five feet if the target is larger than the Mech. Alternatively, the Mech can use the grappling hook to pull the mech towards the location of the point it shot, moving the full sixty feet at the end of the Pilot’s turn.

- **L.A.P. [Light Assault Repeater] (Agility Only).** The Mech can fire three quick shots at up to three different targets, or focus it all on a single target. This replaces a normal attack during the attack action. Dealing 1d4+Intelligence Modifier to enemies it hits. This attack cannot add any other sources of damage to it, such as Techs like DMA Beacon or Abilities like Lethal Error. L.A.P.’s range is 20/40

- **Long Range Targeting Systems (Agility or Armored Only).** This Mech has a focus to aid in techcasting, techs cast through the Mech gain a bonus to their range equal to 30 feet. This applies to Touch Spells, allowing the Mechanist to cast touch spells on enemies or allies that are within 30 feet of the Mech. This bonus applies after any other bonus to a tech’s range. Such as Source Coder’s Distant Tech or Boosted Wifi.

- **H.E.A.V.Y. [High Explosive Armor-Defeating, Variable Yield] (Any).** Sometimes you need a bit of extra firepower on your mech, this is the option for that. Only capable of being shot once a turn because of the weight of the shots. Each shot from the H.E.A.V.Y. does 2d10+Intelligence Modifier Piercing damage to the primary target, dealing 1d10 fire damage around the target. The damage at 15th to 3d10 and 2d10. While a Combat or Agility mech can equip a H.E.A.V.Y. they will find themselves needing to make a Strength Saving throw to stay on their feet after each shot, the DC for this is equal to your Mechanist DC. H.E.A.V.Y.’s range is 80/160

### Call Mech

At 3rd level, the pilot can summon their mech to them as an action. If the mech is within 300 feet to its pilot, it will try its best to reach its pilot within three turns. If the distance is greater than one mile, then the mech will take anywhere from five to ten minutes to reach its pilot.

### Combat Modifications

Starting at 5th level, the Mechanist gains the Extra Attack feature while within their Mech.

In addition, the Mechanist can modify their Mech with a new modification from the options below. Modifications can be changed out during a Long Rest.

- **Two Weapon Fighting (Combat or Armored Only).** The mechanist can use a weapon attachment equipped to its off-hand to make another attack as a Bonus Action. You do not add your Intelligence or Strength modifier to the damage of this attack.

- **Uncanny Dodge (Agility Only).** When an attacker that you can see hits the mech with an attack, you can use your reaction to half the damage against the mech.

- **Leap (Agility Only).** As a Bonus action, your Mech can leap forward equal to half its movement.

- **Combat Analysis (Any).** When you roll a 1 or a 2 on an attack roll with an attack made by the Mech, the pilot can reroll the die and use the new roll.

- **Siege Bunker (Armored Only).** As a bonus action, the Armored Mech can bunker itself, granting it Resistance to all damage while it’s bunkered in, but you cannot move. You cannot activate Siege Bunker after moving. Siege Bunker ends at the start of your next turn.

- **Weaponry Focus (Combat Only).** The Mech can have a third weapon attachment (Or Fourth after level nine), this weapon can be any weapon on the Weapon List.

- **Engine Overclock (Any).** The Mech’s land speed is increased by 20.

- **Amphibious Upgrade (Any).** The Mech’s been retrofitted for the potential for underwater combat, your mech gains a swim speed equal to half its land speed. The Mech is also capable of hovering just above water, as if walking above water.

- **Jump Jets (Any).** The Mech has some jump jets to allow for short bursts of flight. The Mech gains the ability to take flight for a number of turns equal to your Intelligence modifier. The mech can do this a number of times a day equal to your Proficiency Bonus. The flight speed is the same as land speed.

### Greater Weapon Integration

At 9th level, the Mechanist can integrate a third attachment into their Mech. Further, the Mechanist can swap one attachment during any short rest. Furthermore, the Mechanist can now use their Tinker feature on their Suit, applying a single infusion to it.

### Spinal Attachment

At 15th level, the Mech can be fitted with a powerful attachment onto the suit. Each model has its own attachment. Each attachment can be used once and is refilled at the end of a long rest.

- **Combat - Full Combat Assault.** The combat mech can make one attack with each of its attachments, each of these attacks is made with Advantage.

- **Agility - LRM Flurry.** As an action the mech can target any number of creatures within 120-foot radius, firing a stream of long range missiles at the targets. Each targeted creature must make a Dex Save against the Mechanist’s Tech DC. Taking 10d6 on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

- **Armored - Tank Shell Shields.** As an action, the Armored forms a hardlight shield around it, increasing the Armored’s AC by four. The shield grants the Mech 75 temp HP, and allows the Mech to be used as Full Cover while it lasts.
