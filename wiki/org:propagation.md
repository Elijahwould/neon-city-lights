**Organization Name.** Propagation

**Leader.** [Hemlock](/npc:hemlock)

**Organization Type.** Research and Preemptive Containment

**Years in existence.** <1

**Goals.** Locate and contain Old World Threats

**Creed.** *"From the Gardens we Rise."*

**Impact.** Minor

After a band of runners reawakened a nightmarish project from the Old World, some of the revived victims banded together to use their ancient knowledge to pre-empt the many projects and operations of the Old World becoming ticking time bombs. Lead by the secretive Hemlock, they currently are hunting down any remnants of Project: ROSE GARDEN to ensure survivors are safe, and to remove any nightmares that still may lurk in the bunkers-turned-catacombs that the project calls home.
