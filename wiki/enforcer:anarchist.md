## "Some people will only love you if you fit inside their box. Don't be afraid to shove that box up their ass."

Typically, when an Enforcer voids their clause, they look upon their peers with a sneering form of condescending wisdom. No longer bound to corpo-crat ideals or methodologies, wherever a desire to follow a cause once stood, only freedom remains.

However, there are a few who have decided that instead of only liberating themselves from the shackles of corporate lingo, board meetings, and debt collection for all the wrong people, they will instead liberate others as well. Those who strive to follow the clause of anarchy draw their power from the many, many other enforcers who came before them, and from those who have been wronged by the corporate hierarchy of the world.

### Clause Perks

When you take this clause at 3rd level, you gain the following clause perks.

- **Utter Freedom.** As an action, you channel the utmost form of ideological freedom, focusing them into a burst of emotional menace. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you must make a Wisdom saving throw if it can see you. On a failed save, the target is [Frightened](/con:frightened) of you for 1 minute. If a creature frightened by this effect ends its turn more than 30 feet away from you, it may attempt another wisdom saving throw to end the effect on it.
- **Technological Tyrant.** When you're hit with a melee weapon attack, you can use your reaction to add your Proficiency Bonus to your armor class until the end of your next turn. Regardless whether or not the attack hits, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Enforcer level + Charisma Modifier (Minimum of +1) These hitpoints last until the end of your next turn.

### Aura of Anarchy

After you reach seventh level in this clause, whenever a creature which is under the effect of your Get Thee Behind Me starts it's turn, or enters your Get Thee Behind Me radius, it may choose to forgo the bonus to saving throws to instead gain a bonus to melee weapon damage equal to your Charisma Modifier.

This bonus is always applied to the enforcer.

### Too Free to Care

When you reach level 15 in this clause, you gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from mundane weapons. In addition, you also become resistant to entropic damage.

### Angel of Anarchy

At 20th level, with a bonus action, your glee and excitement at the prospect of freedom creates a frightening aura that lasts for 1 minute. The aura reduces any bright light in a 30-foot radius around you to dim light. Whenever an enemy that is frightened by you starts its turn in the aura, it takes 4d10 software damage. Additionally, you and any creatures of your choosing in the aura are draped in deeper shadow. Creatures that rely on sight have disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures draped in this shadow. Your freedom is terrifying.

While the aura lasts, you can use a bonus action on your turn to cause the shadows in the aura to attack one creature. Make a melee tech attack against the target. If the attack hits, the target takes entropic damage equal to 3d10 + your Charisma modifier.

In addition, choose one spell from your clause spell list. You are able to cast it for free, without concentration. You may only choose one spell.

After activating the aura, you can’t do so again until you finish a long rest.
