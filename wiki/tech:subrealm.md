##### Source: Player's Handbook (Demiplane)

*8th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** S

**Duration:** 1 hour

You create a digital door on a flat solid surface that you can see within range. The door is large enough to allow Medium creatures to pass through unhindered. When opened, the door leads to a subrealm that appears to be an empty room 30 feet in each dimension, made of wood or stone. When the tech ends, the door disappears, and any creatures or objects inside the subrealm remain trapped there, as the door also disappears from the other side.

Each time you cast this tech, you can create a new subrealm, or have the digital door connect to a subrealm you created with a previous casting of this tech. Additionally, if you know the nature and contents of a subrealm created by a casting of this tech by another creature, you can have the digital door connect to its subrealm instead.
