**Corporation Name.** Kazanari Automotive Industries, Inc.

**CEO.** Makoto Kazanari

**Years in business.** ~48

**Industry.** Automotive manufacturing

**Motto.** *"We make the wings with which your dreams will take flight."*

**Impact.** Moderate

Kazanari Automotive is one of several automotive manufacturers in Neon City, owning several part factories in the Undercity as well as a general manufacturing plant off to the south of the city, past the slums, primarily operated through fully mechanical robots operating mostly autonomously, albeit with human oversight.

Kazanari Automotive is also the most popular manufacturer of street-ready, old-world style [street scooters](/vehicle:street-scooter) and [motorcycles](/vehicle:motorcycle), with several of their models in heavy circulation all throughout the city. The brand became pervasive around the city and in the zeitgeist of popular culture around Neon City due to its use in popular tokusatsu media that often shows protagonists riding them, before driving their motorcycles into massive monsters to do huge damage right before transforming into masked heroes before the audience's eyes. An ironic side effect of this is that they have subsequently become the favorite bike manufacturer of the [Super Henshin Guild](/org:super-henshin-guild), and rumors abound of a potential sponsorship deal—though no one has openly admitted to such a thing.

Yet.
