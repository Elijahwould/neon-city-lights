**Corporation Name.** Eat It Out Industries

**CEO.** Unknown

**Years in business.** 69 years

**Industry.** Fresh food, raunchy recipes, personalized catering and events

**Motto.** "*Quality you can taste!*"

**Impact.** Minor

Eat It Out Industries (EIO) was founded on the radical belief and desire that there is a connection between how food is raised and prepared, how it tastes, and how it is presented. The right combination is said to bring out an absolutely euphoric sensation that leaves a customer shaken to their core.

To cater to those who crave more than just the mundane, EIO focuses on bringing the best quality and taste via test kitchens, using recipes designed to not only taste euphoric… but to be affordable. The goal was always to ensure these sensations were available to more than just the elite. After all, how else could they expect to turn a profit and have a large following?

EIO is currently expanding beyond the specific niches of pre-existing corporations, working tirelessly with their employees to ensure that every order is enjoyable, fresh, fast, and cumpletely custom from start to finish, using only all-natural and organic additives to flavor their recipes—sourced on site, and by eager hands. For a higher price, you too can step behind the counter or even experience unparalleled tableside service. All of this ties into their motto, their very mission statement of "Quality you can taste!"
