*Consumable (soda), very rare*

It is only after supping upon one of Quantum Cola's™ Black Label Reserve Nanobot Cola's that one recognizes the cloying, artificial sweetness of its lesser counterparts. Typically saved for boardroom execs, Nano-Reserve comes in a rare glass bottle with black and gold packaging, being otherwise unmarked beyond the logo. Featuring less carbonation, the drink flows like fizzling silk, possessing a simpler and purer note of sugar that approaches perfection.

Black Label Reserve bubbles with a faint gold static when agitated that makes it perfect for chugging, or for long sipping experiences.

You regain 10d4+20 hit points when you drink this Black Label Reserve, which you can personally perform as a bonus action. When self-administered as an action, you regain the maximum value of hit points (60).
