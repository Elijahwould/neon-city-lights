*Requirements: any Car*

This modification improves a vehicle's shielding for its action stations. Any station that does not provide cover now provides half cover, and any station that provides half cover now provides three quarters cover. If a vehicle suffers a mishap, roll d20. On a 19 or 20, the polycarbonate shielding is damaged, and must be replaced.
