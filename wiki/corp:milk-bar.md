**Corporation Name:** Milk Bar

**CEO:** Miruka Sprinzen

**Years in Business:** 2 years

**Industry:** Coffee Shop/Cafe, Natural Additives

**Motto:** "*Desire Meets Dairy~*"

**Impact:** Subsidiary [Eat It Out Industries](/corp:eat-it-out)

Milk Bar had its humble beginnings in Neon City as a street cart with a pair of baristas working their jugs off to make sure the jobbers, runners and anyone in-between had sweet snacks and coffee on the go. It has since expanded in size, but keeps its simple beginnings at the center of its design. It’s now a proud subsidiary of Eat It Out Industries.

Not enough milk in your shake? Not enough cream in your pie? A little extra juice in your joe? With Milk Bar, the options are nearly endless. Catering to those with a sweeter tooth with both a kiosk for quick, on demand service and an homage to its original form, the Milk Bar offers a chance for redeemable prizes at store fronts from their rewards system. They certainly live up to the Desire Meets Dairy slogan that keeps the queues full.

Storefronts have a feature the kiosks lack, a rotating Pick Your Pump self-serve bar to top your drinks off right. Eco-Friendly labels provide up to date information, as well as interesting facts about the workers providing their services. All of this is strictly to make sure you know exactly what you’re enjoying, and that your needs are being met.

Prioritizing 'au naturel' additives versus added hormones and aphrodisiacs, Milk Bar is the go-to for a perk-me-up, with quality you can taste.
