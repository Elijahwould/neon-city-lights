##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Shadow Of Moil)

*4th-level biochemistry*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S, M (a cybereye encased in a gem worth at least $$15,000 credits)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Shadow-like nanites encase your body until the tech ends, causing you to become heavily obscured to others. The shadows turn dim light within 10 feet of you into darkness, and bright light in the same area to dim light.

Until the tech ends, you have resistance to neon damage. In addition, whenever a creature within 10 feet of you hits you with an attack, the nanites lash out at that creature, dealing it 2d8 necrotic damage.
