## "Truth be told? Humanity went kinda wild."

*The following is an excerpt from "The New Faces of Humanity", published in 1309 by Gabrielle de la Fontaine*

**Humanity.**

The great seed planted into the sandblasted earth, the life giver that harbored the blackened sprout that dug its roots deep in. From the ground jutted forth a mighty oak. An ancient oak that persists in even the most modern of humans today. That ancient strength, that power that wells within when everything should fail. And from that oak, an inferno. The ineffable bright that gives shape to humanity's inability to stop. To progress. To stumble into the dark and discover more and more. This is the Old Human. The progenitor. Homo-Sapiens. The root. The original sin.

And from that light comes shadow. In the pursuit of knowledge left behind the tools wrought of tar and sacrilege. The early implementation of the very weave of metal we find ourselves surrounded with began with a simple want. The desire to step beyond the human limit and improve the body. And through some manner, or inability, of the construction, the nanites turned. Ransomware microscopic. The body contorts and transforms. The road to hell is well-paved and maintained. At the end of this road is the dead end named the [Netsucker](/netsuckers), with those who failed to completely turn dubbed instead [Netnibblers](/race:netnibbler).

From the inferno comes the petering fires of humanity's last gasps of life. The need to persist as ancient as any animalistic need to survive. First came the [Remakes](/race:remake), where engramization had gone from theory to application in order to brave life beyond life. Then came the [Kaijuborn](/race:kaijuborn), bulwarks against the disasters of the past and the present to survive the hostile future in the making. At least, as long as they were designed to. The [Wukong](/race:wukong), the first leap forward in human engineering, in search of the perfect method of producing soldiers en masse, that ended with failure as the simian-like entity proved unreliable for any method of battlefield application. And then the [Jerboan](/race:jerboan), the revolution. When everything seemed to end in ash, human ingenuity proved that the most unsuspecting of sources can be the most groundbreaking. The humble desert rodent would be the flint to spark the flame once again.

Now a phoenix reborn, the human flame enkindled, and the next generation rises to walk the blasted earth. Heaven denied, the seeds sprout and the [New Human](/race:new-human) is born. The fathers that shan't ever name the leaves will know Neo Homo-Omnia. And alongside them are the fruits of labor. The [Elf](/race:elf), long-lived and the desperate to hold onto the ashes of longevity with aplomb. Power, a sin as old as Humanity itself, roots itself in the future through the Elf. The [Gobbo](/race:gobbo) dances their joyous dance about the flame, capable of adapting in many an environment and meeting the future with wide, mischievous grins. The [Nayaling](/race:nayaling), the future soldier, constructed to subjugate. To conquer and control the sputtering flames. Warriors of the next era, the demons Old Humans feared.

In time, the world was reborn. Earth, Holy Terra, Gaia, many names that reach the same point on the map. Now was the next big leap. The New Human saw the potential this future held and sought to grasp whole the threads of fate. And lo, the [Splicer](/race:splicer) was born. With the tools to create, the primordial essence was theirs to control, though not without risk. From here, new humanity was in control of the console.

From the bones of the Remake spawned the [Artificiate](/race:artificiate), the first genuine step towards android life. The creations of humanity that spun out into sentience, a software spirit encased in layers of triple-bonded carbon tubing, a real ghost in the machine. And from the hard came the soft, the [Hardlight Core](/race:hardlight-core), lost pieces of fractured software who have found a home for their partial existence, driven by the human dream. To create, to forge, to perhaps once again become god.

The Wukong's chaotic nature had inevitably defied the entropy of time. Through the hands of those that simply wanted to see the world be consumed in eternal flame—or the idle mistakes of those who merely did not exercise enough caution—came the [Trash Gremlin](/race:trash-gremlin). An omen to those that seek to fire the engines of construction and experimentation. A warning to many to make sure they properly finish that which they start. When the fire goes untended, things catch and ignite. Pray it isn't something you love or want to eat.

From the fae-like Gobbo branched anew a sprout. That sprout was the [Orcc](/race:orcc). Hardier, heartier, and stern, they are the new oak that seeks to remind the new world that from thriving must one now endure. Nature is an indifferent force, both nurturing and powerful. The ancient human had destroyed the natural beauty of the world and nature has not forgotten this sin. It will be respected, and the noble Orcc will be the one to lead that drive.

From the conquering riders of war, the Nayaling sprouted the [Thulian](/race:thulian). Extremophile children whose branches reach to places others can only dream of, outside of our blue marble and deep within it. They dance about the flame within the void with the twinkling night forever etched into their form, and glide deep at the deepest depths of the ocean, where the pressure makes its own mark unto them. While their purpose is one that has since been subverted, they are nothing if not natural explorers. And what more can the new world ask for than those to seek and explore?

And yet, something old persists. An obsession with the divine, the bright light the flame could only beg to be. The holy, the angelic. Scripture of an unknown text to describe what cannot be described. Words made to form the formless, the maddening expanse between then and now allowed the truly insane to take the step towards literal godhood. What was sought to be the next evolution from the human, from the spliced, from the metal. When the intelligence that humanity wrought from code finally becomes one with that same human intelligence, born from that furious flame is the [AI-Simar](/race:ai-simar), divinity manifest within the margins of the programming. The halo reforms about the flame to protect. To possess. To become.

The ultimate question remains: what will you choose to become?
