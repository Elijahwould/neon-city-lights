##### Source: Player's Handbook (War Caster)

*Prerequisite: The ability to cast at least one tech*

You have practiced casting spells in the midst of combat, learning techniques that grant you the following benefits:

- You have advantage on Constitution saving throws that you make to maintain your concentration on a tech when you take damage.
- You can perform the somatic components of techs even when you have weapons or a shield in one or both hands.
- When a hostile creature's movement provokes an opportunity attack from you, you can use your reaction to cast a tech at the creature, rather than making an opportunity attack. The tech must have a casting time of 1 action and must target only that creature.
