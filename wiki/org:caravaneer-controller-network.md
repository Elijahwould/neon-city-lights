**Organization.** Caravaneer Controller Network (CCN)

**Leader.** None

**Organization Type.** Resource and goods transportation

**Years in Existence.** ~40 years

**Goals.** Confederate wasteland caravans into a semi-autonomous network of groups working towards the same goal

**Creed.** "*From point A to point B, through any storm or crisis: the goods get there.*"

**Impact.** Minor

While the majority of the region's populace lives within Neon City, the wasteland beyond the borders of the city still plays host to small communities of those brave enough to try and survive. Add in untapped ruins to scavenge through, Old World vaults and resource deposits, one would find many reasons for heading out into the wasteland. However, doing so isn’t an easy task. Raiders, Marauders and Monsters abound in the unknown makes getting anywhere a problem for small teams, and above all? Most places were simply picked clean long ago. So, for those that manage the challenging task of finding anything of value and are in dire need of moving these acquired goods to Neon City, the Caravaneers are here to answer the call.

Made up of myriad smaller groups that range in size from single family operations to burgeoning mercenary companies, the Caravaneer Controller Network is less a set-in-stone operation, and more of a loose confederation that sets the groundwork of rules and regulations for each of these groups to follow. In turn, the Network provides lucrative contracts to escort and transport goods, either across the wasteland to different settlements, or to ports of call on the border with Neon City. While anyone can register for the Network, a skilled team of background investigators keep fair tabs on applicants, ensuring that few bandits or raiders slip through the cracks and threaten the integrity of the CCN.

In recent history, there has been a lack of a leader amongst the confederation, and while the Network has maintained itself, the lack of leadership has caused fringe factions to begin to form in the wastes amongst powerful families and businesses.
