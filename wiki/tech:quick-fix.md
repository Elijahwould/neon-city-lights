##### Source: Player's Handbook (Mending)

*Manipulation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (2 spare screws)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

This tech repairs a single break or tear in an object you touch, such as a broken chain link, two halves of a broken key, a torn hoodie, or a leaking soda bottle. As long as the break or tear is no larger than 1 foot in any dimension, you mend it, leaving no trace of the former damage.

This tech can physically repair a technical item or construct, but the tech can't restore technical abilities to such an object.
