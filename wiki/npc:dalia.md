**Name.** Dalia

**Lineage.** Remake

**Age.** 38

**Height and Weight.** 6'2", 290 lbs

**Pronouns.** she/her

**Affiliation.** [Poncy's Bar](/loc:poncys-bar)

**Features.** Unmarked facial plate except for an LCD screen that creates eyes and emotes to express her mood.

**Status.** Alive

Dalia is a standard remake model, who usually is wearing low-profile combat gear as she blends into the furniture at Poncy's. Normally, she sports comfortable clothes and a keychain carrying a small cat plushie.

Picked up by Katze while on a job, and given an unfortunate truth or two, Dalia is a concerningly well-armed remake who acts as additional security for Poncy's.

Though with how bubbly and excitable she is, those she meets wonder how she even can handle the job.

**Owner.** JessicaShtebs, Assorted Beanie#0512

**Creator.** JessicaShtebs, Assorted Beanie#0512

**Fixer Use.** Open for minor gigs.
