**Location Name.** The Starry Night

**Owner.** Pumpkin Spice

**Location Type.** Business

**Years in existence.** <1 year

**Type.** Maid Café

**Area.** [Neon City Uptown Residential](/district:uptown-residential)

**Impact.** Minor

After leaving her old life behind and taking all the money she saved up to this point, Pumpkin Spice fulfilled her lifelong dream of managing a café. True to her roots, she decided to make it a "maid café". No matter their gender, all employees will greet you with a smile and wearing a cute, but modest, maid outfit.

The Starry Night is a small café located in the heart of the Uptown Residential District, naturally inside a beautiful, corpo-controlled neighborhood. The Starry Night is well-loved by the residents in the area, and while the flow of traffic to the establishment seems moderate at best, the benefit of that results in an open seat always available to those who wish to indulge in one of Neon City's finest cafés.

Recently, the café has gained a huge popularity boost after introducing their 'Starry Night' coffee. Pumpkin claims to have found the secret ingredient to making the perfect cup of cappuccino. The mystery as to what that ingredient actually is? The owner of the establishment would prefer you visit to find out, yourself.
