**Impact.** Minor

The Sweat Works is an imposing gym, or at least the front is styled as such. The broad metal and glass facade of the building features moving neon projections of a variety of well-built individuals performing various feats of athleticism, all of them positively drenched with sweat—some of them being runners of some repute.

Inside the establishment, the style remains much the same, sans the ever-present neon projections. Everything inside is large, overengineered to the point of nigh indestructability, undoubtedly masculine, and positively reeking of body heat, sweat, and overcharged pheromones.

The gym has boards posted up all over the location featuring the gym rules.

### Sweat Works Rules

> 1. Return any and all used equipment to rack after use. No one wants to trip over a weight!
> 
> 2. Sanitize your equipment after use. We like your smell, not your germs!
> 
> 3. Don't drop the weights, unless you are on a designated lifting pad.
> 
> 4. No food or drinks besides bottled water. Rotting food isn't what we want here.
> 
> 5. Authentic Sweat Works Gym attire only, but you can wear whatever shoes you like.
> 
> 6. Consent is sexy. Ask before you touch!
> 
> 7. Keep anything too *aggressive* to the Sauna or Showers. They have drains for a reason!
