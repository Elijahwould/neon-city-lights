## "Aliens? No, no. We *made* these ones. Yes, we put the tentacles there, too."

##### Source: [Lasers & Liches: Retroverse](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/product/372678/Lasers--Liches-Players-Mix-Beta), Neon City NightsThanks to Celica#1988 for taking the idea originally submitted by CrimsonSage#5162 and expanding it, Gabby#6361 for writing the lore for the cosmologies, and reny#8234 for the inspirations and ideas!

![Probing Light in the Depths (Deepdive), a Neptunian Thulian Streamer (art: reny, character: Celica)](https://neoncitylights.wdfiles.com/local--files/race:thulian/Deep.Dive.png)

After succeeding with their designs for Nayalings, Old World scientists quickly conceived of the Thulian as a way for humanity to conquer not only what at the time was possible, but as a way to reach even further beyond into spaces where humanity could ill afford to tread unchanged, allowing them to reach into the furthest reaches of the nigh-uninhabitable. Thulians were the foremost inhabitants of the Bezosphere besides old humanity, and in time, became its conquerors. As its broken abandoned pieces fell back to Earth, so did Thulians return to it, where they found they were now faced with a changed world.

There are things they share with their predecessors: their thicker skins, for starters, as well as long tails. The similarities end there, as instead of horns, some Thulian heads can bear many smaller tentacles which serve to heighten their senses, while others have two heavier tentacles that are prehensile and used almost as additional limbs in their own right. The colors of their skin also come in much broader, deeper shades than those of Nayalings', often reflecting the vast, unforgiving universe they were engineered to adapt to.

While Nayalings are regarded with some level of suspicion due their history as conquerors and significantly alien appearance, that pales in comparison to the reactions that Thulians often provoke from the ignorant: to many, they are more akin to aberrant, nigh-eldritch figures couched in human form. What's more, it's rare for Thulians to feel any particular shame about their own appearance, with many Thulians often using their otherworldly natures and appearance to their advantage.

### Roleplaying a Thulian

When roleplaying a Thulian, remember that the different varieties of Thulian were engineered to adapt to the most extreme, hostile environments that humanity would otherwise be unable to tread. This means that your kind have historically spent a lot of their lives isolated away from the rest of humanity—existing far past the fringes of conceivable reality for most commonfolk. Your mannerisms may be completely bizarre to people who have lived on Earth. Think of all the oddities that might come from living in an extreme, otherworldly environment, and how that might inform your actions and interactions with others. In what other ways does your lived experience come across as alien to others? Moreover, how is life adjusting to Neon City when your natural traits may make the experience of the city not as optimal as you'd hoped? With a body that almost seems crafted for a specific purpose, how do you make do when removed from that environment?

### Thulian Naming Conventions

Like their predecessors, Thulian naming conventions tend toward descriptions of sensations. Unlike them, they often tend to be more esoteric and abstract, not always relying on visual descriptors, but often of the feelings the sights, sounds and overall sensations inspire. This includes experiences that a Thulian might have considered beautiful in their life, thus naming their progeny after them.

Some examples are Velvet Shudder Through Vast Landscapes, Glittering Stars Above, Unfathomable Yearning, Brackish Frozen Depths, C-Beams Near Tannhauser, Multicolored Beds of Coral, etc.

# Thulian Features

- **Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1, or increase three different scores by 1.
- **Age.** Thulians mature at the same rate as humans and live about as long.
- **Size.** Your size is Medium. Thulians are between 5 and 8 feet tall.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Demon Seed.** You have advantage on saving throws made against fear effects.
- **Mr. Self Destruct.** For the purposes of observation techs and creature type detection abilities, you read as freak in addition to humanoid.
- **Hyperpower.** As a Thulian, you were designed to thrive and conquer in the absolute most extreme environments. You automatically succeed on any save prompted by an extreme climate as described in chapter 5 of the Dungeon Master's Guide.
  - Additionally, you gain the ability to brush off the effects of radiation, or of damage of a certain type, determined by your cosmology. As a reaction, upon taking damage of the triggering type, or when prompted to make a saving throw to resist the effects of radiation, you gain resistance to damage of that type and auto-succeed on saving throws against the effects of radiation for one minute. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
- **The New Flesh.** Due to curious additions to their DNA, Thulians developed tentacle growths where their Nayaling cousins might have seen a tail and horns. These tentacles grant Thulians curious abilities, though they manifest in different ways.
  - You have advantage on acrobatics checks when you use your tentacle to assist the action.

## Thulian Cosmologies

### Jovian

The original design, Jovians were made specifically to endure the theoretical limits of space and its hazards, especially on space-faring missions that require exploration across a variety of terrain, both real and hypothesized. The Jovian is unique among its peers in that its tentacles double as a mental communications array, allowing it to control its own nanites on a whim and deliver messages to others; this allows a small squad of Thulian to better communicate sensitive information through tightbeam nanite transmission. They are recognizable through their graceful glide and dark form that contrasts the mighty cosmos mimicked across their form. Oddly enough, their long "hair", as one put it, only adds to the lonesome beauty they possess.

- **Into the Void.** You gain nightvision, allowing you to see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light and in darkness as if it were dim light. You discern colors in that darkness as shades of gray.
- **I'm Not From This World.** Jovian Thulians have been engineered to work freely across any surface—in Zero G, or otherwise—and often with nearly no air to breathe. You can hold your breath for up to an hour.
  - Additionally, you have a climbing speed equal to your walking speed. In addition, at 3rd level, you can move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and upside down along ceilings, while leaving your hands free.
- **Zero-Sum.** Your Hyperpower triggers off of entropic damage.
- **The Eater of Dreams.** While your tentacle that serves as your tail mostly serves to help your balance, the tentacles on your head are long and bizarre, almost like writhing hair, and grow oddly close to your brain stem. As a result, the nanites in them find it easy to attune to the nanites in other people's minds. The variation of your the new flesh trait grants you the following abilities:
  - You can control the nanites in your mind to speak directly to the mind of any creature you can see within 60 feet of you. Your communication doesn't give the creature the ability to respond to you in the same way.
  - You have advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks against creatures with an Intelligence score greater than 3 within 30 feet of you.

### Mercurial

What happens when your project leaders realize that space can be incredibly hot? Using radically different techniques and approaches to better create a being that could resist the extreme heat and strange volcanic environments of Earth and beyond, the Mercurial Thulian was born. Using data gathered on Earth and purposefully turned up even further, the Mercurial treats the solar and planetary heat of the universe as a welcome companion. With limitless energy within their core, these walking fusion reactors navigate hot environments and are capable of performing long-term studies on the formation of stars in real time without risk of permanent blindness. Compared to their void-like cousin, Mercurial are more active and expressive in movements, using fiery shapes and sizes to get their point across, and their pores allow them to vent heat out of themselves—something that, with practice, they have evolved to use for traversal. Their tail is often utilized to accompany these gestures, even so far as mimicking behaviors seen by Wukong, oddly enough. Their body is brighter, like the surface of the sun, viewed under a lens that doesn't blind everyone in a several mile radius.

- **Sunspots.** You are immune to the [blinded](/con:blinded) condition.
- **10 Miles High.** In the many experiments that led to the birth of the Mercurial Thulian, they were given the ability to vent large amounts of steam from inside of their body, using it to propel them temporarily through the air and to slow their descents. As part of your movement, you can choose to gain a flying speed of 30 feet. To use this speed, you cannot be wearing heavy armor, and you can only use this movement to move in a straight line. You must land at the end of your turn. If you do not, you begin falling slowly at a rate of 60 feet per round at the end of your turn, taking no fall damage. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
  - Additionally, you are immune to inhaled poisons, toxins and diseases.
- **Burning Bright.** Your Hyperpower triggers off of fire damage.
- **Appendage.** You have simple tentacles on your head that serve little purpose, but the tentacle replacing your tail is hefty, dexterous and prehensile. The variation of your the new flesh trait grants you the following abilities:
  - Your tail is capable of wielding any non-ranged, one-handed weapon you are proficient with.
  - Your tail can be used to initiate a grapple.

### Neptunian

Some scientists decided to turn their gaze below sea level—deep, deep below. Various planets have a multitude of lakes and oceans made of a different variety of liquids that no human could endure. And their own plate tectonics were unknown even to the finest mind. The Neptunian was designed to conquer those theories. Designed to be sensitive to the vibrations and tremors, the Neptunian are unparalleled in their observations of earth-like and watery movements—including alerting to dangers heretofore were only numbers on a page. Their shape is better described as a variation of the Jovian and Mercurial: tentacle-like hair and a tail that served a more functional purpose when used in unison, combined with shades of ocean colors that made them recognizable in a group. Their voice is comparable to the Jovian, but with hints of an odd cadence attached and their ability to navigate waters was unmatched by their cousins.

- **La Mer.** You gain tremorsense, allowing you to detect and pinpoint the origin of vibrations within a 20-foot radius of you, provided that you and the source of the vibrations are in contact with the same ground or substance. This tremorsense can't be used to detect flying or incorporeal creatures.
  - If you are underwater, the range of your tremorsense doubles.
- **Deep.** You can breathe air and water and can hold your breath for up to 30 minutes.
  - Your base swimming speed is 30 feet.
  - Aquatic critters have an extraordinary affinity with your people. You can communicate simple ideas with critters that can breathe water. They can understand the meaning of your words, though you have no special ability to understand them in return.
- **Adrift and at Peace.** Your Hyperpower triggers off of cold damage.
- **Satellite.** The tentacles on your head and tail give you many advantages in everyday life. The variation of your the new flesh trait grants you the following abilities:
  - Your tail is capable of wielding any civilian, non-ranged, one-handed, light weapon you are proficient with.
  - Both the tentacles on your head, when used together, can fulfill the somatic component of any tech, so long as they are free to move.
  - So long as your tail is free and is used to assist you, your swimming speed is increased by 10 feet.
