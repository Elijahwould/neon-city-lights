##### Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

**Deprecated Feat.** This feat cannot be taken by characters since 10/25/2023. A character with this feat may instead want to look at these other feats:

- [CQC Firearm Training](/feat:cqc-firearm-training) for one-handed ranged weapons.
- [Deadly Marksmanship](/feat:deadly-marksmanship) for two-handed ranged weapons.
- [Old World Techniques](/feat:old-world-techniques) for the hunting bow and slingshot.

You have a quick hand and keen eye when it comes to both civilian and military ranged weapons, granting you the following benefits:

- Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain proficiency with military ranged weapons.
- You ignore the loading property of any ranged weapons with which you are proficient.
- Being within 5 feet of a hostile creature doesn’t impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls.
- When you use the Attack action and attack with a one-handed weapon, you can use a bonus action to attack with a pistol or revolver you are holding.
