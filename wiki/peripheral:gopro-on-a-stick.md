##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Rod of Alertness)

*Peripheral, very rare (requires installation)*

**Specs:** 2 lbs., $$1,000,000

This stick has a camera mounted on the top, and the following properties:

***Alertness.*** While holding this rod, you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and on rolls for initiative.

***Techs.*** While holding the stick, you can use an action to cast one of the following techs from it: [Threat Detector](/tech:threat-detector), [Anomaly Detector](/tech:anomaly-detector), [Biohazard Detector](/tech:biohazard-detector), or [Thermovision](/tech:thermovision).

***Protective Aura.*** As an action, you can plant the shaft end of the stick in the ground, whereupon the camera emits bright light in a 60-foot radius and dim light for an additional 60 feet. While in that bright light, you and any creature that is friendly to you gain a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws and can sense the location of any invisible hostile creature that is also in the bright light.

The light stops being emitted and the effect ends after 10 minutes, or when a creature uses an action to pull the rod from the ground. This property can't be used again until the next dawn.
