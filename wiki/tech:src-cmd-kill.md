##### Source: Player's Handbook (Power Word: Kill)

*9th-level conception*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You issue a command through the source code to force a creature you can see within range (within parameters) to die instantly. If the creature you chose has 100 hit points or fewer, it dies. Otherwise, the tech has no effect.
