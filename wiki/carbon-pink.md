## "Cyberpunk Adventures for D&D 5E…"

You may notice that a lot of our sources and content directly references [Carbon Pink](http://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink). If you didn't come from there, you may be wondering, well, what *exactly* **is** it?

Carbon Pink is the original setting and ruleset upon which Neon City Nights was created. Carbon Pink originally was a massive undertaking between Rapscallion and many co-conspirators to create a spicy and fun Cyberpunk variety of window dressing of Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition so that his friends and fans could play with him. At some point, of course, Rapscallion more or less moved on to doing things he was a bit more interested in, such as his own system Vice & Violence, an original setting that does not use D&D rules. As such, Carbon Pink as it were, is mostly left to the fans to mess with.

Neon City Nights owes a huge debt to Carbon Pink, mostly in general conceit and theming. Lore-wise, it is not the same—the events that led to the transformation of the world in Neon City Nights are completely different, as the Great Collapse serves as a major reset, while life continues mostly uninterrupted in Carbon Pink. There are many more places in Carbon Pink to explore than there are (currently) in Neon City Nights, being that most of the action in NCN is centered on Neon City proper.

Originally, the primary reason for this Wiki was to exist as a backup: the Carbon Pink Wiki had gone down for a bit under 24 hours across two separate days, which caused a lot of issues for many of our players who were trying to either create a character, find information about their lineage or class, so on and so forth. As a result, we decided to create our own version of the Wiki. However, our server also has a lot of homebrew rules that aren't exactly in Carbon Pink proper.

In some cases, those things are outright different or expanded on—we no longer use the core Human and Custom Lineage, for example, and have a whole new variety of Human called the [New Human](/race:new-human). We have an entire lineage, [Kaijuborn](/race:kaijuborn), that was created before the version from [All Along the Watchtower](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-NEJcj_BQBH6Z7ftvs1g) came along, even as we inherited from that very same source in spirit in other places, like our version of [AI-Simar](/race:ai-simar). We have our completely own version of [Netsucker-based player race](/race:netnibbler)!

Rules-wise, it would be best to think of Neon City Nights as an **expansion** of Carbon Pink, in the same way that Dungeons & Dragons' 3rd Edition eventually led to 3.5E, which eventually led to Paizo's Pathfinder. We add a bunch of things that are… *mostly* backwards compatible. But it boils down to this: when playing on Neon City Nights, you will be primarily using this wiki as your source for all of your character creation and rules checking needs. We cite our sources where possible, but there is very little that hasn't been changed in some manner, shape or form from the original Carbon Pink sources, so it is important to not complicate things by requiring more than one source.

If you're here to play classic Carbon Pink, you may find that we're not exactly what you're looking for. However, we'd still love to invite you to try our spin on the Carbon Pink system.
