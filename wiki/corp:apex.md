**Corporation Name.** APEX Incorporated

**CEO.** Illrigger

**Years in business.** ~98

**Industry.** Robotics, technology and nanomachinery

**Motto.** *"~"*

**Impact.** Major

APEX specializes in everything robotics, technology and nanomachinery, along with all that falls under and around it. Many drones, turrets and even a few niche weapons come from APEX. The robotics company—supposedly to secure its intellectual properties—enforces a zero-tolerance policy on modifications, with some devices simply ceasing to function if taken apart and others exploding or activating a location signal, calling in a bad surprise at worst.

Their CEO is not much of one. Illrigger was considered to be the leading figure of a group of small time, under-the-table runners who were once facing bankruptcy and are now transformed into a greater player on the field of tech and robotization. He was somewhat revered as a human who pushed the boundaries of technological innovations and mechanical marvels; able to connect to several unmanned drones and vehicles, as well as half feared for those very same drones, now manufactured and out for public use, being made to spy on the general populace… and more.

Some who know Illrigger would have surmised that he'd have no need of leading a corporation like APEX, but as his influence and power grew, so did what he could use it for. Some presumed that he was selling out, given that he was a once controversial and daring runner, now turned mysterious head of a robotech company. In reality, APEX is more of a platform: a base for his operations, a wizard's tower for him to channel his "magic"… so to speak.
