##### Source: Player's Handbook (Transport via Plants)

*6th-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 10 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** 1 round

This tech creates a link between a Large or larger line within range and another line, at any distance, on the same plane of existence. You must have seen or touched the destination line at least once before. For the duration, any creature can step onto the target line and exit from the destination line by using 5 feet of movement.
