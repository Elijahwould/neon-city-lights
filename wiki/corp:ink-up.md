**Corporation Name.** Ink'd Up Tattoos and Body Modifications

**CEO.** Jessica Bourbon

**Years in business.** ~30 years

**Industry.** Tattoos and other Lewd Augments

**Motto.** "*Don't just think—get some ink!*"

**Impact.** Minor

Ink'd Up has a patent on the Nanite Infused "W&T" tattoos, which have a variety of potent effects on the user. They were founded by a group of art students from the University of Neon City with help from the now defunct [Bliss Co.](/corp:bliss-co) They've since branched out as their own company, profiting off the sudden destruction of Bliss and their asset chain of command.

With the market open, they finally put their tattoos up on the market, allowing any tattoo artist or ripperdoc to apply them to their customers, for a small commission, of course. So, if you see someone on the street with a tattoo over their stomach, you can probably assume it's an Ink'd Up special.
