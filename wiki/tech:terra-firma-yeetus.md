##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything/Elemental Evil Player's Companion (Magic Stone)

*Manipulation trick*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** 1 minute

You touch one to three pebbles and inscribe them with technical designs. You or someone else can make a ranged tech attack with one of the pebbles by throwing it or hurling it with a sling. If thrown, a pebble has a range of 60 feet. If someone else attacks with a pebble, that attacker adds your techcasting ability modifier, not the attacker's, to the attack roll. On a hit, the target takes bludgeoning damage equal to 1d6 + your techcasting ability modifier. Whether the attack hits or misses, the tech then ends on the stone.

If you cast this tech again, the tech ends on any pebbles still affected by your previous casting.
