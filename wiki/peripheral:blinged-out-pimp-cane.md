##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Staff of Charming)

*Peripheral, rare (requires installation by a [influencer](/influencer), [ordinator](/ordinator), [shut-in](/shutin), [streamer](/streamer), or [techwizard](/techwizard))*

**Specs:** 4 lb., $$700,000

While holding this cane, you can use an action to expend 1 of its 10 charges to cast [Meet Cute](/tech:meet-cute), [Command Prompt](/tech:command-prompt), or [Translator Pack](/tech:translator-pack) from it using your tech save DC. The cane can also be used as a technical [quarterstaff](/weapon:quarterstaff).

If you are holding the cane and fail a saving throw against a conception tech that targets only you , you can turn your failed save into a successful one. You can't use this property of the cane again until the next dawn. If you succeed on a save against an conception tech that targets only you, with or without the cane's intervention, you can use your reaction to expend 1 charge from the cane and turn the tech back on its caster as if you had cast the tech.

The cane regains 1d8 + 2 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff becomes a nontechnical quarterstaff.
