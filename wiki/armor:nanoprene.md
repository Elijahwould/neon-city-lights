*Medium armor, 4 lb., AC = 14 + Dex modifier (max 2), disadvantage on stealth checks*

You'd think that with a name like nanoprene, which calls to mind a neoprene suit that's been enhanced with nanites, that nanoprene armor would be easy to move around in. And it is, for the most part: it's tighter and thus more restrictive than a full nanofilm or synthweave suit, but the problem is that nanoprene also just is a very squeaky, surprisingly heavy material. If you need protection at a good price, it does the job effectively, but for something that's just offering skin-tight protection, it is alarmingly useless in stealth situations.
