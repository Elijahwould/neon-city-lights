While extending the length of the barrel on a gun may make it slightly more unwieldy, it does help a lot when it comes to extending the range of a heavier gun. Any two-handed ranged weapon (except for the longbow and the M134 Tomgirl) can be modified with this to increase both the distance of both its short and long ranges by 50%.
