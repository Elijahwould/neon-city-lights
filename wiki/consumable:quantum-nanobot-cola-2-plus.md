*Consumable (soda), uncommon*

Quantum Cola's™ health-conscious spin-off of the classic Nano-Cola, Nanobot Cola PLUS, or Nano-Cola+ or Nano-PLUS based on who you ask. The brown, sugary, soda sacrifices some sugar content to accentuate faint hints of vanilla, and the metallic aftertaste of twice the nanite presence by volume! The "+" symbol features prominently on Nano-PLUS packaging, colored in an inverse to its classic counterpart.

Nano-Cola+ bubbles with a faint white static when agitated that makes it perfect for chugging, or for long sipping experiences.

You regain 4d4+4 hit points when you drink this Nano-PLUS, which you can personally perform as a bonus action. When self-administered as an action, you regain the maximum value of hit points (20).
