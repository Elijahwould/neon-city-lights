## "I'm hungry. Where's the closest restaurant?"

![Rose and Natasha Viszla at a convenience store (art by Spliff)](https://neoncitylights.wdfiles.com/local--files/locations/natasha-and-rose.png)

Runners have places to be and people to see. But where exactly *are* those places, and what things are offered in Neon City proper?

Neon City, being as big as it is, has locales for just about anything anyone could dream of. Just because they aren't listed, doesn't mean they don't exist—places are being catalogued as soon as they are found, the locations of nice little holes in the wall being passed around runners as reliable places to both get something to eat and lay low, while interesting stores of all types are shared on BBS posts due to their interesting selection of goods.

But it's not always Neon City. Interesting places can be found outside of Neon City, too: in the outskirts, in the settlements to the outside, in the desert, maybe even as far as the Glass Sea. Just remember: anything that brain of yours can think of can be found.█
