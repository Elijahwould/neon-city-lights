**Organization Name.** Task Force Chimera

**Leader.** Commanders Tarron Hask, Sarah Traynor and Anthony Ward

**Organization Type.** Private Military Contractor (PMC)

**Years in existence.** ~8

**Goals.** Destruction of harmful entities to innocent populations & revitalization of old world hotspots.

**Creed.** "*Each life is of infinite value, and merits sacrificing everything for.*"

**Impact.** Minor

Chimera is a PMC dedicated to peacekeeping along with the revitalization of the old world. They are aligned with neither corporate nor rebel interests and consider both to be equal targets. As a rule of thumb, if what you do is harming innocents, then Chimera will respond.
