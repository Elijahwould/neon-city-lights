##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Illusory Dragon)

*8th-level deception*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You create a volumetric representation of a massive kaiju in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. The deception lasts for the tech’s duration and occupies its space, as if it were a creature.

When the deception appears, any of your enemies that can see it must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of it for 1 minute. If a frightened creature ends its turn in a location where it doesn’t have line of sight to the deception, it can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.

As a bonus action on your turn, you can move the deception up to 60 feet. At any point during its movement, you can cause it to exhale a blast of energy in a 60-foot cone originating from its space.

When you create the kajiu, choose a damage type: acid, cold, fire, lightning, necrotic, or poison. Each creature in the cone must make an Intelligence saving throw, taking 7d6 damage of the chosen damage type on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

The deception is tangible because of the shadow stuff used to create it, but attacks miss it automatically. it succeeds on all saving throws, and it is immune to all damage and conditions. A creature that uses an action to examine the kajiu can determine that it is an deception by succeeding on an Intelligence (Investigation) check against your tech save DC. If a creature discerns the deception for what it is, the creature can see through it and has advantage on saving throws against its breath.
