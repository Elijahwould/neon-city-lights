##### Source: Player's Handbook (Tavern Brawler), Neon City NightsThanks to Celica#1988 for this one!

Accustomed to the rough-and-tumble fighting using whatever weapons happen to be at hand, you gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Strength or Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You are proficient with improvised weapons.
- Your unarmed strike uses a d4 for damage.
- When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike or an improvised weapon on your turn, you can use a bonus action to attempt to grapple the target.
- If you have the [Grappler](/feat:grappler) feat, when you use the Throw Prone special grapple option, you may opt to treat the thrown creature as a thrown improvised weapon. You may throw the grappled creature at another target creature. The target creature must make a Dexterity save (DC = 8 + your Strength modifier + your proficiency bonus). On a failure, you deal your unarmed strike or improvised weapon damage (whichever is higher) to both the thrown creature and the target and they both fall prone. On a success, only the thrown creature takes the damage, and the thrown creature lands on a square adjacent to the target creature.
