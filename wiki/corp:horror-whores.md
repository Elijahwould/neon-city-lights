**Corporation Name.** Horror Whores, Inc.

**CEO.** Siegfried

**Years in business.** < 20

**Industry.** Entertainment, smut

**Motto.** *"From J-Horror to Slashers, we have a wide array of things for you to try. Try Horror Whores today!"*

**Impact.** Defunct

Horror Whores was founded by its current CEO, Siegfried, only a little over 20 years ago. The large minotaur splicer realized soon after meeting his wife that there was a huge untapped market for monster… enthusiasts, as well as a huge desire for simulated dangerous, intimate engagements in a safe, controlled environment. Thus, the Horror Whores were founded as a way to provide this service. Known for its wide array of themed hotels and entertainment center pop ups, it turns out that you, too, can get your mind broken for fun.

Unfortunately for it, Horror Whores did not survive the fall of Bliss Co.—with Bliss having a very large stake in Horror Whores, and the fall of Bliss meaning several people went to cash out, Horror Whores went from more or less staying afloat, to being completely insolvent. They were shuttered almost immediately, with the majority of their assets and IP being sold out to [Gothic Hoard](/corp:gothic-hoard).
