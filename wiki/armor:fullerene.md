*Heavy armor, 6 lb., strength 15, AC = 17, disadvantage on stealth checks*

Fullerene is one of the two major options for maximum protection available to people who primarily use heavy armor. Unlike composite armor, which is a little lighter but offers comparable protection (if you account for the fact that composite armor lets its user dodge more easily), fullerene offers full-body protection: an under-suit of nanoprene has supports and straps to allow the application of several plates made out of several different compound materials. While the armor is not incredibly heavy to use for most people, it still is restrictive enough that you get from it is what you get.
