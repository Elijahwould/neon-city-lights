**District.** Uptown Residential

**Population.** ?

**Type.** Residential

The residential areas circling the Bliss Co. zone, mostly inhabited by the rich and the famous that aren't directly connected to BlissCo.

With the recent collapse of Bliss Co., however, many uptown residents have grown weary of the inevitable rise of a new corporation at the very top. They lived in relative harmony with Bliss Co. largely allowing them to do as they please—just as easily can that now be taken away. As a result, the uptown population remains on edge, and are even less cordial towards those not of their social class.
