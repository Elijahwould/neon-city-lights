**CEO.** Unknown

**Years in Business.** ~90

**Industry.** Spicy sports streaming channels property of SE-SL

**Motto.** "*The leader in Spicy Entertainment.*"

**Impact.** Minor

The Spicy Entertainment Sports League was created after an entrepreneur stumbled upon Old World footage of different television channels dedicated only to sports. Driven by the idea of promoting these various sports that once claimed hundreds of millions of viewers, SE-SL's goal became the recreation of these lost forms of entertainment with members of all lineages, bodies and fetishes, and broadcasting these professional games to the viewers at home, with a spicy twist!

Rumors have recently hinted that SE-SL is planning on creating their own leagues, shifting from exclusive, public broadcasting to producing their own pay-per-view shows.
