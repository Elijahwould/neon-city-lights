##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Ring of Telekinesis)

*Keyring, very rare (requires installation)*

**Specs:** <1 lb., $$1,000,000

While wearing this ring, you can cast the [Nanokinesis](/tech:nanokinesis) tech at will, but you can target only objects that aren't being worn or carried.
