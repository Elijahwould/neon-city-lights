##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Boots of Elvenkind)

*Gizmo, uncommon*

**Specs:** <1 lbs., $$250,000

These fine kicks absorb ambient sound and reduce joint strain. The wearer has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks that rely on moving silently, regardless of the surface they are moving across. Comes in a variety of colors and styles, with optional self-tying laces.
