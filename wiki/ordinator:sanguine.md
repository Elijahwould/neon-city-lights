## “I know that if anyone ever came up to me and asked me to take an untried vaccine, I'd ask ‘Have you taken it?’ and by God, if that person said no, I'd tell them to go to hell.”

(flavor text about your AI being a defunct medical AI. unlike the lifegiver synergy, this AI was repurposed from medical to R&D and cares precious little for your well being versus the resource you provide. it wants you to be a chemical manufactorum and transmute your blood)

### Bonus Proficiency

At 1st level, you gain proficiency with military weapons.

### Bioreaction Beacon

From 1st level, your AI utilizes all available biomass and mysdata, even from those of the wounds you inflicted, to fuel your techs. When you use a tech of 1st level or higher to inflict damage to any bio-harvestable creature, those creatures suffer additional entropic damage equal to 2 + the tech's level.

(Note on bio-harvestable: corporeal wetware and peopleware qualify as bio-harvestable. They either have blood or vital fuel. Incorporeal creatures don't count. Software type creatures count only when encountered in person. Encountering them through proxy, projection, and otherwise lacking corporeality, they don't count.)

### AI Assistance: Vessel of Vessels

Starting at 2nd level, you can use your AI Assistance to briefly commandeer a creature's circulatory and fuel line systems.

As an action, you target a Large or smaller bio-harvestable creature (or their remains) within 60 feet of you. That creature must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your tech save DC or immediately move up to half its movement in any direction of your choice and make a single weapon attack against a creature of your choice within its range. Dead or unconscious creatures automatically fail their saving throw. At 8th level, you can target a Huge or smaller bio-harvestable creature (or their remains.)

### AI Assistance: Transfusion Sync-Drift

Starting at 6th level, you can use your AI Assistance to catalogue a sample of vital fluid or biomass from a bio-harvestable creature that is at least 2 ounces, and that has been spilled or separated no longer than a week ago.

As an action, you can focus on the sample of the creature to sync your vitals through observation. You know their approximate distance and direction from you, as well as their general state of health, as long as they are within 10 miles of you. You can maintain this effect as though you were concentrating on a tech for up to 1 hour.

During your vital sync, you can spend an action to attempt to connect with the sampled creature's subjectivity. The target makes a Constitution saving throw against your tech save DC. If it succeeds, the connection is resisted, ending the vital sync and dealing 2d6 entropic damage to you. Upon a failed save, you can choose to either see through the eyes of or hear through the ears of the target (or suitable analogues) for a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1). During this time, you are blind or deaf (respectively) in regard to your own senses.

### Biochemical Manufactorum

At 8th level, you can synthesize your own vital fluid into nanite-enhancing chemical compounds to recover tech slots. As an action, you can recover tech slots that have a combined level equal to or less than half of your ordinator level (rounded up), and none of the slots can be 6th level or higher. You immediately reduce your hit points by 1d6 per tech slot level recovered. You can’t use this feature again until you finish a long rest

### Conscripted Organ Donors

At 17th level, as an action, you can transmit a hemomagnetic nanite command that extends 30 feet out from you. This aura siphons the nanites and biomass of nearby foes, causing them to deteriorate, malfunction, and hemorrhage.

For 1 minute, any bio-harvested enemy creatures that begin their turn within the aura or enter it or the first time on their turn immediately suffer 2d6 entropic damage. Any bio-harvested enemy creature that would regain hit points while within the aura only regains half the number of hit points as the signal overrides the restoration.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest.
