*Cybernetic enhancement, commercial*

Choose a class: Influencer, Ordinator, Shut-In, Source Coder, Streamer, or Techwizard. You learn a trick of your choice from that class's tech list. Your techcasting ability for this trick depends on the class you chose: Charisma for Influencer, Source Coder, or Streamer; Wisdom for Ordinator or Shut-In; or Intelligence for Techwizard.
