##### Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (Summon Lesser Demons)

*3rd-level manifestation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a vial of blood or blood-like substance from a humanoid killed within the past 24 hours and a forbidden web link)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You utter foul and hateful words, summoning viruses from the chaos of the Hellsites. Roll on the following table to determine what appears.

| d6 | Viruses Summoned |
| --- | --- |
| 1-2 | Two viruses of challenge rating 1 or lower |
| 3-4 | Four viruses of challenge rating 1/2 or lower |
| 5-6 | Eight viruses of challenge rating 1/4 or lower |

The DM chooses the viruses, such as manes or dretches, and you choose the unoccupied spaces you can see within range where they appear. A summoned virus disappears when it drops to 0 hit points or when the tech ends.

The viruses are hostile to all creatures, including you. Roll initiative for the summoned viruses as a group, which has its own turns. The viruses pursue and attack the nearest non-viruses to the best of their ability.

As part of casting the tech, you can form a circle on the ground with the blood used as a material component. The circle is large enough to encompass your space. While the tech lasts, the summoned viruses can't cross the circle or harm it, and they can't target anyone within it. Using the material component in this manner consumes it when the tech ends.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 6th or 7th level, you summon twice as many viruses. If you cast it using a tech slot of 8th or 9th level, you summon three times as many viruses.
