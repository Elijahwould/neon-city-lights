**Impact.** Minor

*"Pizzazz™! The pizza with edible glitter you can **really** taste!"*

Once upon a time, Pizzazz™! was an incredible place to eat: some of the highest quality pizza in all of Neon City, served in a single location deep in the heart of Neon City. Alas, nothing lasts forever, and good times always come to an end. So was the case for Pizzazz™!, whose quality took a nosedive somewhere in the early 1610s when they decided franchising and standardization was a good idea. The brand has not recovered since. Now, cardboard pizza is served all throughout their myriad locations, and people yearn for the days where a few creds could at **least** get you something that was hot, and ready, if not particularly good.

Rumors persist of a single Pizzazz™! location that still adheres to the good old ways, but the location always changes. Still, Flutter occasionally comes alive with information…
