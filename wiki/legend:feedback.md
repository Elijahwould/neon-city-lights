**Name.** Brent "Feedback" Bristol

**Lineage.** New Human (Human w/Kaiju DNA)

**Age.** 30

**Height and Weight.** 9'9", 650 lbs (19'6", 5200 lbs when large)

**Pronouns.** he/him

**Classes/Subclasses.** Renegade (Path of the Juggernaut) 17, Ronin (Hardlight Soldier) 3

**Features.** Hyper-extensive, military-grade cybernetics and Kaiju genetic splicing. Wears a gas mask that constantly pumps a mix of drugs and various other substances directly into his bloodstream

**Status.** Alive

Brent was a native to Neon City long before entering the runner scene. He was originally a member of a small-scale private security company until he and all his co-workers were flatlined by a forgotten Street Legend on an escort job. Luckily, ROID Co. was able to bring him back to the land of the living thanks to his lust for life, and he’s been working as their poster-boy under the alias “Feedback” ever since.

Since making his debut as a runner, chaos seems to follow him wherever he goes, for better or worse. He’s the proud father of six children, and has made quite the reputation for himself as a one-man-army. What few people know is that he’s constantly in a struggle with his cyber psychosis, and is obsessed with the notion of revenge for his death way back when.

*“Knock me down, I bounce back harder, that’s the Feedback effect baby!” — Feedback*
