**Corporation Name.** NANiTE Consumer Technologies and Holdings, LLC

**CEO.** Unknown

**Years in business.** ~98

**Industry.** Consumer-grade and high-end electronics

**Motto.** *"Always there when you need it. NANiTE™."*

**Impact.** Minor

NANiTE CTH LLC is known for making and manufacturing a wide line of electronics, gadgetry and gizmos. The vast majority of things developed by NANiTE are primarily consumer-grade devices of varying degrees of quality, but they are occasionally known to make incredibly high-end devices—while also commanding incredibly high, if somehow still competitive, prices.

Their middling construction quality in the mass-produced area does give them somewhat of a nebulous, untrustworthy reputation, but many people simply swear by the more affordable variants of high-end gear that they offer to many. Still, the fact that the actual CEO of the company is unknown doesn't really help with their public perception of "cheap and disposable gear."
