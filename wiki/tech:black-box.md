##### Source: Player's Handbook (Globe of Invulnerability)

*6th-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Self (10-foot radius)

**Components:** V, S, M (a small black box)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

An immobile, faintly shimmering black box barrier springs into existence in a 10-foot radius around you and remains for the duration.

Any tech of 5th level or lower cast from outside the barrier can’t affect creatures or objects within it, even if the tech is cast using a higher level tech slot. Such a tech can target creatures and objects within the barrier, but the tech has no effect on them. Similarly, the area within the barrier is excluded from the areas affected by such techs.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 7th level or higher, the barrier blocks techs of one level higher for each slot level above 6th.
