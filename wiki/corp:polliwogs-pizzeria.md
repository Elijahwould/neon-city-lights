**Corporation Name.** Polly Polliwog’s Pizzeria

**CEO.** Jim Afton

**Industry.** Entertainment, fast food, historical preservation

**Years in Existence.** ~10 years

**Goals.** Provide a fun and safe place where the public can experience the creature comforts of the old-world.

**Motto.** “More than just a taste.”

**Impact.** Minor

Jim Afton, who seeks to revitalize the family restaurant industry, founded Polly Polliwog’s Pizzeria. A former 32-store [BLAM! Co.](/corp:blam-co) franchisee, he used his earnings from those stores to build his vision. Afton’s fondness for old-world tales of restaurants with amusement park midway games influenced his concept for the pizzeria. But since corporations send children away from the city, Polliwog’s caters to a more mature clientele.

Each location features arcade games, amusement rides and animatronic shows besides serving mediocre pizza. Their primary draw are the arcades, which are all coin-operated and reward players with tickets based on score. Players can redeem tickets for prizes, which can range from cheap novelties to unique trinkets.

Another interest generator for the pizzeria since their start has been its animatronic mascots. The show features Buck on drums, Ziggy on electric guitar, Cookie on the keytar, and the lead singer of the show, Polly Polliwog herself. Every bot, when not on stage, spouts interesting, if inaccurate, factoids about the old-world—primarily facts on the 1970s, 1980s and 1990s. However, each bot has its own, unique personality.

### Animatronics

- **Poly Polliwog.** Polly is a pink frog. She loves to sing, have fun, and is known for her sickly-sweet personality. All animatronics have a powerful voice box capable of reaching astonishing highs and lows. Her appearance is similar to 1970s styles—particularly the hippie subculture.
- **Ziggy the Zebra.** Ziggy is a rainbow-striped zebra. They have the personality of a rocker; brash and prideful. Them being the life of the party, they fire party favors from their legally obtained hand-cannonᵀᴹ in a shower of confetti. Their appearance is similar to 1990s styles—particularly the grunge subculture.
- **Cookie the Crow.** Cookie is a violet crow. She is an aloof, edacious, but blithe bot with a love of pizza. Especially chocolate chip cookie pizzas, of which she makes herself with the oven in her abdomen. She also doubles as a waste disposal bot by “eating” any leftovers. Her appearance is like 1980s styles—particularly the goth subculture.
- **Buck the Buffalo.** Buck is a blue buffalo. His personality is that of a tough stoic with a soft spot, often speaking with a country drawl. The shield bots sans the shield inspired the biggest bot in the band, Buck’s design. Buck bots often function as security drones for their respective pizzeria. He has the simplest appearance, wearing only a black bowler hat and a leather vest.

Poly Polliwog's Pizzeria has been scandal-free since its founding. Some claim that Cookie uses the leftovers she eats to make her chocolate chip cookie pizzas. The pizza chain has rejected these claims. The rumors that a gang of rogue Artificiates have been attacking various pizzerias, however, has gained some traction.
