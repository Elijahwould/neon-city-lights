History is written not by the victors, but through generous donations. The history of the old world lies buried beneath the feet of the city and the world proper. Ancient texts, devices, even books that have not been consumed in neon flame still exist somewhere in the reality many Neon City occupants would rather pretend not exist. After all, why would anyone want to leave?

The following are snippets of said history and locations one might encounter at the edges where the map ends. Parts and pieces to make a whole, but necessary parts to be the better learned runner. That and knowing the slowest route to a pizza joint to impress your friends.

# Places

### [Neon City](/neon-city)

Neon City is a massive den of criminal activity, overlooked by corporate overlords that vie for power over one another and have no qualms with hiring mercenaries, dubbed runners or street jukes by the local scene. The city itself is home to all shapes of life, with humans and splicers being the predominant races, and is divided into major districts.

#### [The Undercity](/undercity)

If you thought Neon City was a thieves' den, wait until you get a load of the Undercity. Part home to the City's undesirables, part cavernous network of server farms and strange locations, and part dangerous connection to the past, the Undercity plays host to some of the stranger aspects of the culture of Neon City. It is just as massive as the city above it and is divided into sectors.

### [The Outskirts](/outskirts)

There's lots of places outside of Neon City proper where civilization has managed to get a foothold. Many of those places are sparse and serve as stopping points for runners who wish to explore the untamed, blasted area to the east of the city. Just like the city itself it is also divided—though less officially—into regions.
