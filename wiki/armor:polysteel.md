*Heavy armor, 6 lb., strength 15, AC = 18, disadvantage on stealth checks*

Polysteel is the other of the two major options for maximum protection available to people who primarily use heavy armor. It's mostly unlike most other armors—it is a full suit of armor that sits atop one's base skin-tight suits and undergarments, and is exhaustive, with very few gaps in the protection, to the point where even the joints are fully covered and protected in adjustable polysteel plating. The material is surprisingly light given the full coverage, which makes it very protective while not being much heavier than the alternatives—and it's got a hefty price tag to match.
