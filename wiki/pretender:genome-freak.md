## "If ever I read Satan’s signature upon a face, it is on that of your new friend."

LiNKER and Nanophage are a painful and scarring binary imbibement that leaves those that survive irrevocably changed, yet some found this physiological autonomy on the very precipice of death an exhilarating high, and snatched it from the jaws of sanity. This might sound familiar, as the community-owned mask seeking evolutionary perfection was a great one that has since split, one half being the mask of the genome freak. Torqued darwinists, splice mandate abolitionists, people who looked at the oddities of the kaijuborn and the thulians and thought, why stop? And so, they developed fleshweaving, instant evolutions spurned on by toxic brews.

### Maxims

When you don this mask at 3rd level, you begin to develop aphormorphic, psychosomatic compounds that temporarily alter the malleability and stress responses of your self. You gain four [Fleshweave Maxims](/pretender:fleshweave-maxims) of your choice. You learn an additional Maxim of your choice at 7th level, and can choose a Maxim you know and replace it with a different Maxim. This occurs again at 11th, 15th, and 18th level.

### Fleshweave

Also at 3rd level, you can concoct a single maxim you know when you finish a short or long rest. At 7th level, the number of maxims you can concoct when finishing a short or long rest increases by one. This occurs again at 15th and 20th level.

As a bonus action you can consume a single concocted maxim, or you can consume any number of concocted maxims you have as an action. The effect and side effect of consumed maxims immediately begin impacting you and last until you finish a short or long rest, unless otherwise noted. While one or more consumed maxim is impacting you, you can use an action to restore your base-pairs and undo the fleshweave brought on, ending the effects and side effects of all consumed maxims.

Your maxims are keyed to your DNA and only induce effects within your flesh, be it meat or metal, and have no effect on other creatures. They are unstable by nature, as your genetic sequence is prone to rearrangement, and become inert if not used before you finish your next short or long rest.

### All Fucked Up

At 7th level, the constant reshaping of your body, from cell to surface, has made you resillient to other sources of genetic alteration and nucleotide corrosion. You gain immunity to poison damage and the poisoned condition, though can choose to be affected by any individual instance of poison damage or the poisoned condition.

Additionally, you can direct your nanophage to cull your weaker internal units to nullify a maxim's negative riders. As a bonus action, you can ignore the side effects of a consumed maxim impacting you for 1 minute. Once you use this feature to ignore side effects, you can not do so again until you finish a long rest.

### Monitor Chip Upgrade: Bioinformatic

At 11th level, your Retributive Monitor Chip can analyze and enforce a target's source code or genome. Any deceptions or technical effects disguising or making a creature invisible immediately end when you chip them, and they cannot benefit from such deceptions or technical effects while chipped. If a creature is reformated or redefined when you chip it, or attempts to reformat or redefine while chipped, it must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw, or revert to its true form or have the attempt fail and be stunned until the end of your next turn.

### Xenomorphic Bleed

At 15th level, you've inherited the peaks of your own advancements into your base state of being, primed to endure and retaliate against even theoretical hazards. You gain the following benefit:

- **Venomous Extremophile.** Your vital fluids or electrical currents can assimilate lesser infections, you gain immunity to disease, though can choose to be affected by any individual instance of a disease, and can choose to carry a single disease while being immune to its effects. Whenever you are hit with a melee attack, the attacking creature takes poison damage equal to your Nanophage modifier and becomes subject to any disease you carry, using your Nanophage DC in place of any DC the disease had — You then lose the carried disease. Additionally, you deal an additional Nanophage Potency of damage when utilizing the BIOHAZARD Lethal Error, and any creature that does not take poison damage from any of your pretender features instead takes half that amount in acid damage (minimum of 1).

Additionally, your Lethal Error feature improves, and you learn the following error.

| Mask Error | Damage Type |
| --- | --- |
| IntrnlFltrErr.BIOHAZARD | Poison |

### Absolute Evosync

At 18th level, the mask has become the face, and every mutable flaw of the body, be it blot, dent, or virus, has become yours to command. Your ability to act as a carrier for an effect you are otherwise immune to now applies to poisons in addition to diseases, and you can act as a carrier for up to three effects, choosing which one you lose and a creature saves against when you are hit with a melee attack.

With this total hazardous integration, your vitality itself is as a maxim for you. As a bonus action, you can immediately end a consumed maxim currently impacting you, then immediately activate the effects of a different maxim you know to take its place. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Nanophage modifier (minimum of 1), regaining all uses when you finish a long rest.
