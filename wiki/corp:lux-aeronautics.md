**Corporation Name.** Lux Aeronautics

**CEO.** Leluxe Prime

**Years in business.** ~85

**Industry.** Luxury Vehicles and AVs

**Motto.** *"Stay classy, stay Lux-urious."*

**Impact.** Minor

Because travel on the ground is so last century. Lux Aeronautics is a premiere corporation for luxury aerial vehicles and other VTOL based manufacturing. They are responsible for producing aerial marvels like the Icarus LX-3 or the Luxstar Aerial Cruiser, ensuring many a corporate exec can venture across Neon-City in comfort, style and most importantly, security from the common folk.

Business partners with [Axigon Arms Dealings](/corp:axigon-arms), Lux Aeronautics was contracted to design a variety of high-end military grade aerial vehicles as well; developing many of the AVs utilized by trauma personnel and the NCPD’s elites such as the ALX-5 Gargouille or its smaller cousin the ALX-3 Grotesque.
