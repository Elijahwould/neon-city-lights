This modification allows any vehicle that has a mechanism that offers cover to properly mount a gun, such that a gun can fire out of the gun port with relative safety, while still giving whoever is behind the cover an adequate amount of protection. It also mounts the gun such that it can be fired without use of both hands, in the event that one is performing another vehicle task (such as driving). It takes an action to properly mount a gun to a gun port.
