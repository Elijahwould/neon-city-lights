##### Source: Player's Handbook

You have mastered ranged weapons and can make shots that others find impossible. You gain the following benefits:

- Attacking at long range doesn't impose disadvantage on your ranged weapon attack rolls.
- Your ranged weapon attacks ignore half and three-quarters cover.
- Any one-handed ranged weapon wielded by you gains the versatile trait. Wielding such a weapon in two hands does not increase its damage die, nor does it qualify for the Marksmanship fighting style, but allows it to use the following feature.
- Before you make an attack with a ranged weapon that you are proficient with and holding in both hands, you can choose to take a -5 penalty to the attack roll. If that attack hits, you add +10 to the attack's damage.
