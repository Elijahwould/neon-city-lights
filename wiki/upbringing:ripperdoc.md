##### Source: Neon City NightsThanks to ActFast231#8365 for this contribution!

Whether you worked as a corporate specialist, or a back-alley dealer, you are adept at installing and maintaining cybernetic augmentations. From new prosthesis to cerebral mods and genetic alterations, you know how to get the most out of a person's body with the wonders of technology. A useful set of skills in the modern world.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Medicine, Technology
- **Tool Proficiencies.** First aid kit, Mechanic's tools
- **Equipment.** Ripperdocs start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A first-aid kit
  - A set of mechanic's tools
  - A set of simple clothes
  - A cyberdoc license
  - A credchip worth $$1,000

### Feature: Cybernetic Specialist

You know how to get the most out of all manner of mechanical augmentations. You have advantage on checks made with mechanic's tools when interacting with cybernetics and machines.
