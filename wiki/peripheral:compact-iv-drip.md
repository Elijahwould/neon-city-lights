##### Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (Staff of Healing)

*Peripheral, rare (requires installation by a [influencer](/influencer), [ordinator](/ordinator) or [shut-in](/shutin))*

**Specs:** 1 lb., $$550,000

Voted the most likely to earn you friends in the field, most likely to increase your rizz, and other democratic accolades, the IV drip has 10 charges, symbolized by the amount of liquid left in the pack. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your tech save DC and techcasting ability modifier: [Combat Dressing](/tech:combat-dressing) (1 charge per spell level, up to 4th), [Auxiliary System Flush](/tech:auxiliary-system-flush) (2 charges), or [Mass Combat Dressing](/tech:mass-combat-dressing) (5 charges).

The IV drip refills 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the IV drip vanishes in a flash of light (how it happens we're still not sure), lost forever.
