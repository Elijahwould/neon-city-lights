## "There are many players in and around Neon City. Best to stay out of sight."

A utopia cannot exist without the pillars of creation and substantiation that uphold the society constructed upon on it. This utopia lives on the rose-tinted lens that is peddled city-wide to ensure none might witness the cracks and chips in the pillars. Those that have finally taken the lens and cast them aside soon realize that nothing appears as rosy as expected. The world beyond the gates of the city contains a history lost and waiting to be found.

Corporate entities are all built for a profit. They are designed to produce something to take one's money in order to produce more of the product. It is a recursive machine that is built on systems older than itself and seeks out said profit by any means necessary. Displacement and terraforming be damned, there's a dollar to be made and a market to be tapped. Some corporations have no such qualms, such as Ignorance and APEX, while others tend to be further insular and isolated from the world at large, such as glitch.tv and the Reliquary. The roots of some corporations are so deeply embedded that it takes nothing short of a miracle (or a black hole antimatter explosive device) to topple one over. And even then, that might not fully remove the vampiric nature some are likened out to be.█
