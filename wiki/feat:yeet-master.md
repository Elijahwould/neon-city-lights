##### Source: [Feats for the Fearsome](https://www.patreon.com/posts/feats-for-16156471) (Javelineer)

*Prerequisite: Thrown Arms Master feat, proficiency with thrown weapons*

Thanks to your advanced skills, you are far deadlier when you decide to yeet things at your enemies. You gain the following benefits:

- When you hit a target with a weapon that naturally has the thrown property, it sticks into their body, shield, or armor, slowing them down. Their base walking speed is reduced by 10 feet until they use an action to remove the weapon.
- If you move at least 25 feet in a straight line on your turn, you have advantage on the first attack you make with a thrown a weapon on this turn.
- Before you make a thrown attack with a weapon, you can choose to take a -5 penalty to the attack roll. If the attack hits, you add +10 to the attack’s damage.
