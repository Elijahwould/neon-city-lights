*1d8 piercing, 2 lb., finesse*

A dueling sword is primarily an easy to handle, one-handed sword, like a rapier, epee, cutlass, or some other weapons, like a chu jian.

### Features

- ***Leading Strike.*** Once per turn, when you miss with an attack against a target while using a dueling sword, your next attack against the same target with the same dueling sword is made with advantage.
- ***Engarde.*** The first time you attack a target with a dueling sword, you gain +1 AC against that target until the start of your next turn.
