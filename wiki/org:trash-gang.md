**Organization Name.** Trash Gang

**Leader.** *Currently leaderless*

**Organization Type.** Scavenger Organization

**Years in existence.** ~20

**Goals.** Product and equipment testing, Exploration and Clean Up

**Creed.** "*One man's trash is our treasure.*"

**Impact.** Moderate

The Trash Gang is a small but growing group, made up primarily by Raccoon (and similar animal) Splicers, and Trash Gremlins. Others are welcome to join, the nature of the gang just leans better towards those of the Trash Panda nature. They’re a surprisingly charitable group, often scavenging through Junk piles, Ruins, and wastelands. Gathering whatever scrap they can and then bringing them back to the gang for them to be re-made into something new. Their creations range from weapons, armor, vehicles to even public shelters, trinkets, and even art pieces!

### Leadership

The Trash Gang is mostly self-sufficient, members often gravitating to their own projects, or simply contributing to all by just stockpiling usable scrap and looking to higher ranked members for guidance. However, there is a chosen elite that stands above all, their identities kept secret from the rest… The Junk Generals.

Each Junk General can individually start projects, pull a certain percentage of resources towards projects, as well as run gigs, and organize their own combat squadrons. These individual actions are however up to scrutiny of their fellow generals. If one is found to be abusing their power they will be stripped of their status, and their identity will be revealed.

### Roles

These describe the general duties of a member, though members aren’t limited to their role. It’s generally what they’re best at.

- **Scavenger.** Scavengers are those who dig through the battlefields, trash heaps, and ruins, gathering treasures, and junk to be brought back to base! They know where to look, how to get it, and get gone!
- **Sorter.** Have a rusted broken gun? A golden toilet seat? A strange glowing metal that you don’t understand? Take it to a Sorter! Sorters know things, it’s their job to identify items, their make-up, and what they can be used for. They often work Closely with Recyclers for projects, and Scavengers in finding gigs or setting them up.
- **Recycler.** Mechanics, Inventors, and all-around nerds. These brilliant minds and hard workers take scrap and turn them into weapons, armor, cosmetics, and Treasures!
- **Incinerator.** Sometimes the best Scrap is in the most perilous of areas. Incinerators are combat specialists who often Join scavengers in their more dangerous dumpster dives to provide extra weaponry and protection. Sometimes though all the good loot hoards seem to run dry. When that happens, Incinerators take up arms in order to refill the trash piles with their enemies' remains!

### Ranks

Ranks describe who is more experienced or senior member within the gang.

- **Lint.** Mostly made up of civvies and Runt Ranked runners.
- **Rubbish.** Made up of higher-level Runts and Scrub runners.
- **Litter.** Made up of Streetwise and Shadow Runners.
- **Trash.** Vigilantes and Street Legends are true Trash material.

### The Power of Color

Working with Trash and Scrap, you see a lot of the drab life sucking grey and brown colors of Rust, Rot, and Ruin. So often Trash gang members will repaint their equipment, or even celebrate when color somehow survives within the junk piles.

So important has color become, that even a superstitious belief system has formed around it—even intertwining itself with the gang’s leadership. It is believed that each color holds a specific quality and that anything that has that color or gets painted that color will obtain said quality. These are the currently agreed upon color qualities:

- **Blue.** Luck
- **Red.** Speed
- **Yellow.** Explosive
- **Purple.** Stealth
- **Orange.** Flames
- **Green.** Chemical
- **Black.** Toughness
- **White.** Deadly
- **Pink.** Beauty/Cuteness

Sidenote: objects that are shiny or reflective (with the notable exception of mirrors and glass) are believed to inherently have more value than others of equal or sometimes better quality. Many even believe that shining an object will further strengthen its color quality.
