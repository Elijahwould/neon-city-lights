##### Source: Player's Handbook (Mage Armor)

*1st-level protection*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a piece of leather)

**Duration:** 8 hours

You touch a willing creature who isn't wearing armor, and a protective magical force surrounds it until the Tech ends. The target's base AC becomes 13 + its Dexterity modifier. The Tech ends if the target dons armor or if you dismiss the Tech as an action.
