##### Source: Player's Handbook (Prayer of Healing)

*2nd-level altercation*

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Up to six creatures of your choice that you can see within range each regain hit points equal to 2d8 + your techcasting ability modifier. This tech has no effect on hacked or constructs.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this tech using a tech slot of 3rd level or higher, the healing increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 2nd.
