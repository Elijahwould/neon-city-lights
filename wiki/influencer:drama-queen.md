## "Drama won't just walk into your life! Either you create it, invite it, or associate with it. So, follow today to make sure you never miss that chance!"

The uncharitable know drama queens as vultures, an insult to the memory of such noble birds, influencers that lurk social media and carefully watch every beef and situationship until they find something juicy enough to pounce on and drag into a forum-spanning, apology video demanding, argument! By their own approximation, however, drama queens simply call things as they see 'em. If all the world is a stage, what's the harm in some literary analysis?

##### Source: Tal'Dorei Reborn (College of Tragedy)

### Insult to Injury

When you join the Channel of the Drama Queen at 3rd level, you've taken to reveling in the misfortune of others and always have something to add. Whenever you or an ally within 30 feet of you roll a 1 on the d20 for an attack roll, an ability check, or a saving throw, you can use your reaction to drop a sick burn and regain one expended use of your Influencer Inspiration feature.

### Start Some Shit

Also at 3rd level, you've mastered the art of the cold read and digging up blackmail. When you or an ally you can see forces a creature to make a saving throw, you can expend one use of your Influencer Inspiration to change the type of saving throw to a Charisma save instead.

If the target fails this save, roll the expended Influencer Inspiration die. The target takes software damage equal to the result, and is filled with anxiety for 1 minute. If the target is reduced to 0 hit points during this time and can speak, they are technically compelled to confess some super hot goss to relieve their conscience before they pass.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Gaslight, Gatekeep, Girlboss

At 6th level, you're great at getting your audience and even your allies to believe your side of the story, none that burn you will survive long. When a creature scores a critical hit against you or an ally within 60 feet of you that you can see, you can use your reaction and expend one use of your Influencer Inspiration to target the attacking creature and dig up some loosely related comment that looks terrible. For 1 minute or until the target suffers a critical hit, any weapon attack against the target scores a critical hit on a roll of 18-20.

At 14th level, the critical hit range of this feature increases to 17–20.

### Malewife, Mansplain, Manipulate

Also at 6th level, your words can twist the affections and wants of others to over-promise to unachievable expectations. When you make an attack roll or a saving throw, you can gain a +10 bonus to the roll, but the next attack roll or saving throw you make takes a -10 penalty. If not used, this penalty disappears when you finish a short or long rest.

You can't use this feature again until you finish a short or long rest, or until you are reduced to 0 hit points.

### Apology Video

Upon reaching 14th level, you can touch a willing creature as an action and allow it absolute sincerity. For 1 minute, the creature is surrounded by somber backing vocals and black and white image filters, granting it the following benefits and drawbacks:

- The creature has a +4 bonus to AC.
- It has advantage on attack rolls and saving throws.
- When the creature hits a target with a weapon attack or tech attack, that target takes an extra 1d10 neon damage.
- Any weapon attack against the creature scores a critical hit on a roll of 18-20.

When this effect ends, the creature immediately drops to 0 hit points and is dying. The creature is also [doomed](/con:doomed) unless it uses what it believes to be its final moments to confess a heartfelt desire. If the confession has as many words as your Charisma modifier, it's sufficiently heartfelt. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
