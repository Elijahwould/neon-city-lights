##### Source: [Carbon Pink](https://slimwiki.com/carbon-pink/upbringings)

You were a corpo-cop, trained to be aggressive, stand-offish, and unquestionably loyal to the questionably thin blue line. You knew how to escalate any problem until a bullet solves it, find someone to pin it on, and bash their head in as a warning. But something on the job snapped you out of this us-and-them mentality, and now all you want to do is get away from the force.

- **Skill Proficiencies.** Athletics, Investigation
- **Tool Proficiencies.** None
- **Weapon Proficiencies.** Baton, Light pistol
- **Equipment.** Ex-cops start their careers with the following equipment:
  - A baton
  - A light pistol
  - A photo of an unsolved crime scene
  - A credchip worth $$1,500

### Feature: Hives of Scum and Villainy

Your experience in enforcing the law, and dealing with lawbreakers, gives you a feel for local laws and criminals. You can easily find the local outpost of a corpo-cop barracks, sheriff’s office or a similar organization, and just as easily pick out the dens of criminal activity in a community, although you’re more likely to be welcome in the former locations rather than the latter.
