##### Source: Neon City Nights

*Weapon (any melee or ranged weapon), legendary (requires installation)*

**Specs:** Variable, $$6,000,000

This weapon frequently whirs, hisses, and steams with a precariously contained series of buffers and automixers constantly brewing high-concentration caustic. You gain a +3 bonus to the attack rolls of this weapon, and it deals an extra 3d4 acid damage on a hit.

When a target you hit with this weapon takes any amount of acid damage as a result of the attack, a number of times per round equal to this weapon’s rarity bonus, you can choose to apply a penalty to their AC equal to this weapon’s rarity bonus until the end of their next turn. A ferrocaustic weapon can only apply this penalty to a number of targets equal to its rarity bonus at a time, and a target can only suffer from the singular highest instance of this penalty at a time.

When a target begins its turn with any amount of AC penalty as a result of damage from a ferrocaustic weapon, it takes a number of d4s in acid damage as determined by the weapon’s rarity.

***Blades.*** A blade is any melee BEA:B/B. Whenever you roll a 20 on your attack roll with a blade, each creature of your choice within 5 feet of the target takes 5 damage of the type dealt by a rare BEA:B/B, 10 damage of the type dealt by a very rare BEA:B/B, and 15 damage of the type dealt by a legendary BEA:B/B.

***Blasters.*** A blaster is any ranged BEA:B/B. Whenever you make an attack roll with a blaster, the ammunition supplies itself from a collection of near-surging nanites. You may still provide your own ammo to benefit from the bonuses of specific ammo, and it is consumed as normal.
